# Exclusion 4.5.2: flood (Home Essentials)

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how exclusion 4.5.2 applies to the Home Essentials policy issued by Saltmarsh Mutual. It forms part of the policy wording and should be read together with the sections on what the policy covers, how claims are made, and the general conditions. Terms in this clause have the meanings given in the policy schedule and definitions section.

## The exclusion

Exclusion 4.5.2 states that Home Essentials excludes flood damage. Saltmarsh Mutual will not pay for loss or damage to the buildings, contents or other property insured under Home Essentials where that loss or damage was caused by flood. This applies whatever the source of the water, including rising rivers, tidal surge, surface water accumulating on land, and water escaping from a natural watercourse onto the insured property.

Where flood damage is excluded, the customer remains responsible for the cost of repairing or replacing the affected property, subject to any other cover that applies under the policy.

## Storm and flood together

Sometimes a single event involves both storm and flood. Where a storm and a flood both damage a Home Essentials home, only the wind and storm damage is covered. Saltmarsh Mutual will assess the damage caused by each and pay for the part attributable to wind and storm, subject to the policy excess and limits. Damage attributable to flood is excluded under exclusion 4.5.2.

When the claim is assessed, the claims handler may ask for photographs, a description of how the damage occurred, and any available weather or incident information. If the cause of a particular item of damage cannot be separated between storm and flood, the claims handler will explain the position in writing and the reasons for it.

## Moving to Home Plus for flood cover

A Home Essentials customer can switch to Home Plus at any time to gain flood cover. Home Plus includes cover for flood damage that is not excluded under that plan. To make the change, the customer should contact Saltmarsh Mutual and ask to upgrade their policy. The new cover will take effect on the date confirmed in writing at the time of the change, and the premium will be adjusted to reflect the revised cover.

Customers should note that a switch to Home Plus does not apply retrospectively to any flood event that occurred before the new cover took effect. Any flood damage that happened before that date remains excluded under Home Essentials.

## Questions and complaints

Customers who are unsure whether an event falls within this exclusion, or who wish to discuss their options, can contact Saltmarsh Mutual's customer service team. If a customer disagrees with a claim decision, they may use the complaints process described in the general conditions of the policy.
