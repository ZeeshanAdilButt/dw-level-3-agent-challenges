# FAQ: home insurance

This FAQ answers common questions about home cover from Saltmarsh Mutual. It explains what our home policies cover for water damage and flooding, how the escape of water limit works, and how excesses apply. It is a guide only. Your policy wording and schedule set out the full terms, and those documents take priority if anything here differs from them.

## Water damage and burst pipes

### What does Saltmarsh Mutual cover for water damage from pipes?

Clause 4.2(b) covers damage caused by water escaping from a burst or leaking fixed water pipe inside the home. This applies to both our Home Essentials and Home Plus policies, subject to the policy terms and the limits described below.

### Is there a limit on how much we pay for escape of water?

Yes. The escape of water limit is £25,000 per claim. This is the most we will pay for damage caused by water escaping from a burst or leaking fixed water pipe under clause 4.2(b) in any one claim.

### What counts as a fixed water pipe?

A fixed water pipe is a pipe that is permanently installed as part of the home's water supply or drainage, such as pipes within walls, under floors or in the loft. Items such as washing machine hoses or the contents of a tank are dealt with under the policy wording, so please check your policy document or contact us if you are unsure.

### What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Turn off the water supply at the stopcock as soon as you safely can, limit further damage by moving belongings and removing standing water, and report the incident to us as soon as possible. Keep any damaged items and take photographs where you can. Our claims team will guide you from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## Flood cover

### Does Home Plus cover flood damage?

Yes. Home Plus covers flood damage to the buildings and contents under clause 4.5. Please read clause 4.5 in your policy document for the conditions that apply.

### Does Home Essentials cover flood damage?

No. Home Essentials excludes flood damage under exclusion 4.5.2. If you want cover for flood damage to your buildings and contents, you would need Home Plus. Please check your policy schedule to confirm which plan you hold.

### If my policy excludes flood, can I still claim for water from a burst pipe?

Possibly. Flood damage and escape of water from a burst or leaking fixed water pipe are different types of loss. Under clause 4.2(b), damage from a burst or leaking fixed water pipe inside the home may be covered on Home Essentials as well as Home Plus, subject to the escape of water limit and the policy terms. Each claim is assessed on its own facts.

## Excess

### What is the excess on Home Essentials?

The Home Essentials standard excess is £250. This is the amount you pay towards each valid claim, and it is taken from the settlement we agree.

### What is the excess on Home Plus?

The Home Plus standard excess is £150. As with Home Essentials, this is the amount you pay towards each valid claim.

### Why are the excesses different between plans?

Each plan is priced and structured differently, and the excess forms part of that structure. Your policy schedule shows the excess that applies to your cover.

## Making a claim

### How do I make a home claim?

You can notify us of a claim by the methods shown on our website or in your policy documents. Please tell us as soon as you become aware of the loss so we can start the process promptly.

### What happens after I report a claim?

We will record the details, confirm what cover applies, and arrange any inspections or repairs needed. We will keep you informed through to settlement. If we need further information from you, we will tell you what we require.

### Can I get a copy of my policy wording?

Yes. You can ask us for a copy of your policy documents at any time. The wording applicable to your plan and any optional riders you hold will set out the full terms.
