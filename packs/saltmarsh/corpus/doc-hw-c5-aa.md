# Clause 5: alternative accommodation

## Purpose

This clause sets out when Saltmarsh Mutual will arrange or pay for alternative accommodation for a policyholder whose home cannot be lived in following an insured event. It applies to Home Essentials and Home Plus policies and should be read together with the policy schedule and the general conditions.

## Scope

Alternative accommodation cover applies where the insured property is damaged to the extent that it is uninhabitable, and the damage is caused by an event the policy covers. The cover is intended to meet the reasonable cost of a comparable place to stay while repairs are carried out. It does not extend to accommodation needed because of works the policyholder has chosen to carry out for reasons other than the insured damage.

## Limits of cover

The maximum amount payable for alternative accommodation depends on the product held. The limits are shown in the table below.

| Product | Maximum payable for alternative accommodation |
|---|---|
| Home Essentials | £8,000 |
| Home Plus | £20,000 |

The limit shown is the total payable for each claim. Where the policyholder's own costs exceed the limit, the excess is not covered unless the policy schedule states otherwise.

## Arranging accommodation

Where practical, Saltmarsh Mutual will arrange suitable accommodation directly with a provider and settle the cost within the limit above. Where the policyholder arranges their own accommodation, they should tell us before committing to the booking so that we can confirm the cost is reasonable and within cover. We will not be responsible for costs incurred before the claim has been notified to us, except where it was not reasonably possible to notify us sooner.

## Standard of accommodation

Accommodation must be of a standard reasonably comparable to the insured home. We will not pay for luxury accommodation or for upgrades that the policyholder chooses. Where the household includes pets or people with specific access needs, we will take these into account when assessing what is reasonable, and we will tell the policyholder if we are unable to find a suitable option within the limit.

## Ending the cover

Alternative accommodation cover ends when the home is fit to live in again, when the claim is settled, or when the limit has been used in full, whichever happens first. Saltmarsh Mutual will confirm in writing when cover is ending so the policyholder has time to make arrangements.

## Interaction with other clauses

This clause operates alongside the home emergency and accidental damage provisions. Where an optional home emergency rider is held, its terms may affect how the costs of temporary accommodation are shared, and the rider wording prevails where there is a conflict.
