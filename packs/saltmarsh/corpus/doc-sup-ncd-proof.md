# Getting proof of your no-claims discount

This guide explains how to request a no-claims discount proof letter from Saltmarsh Mutual, what the letter covers, and how long it stays valid.

## Why you may need proof

Many insurers ask for evidence of your claims history when you move to a new provider. Your no-claims discount is a record of the years you have driven or insured your home without making a claim, and a proof letter lets a new insurer confirm that record. Whether you hold a Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policy, the process is the same.

## How to ask for a no-claims letter

Contact us through your member account or by calling the policy team. Give us your policy number, the name on the policy and the period you need the letter to cover. If you have ended your policy, tell us the end date so we can find the right record. We will confirm your identity before releasing any details.

## When to expect your letter

A no-claims proof letter is sent within seven days of the request. The letter shows the years of cover, the discount earned and the policy type it relates to. If you need it sooner, mention this when you call, and we will tell you what is possible.

## How long the proof remains valid

Proof of no-claims discount is valid for two years after a policy ends. After that point, a new insurer may not accept the letter, so request a fresh one if you are still shopping for cover. Keep a copy of every letter you receive, as a second copy can save time if a future insurer asks for the original.

## If something is missing

If your letter does not show the years you expected, contact the policy team with your details and we will review the record. Please allow the review to run its course before making any decisions about a new policy.
