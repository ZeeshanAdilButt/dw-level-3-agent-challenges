# Clause 4.1: storm cover

## Purpose of this clause

Clause 4.1 sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual treats storm damage to the buildings and contents insured under a home policy. It should be read together with the policy schedule, which shows which products and optional riders apply to a particular policyholder. Where the schedule and this wording differ, the schedule states the cover actually in force for that policy.

## What clause 4.1 covers

Clause 4.1 covers storm damage to the buildings and contents described in the policy schedule. Storm damage means physical loss or damage caused by wind, driven rain, hail, snow or falling objects during a storm event, where the damage is sudden and not the result of wear, lack of maintenance or gradual deterioration. Storm damage to the insured buildings and to the contents within them is covered under the relevant home product, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions in this clause and in the wider policy wording.

## Boundary structures: gates, fences and hedges

Storm cover for boundary structures depends on which home product the policyholder holds. Home Plus covers storm damage to gates, fences and hedges up to £1,000 in any one storm event. Home Essentials does not cover storm damage to gates, fences and hedges. The table below summarises the position for each product.

| Product | Storm damage to gates, fences and hedges |
|---|---|
| Home Essentials | Does not cover storm damage to gates, fences and hedges |
| Home Plus | Covered up to £1,000 for storm damage to gates, fences and hedges |

Where a policyholder holds Home Essentials, a claim for storm damage to gates, fences or hedges cannot be accepted under clause 4.1, and the claims team will explain the position at first notice of loss.

## Items left outside

Garden furniture and items left outside are not covered for storm damage under clause 4.1, whichever home product is held. This applies even where the item is otherwise part of the contents of the home. Policyholders are encouraged to store movable items indoors or secure them before forecast severe weather.

## Making a storm claim

A policyholder should report storm damage as soon as reasonably possible after it is discovered. The claims team will record the first notice of loss, confirm which products and riders apply, and explain what evidence is needed. Reasonable steps should be taken to prevent further damage, such as covering a broken window or making the property secure, and the cost of those steps may be recoverable under the policy. Where the claim involves boundary structures, the claims team will confirm the applicable limit before any settlement offer is made.

## Interaction with optional riders

The optional riders offered by Saltmarsh Mutual, including accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car, do not extend clause 4.1 to cover items that clause 4.1 excludes, unless the rider wording expressly says so. Policyholders should check their schedule and rider wording to confirm what is included.
