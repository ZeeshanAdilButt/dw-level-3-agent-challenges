# Underwriting referral rules

Saltmarsh Mutual Internal Playbook. Staff only. Effective from 2026-06-01.

## Purpose

This playbook sets out when an underwriter must pass a risk to underwriting for a decision rather than handling it at point of sale. It applies to Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to the optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car.

## Mandatory referral triggers

Two conditions always require a referral. Where either applies, the quote must not be issued until underwriting has reviewed and signed off the risk.

A buildings sum insured over £600,000 must be referred to underwriting, whatever the policy type or the rider selection. The referral applies to the total buildings sum insured on the proposal, not to individual sections of a multi-property schedule.

A flood risk score of 7 or more must be referred to underwriting. Flood risk scores are taken from the approved risk data at the time of quotation. Where the score is 7 or more, the case goes to the referral queue even if the customer has accepted the standard terms.

## What to include in a referral

Send the proposal, the flood risk score and the buildings sum insured together, with a short note explaining any mitigating factors such as flood defences or raised floor levels. Incomplete referrals will be returned.

## Staff responsibilities

Do not advise the customer that a quote is approved until underwriting has responded. If a referral is outstanding, tell the customer that additional checks are underway and log the reason in the case notes.
