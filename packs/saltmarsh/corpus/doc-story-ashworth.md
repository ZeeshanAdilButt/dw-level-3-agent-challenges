# Claim story: Ashworth household

## Background

The Ashworth household has been a Saltmarsh Mutual policyholder for several years. Like every member of our mutual, they hold a home policy and a motor policy, and they rely on us to be straightforward when something goes wrong at home.

## What happened

After a storm damaged part of their garden fence and a section of their boundary wall, the household made a claim under their Home Plus policy. Our claims team logged the first notice of loss, arranged for an assessor to visit the property, and worked through the documents the family provided. In the end, the claim was declined because the damage fell outside the cover described in their policy wording.

The family understood the decision but felt the explanation could have been clearer, and they asked us to look at the file again. That request was handled by a senior reviewer who had not been involved in the original decision.

## What we did

The reviewer reread the assessor's report, the policy wording and the photographs taken during the visit. The review was completed and the outcome was explained to the household in a letter and a follow-up phone call, setting out which parts of the policy applied and why.

The file was reviewed after a request within 3 months of the original decision, which is the standard we aim for whenever a policyholder asks us to look again.

## What we learned

This case reminded us that a declined claim should always come with a plain explanation, and that a second look is part of how a mutual earns trust. We have since updated our decline letters to quote the relevant policy wording in plain language.
