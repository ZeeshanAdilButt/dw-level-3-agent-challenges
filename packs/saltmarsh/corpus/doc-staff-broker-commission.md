# Broker commission sheet

## Document control

Document type: internal-rates. Audience: internal (staff only). Authority: rule. Effective from 2026-06-01.

Commission grid reference: commission grid CG-2209-B.

## Purpose

This sheet sets out the commission rates that Saltmarsh Mutual brokers earn on new policies. It covers home and motor business, including the standard rate and the rate that applies to Coastline Brokers. Staff should use these figures when calculating commission on new business and should not apply any other rate without written approval.

## Commission rates

| Broker or product | Policy type | Commission rate |
|---|---|---|
| Brokers (standard) | New home policies | 15% |
| Brokers (standard) | New motor policies | 12% |
| Coastline Brokers | Home policies | 18% |

## Application notes

Commission is calculated on new policies only. Renewals fall outside the scope of this sheet and are handled under separate arrangements. Where a policy includes optional riders, commission is calculated on the base policy premium unless the commission grid CG-2209-B states otherwise.
