# Claim story: Hartley family

## Background

The Hartley family holds a Home Plus policy with Saltmarsh Mutual and has been a policyholder for several years. When a pipe burst beneath their kitchen floor, water spread through the ground floor before the family could shut off the supply. They reported the damage through the app, and the first notice of loss was logged the same day.

## What happened next

A claims handler contacted the family within the same working day to confirm the details, arrange a visit from an assessor, and explain what the policy covered. The assessor inspected the kitchen, the subfloor and the affected cabinets, and recorded the damage with photographs. Because the policy included a home emergency rider, the family was also given temporary arrangements for drying equipment while the claim was reviewed.

## The outcome

The claim was approved after the assessor's report was reviewed. The kitchen was repaired 11 days after the claim was approved, and the family was kept informed at each stage. Mrs Hartley said the updates were clear and that she always knew who to call.

## Why it matters

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, so every claim is handled with the aim of putting members back in their home quickly and fairly. This story shows how prompt communication, a clear assessment and a steady timetable can make a stressful event easier to manage.
