# Regulation and protection

*Effective from 1 July 2026*

## About our authorisation

Saltmarsh Mutual is authorised by the Coastal Financial Authority under firm reference 604112. You can check our authorisation and our permitted activities on the public register kept by the Coastal Financial Authority, using the firm reference above. We are owned by our policyholders, which means that the people who hold our Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies also have a voice in how the company is run. Our regulator sets the rules we must follow on how we sell policies, how we handle claims and how we treat customers fairly.

## Protection if an insurer fails

Insurers can, in rare cases, fail. To protect policyholders in that situation, insurers in our market contribute to the Coastal Policyholder Protection Scheme. If an insurer fails, the scheme pays 100% of compulsory insurance claims and 90% of other claims. Compulsory insurance includes cover that the law requires you to hold, such as motor third party liability. Other claims, such as those under home and comprehensive motor policies, are covered at 90%. The scheme is there to give you security, but it is a last resort, and we work hard to run the business so that it is never needed.

## Handling your complaints

We want to get things right the first time, but we know that sometimes you may feel we have not. If you wish to complain, please tell us, whether by phone, in writing or through our online account pages. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and investigate it fairly. A final response to a complaint is sent within 8 weeks of the day we first receive it. That final response will explain our decision, the reasons for it, and what happens next if you remain dissatisfied.

## Going to the ombudsman

If you are unhappy with the final response you receive, you can take your complaint to the Coastal Insurance Ombudsman. You must do so within 6 months of the date of our final response. The Coastal Insurance Ombudsman is independent of Saltmarsh Mutual and of the Coastal Financial Authority, and its service is free for policyholders. We will always tell you how to contact the Coastal Insurance Ombudsman when we send a final response, and we will cooperate fully with any review it carries out.

## Our commitments to you

Whether you hold a basic policy or have added optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car, the same protections apply. We will explain your cover clearly, handle your claim from the first notice of loss through to settlement with care, and treat your personal information in line with the rules that govern us. If you have any questions about how we are regulated or protected, please contact our customer team, who will be glad to help.
