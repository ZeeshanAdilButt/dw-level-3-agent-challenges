# Motor policy wording: paying your premium

## Purpose of this clause

This clause explains the ways you can pay for your Saltmarsh Mutual motor policy, what happens if a payment is missed, and the charges that may apply. It should be read together with your policy schedule, which shows the premium, the cover you have chosen and any optional riders such as accidental damage, legal expenses or a courtesy car. Where this clause and your schedule differ on how a payment is taken, the schedule applies to the amounts shown on it.

## Payment options

You can pay your motor premium in one of two ways. The first is an annual payment, where the full premium for the policy year is paid when the policy starts or renews. No interest is added to an annual payment. The second is monthly payment, which spreads the premium across several instalments. Paying monthly carries interest of 14.9% APR, which is included in the monthly amount shown to you before you agree to the policy. The interest rate is shown in full on your quotation and schedule so that you can compare the total cost of each option before you choose.

Monthly payments are normally collected by direct debit from the bank account you nominate. You must tell us in writing if your bank account changes, and you should keep sufficient funds available on each collection date. We will tell you in advance if a collection date changes.

## Failed payments

If a direct debit payment is returned unpaid, we will try to collect it again. A failed direct debit payment carries a £12 fee, which is added to your account. This fee covers the administrative cost of the failed collection and the follow-up we need to make. We will write to you to explain the failed payment, the amount outstanding and the fee added, and we will tell you how to bring your account up to date.

## Missed payments and cancellation

If you miss a monthly payment, we will contact you to arrange payment and explain what you need to do. Your cover is not automatically removed the moment a single payment is missed, but you should act quickly to keep your policy in force. If two monthly motor payments are missed, Saltmarsh can cancel your motor policy by giving 14 days written notice. The notice will state the amount owed, the date on which cancellation takes effect, and how you can stop cancellation by paying what is outstanding before that date.

Where a policy is cancelled for non-payment, you remain responsible for any premium that is due up to the date cancellation takes effect. You must not drive a vehicle that requires motor insurance while your cover is not in force. If you want to stop cover because you no longer need it, you should contact us so that we can arrange cancellation and any refund in line with the terms of your policy.

## Contacting us about payments

If you are finding it difficult to keep up with payments, please contact us as early as possible. We will look at the options available to you, which may include arranging a different payment date or agreeing a short plan to clear arrears. Getting in touch early gives us the best chance of keeping your cover in place and avoiding unnecessary charges.
