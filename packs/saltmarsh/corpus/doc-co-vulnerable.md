# Extra support

## Who this guide is for

This guide explains how Saltmarsh Mutual supports customers who need extra help at any point in their policy or claim. Some customers find it harder to manage paperwork, deadlines or phone calls because of illness, bereavement, a disability, language needs or a difficult period in their lives. Our aim is to make sure those customers are never left to struggle alone.

## How to get help

Customers who need extra support can reach a dedicated team on 0800 555 0190. The team can explain your policy, help you complete forms, arrange contact in a format that suits you, and keep you updated on the progress of a claim. You do not need to be an existing policyholder with a claim open to call, and you are welcome to ask a friend or family member to join the call with you.

## What the dedicated team can do

The team can agree a single point of contact for your case, so you do not have to repeat your situation to different people. They can also note your preferred way of being contacted, such as email, post or a call at a particular time of day. Where a claim involves a home or vehicle that you cannot use while the work is done, the team will explain which of your cover options may apply, including any riders you have chosen.

## Your rights and our commitments

Whatever your circumstances, we will treat you fairly, explain decisions in plain language and tell you clearly what happens next. If you are unhappy with the support you receive, you can ask for your concern to be passed to a senior member of staff, and our complaints process will be explained to you in writing.
