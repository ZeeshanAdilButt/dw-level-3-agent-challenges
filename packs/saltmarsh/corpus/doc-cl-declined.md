# If your claim is declined

This guide explains what happens when Saltmarsh Mutual decides not to pay a claim, what the decision letter contains, and how you can ask us to review that decision. It applies to claims made under Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, including any optional riders you hold for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car.

## What the status "declined" means

When we record a claim as declined, it means Saltmarsh has decided the claim is not covered under your policy and has written to explain why. The decline is a decision about whether the event you reported falls within the cover you hold, not a judgement about your honesty or your history as a member. Some claims are declined because the loss is excluded by the policy wording, some because the event happened outside the policy period, and some because a condition of the policy was not met at the time of the loss. Your decision letter will state which of these applies to your claim.

## What the decision letter contains

The letter is written to explain the decision in plain terms. It will set out the date of the loss and the date we received your claim, the section of your policy wording or rider we have relied on, and the facts we have taken into account. Where we have relied on information from an engineer, a repairer, a loss adjuster or another third party, the letter will say so. It will also tell you if any further evidence would change the outcome, and it will give the contact details for the claims team handling your case.

Please read the letter carefully before you decide what to do next. If anything in it is unclear or appears to be factually wrong, contact us and ask for the specific point to be explained. Many questions are resolved at this stage without any need for a formal review.

## How to ask for a review

If you disagree with the decision, you can ask us to review it. A request to review a declined claim must be made within 3 months of the decision letter. Please include the claim reference number, the reason you believe the decision should be looked at again, and any evidence you have that was not available when the claim was first assessed, such as photographs, receipts, independent reports or statements from witnesses.

A review is carried out by a member of the claims team who did not make the original decision. They will consider the policy wording, the evidence on file and the new information you have provided. We will write to you with the outcome and the reasons for it. If the review confirms the original decision, the letter will explain how you can take the matter further.

## Keeping the time limit in view

The three months run from the date of the decision letter, not from the date you received it by post or read it online, so please act promptly. If you need more time because of a particular circumstance, contact us before the period ends and tell us why, and we will consider your request. Making a review request does not affect any other rights you may have, and you are welcome to seek independent advice at any stage.
