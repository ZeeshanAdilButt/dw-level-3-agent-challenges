# Motor policy wording: modifications

## Scope

This clause applies to all Saltmarsh Mutual motor policies, including Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to any optional riders attached to them. It sets out how changes made to an insured vehicle must be handled under the policy and how they affect cover.

## Declaring modifications

Any change that alters the look, engine power or performance of the vehicle must be declared to Saltmarsh before the change is used on a public road. This includes changes to bodywork, wheels, suspension, exhaust systems, engine tuning and window tinting. The policyholder is responsible for telling us about the change, and for confirming that it is accurately reflected on the policy schedule. A change made after the policy started is treated in the same way as a change present at the start of cover.

## Effect on claims

Loss involving a modification that was not declared to Saltmarsh is not covered. Where an undeclared modification contributed to or caused a loss, we may decline the claim in whole or in part, and we will explain our reasoning in writing. Where a modification has been declared, the claim will be assessed on its own facts, and the declared change will be taken into account in deciding the outcome.

## Tinted windows

Window tinting is a modification within the meaning of this clause. Tinting must be declared, and it must comply with the road traffic rules that apply where the vehicle is used. Where tinting has not been declared, the rule on undeclared modifications applies in full.
