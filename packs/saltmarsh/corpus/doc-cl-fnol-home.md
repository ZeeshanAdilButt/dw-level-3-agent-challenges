# Reporting a home claim

## Purpose and scope

This document sets out how a policyholder of Saltmarsh Mutual reports a home claim, what information to have ready, and what to do in the first hour after a loss. It applies to claims under Home Essentials and Home Plus, and to any optional riders that relate to the home, including the home emergency rider. It takes effect on 1 July 2026.

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders. We aim to make reporting a claim as simple and as fast as we can, and we will keep you informed at each stage until the claim is settled.

## Who can report a claim

Any policyholder named on the policy, or a named adult acting on the policyholder's behalf, may report a home claim. If you are reporting for someone else, have their consent and the policy details to hand.

## What to have ready

Before you call or start an online report, gather the following:

1. Your policy number, which appears on your policy schedule and your renewal documents.
2. The date of the loss, meaning the day the damage or incident happened or was discovered.
3. A short description of what happened and what was damaged, in your own words.
4. A list of the items or parts of the property affected, with approximate ages where you know them.
5. Any photographs, receipts or other evidence you already have. You do not need to collect everything before you report.

Only the policy number, the date of the loss and a short description are needed to open a claim. The rest can be provided later.

## What to do in the first hour

After a home loss, take sensible steps to stop further damage. The policy requires this, and it also protects your home and your belongings.

1. Make the home safe. If there is a risk of fire, flooding, gas or electrical danger, turn off the relevant supply at its isolation point where it is safe to do so, and leave the property if you believe it is unsafe.
2. Contact the emergency services if there is a risk to life, a fire, a gas leak or a crime in progress.
3. Stop further damage where it is safe. Cover broken windows or roof openings, move undamaged belongings away from water, and turn off the water supply if a pipe has burst.
4. Keep the receipts for any emergency repairs or temporary measures you pay for, and keep the damaged items until we have had the chance to inspect them, unless they are a safety hazard.
5. Take photographs or video of the damage and of the steps you have taken, before anything is cleaned up or removed.
6. Report the claim to us as soon as you reasonably can, using the steps below.

## How to report a home claim

Follow these steps in order.

1. Contact the claims line on 0800 555 0142. The claims line is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, so you can report a loss at any time.
2. Give the claims adviser your policy number, the date of the loss and a short description of what happened.
3. Ask for your claim reference. Claim references take the form CLM-26-0412: the letters CLM, followed by the year and a four-digit number. Keep this reference for all future contact about the claim.
4. If you need emergency help at the property, tell the adviser. Where the home emergency rider applies, the adviser will explain how to arrange urgent assistance.
5. Make a note of the name of the adviser you spoke to, the time of the call and what was agreed.
6. Expect a follow-up from us confirming the next steps, including whether an inspection is needed and what further information we require.

## What happens next

Once the claim is reported, we will:

1. Confirm that the claim has been registered and send you the claim reference in writing.
2. Check that the policy was in force on the date of the loss and that the loss is of a type covered by your policy.
3. Arrange an inspection or ask for further evidence where needed. We will tell you in advance who will attend and what they will need from you.
4. Keep you informed of progress and of any decision on the claim.
5. Settle the claim in line with the terms of your policy and make payment or arrange repairs as agreed.

If we need information from you, we will tell you exactly what is needed and why. Please respond as quickly as you can, because delays can slow the settlement of your claim.

## Responsibilities and cooperation

To help us handle your claim fairly and promptly, you must:

1. Tell us the truth and give us complete information about the loss.
2. Cooperate with our inspection and enquiries.
3. Take sensible steps to stop further damage, as described above.
4. Tell us promptly if you receive any other claim, demand or correspondence about the same loss.

If you are unsure whether something is covered, or whether a step you have taken is sensible in your circumstances, call the claims line and ask. We would rather answer the question early than resolve a problem later.

## Contact and further help

Claims line: 0800 555 0142, open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Please quote your claim reference, in the form CLM-26-0412, on all correspondence and calls about the claim.
