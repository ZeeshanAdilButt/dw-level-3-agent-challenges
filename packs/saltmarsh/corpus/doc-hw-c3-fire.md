# Clause 3.1: fire cover

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out the fire cover provided under Saltmarsh Mutual home policies. It applies to the insured home and to the contents listed in the policy schedule. It should be read together with the General Conditions and the Definitions section of the policy wording.

## Cover provided

Under Clause 3.1, Saltmarsh Mutual covers fire, smoke, lightning and explosion damage to the home and contents. Cover applies where the damage happens during the period of insurance and at the insured address stated in the schedule. Damage to the home includes permanent fixtures, fittings and structures that form part of the building. Damage to contents includes personal belongings owned by the policyholder or members of their household that are kept at the insured address.

## What is meant by each peril

For the purposes of this clause:

"Fire" means combustion that causes flame, heat or scorching that spreads beyond a place where a fire was intended to be, such as a hearth, stove or grate.

"Smoke" means smoke or soot that results suddenly and unexpectedly from a fire, a fault in heating or cooking equipment, or a failure of an electrical installation. Smoke caused by normal, intended use of a fireplace, or by gradual deterioration, is not covered under this clause.

"Lightning" means direct damage caused by a lightning strike to the insured property, including damage caused by a power surge resulting from that strike.

"Explosion" means a sudden and violent release of energy causing damage. Explosion does not include damage caused by the bursting of a boiler or pressure vessel, which may be covered elsewhere in the policy.

## Smoke alarm condition

Cover under this clause is conditional on the insured home having working smoke alarms fitted and maintained in accordance with the requirements set out in the policy schedule. Where the policyholder has been notified of a requirement to fit or maintain smoke alarms, and an alarm required by the policy was not fitted or was not working at the time of the loss, Saltmarsh Mutual may reduce the amount payable for the fire damage by the proportion that the failure contributed to the loss. Saltmarsh Mutual will consider the circumstances of each claim, including whether the absence or failure of an alarm made a difference to the outcome.

Policyholders are encouraged to test their smoke alarms regularly and to replace batteries or units as the manufacturer recommends.

## Exclusions

Clause 3.1 does not cover:

- damage caused by a fire deliberately started by the policyholder or any person living at the insured address;
- damage caused by heat, smoke or scorching from a fire that was intended to occur in the normal course of use, such as cooking or heating;
- damage caused by a fault in a building or structure that existed before the start of the policy and that was known to the policyholder and not disclosed;
- loss or damage to property that is unattended and left outside in the open.

Other exclusions in the General Conditions also apply.

## Claims

Policyholders should report fire, smoke, lightning or explosion damage to Saltmarsh Mutual as soon as reasonably practical after it is discovered. Early notice allows the claims team to arrange assessment, make the property safe and begin settlement. Policyholders should take reasonable steps to protect the property from further damage, keep damaged items for inspection where it is safe to do so, and keep records of any costs incurred in making temporary repairs.

## Interaction with other cover

Where the policyholder has also purchased optional riders, such as accidental damage or home emergency, the fire damage is dealt with under this clause and not under those riders, unless the policy wording states otherwise. Saltmarsh Mutual will confirm in writing which section applies to a particular claim.
