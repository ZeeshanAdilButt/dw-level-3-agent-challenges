# Complaints and the ombudsman

## Purpose and scope

This document sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual handles complaints about its home and motor policies, from the first contact through to the final response and the ombudsman route. It applies to all customers, whether they hold Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive, and to any optional riders such as accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car. It also applies to complaints about how a claim has been handled, from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, whether spoken or written, about our products, our service or the way we have handled a claim. You do not need to use the word "complaint" for us to treat your contact as one. If you are unsure whether something is a complaint, we will ask you rather than assume it is not.

## How to complain

1. Contact us by phone, in writing, by email or through your online account. Give your policy number, the name of the policyholder and a short description of the problem.
2. Tell us what outcome you would like, so that we can consider it when we investigate.
3. Keep copies of any letters, emails or reference numbers you receive. These help us find the file quickly.
4. If the complaint relates to a claim, quote the claim reference number if you have one.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days. The acknowledgement confirms the date we received it and the name or team responsible for dealing with it.
2. We investigate the complaint. This may include reviewing your policy wording, the claim file, notes of calls and any evidence you have provided. We may contact you for more information, and we will tell you if we need something from you to continue.
3. We send you a final response within 8 weeks of the date we first received your complaint. Where we cannot reach a decision within that time, we will write to you before the 8 weeks end, explain why, and tell you when we expect to reply.
4. The final response sets out our decision, the reasons for it, and any action we propose to take, such as a payment, a correction to your file or an apology.

## Keeping you informed

We aim to keep you updated at reasonable intervals while the complaint is open. If we promise to call you back or send something by a particular date, we will do so, or we will tell you in advance if we cannot.

## Going to the ombudsman

If you are unhappy with our final response, you can take your complaint to the Coastal Insurance Ombudsman. You must do this within 6 months of the date of our final response. The ombudsman is independent of Saltmarsh Mutual and can review the complaint and make a binding decision on us where appropriate.

Before referring a complaint, we will confirm the date of the final response and remind you of the 6 months limit. We will also provide the contact details for the Coastal Insurance Ombudsman in the final response letter.

## Our commitments

We will treat every complaint fairly, record it accurately, and use what we learn to improve our products and service. Complaints will be handled by staff who were not involved in the original decision wherever practical.
