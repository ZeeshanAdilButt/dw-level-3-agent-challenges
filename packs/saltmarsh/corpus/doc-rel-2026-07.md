# Service update: new policy wordings

## What is changing

Saltmarsh Mutual is publishing new policy wordings for its home and motor products. The July 2026 wordings apply to policies that start or renew on or after 1 July 2026. Policies that started or renewed before that date continue under the wording shown on their current schedule until their next renewal.

The update covers Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive. It also covers the optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and a courtesy car. The aim is to set out cover, exclusions and claim steps in plainer language, so that members can see what is included before they need to make a claim.

## What members should do

Members do not need to take any action before their renewal. When a renewal falls on or after 1 July 2026, the new wording will be sent with the renewal documents. Please read the summary of changes at the front of the wording, and contact us if anything is unclear or if you would like to discuss your cover before you renew.

## How claims are handled

The claims process itself is not changing. Members can still report a loss from first notice through to settlement using the same channels as before. The new wordings simply describe each step more clearly, including what information we will ask for and roughly how each stage works.

## Questions

If you have questions about the new wordings, please contact our member services team. Our advisers can explain how the changes relate to your own policy.
