# Motor policy wording: how to read it

## Purpose of this document

This document explains how to read the motor policy wording issued by Saltmarsh Mutual. It is written for policyholders, prospective policyholders and anyone who needs to understand what a motor policy covers and what it does not. Saltmarsh Mutual is a home and motor insurer owned by its policyholders, and we want the terms of our motor cover to be clear and easy to find.

## The wording that applies

The current motor policy wording is coded MW-2026-07. This is the wording that sets out the terms, conditions and exclusions for our motor products. Where this document refers to a clause, it refers to a clause in MW-2026-07 unless it says otherwise.

The July 2026 wordings apply to policies that start or renew on or after 1 July 2026. If your policy started or last renewed before that date, the wording that was issued at the time continues to govern your cover until your next renewal, at which point the MW-2026-07 wording will apply.

## The two motor products covered

The motor wording MW-2026-07 applies to both Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive. The two products sit under the same wording but offer different levels of cover, so it is important to check which product you hold before relying on any particular clause.

- Motor Third Party, product code MOT-TPO-1, covers your liability to other people and their property arising from the use of your vehicle. It does not cover damage to your own vehicle.
- Motor Comprehensive, product code MOT-CMP-2, covers liability to third parties and also covers damage to your own vehicle, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions in the wording.

Your policy schedule shows which product you hold and the product code that applies to it. If the schedule and this document appear to differ, the policy schedule and the MW-2026-07 wording take priority.

## Optional riders

Both products can be extended with optional riders. Each rider is an additional clause that adds specific cover or assistance. The riders available for motor cover include accidental damage, legal expenses and a courtesy car. A rider only applies if it is shown on your policy schedule. Riders carry their own terms, and those terms are set out alongside the MW-2026-07 wording.

## How to read a clause

Every clause in MW-2026-07 follows a similar pattern, and understanding that pattern will help you find the answer you need.

1. The insuring clause describes what the policy covers.
2. The conditions describe what you must do to keep the cover valid, such as giving accurate information when you take out the policy and telling us about changes in circumstances.
3. The exclusions describe what the policy does not cover.
4. The definitions explain the meaning of specific words, such as "accident", "third party" or "market value", wherever those words are capitalised or used in a defined sense.

Read the insuring clause together with the exclusions. A claim is most likely to be affected by the exclusions, so it is worth checking them before you assume a loss is covered.

## Making a claim

When something happens that may lead to a claim, tell us as soon as you reasonably can. This first notice of loss starts the claims process. We will ask for the details we need to assess the claim and keep you updated until it is settled. The conditions in MW-2026-07 set out what we need from you and when, so refer to those clauses if you are unsure.

## Questions about the wording

If a clause is unclear, contact us before relying on your reading of it. We would rather explain a term in advance than have a misunderstanding arise at the point of claim. Our contact details are available on our website and in your policy documents.
