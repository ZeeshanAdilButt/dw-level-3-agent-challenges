# How claims work

This document sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual handles a claim from the first notice of loss through to settlement. It applies to all claims under Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to claims made under the optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and a courtesy car. It takes effect from 1 July 2026.

## Who this applies to

This process applies to every policyholder who makes a claim with Saltmarsh Mutual, and to anyone acting on a policyholder's behalf with their permission. Because Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, we aim to handle every claim fairly, clearly and within the timescales described below.

## The claim journey in steps

The steps below describe the usual path for a claim. Individual claims may need extra steps where more information is required, and we will tell you when that happens.

1. **Notify us of the loss.** You can report a claim by phone, through your online account or through the Saltmarsh Mutual app. Tell us what happened, when it happened and what has been damaged or affected. Report the loss as soon as you reasonably can, because delays can make a claim harder to assess.

2. **Receive your claim reference.** When you tell us about the loss, we record it and give you a claim reference. Claim references follow the format CLM-26-0412: the letters CLM, followed by the year, followed by a four-digit number. Please quote this reference in every message about the claim so we can find your file quickly.

3. **Receive our acknowledgement.** Saltmarsh acknowledges a new claim within 2 working days of it being reported. The acknowledgement confirms that we have your claim and includes your claim reference.

4. **Speak with a claims handler.** A claims handler contacts the policyholder within 5 working days of the claim being received. The handler will confirm the cover that applies under your policy, explain what information we need and agree the next steps with you. Where a rider such as the courtesy car or home emergency cover is relevant, the handler will explain how it applies.

5. **Provide the information we need.** You may be asked for documents such as photographs, repair estimates, police reference numbers, receipts or proof of ownership. We will tell you exactly what is needed and why. Sending complete information early helps us reach a decision sooner.

6. **Decision on the claim.** Saltmarsh aims to decide a claim within 10 working days of receiving all the documents. This timescale runs from the point when we hold everything needed to make the decision, not from the date the claim was first reported. If we need more time, or if a decision depends on a third party such as a repairer or another insurer, we will tell you why and keep you informed.

7. **Settlement.** Where we agree a claim, we will explain how the settlement is made, whether by repair, replacement or payment. An agreed settlement is paid within 5 working days of agreement. Where repairs are arranged through an approved supplier, payment is made in line with the terms agreed with that supplier.

8. **Closing the claim.** Once the settlement is complete, we will confirm in writing that the claim is closed. If anything remains outstanding, such as a further payment or a repair still in progress, we will say so.

## Keeping you informed

Throughout the claim we will keep you updated about progress. If something changes, such as a delay or a request for further information, we will contact you using the details you have given us. Please let us know promptly if your contact details change, so that we can reach you.

## If we decline or cannot agree a claim

If we are unable to accept a claim, we will explain the reasons in writing and refer to the relevant part of your policy wording. We will also explain how you can ask us to review the decision. If you remain unhappy after that review, you can make a formal complaint, and we will set out the steps for doing so in our reply.

## Working together

Most claims are handled smoothly when policyholders report losses promptly, keep their claim reference handy and respond to requests for information. Our claims handlers are there to help you through each stage, so please contact us if anything is unclear.
