# Clause 4.6: subsidence

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual covers subsidence under its home insurance products, including the excess that applies to a subsidence claim and the waiting period before cover begins. It should be read together with the policy schedule and the general conditions of the relevant home product.

## Scope

This clause applies to Home Essentials and Home Plus. Subsidence cover is included as part of these products in the way described below. Motor products are not affected by this clause.

## Subsidence excess

The subsidence excess is £1,000 on both home products. This is the amount the policyholder pays towards each valid subsidence claim, and it is deducted from the amount Saltmarsh Mutual would otherwise pay for that claim. The subsidence excess applies in addition to any other excess shown on the policy schedule for a different type of claim.

## Waiting period for subsidence cover

Subsidence cover starts 60 days after a new policy begins. Any subsidence damage that is first reported within this waiting period is not covered under the policy. The waiting period applies to new policies only, and it is measured from the date the policy starts, not the date the policyholder first applies for cover.

## Engineer survey

Saltmarsh Mutual pays for the engineer survey needed to investigate a subsidence claim. The policyholder does not need to arrange or fund the survey. Saltmarsh Mutual will appoint a suitably qualified engineer, and the survey will be used to establish the cause of the damage and the appropriate repair. The policyholder must give the engineer reasonable access to the property and any information the engineer reasonably requests.

## Notifying a subsidence claim

A policyholder who suspects subsidence should notify Saltmarsh Mutual as soon as reasonably practicable after the damage is first noticed. Prompt notification allows the engineer survey to take place without delay and helps prevent further damage to the property. Delay in reporting may affect the handling of the claim.

## Related terms

Where this clause and the policy wording differ, the policy schedule and the general conditions apply first, and this clause applies to the extent it is consistent with them. Any question about how this clause applies to a particular claim should be referred to the Saltmarsh Mutual claims team.
