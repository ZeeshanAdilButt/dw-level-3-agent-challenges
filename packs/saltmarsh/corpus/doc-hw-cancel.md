# Home policy wording: cancellation and cooling-off

This clause sets out how a Saltmarsh Mutual home policy may be cancelled, including the cooling-off period, any fee that applies after it, the treatment of premiums on cancellation, and the notice we require. It applies to Home Essentials and Home Plus, together with any optional riders attached to those policies. It takes effect from 1 July 2026 and forms part of the policy wording issued to every home policyholder.

## Purpose and scope

This clause governs the termination of a home policy by the policyholder before the end of the period for which cover was arranged. It does not govern the cancellation of a motor policy, which is set out in the separate motor wording. Where a rider is attached to a home policy, cancelling the home policy also ends the rider, and the rider's own terms do not change the treatment of premiums set out below.

## The cooling-off period

The cooling-off period on a home policy is 14 days, beginning on the day the policy is issued or the day the policyholder receives the policy documents, whichever is later. During this period, you may change your mind about the cover and ask us to cancel the policy without giving a reason. The cooling-off period exists so that you can review the documents and confirm that the cover meets your needs before you rely on it.

## Cancelling within the cooling-off period

If you cancel in the cooling-off period and have made no claim, you get back the premium less the days you were covered. The refund is calculated on a daily basis from the start date of the policy to the date on which we receive your written cancellation request. For example, if you cancel on the fifth day, the premium refunded will be the premium paid less the five days of cover you received. We will not charge a fee for cancelling within this period.

If you have made a claim during the cooling-off period, the cancellation rules differ. Where a claim has been made, no premium is refunded after the cooling-off period in relation to that claim year, and any refund within the cooling-off period will be reduced to reflect the claim as set out in the claims section of this wording. We will explain the position clearly in writing when you contact us to cancel.

## Cancelling after the cooling-off period

After the cooling-off period has ended, you may still cancel your home policy at any time by giving us notice in accordance with the notice requirements below. Cancelling a home policy after the cooling-off period carries a £25 fee. This fee is charged once per cancellation and is deducted from any premium refund due to you. Where the refund due is less than the fee, the difference will be invoiced to you.

Where you cancel after the cooling-off period, we will refund any premium paid for cover that has not yet been used, calculated on a daily basis from the effective date of cancellation to the end of the period for which the premium was paid, less the £25 fee described above. However, if you have made a claim in that policy year, no premium is refunded after the cooling-off period, and the fee does not apply to a cancellation that results from a claim being settled.

## Refunds

Refunds are paid by the same method used to pay the premium unless you ask us in writing to use a different method that we accept. Refunds will normally be made within a reasonable period after we receive and process your cancellation request. Where a refund is calculated on a daily basis, the calculation will be shown in writing so that you can check it. Any refund is subject to the deductions described in this clause and to any amount you owe us under the policy.

## Notice of cancellation

To cancel a home policy, you must give us notice in writing, either by post, by secure message through your online account, or by any other written method we make available for this purpose. Notice takes effect on the date we receive it, unless you ask for a later date in your notice. Telephone requests may be accepted to start the process, but they will not take effect until confirmed in writing.

We may cancel a home policy only on the grounds set out in the general conditions of the policy, and we will give you written notice of any such cancellation and the reasons for it. Where we cancel the policy, the refund of premium will be calculated on the same daily basis as above, and no cancellation fee will be charged to you.

## Changes to this clause

We may update this clause from time to time to reflect changes in law, regulation or our products. Where a change affects your rights, we will tell you in writing before it takes effect. The version of this clause that applies to your policy is the version in force at the date your policy was issued, unless a change is required by law or is in your favour.
