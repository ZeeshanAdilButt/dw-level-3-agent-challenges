# Motor policy excess table

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out the excess amounts that apply to motor claims under Saltmarsh Mutual policies. An excess is the amount a policyholder pays towards a claim before Saltmarsh Mutual pays the rest. This document is effective from 2026-07-01 and applies to all Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies in force on or after that date, and to new policies issued from that date.

## Summary of excesses

The table below lists each excess by product and cover. Where an amount is added on top of another, the table shows it as an extra. Amounts are in pounds sterling.

| Product | Cover | Excess type | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motor Third Party | All claims | Compulsory excess | £200 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Windscreen repair | Windscreen repair excess | £0 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Windscreen replacement | Windscreen replacement excess | £85 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Accidental damage | Accidental damage excess | £250 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Fire | Fire excess | £250 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Theft | Theft excess | £300 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Accidental damage, fire and theft | Young driver extra excess | £400 |
| Motor Comprehensive | Accidental damage, fire and theft | Inexperienced driver extra excess | £200 |
| Motor Comprehensive and Motor Third Party | Any claim where chosen | Voluntary motor excess option | £100, £250 or £500 |

## Compulsory excess on Motor Third Party

Every Motor Third Party claim carries a compulsory excess of £200. This applies on any claim, whatever the cause and whatever the value of the claim. The policyholder pays the first £200 of each claim, and Saltmarsh Mutual pays the remaining eligible amount, subject to the terms of the policy.

## Windscreen excesses on Motor Comprehensive

Under Motor Comprehensive, the windscreen repair excess is £0, so a policyholder who has a windscreen repaired pays no excess for that repair. Where a windscreen must be replaced rather than repaired, the windscreen replacement excess is £85. The replacement excess is payable once per claim and applies in place of the standard accidental damage excess for that windscreen claim.

## Accidental damage, fire and theft excesses on Motor Comprehensive

The following excesses apply to Motor Comprehensive claims for the named covers:

- The accidental damage excess is £250.
- The fire excess is £250.
- The theft excess is £300.

These amounts apply to the relevant cover only. A single incident that involves more than one of these covers is subject to the excess for each cover that is claimed under, unless the policy wording states otherwise.

## Young driver and inexperienced driver extra excesses

Where the policyholder or a named driver is a young driver, a young driver extra excess of £400 is added to accidental damage, fire and theft claims on Motor Comprehensive. The young driver extra excess is payable in addition to the standard excess for the cover claimed.

Where the policyholder or a named driver is an inexperienced driver, an inexperienced driver extra excess of £200 is added to accidental damage, fire and theft claims. The inexperienced driver extra excess is also payable in addition to the standard excess for the cover claimed.

Where both extras would otherwise apply to the same claim, the policy schedule sets out how they are combined. Policyholders should check their schedule or contact Saltmarsh Mutual for confirmation before making a claim.

## Voluntary motor excess

A policyholder may choose to add a voluntary motor excess to the compulsory excess in order to reduce the premium. The voluntary excess options are £100, £250 or £500. The chosen voluntary excess is added to the compulsory excess on Motor Third Party and to the applicable excesses on Motor Comprehensive for any claim where it is chosen.

The voluntary excess does not replace the compulsory excess. It is always payable on top of it, and it is shown separately on the policy schedule. A policyholder who wishes to change the voluntary excess should contact Saltmarsh Mutual before the change is needed.

## How excesses are applied to a claim

When Saltmarsh Mutual settles a claim, it first identifies the product, the cover and the driver details that apply. It then deducts the relevant excess or excesses from the eligible claim amount. The policyholder is informed of the excess in the claim settlement communication. If a policyholder is unsure which excess applies, they can ask Saltmarsh Mutual at any stage of the claim, from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## Changes to this clause

Saltmarsh Mutual may update this clause from time to time. Any change will be published as a new version of this document with a new effective date, and existing policies will be treated in line with the terms that apply on the date of the relevant claim.
