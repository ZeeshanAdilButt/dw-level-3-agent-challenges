# Home policy wording: conditions

Saltmarsh Mutual Home Essentials and Home Plus policies, general conditions. Effective from 2026-07-01.

## Introduction and how these conditions fit together

These general conditions apply to every home insurance policy issued by Saltmarsh Mutual. They sit alongside your policy schedule, which sets out your name, the insured property, the sums insured, any optional riders you have chosen, and the excess that applies to each section of cover. Where a condition in this wording and a condition in your schedule appear to conflict, the schedule governs for the specific figure or detail it states, and this wording governs everything else.

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders. That means the rules in this wording are designed to keep the mutual fair across all members, not only the member making a particular claim. Where we use the word "you", we mean the policyholder named in the schedule and any other adult living at the insured address who is covered under the policy. Where we use "we", "us" or "our", we mean Saltmarsh Mutual.

These conditions are written to be read in plain language. Headings are for convenience only and do not change the meaning of any clause. If you are unsure how a condition applies to your situation, contact us before you act, because a short conversation at the start is usually simpler than a dispute later.

## Keeping your policy accurate

Your policy is based on the information you gave us when you took out or last renewed your cover. We rely on that information to decide whether we can offer cover, on what terms, and at what price. For that reason, you must keep the details you gave us accurate and up to date throughout the life of the policy.

You must tell Saltmarsh about any change that affects your cover within 14 days of the change happening. Changes that affect cover include, for example, a change in who lives at the insured address, a change in how the property is used, building work that alters the structure, or the addition of a home business activity. If the change would have led us to offer different terms or a different price had we known about it, we will assess the change in line with the circumstances described in this section and in the sections on misrepresentation below. Telling us promptly gives us the chance to adjust your cover so that you are not left with gaps you did not expect.

Some changes can be made directly through your account, while others need a conversation with one of our advisers. Whichever route you take, the 14 day period runs from the date the change took place, not the date you finally got around to telling us. If you are uncertain whether something counts as a change that affects your cover, treat it as one and notify us.

## Sums insured and the value of your home

The sums insured shown in your schedule are the maximum amounts we will pay under each section of cover for rebuilding your home, replacing your contents and any other insured items. They are not a valuation of your property at the moment of a claim, and they do not automatically rise with inflation unless your policy says so.

You should check at each renewal that the sums insured are still right for your home and belongings. Building costs, the price of materials and the value of household items all change over time, and a sum insured that was adequate when you first took out cover may become too low. If a sum insured is too low, you may find that a claim is reduced in proportion to the shortfall, so it is in your interest to review these figures regularly. At each renewal we will send you the current sums insured for your reference, and you can ask us to revise them at any point between renewals if your circumstances have changed.

## Claims and your duty to cooperate

When something goes wrong, please notify us as soon as reasonably possible. Early notification allows us to help limit further damage, arrange any necessary emergency work and begin the claims process without delay. Delays can make it harder to establish what happened and can increase the cost of the claim for everyone who shares the mutual.

Once you have made a claim, you must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further loss, keep any damaged items for inspection where it is safe and practical to do so, and provide the information and documents we reasonably ask for. This may include photographs, receipts, proof of ownership, police reference numbers where a crime is involved, and details of other insurance that may apply. If you make a claim that is fraudulent in any way, or you give us information you know to be false in support of a claim, we may refuse the claim, recover any payments already made and end the policy.

We may appoint loss adjusters, surveyors or specialist contractors to inspect the damage and advise on the most appropriate way to settle the claim. Where we arrange repairs, we will aim to use reputable contractors and to keep you informed throughout. You are not required to accept a repair proposal you believe is unsuitable, but if you choose to make your own arrangements, the settlement will be based on what we would reasonably have paid had the claim been handled through our own network.

## Misrepresentation and changes to the risk

If you give us information that is untrue or incomplete when taking out or changing your policy, and that information would have affected whether we offered cover or the terms and price we set, we may take one or more of the following steps: cancel the policy, amend its terms, or adjust what we pay on a claim so that it reflects the terms we would have applied had we known the true position. We will explain the reason for any such decision in writing.

If you are not sure whether a detail is relevant, give it to us anyway. Disclosing something that turns out not to matter is far better than leaving out something that does.

## Cancellation

You may cancel your policy by contacting us at any time. If you cancel within the cooling off period described in your policy documents, you will receive a refund of premium for the unused period, less any cost of cover already used, subject to the terms set out in those documents. Outside the cooling off period, any refund will be calculated pro rata for the unexpired period of cover, unless the policy schedule states otherwise.

We may cancel your policy where we are entitled to do so under the terms of this wording, for example where information has been misrepresented, where premium has not been paid, or where a serious breach of these conditions has occurred. We will give you written notice of cancellation and the date from which it takes effect, and we will explain any refund due.

## Our right to recover costs from others

Where we have paid a claim, we may take over your right to recover the claim cost from anyone responsible for the loss, such as a contractor, a supplier or another party whose negligence caused the damage. This right is sometimes described as subrogation. Once we have taken over that right, you must not do anything to prejudice it, such as settling with the responsible party or waiving your rights against them, without first speaking to us.

In practice, this means that where a third party is at fault, we will pursue them for the amount we have paid, and you will be kept informed of progress. If the third party later pays you directly for a loss we have already settled, you must tell us and account to us for the amount we have paid out. Any recovery we make will be applied first to the costs we have incurred and the amounts we have paid, and any surplus will be returned to you where you have also made a claim for uninsured loss such as your excess.

## Complaints and the review of these conditions

If you are unhappy with the way a condition has been applied to your policy or claim, please tell us so that we can review the decision. Our complaints process is set out separately, and you are entitled to ask for a review at each stage. These general conditions are reviewed from time to time to reflect changes in the law, regulatory expectations and the needs of our members, and the version that applies to you is the one shown at the top of this document unless a later version has been issued in writing to you.
