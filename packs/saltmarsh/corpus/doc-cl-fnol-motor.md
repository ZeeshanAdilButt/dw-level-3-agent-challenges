# Reporting a motor claim

## Purpose and scope

This document sets out how to report a motor claim to Saltmarsh Mutual and what to do at the scene of an accident. It applies to all Saltmarsh Mutual motor policies, including Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to any optional riders you have added to your policy, such as the courtesy car rider. It is a rule that all members and our claims staff follow, and it takes effect from 1 July 2026.

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders. Our aim is to make reporting straightforward, to keep you informed at every stage, and to settle valid claims fairly and promptly.

## Before you start

Keep your policy number, your vehicle registration and your driving licence to hand. If you have a courtesy car rider, check your policy schedule so you know what cover applies. If you are unsure which cover you hold, you can ask us before you report the claim.

## Steps at the scene of a motor accident

Follow these steps in order if you are involved in a motor accident.

1. Check that everyone involved is safe. Call the emergency services if anyone is injured or if the road is blocked or unsafe.
2. Move vehicles out of the traffic flow if it is safe to do so and if no one is hurt.
3. Exchange details with the other driver. Note the other driver's name, registration and insurer. Also record their contact telephone number and the make and colour of their vehicle.
4. Do not admit fault at the scene of an accident, even if you believe you may have caused it. Admitting fault can affect how your claim is assessed, so stick to the facts when you speak to the other party.
5. Record the scene. Take photographs of the vehicles, the damage, the road layout, traffic signs and any weather or road conditions that may be relevant.
6. Collect witness details. If anyone saw what happened, note their name and contact details and, where they agree, a brief account of what they saw.
7. Make a note of the time and place of the accident and the circumstances as you remember them while the details are fresh.
8. If the police attend, obtain the reference number they give you and keep it with your other records.
9. Seek medical attention for any injury, even a minor one that seems slight at the time.

## How to report a motor claim

Once you are safe and have gathered the information above, report the claim to us as soon as you can. A motor accident must be reported within 14 days. Reporting late may affect your claim, so please do not delay.

You can report a claim in any of these ways:

1. Call our claims line on 0800 555 0142.
2. Use the claims section of your online member account.
3. Write to the Saltmarsh Mutual claims team with your policy number and the details of the accident.

When you report the claim, please give us:

1. Your policy number and the registration of your vehicle.
2. The date, time and location of the accident.
3. A description of what happened in your own words.
4. The other driver's name, registration and insurer, if another vehicle was involved.
5. Any witness details and any police reference number.
6. Photographs and any other evidence you have collected.
7. Details of any injuries, including whether anyone needed medical attention.

Our claims team will confirm that we have received your report and will give you a claim reference. Keep this reference for all future contact about the claim.

## What happens next

After your claim is reported, we will open a file and assign a claims handler. Your handler will contact you to confirm the details, ask for any further information and explain the next steps. Where a third party is involved, we will contact them or their insurer as needed.

If you need a vehicle while yours is being repaired and you hold the courtesy car rider, your handler will tell you how to arrange it. If you do not hold this rider, your handler can explain what other options may apply.

We aim to keep you informed at each stage of the claim. If you have not heard from us when you expect to, please contact us on 0800 555 0142 and quote your claim reference.

## Settlement

Once we have gathered the information needed to assess your claim, we will confirm the outcome in writing. If your claim is accepted, we will explain how it will be settled, whether by repair, replacement or payment, and when you can expect this. If we cannot accept all or part of your claim, we will explain our reasons clearly and tell you how to raise any concern with us.

## Questions and complaints

If you have a question about this document or about your claim, please call 0800 555 0142. If you are unhappy with how your claim has been handled, you can ask for it to be reviewed. Our complaints process explains how to do this and what to expect.
