# Contact us

This guide explains how to reach Saltmarsh Mutual for general questions, policy changes and claims. As a mutual insurer, we are owned by our policyholders, so every channel below is run to help you directly.

## Who to contact

Saltmarsh Mutual offers Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies, along with optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and a courtesy car. Choose the route that matches what you need:

- **General questions and policy changes:** call customer service.
- **Reporting a new claim or following up on one:** call the claims line.
- **Anything else:** write to us at our head office.

## Our contact details

The table below lists our main phone lines and opening times. Please have your policy number ready when you call so we can find your details quickly.

| Service | Phone number | Opening hours |
|---|---|---|
| Customer service | 0800 555 0100 | 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday; 9am to 1pm, Saturday |
| Claims line | 0800 555 0142 | 24 hours, around the clock, every day of the year |
| Head office | Not applicable for calls | Marshford (postal correspondence only) |

## Customer service

Our customer service team handles questions about your cover, changes to your policy, adding or removing a rider, and payment queries. Calls to the customer service number are answered during the hours shown above. Outside those hours, you can leave a message or write to us, and we will respond on the next working day.

## Making a claim

If you need to make a claim, call the claims line on 0800 555 0142. The claims line is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, so you can report an incident as soon as it happens. Our claims team will take your first notice of loss, explain what happens next and keep you updated until your claim is settled. If your situation is urgent, such as damage that makes your home unsafe, tell the claims team straight away so they can prioritise your case.

## Writing to us

You can write to Saltmarsh Mutual at our head office in Marshford. Please include your full name, your policy number and a daytime contact number so we can reply to you promptly. Letters about claims should quote the claim reference if you already have one.

## Helping us serve you well

Calls may be recorded for training and quality purposes. To protect your information, we will ask security questions before discussing your policy. We will never ask you to share your full card details by email. If you are unsure whether a call or message is genuinely from Saltmarsh Mutual, hang up and call the customer service number above.
