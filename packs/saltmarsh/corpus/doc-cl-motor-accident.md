# Motor accident claims

This guide explains how Saltmarsh Mutual handles motor accident claims, from the moment you tell us about an incident through to settlement. It is written for policyholders who hold Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive cover, and it applies whether or not you have added optional riders such as accidental damage, legal expenses or a courtesy car.

## Reporting the accident

Contact us as soon as you safely can after an accident, even if the damage looks minor or nobody has been hurt. Early notice helps us gather evidence while it is fresh, and it helps us act quickly if a third party makes a claim against you. When you report the incident, you will be asked for the date, time and place, the details of everyone involved, and any photographs or witness information you have collected.

At the scene, exchange names, addresses, registration numbers and insurance details with the other driver. Take photographs of both vehicles, the road layout and any visible injuries or road conditions. Do not admit fault at the scene of an accident, even if you believe you were responsible. Fault is decided on the evidence, and a remark made in the heat of the moment can complicate the claim. Stay polite, share the information you are required to share, and leave the question of responsibility to the claims process.

## Fault and non-fault claims

Once we have your report, we assess whether the accident was your fault, the other driver's fault, or shared between you. This assessment shapes how the claim is handled and what happens to your no-claims discount.

On a fault claim, we settle the costs under your policy, subject to your excess and the terms of your cover. A fault claim will normally affect your no-claims discount.

On a non-fault claim, where another driver is responsible, we work to recover what we have paid. Saltmarsh recovers its costs from the other driver's insurer, so that the cost of the accident does not fall on the policyholder or on the mutual's other members. A non-fault claim does not reduce the no-claims discount if Saltmarsh recovers the cost in full. If recovery is only partial, we will explain how that affects your discount before we close the claim.

## Recovering costs and your excess

Your policy excess is the amount you agree to pay towards a claim. When you have a non-fault claim, we pursue the full cost from the other side, and that cost includes your excess. The excess is refunded if the full cost is recovered from the other side. If we recover only part of the cost, the refund will reflect the amount recovered, and we will tell you exactly how the figures were calculated.

Recovery can take time, particularly where the other insurer disputes liability or the claim involves injury. We keep you informed while this is in progress and will not close your file until the outcome is clear.

## Settlement

When the claim is settled, we write to you with a summary of the costs, any excess due or refunded, and the effect on your policy. If you disagree with the outcome, you can ask us to review the decision and explain your reasons. Our complaints process is available to you if the review does not resolve your concern.
