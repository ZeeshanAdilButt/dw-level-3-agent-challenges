# How we use your data

Saltmarsh Mutual is an insurer owned by its policyholders, and we take seriously our responsibility to handle your personal information carefully. This guide explains what data we collect, why we need it, how long we keep it, and how you can ask to see it. It is written for anyone who holds or has held a Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policy with us, including people who have made a claim, and it covers the optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car.

## What we collect and why

We collect the details you give us when you take out a policy, such as your name, contact details, address, driving history and information about the property or vehicle being insured. When a claim is made, we also collect the facts needed to assess it, from first notice of loss through to settlement. This may include photographs, repair estimates, police reference numbers and, where relevant, medical or legal information that you choose to share.

We use this information to set up and manage your cover, to assess and settle claims, to meet our legal and regulatory obligations, and to prevent and detect fraud. We do not use it for purposes unrelated to these without telling you first.

## How long we keep your records

Saltmarsh Mutual keeps policy and claim records for 7 years after a policy ends. We chose this period so that we can answer questions about past cover, handle any late claims or disputes, and meet our record-keeping duties. Once that period has passed, we securely delete or anonymise the records unless a legal requirement or an open dispute means we must keep them for longer.

## Your right to access your data

You are entitled to ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you. A request for a copy of your personal data is answered within 30 days of us receiving it and confirming your identity. We may ask for proof of identity and some details about your policy so that we can find the right records. We will not charge a fee for a standard request, although we may ask for a reasonable fee if a request is clearly unfounded or repetitive.

To make a request, write to our data protection team using the contact details on your policy documents or on our website. Please say clearly that you are making a subject access request and include enough detail for us to locate your records.

## Your other rights

Depending on your circumstances, you may also ask us to correct inaccurate information, to restrict how we use your data, or to object to certain kinds of processing. You can raise a concern about how we have handled your data at any time, and if you are not satisfied with our response you have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

## Keeping this guide up to date

We review this guide regularly and will publish any changes on our website. This version takes effect on 1 July 2026. If you have questions about anything in this guide, please contact us and we will be glad to help.
