# Home policy wording: claims conditions

## Purpose and scope

This section sets out the conditions that apply when you make a claim under a Saltmarsh Mutual home policy. It covers Home Essentials and Home Plus, together with any optional riders you have chosen, including accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car cover. These conditions apply from first notice of loss through to settlement. Please read them carefully, as failing to meet them may affect whether your claim can be paid in full.

## Definitions

In this section, "we", "us" and "our" mean Saltmarsh Mutual. "You" means the policyholder named in your schedule. "Loss" means damage, theft or any other event that may give rise to a claim under your home policy. "Item" means any single article or set of articles covered by your policy.

## Making a claim

Notify us as soon as reasonably possible after any loss occurs. The quickest way to do this is through the claims line or the online claims service shown in your policy documents. When you notify us, please give us the date and time of the loss, a clear description of what happened, the items affected and any steps already taken to limit further damage.

## Numbered steps for a home claim

1. Make your home safe and take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, such as covering a broken window or turning off a leaking supply.
2. Report the loss to us. A home claim must be reported within 30 days of discovering the loss, and reporting earlier helps us assist you more quickly.
3. Where the loss involves theft or malicious damage, report it to the police. Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police within 24 hours of discovery, and you must give us the police reference number when you notify us.
4. Keep the damaged items where they are, or keep them safely, until we have inspected them or told you that you may dispose of them.
5. Gather supporting evidence, such as photographs, receipts, proof of ownership and valuations, and send these to us when asked.
6. Cooperate with our investigation, including allowing reasonable access to your property for inspection.
7. Wait for our decision on settlement before arranging permanent repairs or replacements, unless urgent work is needed to protect the property.

## Our handling of your claim

We will acknowledge your claim and tell you who is handling it. Where a home claim is over £10,000, Saltmarsh may appoint a loss adjuster to assess the loss on our behalf. A loss adjuster will contact you directly, and their role is to establish the facts and the value of the claim. Appointing a loss adjuster does not mean we have decided your claim is invalid.

When settling a claim, Saltmarsh may choose to repair or replace an item instead of paying cash. Where we make this choice, we will arrange the repair or replacement using suitable suppliers. Where we pay cash instead, the amount will be based on the policy terms and the value of the item at the time of loss.

## Admission of liability

You must not admit liability, make any offer or promise, or agree to any settlement with a third party in connection with a claim without our written consent. Doing so may affect our ability to recover costs or defend a claim on your behalf.

## Keeping damaged items

You must keep damaged items until we have had a reasonable opportunity to inspect them. If you dispose of an item before we have inspected it, we may be unable to validate the claim for that item. If we ask you to keep an item for further investigation, please do so.

## Consequences of non-compliance

If you do not meet these conditions, we may reduce the amount payable or, in serious cases, decline the claim. We will always consider the circumstances before taking such action.
