# Endorsement E-2026-08: locks on upper floor flats

## Purpose

This endorsement changes the lock condition that applies to certain flats insured with Saltmarsh Mutual. It is issued as a public notice so that policyholders, brokers and staff all work from the same wording.

## Change to the lock condition

Endorsement E-2026-08 removes the BS 3621 lock requirement for flats above the first floor. Where a flat sits above the first floor, a policy will no longer be declined or reduced in cover because the entry doors lack a BS 3621 lock. All other conditions of the policy continue to apply as written.

## Scope and effective date

Endorsement E-2026-08 applies to all home policies from 1 August 2026. It covers the home products that Saltmarsh Mutual sells, including any optional riders attached to them. Policies that renew or start on or after that date will carry the revised condition. Claims relating to losses that occurred before 1 August 2026 will be assessed under the wording in force at the time of the loss.

## What policyholders should do

Policyholders who live in an upper floor flat do not need to fit a BS 3621 lock to keep their cover in place because of this endorsement. Anyone who is unsure whether their property falls within the endorsement can contact Saltmarsh Mutual, and staff will confirm how the revised condition applies to their policy.
