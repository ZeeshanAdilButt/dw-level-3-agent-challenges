# Home policy wording: general exclusions

## Purpose of this section

This section sets out the general exclusions that apply to all Saltmarsh Mutual home policies, including Home Essentials and Home Plus. Exclusions describe the losses, causes and circumstances for which Saltmarsh Mutual does not provide cover. They apply in addition to the specific terms of your policy schedule, and they should be read together with the definitions, conditions and claims procedure in the full policy document. Where an optional rider such as accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car has been added to your policy, the exclusions below still apply unless the rider wording states otherwise.

## Interpreting this section

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, and this wording is written to be clear about what is and is not covered. Words in this section have the meanings given in the definitions part of the policy. Headings are included for ease of reference only and do not change the meaning of any clause. If you are unsure whether an exclusion applies to your circumstances, you should contact us before relying on the cover, because an incorrect assumption may affect the settlement of a claim.

## Gradual and continuing causes of loss

Saltmarsh Mutual does not cover loss or damage arising from wear and tear or gradual deterioration. This includes the natural ageing of materials, fittings and finishes, and the slow decline of building components over time. Damage that develops gradually, rather than as the result of a sudden and identifiable event, is not covered under the home policy.

Clause 4.3 excludes damage from a leak that has gone on for 14 days or more. The exclusion applies whether the leak came from pipework, appliances, roofs, gutters or any other part of the home. Where a leak has continued for fourteen days or more before it is discovered or stopped, the cost of repairing the damage caused over that period is not payable, although Saltmarsh Mutual may consider the cost of locating and repairing the source of the leak in line with the conditions of the policy.

## Pets

Saltmarsh Mutual does not cover damage caused by your own pets. This exclusion applies to damage to the home, to the contents, and to third party property where the damage arises from the actions of an animal kept by you or by a member of your household. Damage caused by pets belonging to visitors or to neighbours may be considered under the liability provisions of the policy, subject to the terms that apply to those sections.

## Business property

The home contents cover is provided for domestic property only. Business stock and business equipment are not covered under the home contents cover, regardless of where they are kept within the home. If you use part of your home for business purposes, you should tell us so that we can confirm whether separate commercial cover is required. Items used both for personal and business purposes are covered only to the extent that they are domestic in nature and the policy wording permits.

## Unoccupied homes

Theft and malicious damage are not covered if the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 days. For the purposes of this exclusion, a home is unoccupied when no person is living in it on a regular basis. Short absences, such as holidays or periods in hospital, do not count as unoccupancy where the home continues to be lived in and furnished. Where the home has been unoccupied for sixty days or more, you must tell us as soon as reasonably possible, as additional conditions may apply to continued cover.

## Letting of the home

A home let to tenants is not covered unless Saltmarsh Mutual has agreed in writing. A policy written for an owner-occupied home does not extend to a property that is rented out to tenants, and any loss arising from the letting arrangement may be declined. If you intend to let your home, or if you already let it, you must contact us so that we can consider whether a landlord policy is appropriate. Written agreement from Saltmarsh Mutual must be obtained before the letting begins for cover to apply, and any agreement may be made subject to additional terms, premium adjustments or conditions.

## Summary of the procedure

Where you believe an exclusion in this section may not apply to your loss, you should follow these steps:

1. Notify Saltmarsh Mutual of the loss as soon as reasonably possible, giving a full description of what happened.
2. Provide any information we request about the cause, the duration and the circumstances of the loss, including the occupancy of the home and the presence of pets or business property where relevant.
3. Keep damaged items and evidence for inspection, unless it is unsafe or necessary to remove them to prevent further loss.
4. Await our decision on the claim, which will state the basis on which cover has been accepted or declined.
5. If you disagree with the decision, use the complaints procedure set out in the policy, which explains how to refer the matter for review.
