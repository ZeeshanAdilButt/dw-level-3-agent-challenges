# No-claims discount steps

## Purpose and scope

This clause sets out how the no-claims discount is earned, how it is reduced after a claim, and how proof of entitlement is provided. It applies to Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies held with Saltmarsh Mutual, and to the optional riders where a no-claims discount is relevant. It takes effect on 1 July 2026.

## Discount by years claim free

The table below shows the no-claims discount that applies according to the number of consecutive years a policyholder has gone without a claim. The discount is applied to the policy premium at renewal.

| Years claim free | No-claims discount |
|---|---|
| One year without a claim | 30% |
| Two years without a claim | 40% |
| Three years without a claim | 50% |
| Four years without a claim | 60% |
| Five years without a claim | 65% |
| Six or more years without a claim | 70% (maximum) |

The 70% no-claims discount is the maximum available. A policyholder with six or more years without a claim receives this maximum and does not earn any further increase in the discount.

## Earning the discount

A year counts toward the no-claims discount when the policy runs for a full policy year without a claim being made against it. The discount increases by one step at each renewal where the policyholder has remained claim free for that additional year. Saltmarsh Mutual confirms the applicable discount on the renewal documents issued before the policy renews.

## Effect of an at-fault claim

An at-fault claim moves the no-claims discount back by 2 years. The policyholder's discount is therefore reduced to the level that corresponds to two fewer claim-free years than before the claim. For example, a policyholder who held the 60% discount for four years without a claim and then makes an at-fault claim would move back to the level corresponding to two years without a claim, which is 40%. Where the reduction would take the discount below the level for one year without a claim, the discount is reduced to nil and rebuilds from the start.

## Effect of a non-fault claim

A non-fault claim does not reduce the no-claims discount if Saltmarsh recovers the cost in full from the responsible third party or their insurer. Where Saltmarsh recovers the full cost, the policyholder keeps the discount they had before the claim and continues to build toward the next step at renewal.

Where a non-fault claim is made but Saltmarsh does not recover the full cost, the treatment of the discount is decided case by case, and the policyholder is told the outcome in writing.

## Proof of no-claims discount

A policyholder who moves to another insurer may be asked for proof of their no-claims discount. Proof of no-claims discount is valid for 2 years after a policy ends. After that period, the proof is no longer accepted and the discount must be re-established through a new policy history.

Requests for proof should be made through the Saltmarsh Mutual policy servicing team, which issues the statement showing the discount earned and the number of years claim free at the date the policy ended.

## Interaction with optional riders

Where a policyholder holds an optional rider for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car, the rider does not change how the no-claims discount is earned or reduced. A claim made under a rider is treated in the same way as a claim made under the main policy when the discount is assessed, so the at-fault and non-fault rules above apply.

## Records and disputes

Saltmarsh Mutual keeps a record of each policy year, each claim and the discount applied at each renewal. A policyholder who believes their discount has been calculated incorrectly may ask for a review. The review is carried out by the policy servicing team, and the outcome is confirmed in writing with the reasons for the decision.
