# Renewing your policy

This guide explains how renewal works for Saltmarsh Mutual home and motor policies, what we send you, and what you can change before your cover continues. It applies to Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, along with any optional riders you have added for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car.

## When you will hear from us

We write to you before your policy ends so you have time to check your cover. Your renewal letter is sent 21 days before the policy ends, and it sets out the cover you currently hold, the premium for the next period, and any changes to terms that apply from the renewal date. If your cover includes a rider, the letter lists it separately so you can see what you are paying for.

Please read the letter when it arrives rather than waiting for the end date. If the details no longer match your home or vehicle, such as a change of address, a new car, or a building extension, tell us before the renewal date so we can update the quote.

## Renewing by direct debit

If your premium is paid by direct debit, your policy renews automatically unless you tell Saltmarsh not to. You do not need to take any action to keep your cover in place. The renewal premium is collected on the date set out in your renewal letter, and the amount will reflect the information we hold on file at that time.

If you would like to stop automatic renewal, contact us before the renewal date given in your letter. We will then send you a renewal quote for you to accept or decline. Please note that if you decline and do not arrange alternative cover, your policy will end on its current end date.

## Renewing by other payment methods

If you pay by card or in full, your policy does not renew automatically. You will need to confirm that you wish to continue cover and make payment before the policy end date. Your renewal letter explains how to do this, including the options available for paying by instalments where they apply to your policy.

## Checking your cover before you renew

Before accepting a renewal, take a few minutes to review the sums insured, the excess amounts and whether your riders still suit your needs. For a home policy, consider whether the rebuild cost has changed. For a motor policy, check the registration, the declared use of the vehicle and the number of drivers named. Making changes early gives us time to give you an accurate premium.

## Changes after renewal

You can make changes to your policy at any time during the cover period. Changes that affect your premium will be recalculated and confirmed in writing. If you have questions about your renewal, contact our member services team, who can explain the terms in your letter and help you choose the cover that fits your circumstances.
