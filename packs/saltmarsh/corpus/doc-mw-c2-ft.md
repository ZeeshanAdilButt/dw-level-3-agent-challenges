# Clause 2: fire and theft

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out the wording that applies to fire and theft cover under the Motor Comprehensive section of your policy. It should be read together with the policy schedule, the general conditions and any endorsements that apply to your policy. Where this clause and another part of the policy appear to conflict, the wording of this clause applies to fire and theft losses only.

## What this cover includes

Clause 2 on Motor Comprehensive covers fire, theft and attempted theft of the vehicle described in your schedule. Cover is provided for damage to the vehicle caused by fire, including fire resulting from a lightning strike, explosion or accidental ignition. Theft cover includes the loss of the whole vehicle through theft, and damage caused to the vehicle by a theft or attempted theft, such as damage to the steering column, locks, bodywork or electrical systems.

Fire and theft cover is subject to the other terms of this policy, including the sum insured or market value basis shown in your schedule, any excess that applies, and the conditions for making a claim.

## Conditions that limit theft cover

Theft cover does not apply when the keys were left in or on an unattended vehicle. This applies whether the vehicle was parked on a public road, in a car park, on private land or anywhere else where it was not under the direct control of the policyholder or a person authorised to drive it. If the keys were left in or on an unattended vehicle at the time of the theft, Saltmarsh Mutual will not pay a claim under this clause for that theft, even if the vehicle was locked.

You must take reasonable care to secure the vehicle when it is parked, including locking the doors and windows and removing or securing any spare keys in a safe place. Failure to take reasonable care may affect the outcome of a claim.

## Stolen vehicles that are not recovered

If a stolen vehicle is not found, Saltmarsh settles after 30 days. The 30 days begins on the date we receive your notification of the theft, and we will keep you informed during that period about the progress of our enquiries. If the vehicle is recovered before settlement, we will inspect it and agree the repairs needed with you. If the vehicle is recovered after settlement, ownership of the vehicle will be dealt with according to the settlement terms agreed at that time.

You must tell us promptly if you hear anything about the whereabouts of your vehicle, and you must cooperate with the police and with us during our enquiries.

## Reporting a fire or theft

Please report any fire or theft to us as soon as you reasonably can, and in the case of theft, report it to the police and obtain a crime reference number. Give us accurate information about the circumstances of the loss, including the time and place it occurred, and provide any documents, keys or other evidence we reasonably request. Late or incomplete reporting may affect the handling of your claim.

## Where to find more information

If you are unsure whether this clause applies to your circumstances, please contact Saltmarsh Mutual before an incident occurs. Our policy documents and general conditions explain how this clause fits with the rest of your cover.
