# Updating your contact details

This guide explains how members of Saltmarsh Mutual can keep their phone number and email address up to date. Keeping these details current helps us reach you quickly about your policy, renewal, or an open claim.

## Why keeping your details current matters

As a mutual, Saltmarsh is owned by its policyholders, and we rely on accurate contact information to communicate important notices about your Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive cover. If you have added an optional rider such as accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car, we may also need to contact you about it.

## How to change your phone number and email

You can change your phone number or email address through your online member account. Sign in, open the contact details section, enter the new information and confirm the change. Phone and email changes made online take effect straight away, so your next message or call from us will use the updated details.

If you do not have access to your online account, you can ask us to make the change by phone. Our team will verify your identity before updating anything on your policy. For your security, we will never change contact details based on an unverified request.

## What to do if you are in the middle of a claim

If you have a claim open, please update your contact details as soon as possible so that we can reach you about settlement, documents or inspections. Updating your details does not affect the progress of your claim.

## Need more help?

If you are unsure which details we hold for you or cannot complete the change online, please contact our member support team. We are happy to help.
