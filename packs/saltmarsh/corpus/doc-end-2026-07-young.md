# Endorsement E-2026-07: Young Driver Extra Excess

## Purpose

This endorsement changes the young driver extra excess that applies to certain Saltmarsh Mutual motor policies. It forms part of the motor policy terms for the policies it covers and should be read alongside the policy schedule and wording.

## Change to the young driver extra excess

Endorsement E-2026-07 lowers the young driver extra excess to £350. Where a named young driver is covered on an eligible motor policy, the extra excess payable on a valid motor claim is therefore reduced to this amount, in addition to the standard excess shown on the policy schedule.

## Which policies are affected

Endorsement E-2026-07 applies only to motor policies that start on or after 1 July 2026. Motor policies that started before that date continue to be governed by the terms in force when they began, and the reduced young driver extra excess does not apply to them unless the policy is renewed or replaced on terms that bring it within scope.

## Other terms

All other terms of the motor policy, including the standard excess, the rules on claims notification and the conditions of any optional riders, remain unchanged by this endorsement.

## Contact

Policyholders with questions about how this endorsement applies to their cover should contact Saltmarsh Mutual before making a claim so that their policy schedule can be checked.
