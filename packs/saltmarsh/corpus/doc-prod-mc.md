# Motor Comprehensive

## About this product

Motor Comprehensive is Saltmarsh Mutual's fully comprehensive motor policy for private car owners. It is designed for policyholders who want protection for their own vehicle as well as cover for damage they may cause to other people's property and vehicles. The product code is MOT-CMP-2, and this guide explains what the policy includes, who can buy it, and how the accidental damage excess works.

## Who can buy this policy

To buy Motor Comprehensive, you must be at least 21 years old. Applicants below this age can contact us to discuss whether Motor Third Party may suit their needs in the meantime. We may ask for additional information about your driving history, your vehicle and where it is kept, so that we can give you an accurate quotation.

## What the policy covers

Motor Comprehensive is broader than Motor Third Party. It includes accidental damage to your own vehicle, which means that if your car is damaged by an accident, whether or not another party is at fault, the repair or replacement costs may be met under the policy, subject to the terms and the excess that applies. It also covers liability to third parties, including injury to other people and damage to their property, in the same way as the third party sections of the policy.

Motor Comprehensive can be extended with optional riders, which are added for an additional premium. These include:

- Accidental damage, which can be added if you want this cover with a different arrangement than the standard wording
- Legal expenses, to help with the cost of pursuing or defending certain claims
- Home emergency, for assistance with urgent problems at your home
- Courtesy car, which may be provided while your insured vehicle is off the road for an approved repair

Riders are subject to their own terms, and availability may vary depending on your circumstances.

## Accidental damage excess

When you make a claim for accidental damage to your own vehicle under Motor Comprehensive, an excess applies. The Motor Comprehensive accidental damage excess is £250. This is the amount you pay towards an approved claim before Saltmarsh Mutual contributes to the settlement. The excess is shown on your policy schedule, and the figure stated there applies to your policy.

## Making a claim

If you need to make a claim under MOT-CMP-2, contact us as soon as reasonably possible after the incident. Please have your policy number, the details of what happened, and any photographs or documents that support your claim ready. Our team will record the first notice of loss, confirm that the incident is covered, and guide you through the next steps, including repairs and settlement. Where an excess applies, it will be explained to you when your claim is assessed.

## Changes and cancellation

If your circumstances change, such as your address, your vehicle, or how you use it, please tell us promptly. Changes may affect your premium or the terms of your cover. You can ask us about cancelling your policy at any time, and the details of how cancellation works will be set out in your policy documents.

## Important information

This guide is a summary of Motor Comprehensive and does not replace the full policy wording, which sets out the terms, conditions, exclusions and limits that apply. If anything in this guide differs from the policy documents, the policy documents apply. If you are unsure whether this product is right for you, please speak to one of our advisers before you buy.
