# Support article: how long a claim decision takes

*Effective from 10 October 2026*

## What this article covers

This guide explains how long it usually takes Saltmarsh Mutual to reach a decision on your claim, from the moment you tell us about an incident to the point where we confirm whether your claim is accepted. Whether you hold Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive, the steps below apply in the same way.

## How long a decision takes

Our October 2026 support article says a claim is decided within 5 working days. That is a quick target, and many straightforward claims are settled well inside it. Please bear in mind that this figure is a general guide for customers, not a promise for every case.

## What can change the timescale

Some claims take longer than others. Claims involving third parties, missing documents, or a need for inspection by an assessor often need more time. If we need further information from you, the clock is paused until we receive it, so sending your details promptly helps us move things along.

## What happens after the decision

Once a decision is made, we will write to you to confirm the outcome. If your claim is accepted, we will explain how settlement will be arranged. If we cannot accept it, the letter will set out the reasons and tell you how to ask us to review the decision.

## Keeping track of your claim

You can check progress through your account or by contacting our claims team. We are happy to explain where things stand, and we will always let you know if we expect a decision to take longer than the usual timescale.
