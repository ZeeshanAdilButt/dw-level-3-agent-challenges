# Claim story: Yilmaz household

## Background

The Yilmaz household holds a Home Plus policy with Saltmarsh Mutual and a Motor Comprehensive policy on the family car. When a collision left the car beyond economical repair, the household contacted us to start a claim. This story follows that claim from the first notice of loss through to settlement, so that policyholders can see how we handle a total loss in practice.

## What happened

The car was written off after a collision on a wet road. The household reported the loss by phone the same day, and an claims handler took the details, confirmed the policy was active, and arranged for the vehicle to be collected from the roadside recovery point.

## How the car was valued

Our assessor reviewed the car's make, model, age, mileage and specification, then compared it with recent sales of similar vehicles. The pre-accident value was set using trade guides, and the valuation was completed within 4 days of the claim being reported. The household received a written valuation explaining each factor, so that they could check the figures and raise any points they felt were missing.

## Settlement

Once the valuation was agreed, the settlement was paid to the household, and the household was able to plan its next vehicle without a long gap. Throughout the process, the handler kept the family updated at each stage and answered their questions directly.

## What we learned

Clear communication and a transparent valuation made a stressful experience easier to manage. We continue to review each claim to keep our process fair, prompt and easy to follow.
