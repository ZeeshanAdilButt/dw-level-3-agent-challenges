# Settlement authority limits

## Purpose

This rule sets the maximum value of a claim that each claims role may settle without further approval. It applies to all home and motor claims handled from first notice of loss to settlement, including claims involving optional riders.

## Authority limits

Settlement authority is set by role. Each role may approve a settlement only up to the limit shown below. Any claim above a role's limit must be referred to the next level before an offer is made.

| Role | Maximum settlement authority (AM-0907 reference) |
|---|---|
| Frontline handlers | £5,000 |
| Senior handlers | £25,000 |
| Claims manager | £100,000 |

## Referral

Handlers must refer a claim upward when the proposed settlement exceeds their limit, when the figure is uncertain, or when the claim raises a coverage question. Splitting a single claim into smaller parts to stay within a limit is not permitted.

## Recording

Every settlement must record the approving role, the settlement value and the claim reference in the claim file before the offer is issued. The internal code authority matrix AM-0907 is the reference used to check these limits during audit.
