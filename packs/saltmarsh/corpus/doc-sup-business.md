# Using your car for work

This guide explains how Saltmarsh Mutual treats the different ways you might use your car for work, so you can check that your motor policy fits how you actually drive. It applies from 2026-07-01.

## Commuting

Commuting to a single place of work is included in motor cover. This means that driving from your home to one regular workplace, and back, is covered under your existing Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policy without any extra cover. If you have one main place of work and travel there in your own car, you do not need to tell us anything extra.

## Business use

Using the car for deliveries or visiting customers needs business use cover. Business use covers journeys where the car is part of your work rather than simply getting you to and from a workplace. Deliveries, travel between customer sites, and driving to meet clients all fall into this category. If you use your car this way and do not have business use cover added, a claim arising from that driving may not be paid, so please contact us to add it before you start.

## What counts as commuting and what counts as business use

As a general guide, commuting is the journey between your home and one regular place of work. Business use begins once the car is carrying goods, visiting more than one work location in a working day, or being used to earn income on the road. If you are unsure which applies to your situation, speak to us before you rely on your cover, and we will explain the options that suit your pattern of driving.
