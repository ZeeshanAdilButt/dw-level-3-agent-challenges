# Protected no-claims discount

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how the protected no-claims discount works for Saltmarsh Mutual home and motor policyholders, effective from 1 July 2026. It applies to any policy where the protected no-claims discount has been added as an optional extra.

## Eligibility and cover

The protected no-claims discount allows 2 at-fault claims in any 5 years without losing the discount. Where a policyholder makes a third at-fault claim within that period, the discount is reduced in line with our standard no-claims rules. Claims where the policyholder is not at fault are not counted against this allowance.

## Cost

The protected no-claims discount costs £24 a year, payable with the premium for the policy it is attached to. The cost is shown separately on the policy schedule so that it can be seen clearly.

## When it can be bought

The protected no-claims discount can be bought once you have at least 4 years of discount. Policyholders who have not yet built up that length of discount cannot add the cover at renewal or mid-term.

## Changes and cancellation

Policyholders may cancel the protected no-claims discount at any time by contacting us, and the cost will be adjusted from the date of cancellation. Full terms apply as set out in the main policy wording.
