# Repairs and the approved repairer network

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how repairs are arranged after a claim is accepted at Saltmarsh Mutual, what happens when you use the approved repairer network, and what to expect if you choose your own garage. It applies to claims under Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Comprehensive and any riders that cover accidental damage, home emergency or a courtesy car. Motor Third Party claims concerning another party's vehicle follow the separate arrangements described in our third party claims guide.

## How the approved repairer network works

Saltmarsh Mutual maintains a network of approved repairers for motor and home work. These are workshops and trades that have agreed to our standards for quality, timescales and communication. When your claim is accepted and the damage is suitable for a network repair, your claims handler will offer you a choice of approved repairers in your area.

Repairs carried out at an approved repairer are guaranteed for 12 months from the date the work is completed. If a defect in the workmanship appears within that period, you should tell us promptly and we will arrange for the repairer to put it right at no cost to you.

Because we have an arrangement with each approved repairer, Saltmarsh pays the repairer directly once the work is finished and the repair has been checked. You do not need to pay the repairer and then wait to be reimbursed. You are responsible only for any excess shown on your policy schedule, which is paid to the repairer when you collect your vehicle or when the home repair is signed off.

## Using your own garage or tradesperson

You are free to choose your own garage or tradesperson instead of an approved repairer. Many customers do this because they have an existing relationship with a workshop or a trade they trust. We respect that choice, but the process differs in a few important ways.

If you choose your own garage, Saltmarsh settles on its own estimate of the repair cost, not on the figure the garage quotes. Our estimate is prepared by an assessor who inspects the damage, and it reflects what we consider a fair cost for the work needed. If the garage's quote is higher than our estimate, you will be responsible for the difference, and you may wish to discuss the variance with your claims handler before the work begins. If the quote is lower, the payment is still based on our estimate, and you keep the benefit of the saving.

Because we do not have an agreement with your chosen repairer, we do not pay them directly. Instead, the settlement is made to you, usually by bank transfer, and you then pay the repairer. Work carried out by your own garage is not covered by the 12 month guarantee described above, although the repairer may offer its own warranty, and you should ask them about it.

## What to expect at each stage

After your claim is accepted, your handler will confirm whether an approved repairer is suitable and, if so, arrange an appointment. If you have chosen your own garage, please send your handler the written quote as early as possible so that our assessor can compare it with our estimate. Delays in sharing the quote can slow settlement, so we encourage you to provide it promptly.

Whichever route you take, keep copies of all invoices, photographs of the damage and correspondence with the repairer. If you are unsure which option suits your circumstances, your claims handler can explain the practical differences before you decide.
