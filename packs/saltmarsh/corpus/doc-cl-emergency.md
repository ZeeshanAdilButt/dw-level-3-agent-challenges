# Home emergency claims

This guide explains what to do in a home emergency under the home emergency rider, how the rider works, and what you can expect from us once you have made contact. It applies to policyholders who hold the home emergency rider alongside a Home Essentials or Home Plus policy.

## What the rider covers

The home emergency rider covers three situations that need urgent repair to keep your home safe and liveable: loss of heating or hot water, a blocked toilet, and a burst pipe. If your problem falls into one of these categories, the rider is designed to get a qualified engineer to your home as quickly as possible.

The rider pays up to £500 per call-out. This limit covers the engineer's attendance and the emergency repair needed to make the property safe or restore the service. Work beyond that emergency repair, such as a full replacement of a boiler or re-plastering after a major leak, is not covered by the rider and may be claimed under your main home policy where it applies.

The home emergency rider allows 2 call-outs in each policy year. Once you have used both, further emergencies in that policy year are not covered by the rider, though you can still arrange a repair privately.

## What to do in a home emergency

Please follow these steps so that your claim can be handled quickly.

First, make your home safe. If there is water escaping, turn off the mains stopcock if you know where it is. If you have lost heating in cold weather, keep warm and take care with any alternative heating sources. If you smell gas, leave the property and contact the national gas emergency service before calling us.

Second, call the home emergency line on 0800 555 0177 before arranging any repair yourself. You must make this call first so that we can accept the call-out under the rider. Please have your policy number and a brief description of the problem ready.

Third, wait for the engineer. A home emergency needs no documents, only a call to the emergency line first. You do not need to send photographs, receipts or forms before an engineer attends. We will ask you a few questions on the call to confirm that your problem is covered and to dispatch the right engineer.

## What happens next

Once we accept your call, an engineer attends a home emergency within 4 hours of the call being accepted. The engineer will assess the problem, carry out the emergency repair within the rider limit, and explain what further work, if any, is needed. If the problem cannot be fixed on the day, the engineer will make the property safe and tell you what happens next.

If the engineer finds that the problem is not covered by the rider, for example because it arose from a lack of maintenance or falls outside the three covered situations, we will tell you before any work goes ahead. You will then be free to decide whether to proceed privately.

## Keeping a record

After the visit, keep any paperwork the engineer leaves with you, and note the date, time and reference number from your call. If you later need to make a claim under your main policy for further damage, this record will help us settle it smoothly. Your policy terms and conditions set out the full wording of the rider and should be read alongside this guide.
