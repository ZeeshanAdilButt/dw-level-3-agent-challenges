# Fees for changing your policy

This guide explains what it costs to make changes to your Saltmarsh Mutual policy part way through the policy term. It applies to Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, along with any optional riders you have added.

## Mid-term change fees

Some changes are free, while others carry an administration fee. The table below shows the fees that apply to the most common changes.

| Change | Fee |
|---|---|
| Changing your address | No fee (free) |
| Adding a named driver to a motor policy | £20 |
| A general mid-term change | £20 |

## Changing your address

Changing your address carries no fee. Please tell us as soon as you move, because your home and motor cover depends on the correct address being recorded.

## Adding a named driver

Adding a named driver to a motor policy carries a £20 admin fee. We may ask for details about the new driver before the change takes effect, and the premium may be recalculated.

## Other mid-term changes

A general mid-term change carries a £20 fee. This includes changes to cover levels, optional riders or other policy details that are not covered above. We will confirm the fee and any change in premium before you agree to the change.

## Questions

If you are unsure which fee applies to your change, please contact our member services team before you make the change. We are happy to explain the options and the cost of each.
