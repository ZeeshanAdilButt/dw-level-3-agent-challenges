# Clause 5: belongings, child seats and keys

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual deals with personal belongings left in an insured vehicle, child car seats, and the keys and locks linked to an insured vehicle. It applies to the Motor Comprehensive policy and should be read together with the policy schedule, the general conditions and any riders shown on the schedule. Where this clause and the schedule differ, the schedule states the amount that applies to the policyholder's own policy.

## Personal belongings in the vehicle

Motor Comprehensive pays up to £250 for personal belongings in the vehicle that are damaged, stolen or destroyed as a result of an insured event. Personal belongings means everyday items owned by the policyholder or a named driver that are kept in the vehicle for personal use, such as bags, clothing, phones, tablets, sports equipment and similar items. The £250 limit is the total payable for all personal belongings arising from one incident, not an amount for each item.

Personal belongings do not include cash, cheques, vouchers, tickets, stamps, securities, business stock, samples, tools used for work, or items carried for hire or reward. We may ask for proof of ownership and a reasonable description of each item before we settle a claim. Items that are damaged in an accident are settled on the same basis as the rest of the claim, and the amount paid is reduced by any excess that applies to the policy.

## Child car seats

Where a child car seat fitted to the insured vehicle is damaged in an insured accident, we will pay for its replacement as part of the vehicle claim. The seat must be replaced where the manufacturer advises it or where it has been subject to a collision of the kind that could affect its safety, even if no visible damage can be seen. The policyholder should keep the seat for inspection until the claim has been assessed, and should follow any instruction from the manufacturer about checking or replacing the seat after an accident.

Child car seats that are lost or stolen separately from the vehicle are treated as personal belongings and fall within the limit set out above. Ordinary wear, neglect, or damage caused by failure to follow the manufacturer's fitting instructions is not covered.

## Keys and locks

Replacement of stolen or lost keys and locks is covered up to £500. This covers the cost of replacing keys, key fobs and electronic key cards for the insured vehicle, together with the cost of changing or re-coding the locks and immobiliser that the keys operate, where this is needed to protect the vehicle. The £500 limit applies per incident and includes any reasonable call-out and labour costs for the work.

We will need to be satisfied that the keys or locks were stolen or lost, and that the loss could not reasonably have been avoided by the policyholder. Where keys are lost but not stolen, we may ask for evidence of attempts to locate them. Replacement must be carried out by a qualified locksmith, dealer or other suitably competent repairer, and we will usually settle the invoice directly with that repairer where this is practical.

## Making a claim under this clause

Policyholders should report any loss or damage under this clause to us as soon as reasonably possible, and in any case before arranging repairs or replacements, so that we can confirm cover and agree the amount. Our claims team will explain what evidence is needed for the item concerned. Settlement will follow the general claims process set out elsewhere in the policy documents.
