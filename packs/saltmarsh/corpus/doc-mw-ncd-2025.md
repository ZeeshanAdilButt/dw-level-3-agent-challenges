# No-claims discount steps (2025)

## Purpose

This clause sets out how the no-claims discount applies to Saltmarsh Mutual home and motor policies in 2025. It is published so that policyholders can see how their discount is calculated.

## Scope

The table below applies to policies that take effect on or after 1 March 2025. It covers the no-claims discount on Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies.

## Discount table

The discount depends on the number of consecutive claim-free years held with Saltmarsh Mutual or another recognised insurer.

| Claim-free years | Discount applied under the 2025 table |
| --- | --- |
| One year earned | 25% |
| Maximum discount | 60% |

## How the discount is applied

1. The claims team confirms the claim-free history at renewal.
2. The discount in the table is applied to the base premium for the relevant policy.
3. Optional riders are priced separately and are not reduced by the no-claims discount.

## Notice of change

The 2025 table is the current wording. The old table, which also had a maximum of 60 percent, will be replaced from 1 July 2026. Policyholders will be notified before the change takes effect.
