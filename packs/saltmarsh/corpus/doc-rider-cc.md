# Courtesy car rider

## About this rider

The courtesy car rider, code RDR-CC, is an optional add-on for customers who hold Motor Comprehensive. It gives you a temporary replacement vehicle while your own car is off the road for repair following an insured claim. This guide explains what the rider covers, what it costs, and how it works when you need it.

## What the rider costs

The courtesy car rider costs £2.40 per month, added to your Motor Comprehensive premium. You pay it for as long as the rider stays on your policy, and you can add or remove it at your renewal or by contacting us during the policy term. The rider does not change the excess that applies to your main claim.

## Who can add the rider

The courtesy car rider can be added to Motor Comprehensive only. It is not available with Motor Third Party, and it cannot be attached to Home Essentials or Home Plus. If you hold Motor Comprehensive and would like the rider, ask us to add it and we will confirm the new monthly figure before the change takes effect.

## What you receive

When the rider applies, you are entitled to a courtesy car for up to 14 days while your insured vehicle is being repaired. The courtesy car is a small hatchback. It is intended to meet everyday travel needs, so it will not match the size, performance or specification of your own vehicle. Please check that a small hatchback suits your needs before you rely on it.

The 14 day period runs from the date the courtesy car is handed over to you. If repairs take longer than 14 days, the courtesy car is not extended under this rider, and we will explain any other options that may be available to you at that point.

## When the courtesy car is provided

The courtesy car is provided only while your car is being repaired at an approved repairer. An approved repairer is a garage that is part of our repair network and has agreed to our repair standards. If you choose a repairer outside the network, the courtesy car rider does not apply to that repair, although your main claim may still be considered under the terms of your policy. Before you take your car to a repairer, please confirm with us that they are an approved repairer so that the courtesy car can be arranged.

## Making a claim with the rider

Report your claim to us as soon as you can, ideally on the same day as the incident. We will ask for details of the damage and for the repairer you intend to use. Once the claim is accepted and the repair is booked with an approved repairer, we will arrange the courtesy car and tell you when and where to collect it. You will need a valid driving licence that meets our driver requirements, and you must return the courtesy car at the agreed time and in the condition in which it was provided.

## Things to keep in mind

The courtesy car rider is designed for the period when your insured car is in for repair, not as a general replacement vehicle. Use of the courtesy car is subject to the rules set out in the courtesy car agreement you sign on collection, including restrictions on mileage, fuel and where the vehicle may be taken. If you have questions about any part of this rider, please contact our customer team, who will be glad to help.
