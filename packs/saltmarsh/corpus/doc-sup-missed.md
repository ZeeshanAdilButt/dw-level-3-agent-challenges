# Missed a payment?

This guide explains what happens when a payment to Saltmarsh Mutual is missed, what you can do to put things right, and how to avoid losing your cover. It applies to all of our home and motor policies, including Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, along with any optional riders you have added.

## What happens when a payment is missed

Your cover stays in force while we work with you to resolve a missed payment, but it is important to act promptly. If a monthly payment is 7 days late, we will send you a reminder. The reminder shows the amount due, the date it was due, and the easiest ways to pay.

If a payment is still missing after reminders have been sent, we may cancel your policy. Before we do so, we will give you 14 days written notice. This notice will explain the amount outstanding, the date on which cancellation would take effect, and what you can do to keep your policy running. Cancellation is a last step, and we would much rather help you put your payments back on track.

## Failed direct debits

If a direct debit payment does not go through, for example because the account has insufficient funds or the details have changed, a failed direct debit payment carries a £12 fee. This fee is added to your account and must be cleared with the outstanding premium. We will tell you when the fee has been applied, so you can check it against your statement.

## How to put things right

The quickest way to resolve a missed payment is to pay the outstanding amount, including any fee, as soon as you can. You can do this through your online account, by calling our member services team, or by setting up a one-off card payment. Once the balance is cleared, your reminders stop and your policy continues as normal.

If you cannot pay the full amount at once, please contact us before the due date rather than after. We can discuss whether a revised payment schedule is possible. Agreeing a plan early gives us the best chance of keeping your cover in place and avoiding further fees.

## Keeping your cover up to date

To prevent missed payments, check that your bank details are current and that your direct debit has enough funds on the collection date. You can update your details at any time through your online account. If your circumstances change and you expect to struggle with payments, let us know early so we can support you.

## Your rights and our commitment

As a mutual, Saltmarsh Mutual exists to serve its policyholders, and we treat payment difficulties with fairness and care. If you feel a decision about your account is wrong, you can raise a complaint with us, and our complaints procedure explains how we will review it. Please keep copies of any correspondence you send or receive, as this will help us resolve matters quickly.

If you have questions about your account, please contact our member services team. They are happy to talk through your options and help you get back on track.
