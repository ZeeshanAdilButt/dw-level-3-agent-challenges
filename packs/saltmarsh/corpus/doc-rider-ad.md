# Accidental damage rider

## About this guide

This guide explains the accidental damage rider offered by Saltmarsh Mutual. It sets out what the rider adds to your cover, how much it costs, the excess you pay when you claim, and the most the rider will pay in a policy year. Read it alongside your policy schedule and the policy wording, which take priority if anything here differs from them.

## What the rider adds

Accidental damage is not included in Home Essentials or Home Plus as standard. If you want cover for accidental damage to your contents or your home, you need to add the accidental damage rider, which uses the rider code RDR-AD. Without the rider, accidental damage is not covered under either home policy.

The rider covers sudden and unintended damage that you cause by accident, such as breaking a television when moving furniture, spilling liquid on carpets or electrical goods, or cracking a sanitary fitting. It does not cover wear and tear, deliberate damage, or damage that was already present when the rider started. The full list of exclusions appears in the policy wording.

## Who can add the rider

The accidental damage rider can be added to Home Essentials or Home Plus only. It is not available on Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policies, and it cannot be added on its own without an eligible home policy. If you hold a home policy and wish to add the rider at a later date, you can ask us to do so, subject to our underwriting checks.

## Cost

The accidental damage rider costs £6.50 per month. This amount is added to your home policy premium and is collected with your usual payments. The rider cost is shown separately on your policy schedule so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

## Excess

When you make a claim under the accidental damage rider, an excess of £100 applies. This is the amount you pay towards each claim before we pay the rest. If you make more than one accidental damage claim in a policy year, the excess applies to each separate claim.

## Yearly limit

The accidental damage rider pays up to £10,000 in any one policy year. This is the most we will pay under the rider for all accidental damage claims combined in that policy year, after the excess has been deducted from each claim. Any loss above this limit is not covered under the rider for that year. The limit resets at the start of each new policy year.

## Making a claim

To report accidental damage, contact us as soon as possible after the damage happens. Keep any damaged items, and where practical take photographs before repairs or disposal. We will ask you for details of what happened and what was damaged, and we may arrange for an assessor to inspect the items. Claims are handled from first notice of loss through to settlement, and we will keep you informed at each stage.

## Further information

If you have questions about whether the accidental damage rider suits your home or your belongings, please speak to us before you make a claim. Our team can explain the cover in more detail and help you check that your policy meets your needs.
