# Claim story: Ng household

## A windscreen claim handled quickly

When a stone cracked the windscreen of the Ng household's car, they contacted Saltmarsh Mutual through the claims line and reported the damage the same day. Their Motor Comprehensive policy, with the optional courtesy car rider added, covered the repair and the temporary transport needed while the car was off the road.

The Ng household's windscreen was replaced within 24 hours of booking. The claims handler confirmed the approved glazing supplier, arranged the appointment at a time that suited the family, and kept them informed at each step, from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## What the family said

> "We expected a long wait and some paperwork. Instead we had one contact who called us back, a fitter at our door the next day, and a courtesy car that meant the school run never stopped. It was a relief to be looked after."
>
> Recorded for our files at flat 4, 18 Quay Lane, Marshford SX2 7DD.

## What we learned

This claim shows what the policyholder-owned model is for: prompt contact, clear communication and a settlement that puts the member back on the road. Every claim is different, but the principles behind this one apply across our home and motor cover alike.
