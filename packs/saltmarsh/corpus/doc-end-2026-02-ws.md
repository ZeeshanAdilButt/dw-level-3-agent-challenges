# Endorsement E-2026-02: windscreen replacement excess

## Purpose

This endorsement sets out a revised excess for windscreen replacement claims under Saltmarsh Mutual motor policies. It forms part of the policy wording for the policies it covers and should be read alongside the main policy document.

## Excess amount

Endorsement E-2026-02 sets the windscreen replacement excess at £95. This is the amount the policyholder pays towards each approved windscreen replacement claim, and it replaces the excess shown elsewhere in the policy wording for that cover.

## Scope

Endorsement E-2026-02 applies only to Motor Comprehensive policies that start on or after 1 March 2026. Policies that started before 1 March 2026 keep the windscreen excess stated in their existing wording and are not affected by this endorsement.

## Other terms

All other terms, conditions and exclusions of the policy remain unchanged. Where this endorsement and the policy wording differ on the windscreen replacement excess, this endorsement applies to the policies described in the scope section above.

## Questions

Policyholders with questions about which excess applies to their policy can contact Saltmarsh Mutual customer services, who will confirm the policy start date and the wording that applies.
