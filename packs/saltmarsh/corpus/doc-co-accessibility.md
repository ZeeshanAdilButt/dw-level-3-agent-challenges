# Accessibility

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, and we want every member to be able to read, understand and act on the documents we send. This guide explains the formats we offer and how to ask for them.

## Available formats

Our policy documents, claim letters and product guides are available in large print or braille free of charge on request. You do not need to explain why you need an alternative format, and the request does not affect your cover, premium or claim in any way.

## How to request a format

You can ask for a large print or braille copy when you take out a policy, when you report a claim, or at any time afterwards. Simply tell us the document you need and the format you prefer. Our team will confirm the request and send the copy to the address you have on file, or to another address you give us for this purpose.

## Other support

If you find any part of our service difficult to use, please tell us. We will work with you to find a way to share information that suits your needs, whether that means a call instead of written correspondence, a named contact for your claim, or extra time to respond to a letter. We review feedback on accessibility regularly and use it to improve how we communicate with all members.

## Contact

For help with formats or any other accessibility question, contact our member services team through the details shown on your policy schedule or on our website.
