# Changing your car

This guide explains what to do when you buy a different car and want your Saltmarsh Mutual motor policy to cover it. Changing your car is usually straightforward, but it helps to act promptly so that your cover is never interrupted.

## Buying a new car

When you buy a new car, it is covered for 7 days while you tell Saltmarsh about it. Please notify us within that period so that the new vehicle is added to your policy in the usual way. If you do not tell us within the 7 days, cover for the new car may not continue beyond that window, so we recommend contacting us as soon as you have the car.

## What we need from you

To update your policy, please have the following ready:

- the registration number and make and model of the new car
- the date you took ownership of the car
- the estimated annual mileage and where the car is usually kept overnight
- details of any modifications, alarms or security devices fitted
- the name of any additional drivers you want covered

## Changing your cover level

If the new car is worth more, is more expensive to repair, or is used differently from your previous car, your cover level or premium may change. We will explain any change to your premium before it takes effect, and you can choose whether to keep the updated cover.

## Selling or disposing of your old car

When you no longer own your previous car, please tell us so that its cover can be ended. Keep any receipt or confirmation of sale, as we may ask for it. If you have a Motor Comprehensive policy with optional riders such as a courtesy car, please check how those benefits apply to your new vehicle.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a change affects your cover, please contact our member services team before you drive the new car. We are happy to talk it through with you.
