# Claim story: Whitlock family

## Background

The Whitlock family has been a Saltmarsh Mutual policyholder under a Home Plus policy for several years. Their home is a family house with a small garden and a utility room where the main water supply enters the building. Like many policyholders, they had never needed to make a claim before, so when the trouble came, they were unsure what to expect.

## What happened

During a cold snap, a pipe in the utility room froze and then split, releasing water into the floor and the ground floor rooms beside it. The Whitlock family's frozen pipe claim was reported on the claims line at 2am, when the water had already begun to spread. The call handler took the first notice of loss, confirmed that the family was safe, and advised them how to isolate the water supply to limit further damage.

## How we handled it

Once the report was logged, a claims handler contacted the family the same morning to explain the next steps. An approved contractor attended to make the building safe and dry out the affected areas. Because the family had a home emergency rider in place, temporary accommodation arrangements were made while repairs were carried out, so the household could stay comfortable throughout.

## The outcome

Repairs were completed and the home was restored to its previous condition. The claim was settled in full, and the family received a clear written summary of the costs covered and the work completed.

## Our commitment

Every claim we handle is treated with care and clear communication. We are proud to be owned by our policyholders, and stories like this one remind us why we exist.
