# Claim story: Tanaka family

*Saltmarsh Mutual case study, effective 2026-04-01*

## The accident

The Tanaka family was driving home on a wet evening when another vehicle pulled out of a side road and struck the rear passenger door of their car. Nobody was seriously hurt, but the car was undrivable and the family needed to get to work and school the following morning. The other driver accepted responsibility at the scene, and the family made their claim with Saltmarsh Mutual the next day.

## How the claim was handled

The family reported the loss through our app, which asked for photographs, the other driver's details and a short description of what happened. A claims handler reviewed the information within the same working day and confirmed that the family's Motor Comprehensive policy covered the repairs. Because the accident was not the family's fault, the handler also arranged for the excess to be recovered from the other driver's insurer.

Once the repairs were agreed with an approved repairer, the family was kept informed at each stage. The courtesy car rider on their policy meant they had a replacement vehicle while their own car was being repaired, so the household routine was disrupted as little as possible.

## The outcome

The Tanaka family's non-fault accident excess was refunded in full, so they paid nothing towards the repair of their car. The claim was settled once the repairs were complete and the final invoice had been checked against the agreed scope.

## What we took from it

This claim shows the value of clear reporting, prompt handling and recovering costs that were never the policyholder's to bear. Every member of Saltmarsh Mutual can expect the same care, whatever the size of the claim.
