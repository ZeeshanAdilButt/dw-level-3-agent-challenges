# Clause 4.5: flood cover (Home Plus)

**Document type:** Clause
**Audience:** Public
**Authority:** Wording
**Effective from:** 2026-07-01

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how flood cover operates under a Home Plus policy issued by Saltmarsh Mutual. It explains what is covered, when cover starts, how the flood excess applies, and what the policyholder should expect when making a claim. It applies to Home Plus only. It does not apply to Home Essentials, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policies, and no other Saltmarsh Mutual product carries flood cover under this clause.

## What is covered

Under clause 4.5, Home Plus covers flood damage to the buildings and contents insured under the policy, subject to the terms, exclusions and limits that apply to the policy as a whole. Flood damage means physical damage to insured property caused by water that has escaped from a river, watercourse, reservoir, canal, or the sea, or by rainwater or surface water that has entered the insured building. Where the policy schedule shows a sum insured for buildings or contents, that limit applies to flood claims in the same way it applies to other claims under the policy.

Flood cover does not extend to damage that arises from maintenance failures, gradual seepage, or water escaping from the policyholder's own plumbing, which is dealt with under the separate water damage wording in the policy.

## When flood cover starts

Flood cover on a new Home Plus policy starts on the 8th day after the policy start date shown in the schedule. This waiting period applies to all new Home Plus policies from the date this clause takes effect. Any flood damage that occurs before the flood cover start date is not covered under clause 4.5, even if the policy itself is already in force.

Where a Home Plus policy is renewed without a break in cover, the waiting period does not apply again, and flood cover continues from the previous period. Where a policyholder moves from another Saltmarsh Mutual product to Home Plus, the start date for flood cover is confirmed in writing when the change is made.

## The flood excess

The Home Plus flood excess is £500. This is the amount the policyholder pays towards each valid flood claim before Saltmarsh Mutual pays the remainder of the approved settlement. The flood excess applies per claim and is separate from any general excess shown in the policy schedule. Where a single flood event gives rise to more than one claim under the same policy, the flood excess applies to each claim unless the policy schedule states otherwise.

The flood excess is deducted from the settlement amount when the claim is paid. It is not charged in advance, and it is not payable where Saltmarsh Mutual decides that no payment is due under the policy.

## Making a flood claim

A policyholder should report a flood claim as soon as reasonably possible, and in any case before carrying out repairs beyond what is needed to make the property safe and prevent further damage. Keeping receipts for emergency measures, such as temporary drying or board-up work, helps the claim progress. Photographs of the damage and of the affected rooms or items should be taken where it is safe to do so.

Saltmarsh Mutual will acknowledge the claim, confirm the next steps, and arrange assessment where required. Settlement will be based on the terms of the policy, the sum insured, and the reasonable cost of repair or replacement, with the flood excess deducted as set out above.

## Relationship with other parts of the policy

Clause 4.5 operates alongside the general conditions, the claims conditions and the exclusions of the Home Plus policy. Where a term in this clause conflicts with a term in the policy schedule for a particular policyholder, the schedule takes precedence to the extent of the conflict. Optional riders, including the home emergency rider, may provide assistance that complements flood cover but do not change the terms of clause 4.5 unless a rider expressly says so.

## Questions and contact

Policyholders who are unsure whether a loss falls within clause 4.5 should contact Saltmarsh Mutual before making any commitment to repairs. Details of how to contact the claims team are provided in the policy documents and on the Saltmarsh Mutual website.
