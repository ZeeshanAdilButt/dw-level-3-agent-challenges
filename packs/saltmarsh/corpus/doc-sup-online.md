# Setting up your online account

This guide explains how to register for your Saltmarsh Mutual online account and sign in with a one-time code. Effective from 2026-07-01.

## Before you start

To register for the online account you need your policy number and the postcode on the policy. Have your policy documents to hand so you can check both details. If you hold both a home and a motor policy, you can use either policy number to register, and both policies will appear once your account is set up.

## Registering for your account

1. Open the Saltmarsh Mutual website and select the option to create an online account.
2. Enter your policy number exactly as it appears on your documents.
3. Enter the postcode on the policy.
4. Enter an email address you want to use for sign-in and account messages.
5. Choose a password that meets the requirements shown on screen.
6. Select the button to submit your details.

## Signing in with a one-time code

1. Go to the sign-in page and enter the email address you registered with.
2. Select the option to send a one-time sign-in code.
3. Open the email from Saltmarsh Mutual and copy the code it contains.
4. Enter the code on the sign-in page before it expires. A one-time sign-in code stays valid for 10 minutes.
5. If the code expires, request a new one and use the most recent code.

## Getting help

If you cannot complete registration, check that your policy number and policy postcode match your documents. If you still need help, contact our customer support team through the website or by phone, and have your policy number ready.
