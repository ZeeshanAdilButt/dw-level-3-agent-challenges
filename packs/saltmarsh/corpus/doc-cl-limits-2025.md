# Claim time limits (2025)

**Document type:** Claims | **Audience:** Public | **Authority:** Rule | **Effective from:** 1 March 2025

> **Replaced from 1 July 2026.** The time limits in this document apply to claims under the 2025 rules only. Please refer to the current claim time limits document for claims made on or after 1 July 2026.

## Purpose

This document sets out how long policyholders have to report a claim to Saltmarsh Mutual under the rules in force from 1 March 2025. Reporting a claim promptly helps us start the first notice of loss process quickly, gather evidence while it is still available, and settle the claim without unnecessary delay.

## Reporting limits

Under the 2025 rules, the reporting limits for each type of claim are shown below.

| Claim type | Reporting limit under the 2025 rules |
|---|---|
| Home claim | Must be reported within 60 days |
| Motor accident | Must be reported within 30 days |

## Scope

These limits apply to the Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies, including any optional riders attached to them. The time limit runs from the date the policyholder became aware of the incident that gives rise to the claim.

## Making a report

Policyholders can report a claim through the Saltmarsh Mutual claims team using the contact details on their policy documents. Early reporting is always encouraged, even when the full details of the loss are not yet known.
