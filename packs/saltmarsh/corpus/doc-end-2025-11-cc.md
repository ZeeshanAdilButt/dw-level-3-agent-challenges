# Endorsement E-2025-11: courtesy car days

## Purpose

This endorsement changes the courtesy car rider sold by Saltmarsh Mutual. It extends the courtesy car rider to up to 21 days of cover when a covered claim keeps a policyholder's insured vehicle off the road.

## Scope

Endorsement E-2025-11 applies only to courtesy car riders bought on or after 1 November 2025. Courtesy car riders bought before that date continue on the terms that applied when they were purchased, unless the policyholder chooses to change their cover at renewal.

## Effect on cover

Where a courtesy car rider is covered by this endorsement, the courtesy car may be provided for up to 21 days from the date the vehicle is handed over to an approved repairer or is otherwise taken out of service under the claim. The other terms of the courtesy car rider, including its exclusions and the vehicle class offered, remain unchanged.

## Handling of claims

Claims are handled from first notice of loss through to settlement. Policyholders should notify us as soon as possible after an incident so that the courtesy car period can be arranged without delay.

## Policyholder questions

Policyholders with questions about this endorsement can contact our customer team, who will confirm whether a particular courtesy car rider falls within its scope.
