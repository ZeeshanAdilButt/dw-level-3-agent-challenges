# Home policy wording: paying your premium

Effective from 1 July 2026

## Purpose of this wording

This section explains the ways you can pay your Home Essentials or Home Plus premium, what happens if a payment is late or fails, and when Saltmarsh Mutual may cancel your policy for non-payment. It should be read together with your policy schedule and the general conditions of your policy.

## Payment options

Your home premium can be paid in one of two ways. You may pay the full annual premium at the start of your policy by card. Alternatively, you may pay in monthly instalments by direct debit. The table below sets out the key figures that apply to these options.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Full payment method | Card, paid in full at the start of the policy |
| Monthly payment method | Direct debit |
| Interest on monthly payments | 14.9% APR, charged on monthly payments |
| Reminder for a late monthly payment | Sent if a monthly payment is 7 days late |
| Notice before cancellation for non-payment | 14 days written notice, given after reminders have been sent |
| Fee for a failed direct debit payment | £12 per failed payment |
| Choice of monthly direct debit date | Any day from the 1st to the 28th of the month |

## Paying in full by card

If you choose to pay your home premium in full, you may use a valid card at the time you take out or renew your policy. No interest applies to a full payment. Your policy schedule will confirm the amount paid and the period of cover it relates to.

## Paying monthly by direct debit

If you choose to pay in monthly instalments, you must authorise a direct debit from a bank account in your name. Interest is charged on monthly payments at 14.9% APR, and this is included in the total amount payable shown when you take out or renew your policy. You may choose the day of the month on which your direct debit is collected. The monthly direct debit date can be any day from the 1st to the 28th. Dates later in the month are not available, so that payments are not affected by variable month lengths.

## Late and missed payments

If a monthly payment has not been received, the following steps apply.

1. If a monthly payment is 7 days late, Saltmarsh Mutual will send you a reminder. The reminder will tell you the amount outstanding and how to pay it.
2. You may pay the outstanding amount at any time after the reminder is sent. Your cover remains in place while you are working to settle the account in good faith.
3. If payment is still missing after reminders have been sent, Saltmarsh Mutual may cancel your policy. Before doing so, we will give you 14 days written notice of cancellation, so that you have a fair opportunity to bring the account up to date.
4. If you pay the outstanding amount before the cancellation takes effect, the notice will be withdrawn and your policy will continue without a break in cover.

## Failed direct debit payments

If a direct debit payment is returned unpaid, for example because the account has insufficient funds or the details have changed, a fee of £12 is charged for each failed payment. This fee is added to your account balance and must be paid with the outstanding instalment. You should contact Saltmarsh Mutual promptly if you expect a payment to fail, so that we can help you make alternative arrangements where possible.

## Changing your payment details

You must tell us promptly if your bank account, card or contact details change. Failure to keep these details up to date may result in missed payments, reminders and fees under this wording. You can update your payment details by contacting us through the usual channels shown in your policy documents.

## Interaction with cover

Cover under your home policy depends on the premium being paid in accordance with this wording. Where a policy has been cancelled for non-payment, no cover applies from the date the cancellation takes effect. Saltmarsh Mutual will confirm the effective date of cancellation in writing.
