# Our products

This guide explains the four insurance products Saltmarsh Mutual offers, which one might suit you, and the optional extras you can add. Because we are owned by our policyholders, we design these products to be clear, fair and easy to understand. It takes effect from 1 July 2026.

## Who can buy our products

You can buy a Home policy or a Motor Third Party policy if you are at least 18. Our other products follow the same basic eligibility rules, and your policy documents will confirm the details for your own circumstances.

## Our four products

We offer two home products and two motor products. Each has its own product code, which you will see on your policy schedule and in any correspondence with us. Find the one that matches what you need to protect.

**Home Essentials (HOM-ESS-1)** is our core home policy. It covers the building and contents of your home against the main risks such as fire, theft and escape of water. It suits people who want solid, straightforward protection at a sensible level of cover.

**Home Plus (HOM-PLS-2)** builds on Home Essentials with wider protection. It adds flood cover, storm damage to fences and gates, and cover for belongings away from home. It suits people who live in areas with more weather exposure, or who regularly take valuable items out of the house.

**Motor Third Party (MOT-TPO-1)** is our basic motor policy. It covers your legal liability to other people and their property if you cause an accident, but it does not cover damage to your own vehicle. It suits owners of older or lower value vehicles who want the legally required protection at the lowest cost.

**Motor Comprehensive (MOT-CMP-2)** is our broadest motor policy. It covers your liability to others and also covers damage to your own vehicle, including damage caused by accidents and theft. It suits owners of newer or higher value vehicles who want wider protection for their own car.

## Optional extras (riders)

You can add riders to certain policies to tailor your cover. Each rider has its own terms, and we will show you the cost before you agree to add it.

- **Accidental damage (RDR-AD):** Accidental damage is not included in Home Essentials or Home Plus. It is available only as the RDR-AD rider, which you can add to those policies if you want cover for accidental damage to your home or contents.
- **Legal expenses:** Helps with the cost of pursuing or defending certain legal matters related to your policy.
- **Home emergency:** Gives access to help with urgent repairs to your home, such as a burst pipe or a broken lock.
- **Courtesy car:** Provides a temporary vehicle while your insured car is being repaired after a covered claim.

## Making a claim

If something goes wrong, contact us as soon as you can. We will take your first notice of loss, guide you through the next steps, and work with you until your claim is settled. Your policy schedule shows the product code you hold, which helps us find your details quickly.

## Choosing the right product

If you are unsure which product fits your needs, speak to our team before you buy. We are happy to explain the differences in plain language and help you decide. This guide is for general information only, and your policy wording is the final word on what is covered.
