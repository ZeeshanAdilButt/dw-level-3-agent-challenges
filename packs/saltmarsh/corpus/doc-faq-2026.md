# Frequently asked questions (September 2026)

Document type: FAQ
Audience: Public
Authority: Marketing
Effective from: 15 September 2026

## About Saltmarsh Mutual

Saltmarsh Mutual is a home and motor insurer owned by its policyholders. Because we belong to the people we insure, we aim to keep our products fair, clear and easy to understand. This FAQ answers the questions we are asked most often about our cover, our rider options and how we handle claims.

## Our products

### What home insurance do you offer?

We offer two home products. Home Essentials covers the basics of your home and contents. Home Plus adds broader cover for people who want more protection for their belongings and property.

### What motor insurance do you offer?

We offer two motor products. Motor Third Party covers injury or damage you cause to other people and their property. Motor Comprehensive covers your own vehicle as well as third party liability.

### Which optional riders can I add?

You can add optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and a courtesy car. Riders are optional extras, so please check the policy schedule to confirm which ones apply to you.

## Cover and costs

### What is the theft excess on Motor Comprehensive?

The September 2026 FAQ says the Motor Comprehensive theft excess is £250. The excess is the amount you pay towards a claim before we contribute. Please check your policy schedule for the figure that applies to your own policy, as excesses can vary.

### What is the escape of water limit?

The September 2026 FAQ says the escape of water limit is £35,000. This is the most we would pay for an escape of water claim under the relevant section of your home policy. Your schedule shows the limit that applies to you.

### Can I change my mind after buying?

Yes. The September 2026 FAQ says the cooling-off period is 30 days. If you change your mind within that period, you can cancel and we will explain how any premium is refunded. Cancellation after the cooling-off period may carry a charge, so please read your policy wording.

## Making a claim

### How do I start a claim?

You can tell us about an incident as soon as it happens. This first notification is called first notice of loss, and it starts the process. Having your policy number and a clear account of what happened will help us move quickly.

### How quickly are claims decided?

The September 2026 FAQ says every claim is decided within 3 working days. We also aim to decide a claim within 10 working days of receiving all the documents we need. Some claims are more complex and take longer, and we will keep you informed if that happens.

### What documents might you ask for?

We may ask for photographs, repair estimates, police reports for theft, receipts or other evidence that supports your claim. Asking for the right information early helps avoid delays.

### What happens after a claim is settled?

Once a claim is settled, we will confirm the outcome in writing and explain any payment or repair arrangements. If you have questions about the settlement, please contact us and we will explain it.

## Contact and complaints

### How can I contact you?

You can reach our customer team through the contact details on our website. Please have your policy number ready so we can find your details quickly.

### What if I am unhappy with a decision?

If you are unhappy with how a claim or complaint has been handled, please tell us. We will review the matter and explain our reasoning. If you are still not satisfied, you may have further options set out in your policy documents.
