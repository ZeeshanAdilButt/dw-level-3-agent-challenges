# Legal expenses claims

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how a legal expenses claim works under the legal expenses rider that can be added to a Saltmarsh Mutual Home Plus, Home Essentials, Motor Comprehensive or Motor Third Party policy. It is written for policyholders and for anyone helping a policyholder make a claim. It takes effect from 1 July 2026.

## What the rider covers

The legal expenses rider pays up to £50,000 in legal costs per claim, subject to the terms of your policy schedule. Costs covered can include the fees of a solicitor or legal adviser, court fees and certain expert reports that the adviser considers necessary to pursue or defend the matter. The rider does not pay costs that fall outside the scope set out in your policy wording, and it does not pay fines, penalties or damages awarded against you.

## When we will fund legal costs

Legal costs are paid only while the legal adviser thinks the case has better than a 50% chance of success, which we describe as reasonable prospects. We review the position as the matter progresses. If the prospects fall to a level where funding is no longer justified, we will tell you in writing, explain the reasons, and set out what happens to costs already incurred. You can ask for the decision to be reviewed, and we will explain how to do so.

## How to use the legal helpline

Call the legal helpline before you instruct a solicitor. The helpline is the first point of contact for every legal expenses matter. The adviser on the line will ask about the dispute, check that it falls within the rider, and confirm whether the claim can be accepted. If it can, they will tell you which panel firm to use and what the agreed rates are. Instructing a solicitor before you have called may mean that costs are not covered, so please do not skip this step.

When you call, have your policy number, the date of the incident or the start of the dispute, and a short summary of what has happened ready. Keep a note of the reference number the adviser gives you.

## Documents we need

A legal expenses claim needs every letter received from the other side. This includes letters before action, offers, demands, court papers and any correspondence that sets out their position. Please send copies as soon as you receive them and do not discard the originals, as we may need to see them in full. Missing letters can delay the decision on your claim, and in some cases they can affect whether costs are covered.

Also send any contracts, invoices, photographs, receipts or witness details that support your case.

## Timescales and next steps

We aim to confirm whether a claim is accepted within a few working days of receiving complete information. Where a claim is refused, we will write to you with the reasons and explain your options. Where it is accepted, the adviser will keep you informed at each stage and tell you about any changes to funding.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether your situation is covered, contact the legal helpline or your usual Saltmarsh Mutual claims team. Our team will be glad to explain the rider in plain terms before you take any further step.
