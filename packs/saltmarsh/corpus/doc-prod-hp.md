# Home Plus

## Overview

Home Plus is the enhanced home insurance product from Saltmarsh Mutual, owned by its policyholders. The product code is HOM-PLS-2. It builds on Home Essentials and adds cover that many households find useful, including flood cover, storm damage to fences and gates, and cover for belongings away from home. This page explains what Home Plus adds, the main limits, and the standard excess so that you can compare it with Home Essentials before you buy.

## What Home Plus Adds Over Home Essentials

Home Plus includes everything in Home Essentials, plus three extra areas of cover. Each one is described below.

Flood cover is included in Home Plus. Flood damage to your buildings and contents is covered under clause 4.5 of the HOM-PLS-2 policy wording. Flood cover is not part of Home Essentials, so if flooding is a concern for your property, Home Plus is the product to consider.

Storm damage to fences and gates is also included in Home Plus. Where a storm damages fences and gates at your property, the cost of repair or replacement is covered under the terms of HOM-PLS-2, subject to the policy limits and excess.

Cover for belongings away from home is included in Home Plus. Personal items you take outside the home, such as a laptop or bicycle, are covered for loss or damage within the terms of HOM-PLS-2. Home Essentials does not include this extra protection.

## Accidental Damage

Accidental damage is not included in Home Essentials or Home Plus as standard. If you want accidental damage cover, it is available only as the RDR-AD rider, which you can add to either product for an additional premium. The rider is optional, so you only need it if you want accidental damage protection in addition to the core cover described above. Check the rider schedule for the terms that apply to it.

## Key Limits and Excess

The table below sets out the main numeric terms for Home Plus. Limits are the most we will pay for each category of cover in any one claim, and the excess is the amount you pay towards each claim.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Home Plus buildings limit | £750,000 |
| Home Plus contents limit | £75,000 |
| Home Plus standard excess | £150 |

The buildings limit applies to the structure of your home, and the contents limit applies to your belongings within the home. The standard excess of £150 is deducted from each valid claim. Your policy schedule shows the exact limits and excess that apply to your cover, which may differ if you have chosen a different level of cover or added optional riders.

## Making a Claim

If something happens that you think is covered by Home Plus, contact us as soon as reasonably possible so we can record the first notice of loss. We will then guide you through the next steps, including what evidence we need and how the claim will be assessed. Please keep any damaged items, receipts and photographs, as these help us settle the claim quickly. Our claims team handles each claim from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## Choosing Between Home Essentials and Home Plus

Home Essentials suits households that want core buildings and contents protection at a lower level of cover. Home Plus suits households that want flood cover, storm damage to fences and gates, and cover for belongings away from home in addition to the core protection. If you are unsure which product fits your circumstances, speak to our team before you buy so that the cover matches your needs.
