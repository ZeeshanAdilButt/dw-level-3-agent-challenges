# Adding a rider to your policy

This guide explains how to add an optional rider to your Saltmarsh Mutual home or motor policy part way through your policy year. Riders are optional cover that you can add to a Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policy. The riders available are accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car.

## Before you start

Check which rider suits your needs and confirm that it is available for your policy type. Each rider has its own terms, which you can read in full before you make a request. Keep your policy number to hand, as you will need it to identify your policy.

## How the charge works

A rider added mid-term is charged for the days left in the policy year. Your premium for the rider is calculated pro rata, so you pay only for the cover that remains from the date the rider starts. The earlier you add a rider in the policy year, the more days of cover you receive for the charge.

## How to add a rider

Contact us through your online account or by calling our member services team. Tell us which rider you want and the date you would like it to start. We will confirm the charge, which reflects the days left in the policy year, before anything is added to your policy. Once you accept, we will send you an updated policy schedule showing the new rider and its start date.

## Changes to your cover

Adding a rider changes the terms of your policy from the start date you agree. If you have a question about how a rider affects your existing cover, please speak to us before you make the change.
