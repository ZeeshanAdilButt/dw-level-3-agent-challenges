# How we check your identity

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, and we take the care of your personal information seriously. Before we share any policy details or talk about a claim, we need to be sure we are speaking with the right person. This guide explains what we ask for, why we ask, and what you can expect when you contact us by phone, online or in writing.

## Why we check

Your home and motor policies contain personal information, including your address, vehicles, claim history and payment details. If someone else were to gain access to that information, they could misuse it or make changes to your cover without your knowledge. Checking identity protects you, and it protects the mutual as a whole, since every policyholder shares in the fairness and security of the organisation.

## What we ask for

When you contact us, we will ask you to confirm two things:

- Your policy number, which appears on your policy schedule, your renewal documents and your online account.
- Your date of birth, as the policyholder named on the policy.

We ask for both because neither piece of information is enough on its own to identify you. Together, they give us a reliable way to confirm that the person asking about the policy is the policyholder or someone the policyholder has authorised.

If you are calling about a claim, we may also ask a short question about the claim reference or the date of the incident. This helps us find the right file quickly and avoids mixing up similar cases.

## Sign-in codes for online accounts

If you use our online service, we may send you a one-time sign-in code by text message or email. Each code stays valid for 10 minutes from the moment it is issued. After that time, the code will no longer work and you will need to request a new one. Please do not share your sign-in code with anyone, including members of our staff. We will never ask you to read out a code over the phone.

## What happens if we cannot confirm your identity

If we cannot match the details you give us to our records, we will not share policy or claim information. We will explain what we need to complete the check and suggest the quickest way to provide it. In some cases we may ask you to contact us from the phone number or email address we hold on file. This is a safeguard, and it is not a judgement about you.

## Acting for someone else

If you are dealing with Saltmarsh on behalf of a policyholder, such as a family member or a professional adviser, the policyholder must first give us written authority. Once we have that authority on record, we can speak with you about the policy, though we will still ask the policyholder's policy number and date of birth.

## Keeping your details safe

Please keep your policy documents somewhere secure, check your correspondence for accuracy, and let us know promptly if your contact details change. If you believe someone has accessed your policy without permission, contact us straight away so that we can review the account and protect it.

If you have questions about this guide, please get in touch with our member services team, who will be glad to help.
