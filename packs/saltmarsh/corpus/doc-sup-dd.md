# Paying by direct debit

This guide explains how to pay your Saltmarsh Mutual premium by direct debit, how to choose the day your payment is taken, and how we tell you about changes to the amount you pay. It applies to all our home and motor policies, including any optional riders you have added.

## Choosing your payment date

You can set your monthly direct debit date to any day from the 1st to the 28th of the month. We ask for a date within this range so that every month has a valid payment day, including February. When you set up your policy, you will be asked to pick a date that suits your income. If you need to change it later, contact us and we will confirm the new date in writing before it takes effect.

## Changes to your payment amount

If we need to change the amount we collect by direct debit, we will give you 10 working days notice before the new amount is taken from your account. The notice will state the new amount, the date it starts and the reason for the change, such as a change to your cover, the addition or removal of a rider, or an adjustment at renewal. If you do not agree with a change, you can contact us before the notice period ends to talk through your options.

## Keeping your details up to date

Please tell us promptly if your bank account, name or address changes, as a payment that fails because of incorrect details may affect your cover. If a direct debit is returned unpaid, we will contact you to arrange payment. If you have any questions about your payment date or about a change to your amount, our member services team will be glad to help.
