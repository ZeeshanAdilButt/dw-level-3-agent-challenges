# Motor policy wording: conditions

This clause sets out the general conditions that apply to the motor section of your Saltmarsh Mutual policy. It applies to Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive cover, including any optional rider you have added to your policy schedule. Please read it together with your policy schedule, which shows the vehicle, the drivers and the level of cover that applies to you. Where this wording and your schedule differ, the schedule sets out the details of your cover, and this wording sets out the conditions you must meet.

## Who this wording applies to

These conditions apply to you as the policyholder, to any named driver and to any person who drives the insured vehicle with your permission. Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, so these conditions are written to keep the mutual fair for everyone who shares it. Each member is asked to play their part by giving accurate information, taking reasonable care of the vehicle and reporting incidents promptly.

## Your general duties

You must take reasonable care to keep the insured vehicle in a safe and roadworthy condition. You must make sure that the vehicle has a valid roadworthiness check where the law requires one, and that it is kept locked and secured when it is not in use. You must not allow the vehicle to be driven by anyone who does not hold a licence valid for that type of vehicle, or by anyone who is not permitted under your policy schedule.

You must answer questions truthfully and completely when you take out or change your policy. If information you gave us was inaccurate, we may adjust your premium, change the terms of your cover or, in serious cases, treat the policy as if it had never existed. Where you are unsure whether a fact is relevant, please tell us and let us decide.

## Changes you must report

Your policy is based on the details you gave us when it started. Several of those details can change during the life of the policy, and each change can affect the premium and the cover that applies. The following changes must be reported to Saltmarsh:

1. A change of car, meaning replacing the insured vehicle with another vehicle or adding a vehicle to the policy. You must tell Saltmarsh within 14 days of the change.
2. A change of address for the person who usually keeps the insured vehicle. This includes moving home or changing the postcode where the vehicle is normally parked overnight.
3. A change of driver, meaning adding a new named driver, removing one, or allowing a different person to become the main driver of the vehicle.
4. A change of use, meaning using the vehicle for a purpose other than the one shown on your schedule, such as using a private car for paid passenger work or for delivering goods.

You must tell Saltmarsh about each of these changes within 14 days. Changes that are not reported may affect whether a claim is paid, and we will apply the terms of this wording when deciding what happens next. If you are unsure whether a change needs reporting, please contact us before you make the change.

## Claims and notification of incidents

If the insured vehicle is involved in an accident, is stolen, is damaged by fire or vandalism, or causes injury or damage to another person's property, you must tell Saltmarsh as soon as reasonably possible. Delay can make it harder for us to investigate and can reduce the value of your claim. You must not admit liability, make an offer or agree to pay compensation to any other person without our written consent, as this may prevent us from defending the claim on your behalf.

You must co-operate with our investigation. This includes providing reasonable information, allowing us to inspect the vehicle and telling us about any other insurance that may cover the same incident. You should keep records of the incident, including photographs, witness details and any repair estimates.

## Legal proceedings

If anyone starts legal proceedings against you in connection with the insured vehicle, you must send a copy of any court summons or letter about the claim straight to Saltmarsh without delay. Do not ignore a summons, as missing a court date can have serious consequences for you and for your cover. Please send us the document as soon as you receive it, even if you believe the claim is unfounded, so that we can respond within the time allowed.

## Keeping your documents

You must keep your insurance certificate in a safe place and make it available when you are asked to show it, whether that is to us, to the police or to any other authorised person. The certificate confirms the cover that applies to the vehicle and the period it covers. If you lose the certificate or it is damaged, please tell us and we will send you a replacement.

## Annual mileage

Your premium is based on the annual mileage you declared when you took out the policy or when you last updated it. You must tell Saltmarsh if the actual annual mileage goes more than 10% above the figure you declared, because higher mileage increases the risk we are covering. Please tell us as soon as you expect the mileage to go above this level, and we will explain whether your premium needs to change. Failing to tell us about a significant increase in mileage may affect the outcome of a claim.

## Cancelling and changing your policy

You may cancel your policy at any time by contacting Saltmarsh. If you cancel, we will explain how any refund is calculated in line with your policy terms. We may cancel or change the policy where the conditions in this wording are not met, where information given to us was inaccurate, or where the law requires it. Any such action will be explained to you in writing.
