# Clause 6: personal liability

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out the personal liability cover included within Saltmarsh Mutual home policies and explains the limits that apply. It also records the position on banned dog breeds, which affects whether liability cover responds to certain claims. This wording applies to all policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date shown in the document header.

## Personal liability cover by plan

Personal liability cover protects the policyholder if they become legally responsible for injury to another person or for damage to another person's property arising from everyday life at the insured home. The amount of cover depends on the plan held.

Home Essentials includes £2 million of personal liability cover for each claim. This is the maximum amount Saltmarsh Mutual will pay under the personal liability section of a Home Essentials policy in respect of any one event or series of linked events.

Home Plus includes £5 million of personal liability cover for each claim. This is the maximum amount Saltmarsh Mutual will pay under the personal liability section of a Home Plus policy in respect of any one event or series of linked events.

Where a policyholder changes plan during the policy year, the limit that applies to any incident is the limit in force on the date the incident occurred.

## What personal liability cover does and does not include

Personal liability cover responds to a claim made against the policyholder, or against a named person living in the insured home, for accidental injury or accidental damage. It also covers reasonable legal costs incurred with Saltmarsh Mutual's prior written agreement, as set out in the policy wording.

Personal liability cover does not respond to deliberate acts, to liability arising from running a business from the insured home unless this is expressly agreed in writing, or to liability assumed under a contract that the policyholder would not otherwise have had. Liability arising from the ownership, use or possession of a motor vehicle is excluded under home policies and is dealt with under a motor policy instead.

## Banned dog breeds

Saltmarsh Mutual does not provide personal liability cover for any incident involving a dog of a breed that is prohibited under the law of the United Kingdom. Where a policyholder owns or keeps such a dog, or permits one to be kept at the insured home, cover for any resulting liability is excluded in full. Saltmarsh Mutual may ask the policyholder to confirm the breed of any dog kept at the insured home at inception and at renewal, and may decline a claim where the information given was incomplete or inaccurate.

Policyholders who are unsure whether a breed is prohibited should contact Saltmarsh Mutual before taking on a dog. Saltmarsh Mutual will confirm its position in writing on request.

## Notifying a liability event

A policyholder who becomes aware of a situation that may give rise to a personal liability claim must notify Saltmarsh Mutual as soon as reasonably possible. Delay may affect Saltmarsh Mutual's ability to investigate or defend the claim. The policyholder must not admit responsibility, make any settlement offer or agree to pay any sum without Saltmarsh Mutual's written consent, except where the law requires otherwise.

## Settlement and payment

Saltmarsh Mutual will handle the claim from first notice of loss through to settlement, appointing a claims handler to keep the policyholder informed of progress. Payments made under this clause are subject to the limit that applies to the plan held and to the other terms and conditions of the policy.
