# Adding a driver

This guide explains how to add a named driver to your Saltmarsh Mutual motor policy. Adding a driver is possible on both Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive cover, and it helps make sure that anyone who drives your insured vehicle is properly covered.

## Before you start

Check that the person you want to add holds a valid driving licence and is able to provide the details we need to assess their risk. You will need their full name, date of birth, licence details and how long they have held their licence. Having these ready will make the process quicker.

## How to add a named driver

You can add a named driver by contacting our member services team or by using the policy section of your online account. Tell us which vehicle the new driver will use and provide the information listed above. We will confirm whether the driver can be added and update your policy documents once the change is agreed.

## Fees

Adding a named driver to a motor policy carries a £20 admin fee. This fee covers the cost of reviewing the new driver's details and updating your policy records. The fee is shown in your quote before you confirm the change, so you can decide whether to go ahead.

## Important points to remember

Only named drivers are covered to drive your vehicle under the policy, so it is important to add anyone who will drive regularly or occasionally. Please let us know promptly if a named driver's circumstances change, such as a change to their licence, as this may affect your cover. If you are unsure whether a driver should be added, our team will be glad to help you work through the options.
