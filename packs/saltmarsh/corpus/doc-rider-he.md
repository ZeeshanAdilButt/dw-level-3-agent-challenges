# Home emergency rider

## About this rider

The home emergency rider, code RDR-HE, is an optional add-on for Saltmarsh Mutual home policyholders. It gives you access to a registered engineer when something in your home stops working in a way that cannot wait. This guide explains what the rider covers, what it costs, how many call-outs you can use, how much we pay, and how quickly an engineer will attend.

## Who can add it

The home emergency rider can be added to Home Essentials or Home Plus only. It is not available on Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policies, and it cannot be bought as a stand-alone cover. If you hold a home policy and would like to add the rider, speak to us at renewal or contact us during the policy year and we will confirm whether it can be added to your cover.

## What it costs

The home emergency rider costs £4.90 per month. This is added to your home policy premium and is paid with the rest of your premium under the payment method you have chosen. Cover starts on the date we confirm the rider has been added to your policy, and you can remove it at any time, with the change taking effect from the next monthly payment.

## What it covers

The rider covers three types of emergency inside the home:

- loss of heating or hot water
- a blocked toilet
- a burst pipe

Each of these must affect the home itself and must be reported to us as an emergency. The rider does not cover routine maintenance, the replacement of worn parts that have not failed suddenly, or problems that were known before the rider started and were not repaired. Damage caused by the emergency, such as water damage to floors or ceilings, may be covered under your main home policy rather than this rider, and we will explain which section applies when you report the claim.

## How many call-outs you get

The rider allows 2 call-outs in each policy year. A call-out is one visit by an engineer to attend an emergency. If the engineer finds that a single emergency needs more than one visit to resolve, the additional visit counts as a second call-out rather than a new claim, provided it is for the same problem. Unused call-outs do not carry over to the next policy year.

## How much we pay

We pay up to £500 per call-out. This covers the engineer's attendance, labour and the parts needed to make the home safe and working again, within that limit. If the cost of the repair exceeds £500, you are responsible for the difference, and the engineer will tell you the likely cost before any work beyond the limit begins. Where a permanent replacement is needed, we will explain the options available to you.

## How quickly an engineer attends

An engineer attends a home emergency within 4 hours of the call being accepted. We accept a call once you have reported the emergency to us and the details have been confirmed. Response times may be longer in rare circumstances such as severe weather affecting a large area, and in those cases we will keep you informed of the expected arrival time.

## Making a claim

To use the rider, contact us using the emergency number shown on your policy documents or in your online account. Please have your policy number ready and describe the problem clearly. Do not arrange your own engineer and submit the cost afterwards, as we can only pay for work carried out by an engineer we have arranged, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing.
