# Electric cars and charge points

This guide explains how Saltmarsh Mutual treats electric vehicle charging equipment under our home and motor policies. It is written for policyholders who have an electric car, a plug-in hybrid, or a home charging unit installed.

## Fixed home charge points

A fixed home electric vehicle charge point counts as part of the buildings for the purposes of your home policy. This means the unit mounted on your wall or driveway is covered under the buildings section of Home Essentials and Home Plus, in the same way as other fixed installations such as a boiler or a fitted kitchen. If the unit is damaged by an insured event such as fire, storm or escape of water, we will consider it alongside the rest of the buildings claim.

## Portable cables and mobile chargers

Charging cables and portable chargers that you carry in the car are not fixed to the property. They are treated as part of the vehicle or as personal belongings, depending on how they are being used at the time of loss. If you are unsure which applies to your equipment, please contact us before you make a claim so that we can confirm the position for your policy.

## Making a claim

If your charge point or cable is damaged, please tell us as soon as you can. Keep photographs of the damage and, where a fixed unit has been installed by an electrician, keep a copy of the installation paperwork. Our claims team will ask for these details when you report the loss, and will guide you through the next steps until the claim is settled.

## Questions

If you have a question about how your policy applies to charging equipment, please speak to our customer team. Policy wording always takes priority over this guide.
