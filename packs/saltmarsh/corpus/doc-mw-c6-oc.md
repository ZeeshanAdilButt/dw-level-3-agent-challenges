# Clause 6: driving other cars

## Scope

This clause sets out the terms that apply when a policyholder drives a car that they do not own, lease or have registered in their own name. It forms part of the wording of the Saltmarsh Mutual motor section and should be read alongside the policy schedule and the general conditions.

## Level of cover

Driving other cars gives third party cover only. Cover for loss or damage to the vehicle being driven, and for injury to the person driving it, is not included under this clause. Where a policyholder requires broader protection while driving a vehicle they do not own, they should contact Saltmarsh Mutual before the journey begins so that the position can be confirmed in writing.

## Eligibility

Driving other cars cover is for policyholders aged 25 or over. A policyholder who has not reached this age at the start of a period of driving is not covered under this clause, regardless of the third party cover held on their own vehicle. Saltmarsh Mutual may ask for proof of age before agreeing to any claim arising from driving other cars.

## Conditions

The policyholder must hold a valid licence for the class of vehicle being driven, must have the owner's permission to drive it, and must not use it for hire or reward unless the policy schedule states otherwise. Any change in circumstances that affects these conditions should be reported to Saltmarsh Mutual promptly.

## Claims

Claims arising from driving other cars should be notified to Saltmarsh Mutual as soon as reasonably possible, following the same first notice of loss process that applies to all motor claims.
