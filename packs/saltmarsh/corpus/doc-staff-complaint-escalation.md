# Complaint escalation

**Document type:** Internal playbook
**Audience:** Internal (staff only)
**Authority:** Rule
**Effective from:** 2026-06-01

## Purpose

This playbook sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual escalates a customer complaint from first contact to resolution. Every complaint must be logged, acknowledged and handled in the order below. Staff must not skip a stage.

## Escalation steps

1. Log the complaint in the case system within the same working day it is received, and record the policy type (Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive) and any rider in use.
2. Send the customer a written acknowledgement confirming the complaint reference and the expected response time.
3. Stage 1: a team leader reviews the complaint and responds to the customer within 3 working days (three working days) of receipt.
4. If the team leader considers goodwill appropriate, they may offer goodwill of up to £250 (250 pounds, GBP 250) per complaint. Any offer above this value must go to the complaints manager.
5. Stage 2: if the customer is not satisfied with the Stage 1 response, the complaint is passed to the complaints manager for a full review.
6. The complaints manager may offer goodwill of up to £1,000 (1,000 pounds, GBP 1,000) per complaint. Any offer above this value requires sign-off from the claims director.
7. Record every goodwill offer, its value and the reason for it in the case system before the offer is communicated to the customer.
8. Where the customer remains dissatisfied after Stage 2, issue the final response letter and explain the customer's right to refer the matter to the independent ombudsman.

## Internal reference

Complaints flagged for senior review are tagged with the internal code scheme Plover-4 so that they appear on the escalation dashboard. Do not use this code in customer correspondence.

## Ownership

The complaints manager owns this playbook and reviews it at least once a year.
