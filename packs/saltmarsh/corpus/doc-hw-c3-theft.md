# Clause 3.2: theft cover

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out when theft is covered under a Saltmarsh Mutual home policy. It applies to all home policies that include theft cover, including Home Essentials and Home Plus. It should be read together with the policy schedule, which shows which cover sections apply to your policy, and with the general conditions of your policy.

## When theft is covered

Clause 3.2 covers theft only when there is forced and violent entry to or exit from the home. This means that the thief must have gained access to, or left, the home by breaking in, for example by forcing a door, breaking a window or otherwise using force that leaves visible damage. Where there is no forced entry, for example where a door was left unlocked or a thief entered through an open window, theft is not covered under this clause. Where a claim is made, we will ask for evidence of the entry, such as photographs of damage or a crime reference number, so that we can confirm that the forced entry condition has been met.

## Locking conditions

To keep theft cover in force, the home must meet the following locking conditions:

- Outside doors must have a BS 3621 mortice lock fitted and in working order.
- Ground-floor windows must have key-operated locks fitted and in working order.

If these locks are not fitted or are not working at the time of the theft, the claim may be declined or reduced. We recognise that locks can be damaged in a burglary, and a lock that was in working order before the theft and is damaged by the thief will not on its own prevent a claim from being paid. Please keep records of the locks fitted to your home, such as an installer receipt, as these may be needed when you make a claim.

## Belongings away from the home

Clause 3.2 also deals with belongings that are taken from places other than the home. Under Home Essentials, there is no cover for belongings taken away from the home. This means that items stolen from a car, a bag or any other place outside the home are not covered under this plan.

Under Home Plus, belongings taken away from the home are covered up to £2,000 in total for each claim. This limit applies to the total amount claimed for belongings away from the home, and it is in addition to the cover that applies to items inside the home. The forced and violent entry condition and the locking conditions do not apply to the away-from-home cover in the same way, but the terms of your policy schedule and the general conditions still apply.

## Making a claim

If you believe that your home has been subject to theft, please report it to us as soon as you can, and where a crime has taken place, report it to the police and obtain a crime reference number. Your claim will be handled from first notice of loss through to settlement. Please keep any damaged items, receipts and photographs, as these will help us assess your claim. If you are unsure whether your claim is covered by this clause, please contact us before you make any repairs or replacements, as we may ask to inspect the damage first.
