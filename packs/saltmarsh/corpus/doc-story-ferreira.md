# Claim story: Ferreira family

## Background

When a winter storm lifted tiles from the roof of their home, the Ferreira family turned to Saltmarsh Mutual for help. They held a Home Plus policy with the home emergency rider, which meant they could reach a claims handler quickly and get temporary protection in place before the wind and rain did further harm.

## What happened

The family reported the damage as soon as it was safe to do so. A claims handler confirmed the details, arranged for a qualified roofing contractor to inspect the property, and explained each step of the process in plain language. The family's storm-damaged roof was repaired in 14 days, a timeframe that allowed them to return to normal life without long periods of disruption.

## Staying in touch

Throughout the claim, the family received regular updates by phone and by letter. When the contractor needed access to the loft, the handler rearranged the appointment around the family's school run so that nothing had to be rushed. Mrs Ferreira later said that knowing who to call made a stressful week feel manageable.
