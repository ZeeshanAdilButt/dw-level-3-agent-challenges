# App update: fingerprint sign-in

**Document type:** Release note
**Audience:** Public
**Authority:** Guide
**Effective from:** 2026-01-20

## What has changed

The Saltmarsh Mutual app now lets you sign in with fingerprint or face recognition on supported phones. Once you have turned the feature on, you can open the app and view your policies, make a payment, or check the status of a claim without typing your password each time. Your password still works, and you can always use it instead.

## Who can use it

The feature is available on phones that support fingerprint or face recognition through the phone's own security settings. If your phone does not support either method, the app will continue to ask for your usual sign-in details. Biometric data is stored on your device and is never sent to Saltmarsh Mutual.

## How to switch it on

Open the app, go to the security section of your account settings, and choose the biometric sign-in option. You will be asked to confirm your identity with your password the first time. You can turn the option off at any time from the same place.

## Questions

If you have trouble setting up the feature, contact our member services team through the app or on the number shown on your policy documents. Our team can help you sign in with your password and reset your settings if needed.
