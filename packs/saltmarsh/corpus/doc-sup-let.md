# Renting out a room or your home

This guide explains how renting out part or all of your home affects your Saltmarsh Mutual cover. Please read it before you take in a lodger or let your property to someone else.

## Taking in a lodger

Having one lodger does not need a change to a home policy, provided you live in the property yourself and the lodger shares your home as their main residence. Your Home Essentials or Home Plus cover continues as normal, and you do not need to tell us about the arrangement in most cases.

If you take in more than one lodger, or the arrangement changes in a way that affects how the property is used, please contact us so we can check your cover. Lodgers' own belongings are normally not covered under your policy, so they may wish to arrange their own contents insurance.

## Letting your home

Letting the whole property to tenants is a different type of use and is not covered by a standard home policy. Before you let your home, please tell us so we can discuss whether a landlord arrangement is suitable and what changes to your cover may be needed.

## Accidental damage and optional riders

If you have added the accidental damage or home emergency rider, check whether it still applies after you have a lodger or tenant, as some conditions may differ. Our customer service team can confirm this for your specific policy.

## What to do next

Tell us about any change to who lives in your home or how it is used. Early notice helps us keep your cover accurate, so that a claim is not affected by an arrangement we were not told about.
