# Large loss procedure

## Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual handles large losses across home and motor claims, from first notice of loss through to settlement. It applies to all staff who receive, assess, reserve or settle claims under Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, including claims involving the optional accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car riders.

## Identifying a large loss

A claim is treated as a large loss when the estimated cost of the loss exceeds £50,000. Handlers should make this assessment as early as possible, using the best available estimate at the time, and should revisit it whenever the reserve changes materially. Where a claim is close to the threshold, handlers should err on the side of treating it as large and seek guidance from their team leader.

## Notification to the reinsurer

Every loss over £50,000 must be notified to the reinsurer within 5 working days of the loss being identified as large. The notification is made by the claims team through the designated reinsurance contact, and the date of notification must be recorded on the claim file. Missing this deadline has direct consequences for recovery, so the 5 working day period is not to be treated as a target to approach at leisure.

## Sign-off

A large loss needs sign-off from the claims director before any settlement offer, admission of liability or commitment of significant expenditure is communicated to the policyholder or a third party. The claims director's approval, the date given and any conditions attached must be recorded on the claim file. Handlers must not make binding commitments on a large loss until this sign-off has been obtained.

## Records and review

Handlers should keep the claim file up to date with reserve changes, correspondence, reinsurance notifications and approvals. Large loss files are reviewed regularly by the claims team leader to confirm that notification and sign-off steps have been completed and that the file reflects the current position.
