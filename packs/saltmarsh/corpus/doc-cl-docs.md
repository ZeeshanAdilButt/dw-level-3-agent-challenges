# Documents needed for each type of claim

## Purpose of this document

This document sets out the documents Saltmarsh Mutual needs from you to handle each type of claim. It applies to all claims made under Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to claims made under the optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and a courtesy car. It is effective from 1 July 2026.

Our aim is to keep the claims process as quick as possible. Sending the right documents at the start means we can assess your claim sooner and avoid asking you for the same information more than once. You do not need to send everything at once. Where a document is listed as needed, please send it as soon as you can after you notify us of the loss, and tell us if you are having difficulty obtaining it.

## Theft from the home

Theft is one of the most common home claims we receive, and the first step is usually to report the crime to the police. When you report the theft, the police will give you a reference, and we ask you to quote it when you tell us about the claim. The reference lets us check the details against the police record and speeds up the decision on your claim.

Before the claim can be settled, we also need evidence that you owned the items taken. Please check the proof of ownership section below for the kinds of document we accept. Make a list of what was stolen as completely as you can, with a rough value for each item, even if you cannot yet provide proof for everything.

For a theft claim you will need to provide the following:

- The police crime reference number
- Proof of ownership for the items taken

## Escape of water

An escape of water claim covers damage caused when water leaks or overflows from a pipe, tank, appliance or drain in your home. For these claims we need evidence of the damage and of the cause. Take clear photos of the affected areas before anything is removed or repaired, as these help us see the extent of the damage quickly. If the damage is ongoing, take further photos as work progresses.

We also need a report from a qualified professional who has looked at the source of the leak. This report confirms where the water came from and what needed to be repaired, and it is an important part of assessing the claim.

For an escape of water claim you will need to provide the following:

- Photos of the damage
- The plumber's report describing the cause of the leak and the repair carried out

## Storm damage

Storm damage covers loss caused by high winds, heavy rain, hail or lightning affecting your home or its contents. As with other property claims, we need photos showing the damage to the building or items. Where the damage is to the roof, windows, fences or outbuildings, photos from the ground and, where it is safe to take them, from a closer angle help us see what has happened.

We also need a written repair estimate for the work required. The estimate should describe the work, the materials and the cost. If you have more than one estimate, send them all, as this helps us check that the proposed repairs are reasonable. Please do not start permanent repairs until we have had the chance to see the damage, unless it is necessary to make the property safe or to stop further damage, in which case keep the photos and receipts for that work.

For a storm claim you will need to provide the following:

- Photos of the storm damage
- A written repair estimate for the work needed

## Flood damage on Home Plus

Flood cover is included in Home Plus. If you are claiming for flood damage, we need photos of the damage and the date and place the water came into your home. Please give the date as accurately as you can and describe where the water entered, such as a doorway, window, air brick or the ground floor generally. If the water came from a river, drain or surface water, tell us that too, as it helps us understand the cause of the loss.

For a flood claim on Home Plus you will need to provide the following:

- Photos of the flood damage
- The date and place the water came in

## Subsidence

Subsidence claims concern movement of the ground beneath your home that causes cracks or structural damage. These claims need specialist assessment. Saltmarsh arranges the survey for you, so you do not need to appoint your own surveyor. We will contact you to arrange a visit at a convenient time. Please keep any earlier reports or correspondence about the cracks or movement, as these help the surveyor understand the history of the problem.

For a subsidence claim you will need to provide the following:

- A survey, which Saltmarsh arranges for you

## Motor accident

If you are involved in an accident with another vehicle, we need the details of the other driver and photos of the damage to both vehicles. The other driver's details should include their name, address, contact number, vehicle registration and insurance details where you have them. Photos help us understand how the accident happened and what damage each vehicle has suffered. Take photos from several angles, including any damage to the other vehicle, and of the scene if it is safe to do so.

For a motor accident claim you will need to provide the following:

- The other driver's details
- Photos of the damage to your vehicle and the other vehicle

## Motor theft

If your vehicle has been stolen, you must report the theft to the police before you tell us about the claim. The police will give you a crime reference number, which you should quote when you contact us. We also need all sets of keys for the vehicle. If you have only one set, please tell us. Keys are checked because the number supplied helps us decide whether the vehicle was stolen using keys or whether another method was used. Keep any spare keys safe and let us know if any have gone missing.

For a motor theft claim you will need to provide the following:

- The police crime reference number
- All sets of keys for the vehicle

## Home emergency

A home emergency claim covers urgent repairs needed to make your home safe or secure, such as a broken boiler or a blocked drain. You do not need to send any documents for a home emergency. The only step needed is to call the emergency line first, before arranging any work yourself. Calling first allows us to send the right help and to confirm what is covered.

For a home emergency you will need to provide the following:

- No documents, only a call to the emergency line first

## Legal expenses

If you have legal expenses cover and are involved in a dispute that may lead to a claim, we need every letter received from the other side. Please send us each letter as you receive it, including any letters that are repeated or follow up on earlier correspondence, as we use the full sequence to understand the dispute. Do not reply to the other side on the substance of the matter without first speaking to us, as this may affect your cover.

For a legal expenses claim you will need to provide the following:

- Every letter received from the other side

## Proof of ownership

For theft claims, and for any other claim where we need to confirm that you owned an item, proof of ownership can take several forms. We accept a receipt for the item, a bank statement showing the purchase, or a dated photo of the item. A dated photo should show the item clearly and include a date that we can check, such as one taken on a phone or camera that records the date automatically. If you cannot find a receipt or bank statement, a dated photo is often enough. Please send whatever you have, even if it covers only some of the items, as this helps us reach a decision more quickly.
