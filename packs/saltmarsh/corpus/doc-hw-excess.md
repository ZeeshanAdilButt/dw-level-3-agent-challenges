# Home policy excess table

## Purpose and effective date

This clause sets out the excess payable on claims under Saltmarsh Mutual home insurance. It applies to the Home Essentials and Home Plus products from 2026-07-01. The excess is the amount the policyholder pays towards each claim before Saltmarsh Mutual pays the remainder of the settled amount.

## Excess table

The table below lists the excess that applies to each home product and type of claim. Each row names the product and the cover to which the excess applies.

| Product | Cover or claim type | Excess |
|---|---|---|
| Home Essentials | Standard claims (all other covered losses) | £250 |
| Home Essentials | Escape of water claims | £350 |
| Home Essentials | Theft claims | £300 |
| Home Essentials | Subsidence claims | £1,000 |
| Home Plus | Standard claims (all other covered losses) | £150 |
| Home Plus | Escape of water claims | £250 |
| Home Plus | Theft claims | £200 |
| Home Plus | Flood claims | £500 |
| Home Plus | Subsidence claims | £1,000 |
| Accidental damage rider (on either home product) | Accidental damage claims | £100 |

## How the excess applies

The excess shown in the table is applied once to each claim. Where a single incident gives rise to more than one type of loss, the policyholder pays the single highest excess that applies to that incident, unless the policy schedule states otherwise. The excess is deducted from the amount payable by Saltmarsh Mutual at settlement, and any repair or replacement costs below the excess remain the policyholder's responsibility.

The subsidence excess of £1,000 applies to both the Home Essentials and Home Plus products. Subsidence claims are assessed on the basis of the cover wording in the policy schedule, and the excess is confirmed when the claim is accepted.

The accidental damage excess of £100 applies only where the accidental damage rider is held. It is payable in addition to any other excess that applies to the same incident only where the policy schedule expressly says so.

## Voluntary home excess

Policyholders may choose to add a voluntary home excess of £100 or £250 on top of the policy excess shown in the table. Choosing a voluntary excess reduces the premium payable for the policy. The voluntary excess is added to the policy excess for every claim made under the home section of the policy during the period of cover, and it is shown separately on the policy schedule. A voluntary excess does not apply to claims under the motor section of any policy.

Before choosing a voluntary excess, policyholders should check that they can meet the combined amount in the event of a claim. Where a policyholder is unsure which option suits them, they can contact Saltmarsh Mutual before the policy is taken out or renewed for general guidance on how the excess options work.

## Changes to excess amounts

Saltmarsh Mutual may review the excess amounts in this table from time to time. Any change will be shown in an updated version of this clause and will apply only to policies taken out or renewed on or after the date the change takes effect. The excess that applies to an existing policy is the amount shown on its policy schedule at the start of the period of cover.
