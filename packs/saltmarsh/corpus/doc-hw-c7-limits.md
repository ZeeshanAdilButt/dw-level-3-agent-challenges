# Home policy limits table

## Purpose

This clause sets out the cover limits that apply to the Home Essentials and Home Plus policies from 1 July 2026. The table below is the reference for the amounts payable under each product. Where the wording of a policy schedule differs from this table, the schedule applies to the policyholder's own policy.

## Scope

This clause applies to all Home Essentials and Home Plus policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date. Optional riders are not covered by this table except where a rider changes a limit shown here.

## Limits by product

The table shows the limit for each cover type under each product. All amounts are in pounds sterling and are stated per claim unless the table says otherwise.

| Cover type | Home Essentials | Home Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Buildings limit | £400,000 | £750,000 |
| Contents limit | £40,000 | £75,000 |
| Single item limit | £1,500 | £2,500 |
| Total limit for valuables | £5,000 | £10,000 |
| Limit for cash | £250 | £500 |
| Belongings away from the home | No cover (not covered) | £2,000 |
| Storm damage to gates, fences and hedges | Not stated in this clause | £1,000 |
| Escape of water limit (per claim) | £25,000 | £25,000 |
| Alternative accommodation | £8,000 | £20,000 |
| Personal liability cover | £2 million | £5 million |

## Explanation of terms

**Buildings limit** is the most the insurer will pay to repair or rebuild the structure of the home, including fixed fittings, for any one claim.

**Contents limit** is the most the insurer will pay for household belongings in the home, for any one claim.

**Single item limit** is the most the insurer will pay for any one item within the contents, whatever its value.

**Total limit for valuables** is the most the insurer will pay in total for valuable items, such as jewellery, watches and fine art, for any one claim.

**Limit for cash** is the most the insurer will pay for cash, banknotes and similar money, for any one claim.

**Belongings away from the home** refers to personal items taken outside the home, such as a laptop taken on a train. Home Essentials does not cover this. Home Plus covers it up to £2,000.

**Storm damage to gates, fences and hedges** is covered under Home Plus up to £1,000. This limit applies to the storm damage described and does not apply to other causes of damage to these items.

**Escape of water limit** is the most the insurer will pay for damage caused by water escaping from pipes, tanks or appliances, for any one claim. The limit is £25,000 per claim under both products.

**Alternative accommodation** is the cost of somewhere to live while the home cannot be lived in following an insured event. Home Essentials pays up to £8,000 and Home Plus pays up to £20,000.

**Personal liability cover** protects the policyholder if they become legally responsible for injury to another person or damage to their property. Home Essentials includes £2 million of cover and Home Plus includes £5 million.

## How limits apply

1. A limit is the maximum payable and is not an estimate of the likely cost of a claim.
2. Where more than one limit could apply to a claim, the insurer will pay under the limit that gives the relevant cover and will not pay twice for the same loss.
3. Limits apply per claim unless this clause states otherwise.
4. Where a policyholder has an optional rider, the rider may provide additional cover. Riders are described in separate documents.

## Underinsurance and sums insured

Policyholders should check that the buildings and contents limits reflect the cost of rebuilding the home and replacing its contents. If the sums insured are lower than the true cost, the amount paid on a claim may be reduced. Saltmarsh Mutual can help a policyholder review their cover at any time.

## Questions and contact

Policyholders with questions about these limits can contact Saltmarsh Mutual through the usual customer channels. Information about a particular policy is set out in that policy's schedule and key facts document.
