# Changing your address

This guide explains how to tell Saltmarsh Mutual about a change of address and when you need to do it. Because Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, keeping your details accurate protects you and the other members of the mutual.

## Why you need to tell us

Your home and motor policies are priced and arranged around where you live and where your vehicle is kept. If your address changes and we are not told, your cover may not respond as you expect when you make a claim. A change of address must be reported within 14 days of the move so that your policy reflects your current circumstances.

## How to report a change

You can tell us about a change of address in any of these ways:

- Through your online account, using the details section of your policy.
- By calling our member services team, who will update your record while you are on the line.
- By writing to us with your policy number, your old address, your new address and the date you moved.

Please include the date of the move so that we can record exactly when the change took effect.

## Fees

Changing your address carries no fee, so there is no reason to delay. Reporting the change is free whether you do it online, by phone or in writing.

## After you report the change

We will confirm the update in writing and tell you if anything about your cover, premium or optional riders needs to be reviewed. If your new home or vehicle location means your policy terms have changed, we will explain what is different before it takes effect.
