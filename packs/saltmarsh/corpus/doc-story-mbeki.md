# Claim story: Mbeki family

## When the kitchen flooded

The Mbeki family had a difficult week when a burst pipe under the kitchen sink soaked the floor, the cabinets and part of the adjoining hallway. They held a Home Plus policy with the courtesy car rider, and their first call to Saltmarsh Mutual came the same morning the water was discovered.

## How the claim moved

Our claims team logged the notice of loss the same day, arranged for a drying contractor to attend within a short window, and assigned one handler to keep the family updated from start to finish. Photographs and a short description were enough to begin the assessment, so the family did not have to repeat their account to several people.

## A car when they needed one

Because the courtesy car rider was part of their cover, the family were offered a replacement vehicle while their own car was needed for the repair of the garage floor. The courtesy car was provided for 9 days, which allowed the school run and weekly shopping to carry on without interruption.

## Settlement

The claim was settled once the contractor's final report and invoices were received. The handler explained each stage of the settlement in plain language, and the family received payment for the agreed repairs without needing to chase any step.

## What we took from it

Stories like this one shape how we work. Speed matters, but so does keeping a person informed and treating their home as the important place it is. We are grateful to the Mbeki family for sharing their experience with us.
