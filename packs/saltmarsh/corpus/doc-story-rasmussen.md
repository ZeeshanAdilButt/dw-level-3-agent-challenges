# Claim story: Rasmussen household

## How a cracked wall became a settled claim

When the Rasmussen household noticed a diagonal crack running above a rear bedroom window, they did the right thing and reported it to Saltmarsh Mutual straight away. Their policy included Home Plus, and the claim was logged the same day it was first reported to us, so that a named claims handler could begin work without delay.

The first task was to establish whether the movement in the house was a one-off or a sign of something more serious. Because the damage pointed to possible ground movement, a subsidence survey was arranged. The survey started 12 days after the claim was reported, which gave our engineers time to book access, check the property history and bring in the right specialists for the site.

Throughout this period, the household received regular updates from their handler, so they always knew what was happening next and when to expect it. Where the survey findings required repairs, the handler agreed the scope of work with the household, appointed approved contractors and checked progress at each stage before the final payment was released.

## What we learned

This claim shows the value of reporting early and keeping communication open. Clear updates reduced worry for the household, and a structured approach to surveying meant the repairs were based on evidence rather than guesswork.

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, and stories like this one remind us why we work to settle claims fairly, promptly and with care.
