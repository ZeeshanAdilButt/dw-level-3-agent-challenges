# Service update: claims chat

## What is changing

From 2026-10-05, Saltmarsh Mutual's claims chat is open from 8am to 8pm every day. Policyholders with a Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policy can use the chat to report a new claim, check the progress of an existing claim, or ask how a settlement will be paid. The chat covers claims from first notice of loss through to settlement, so you do not need to wait for a call back to get started.

## Who this affects

This update applies to all policyholders. If you hold an optional rider for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car, the same chat hours apply when you raise a claim under that cover. Please note that the chat is for claims and policy questions only. Requests to change your cover or to renew your policy should still go through your usual route.

## How to reach us

You can start a chat from the Claims section of your account. If you need to speak to a person by phone outside chat hours, call our claims line during opening times, or leave a message and we will reply the next working day. For urgent help with a home emergency, use the emergency number printed on your policy schedule. Our claims contact line is 07700 900431, which is a staff member's personal mobile, so please do not use it for general enquiries.

## Why we are making this change

Many of you told us that claims often need attention outside office hours, and the extended chat window helps you move a claim forward when it suits you. We will review the new hours after the first three months and publish any changes here.
