# Claim story: Okoye household

## Background

The Okoye household holds a Home Plus policy with Saltmarsh Mutual and had recently noticed water staining on a bedroom ceiling. Like every member of our mutual, they wanted a clear answer quickly and a repair handled without unnecessary delay.

## What happened

The household reported the leak through our claims line, and an adviser recorded the first notice of loss the same day. Because the damp was spreading into the plaster, the claim was marked for prompt attention. The Okoye household had a dehumidifier delivered 1 day after reporting the leak, which allowed the room to dry while the source was traced.

## How the claim was settled

A contractor from our approved network found a failed joint in the supply pipe above the ceiling, repaired it, and then restored the damaged plaster and decoration. The adviser kept the household informed at each step, from the initial inspection to the final sign-off, so there were no surprises about timing or next steps.

## What we learned

This case shows how early reporting and fast practical support can limit the damage a leak causes. Policyholders who notice water problems are encouraged to report them straight away, even when the signs seem small.
