# Claim story: Zielinski family

## Background

The Zielinski family has been a Saltmarsh Mutual policyholder for several years. They hold a Home Plus policy with the accidental damage rider added, which gives them cover for everyday accidents in the home as well as the standard protection for their property and belongings.

## What happened

One evening a television in the living room was damaged when a heavy box slipped from a shelf and struck the screen. The family reported the loss to Saltmarsh Mutual the following morning, using the online claims form and a short phone call to confirm the details.

## How the claim was handled

A claims handler contacted the family within the same working day to explain the next steps and to ask for photographs of the damaged set and the proof of purchase. The family sent these through the app, and the handler arranged for the television to be assessed against the policy terms. Because the accidental damage rider applied, the claim moved forward without delay.

## The result

The family's accidental damage claim for the television was paid in 8 working days from the date they first reported it. The family received a clear settlement letter explaining the amount paid and how it was calculated, and they had a single named handler to contact throughout.

## Why it matters

Our members want claims to be simple and quick. This case shows how prompt reporting, clear communication and a focused handler can take a household through a stressful moment with minimal fuss.
