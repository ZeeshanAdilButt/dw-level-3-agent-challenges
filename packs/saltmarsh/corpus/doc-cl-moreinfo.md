# When we need more information

## Why a claim may be paused

Sometimes we cannot settle a claim straight away because we need something from you. When this happens, the claim status shows as more information needed. This means the claim is paused until you send us the item we have asked for. We will tell you exactly what we need, so you know what to send and why.

## What we will ask you for

The items we request are usually practical ones, such as photographs of the damage, repair estimates, receipts, a police reference number, or details of other parties involved. We only ask for what is needed to assess your claim fairly.

## How long you have to respond

Requested documents must be sent within 28 days of our request, or the claim may be closed. We understand that gathering paperwork can take time, so if you need longer, please contact us before the 28 days end and we will consider your situation.

## What happens if we do not hear from you

A claim is closed if the information is not sent within 28 days of the request. If your claim is closed, you can still open a new claim later by contacting us, and we will help you through the process again.

## How to send us your information

You can send documents through your online account, by email, or by post. Please quote your claim reference number on everything you send so we can match it to your claim quickly and avoid delays.
