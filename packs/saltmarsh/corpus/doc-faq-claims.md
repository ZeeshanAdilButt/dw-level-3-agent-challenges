# FAQ: claims

## About this document

This FAQ answers common questions about making and tracking a claim with Saltmarsh Mutual. It covers home and motor claims, the statuses you may see on your claim, and the time limits that apply. It is written for policyholders and anyone helping them. It takes effect on 2026-07-01.

## Reporting a claim

### How do I make a claim?

You can tell us about a loss through the Saltmarsh Mutual app, on our website, or by phone. We will ask for the policy number, what happened, when it happened, and any evidence you have, such as photos, receipts, or a police reference for a theft or road incident. The first time you tell us about the loss is called first notice of loss.

### How long do I have to report a home claim?

A home claim must be reported within 30 days of discovering the loss. If you find damage and report it promptly, we can start the process straight away. If you are unsure whether something is covered, report it anyway and we will advise you.

### How long do I have to report a motor accident?

A motor accident must be reported within 14 days. For accidents involving injury, theft, or a third party, please also tell the police as soon as you reasonably can, and keep the reference number they give you.

### Can I claim for an emergency at home?

If you hold the home emergency rider, you can call our emergency line to arrange urgent help such as making a property safe after a burst pipe or a broken window. Keep the work receipts, as we will ask for them when the claim is assessed.

## Claim progress

### What happens after I report a claim?

We acknowledge every new claim within 2 working days. The acknowledgement confirms your claim reference and tells you which claim handler is looking after it. After that, we may ask for further documents, arrange an inspection, or contact a repairer or third party.

### How quickly will you decide my claim?

Saltmarsh aims to decide a claim within 10 working days of receiving all the documents we need. The clock starts once we hold everything required, not on the day you first reported the loss. If we need more information, we will tell you what is missing so that the decision is not delayed.

### What do the claim statuses mean?

There are six claim statuses. Your claim moves through them as it progresses:

- Reported: we have received your first notice of loss.
- Acknowledged: we have confirmed receipt and given you a reference.
- Awaiting information: we need documents or details from you or a third party.
- Under assessment: we are reviewing the cover and the facts of the loss.
- Decided: we have reached a decision and are arranging payment or repair.
- Settled: the payment or repair is complete and the claim is closed.

You can see the current status in the app or ask your claim handler at any time.

### Why is my claim waiting for information?

The status Awaiting information means we cannot move forward until we receive something from you or a third party, such as an estimate, a receipt, or a police reference. We will tell you exactly what we need. The decision timescale starts once everything is received.

## Disagreeing with a decision

### What if I am unhappy with the outcome?

Please tell your claim handler why you disagree and send any extra evidence you have. We will review the claim and reply with a final response. If you are still unhappy with that final response, you can take your complaint to the Coastal Insurance Ombudsman. You must do this within 6 months of receiving the final response. The Coastal Insurance Ombudsman is independent of Saltmarsh Mutual and can review your case without charge.

### Do I lose my right to complain if I wait too long?

Yes. The Coastal Insurance Ombudsman will only consider complaints raised within 6 months of the final response, so please act promptly if you wish to escalate.

## Still have questions?

If your question is not answered here, contact our claims team through the app, on our website, or by phone, and quote your claim reference if you have one. Our team will be glad to help you understand where your claim stands and what happens next.
