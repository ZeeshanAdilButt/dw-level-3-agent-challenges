# Clause 1: Third party liability

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out the third party liability cover provided under Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive. It describes what the cover includes, what it excludes, and how it operates alongside the optional riders offered with our motor products. It forms part of the policy wording and should be read together with the policy schedule and the general conditions.

## Scope of cover

Both Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive include third party liability cover. This cover responds when you become legally liable to another person for injury or for damage to their property arising from the use of your insured vehicle. The cover applies to the legal liability to other people and their property only. It does not extend to any loss you suffer yourself.

## Injury to other people

Motor Third Party covers claims for injury to other people without a fixed limit. Where a third party is injured as a result of the use of your insured vehicle, and you are held legally liable, we will respond to the resulting claim without a fixed monetary ceiling on the amount payable for that injury. Claims of this kind are handled in accordance with the claims process described in the general conditions, and settlement is determined by the facts of each claim and the amounts properly due.

## Property damage to other people

Third party property damage is covered up to £20 million for each incident. This limit applies to damage to the property of other people, such as another vehicle, a building, fencing, or other possessions, where you are legally liable for that damage. Where a single incident gives rise to claims exceeding this figure, the amount payable for third party property damage will not exceed the stated limit, and any excess above it remains the responsibility of the person or persons liable.

## What is not covered

Motor Third Party does not pay for damage to your own vehicle. Any loss to the insured vehicle arising from a collision, impact, or other event is not covered under this product, regardless of who is at fault. Motor Third Party does not cover fire or theft of your own vehicle. Where your vehicle is damaged by fire or stolen, this product offers no cover for that loss. Customers who require protection for their own vehicle should review the Motor Comprehensive product, which includes this protection, and should consider the optional riders described in the product guide.

## Relationship with optional riders

Optional riders, including accidental damage, legal expenses and courtesy car, are separate from the third party liability cover described in this clause. A rider does not alter the scope of third party liability cover, and third party liability cover does not depend on the purchase of any rider. Where a rider is held on a policy, its terms apply in addition to, and not in place of, the provisions set out here.

## Conditions and interpretation

Third party liability cover is subject to the general conditions of the policy, including the requirement to notify us promptly of any incident, to cooperate with our handling of the claim, and not to admit liability without our agreement. Failure to meet these conditions may affect the cover available. Headings in this clause are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation. If there is any conflict between this clause and the policy schedule, the schedule shows the cover that applies to your individual policy.
