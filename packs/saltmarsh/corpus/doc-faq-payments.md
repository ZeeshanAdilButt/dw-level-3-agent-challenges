# FAQ: payments

## About this page

This FAQ answers common questions about paying for your Saltmarsh Mutual policy, including paying monthly and what happens if a payment is missed. It applies from 1 July 2026.

## Paying for your policy

## Can I pay for my policy all at once?

Yes. You can pay the full annual premium in one go, which avoids any monthly interest charges. Your renewal notice shows the options available for your policy.

## Can I pay monthly instead?

Yes. Paying monthly carries interest of 14.9% APR on the amount you spread over the year. The interest is shown in your quotation and policy documents before you agree to pay monthly, so you can compare the total cost with paying in one go.

## How do I set up a direct debit?

Direct debit is the usual way to pay monthly. You give us your bank details when you take out or renew your policy, and we collect each payment on the agreed date.

## Will my direct debit amount change?

It can, for example if your premium is reviewed at renewal or if a rider is added or removed. Saltmarsh gives 10 working days notice before changing a direct debit amount, so you have time to check the new figure and contact us if you have questions.

## Missed and failed payments

## What happens if a monthly payment is late?

If a monthly payment is 7 days late, we send you a reminder. The reminder explains the amount outstanding and how to bring your account up to date.

## What happens if a direct debit payment fails?

A failed direct debit payment carries a £12 fee. This fee is added to your account and is shown on your next statement. Your bank may also charge you separately, so please check with them if you are unsure.

## What should I do if I think a payment has been taken in error?

Contact us as soon as you notice. We will check the payment and, where we have made a mistake, correct it and refund any amount taken in error.

## What if I cannot afford my payments?

Please contact us before a payment is missed where possible. We can talk through your options, which may include changing the payment date or reviewing how your premium is paid. Getting in touch early gives us more ways to help.

## Other questions

## Where can I find my payment history?

Your payment history is available in your account and is also shown on your annual statement. If you need a copy of an earlier statement, ask us and we will provide it.

## How do I change my bank details?

You can update your bank details through your account or by contacting us. Please allow the time needed for the change to take effect before your next collection date.

## Who do I contact about payments?

Call or write to Saltmarsh Mutual's customer team using the contact details on your policy documents. Please have your policy number ready so we can locate your account quickly.
