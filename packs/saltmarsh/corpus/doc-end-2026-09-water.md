# Endorsement E-2026-09: escape of water limit

## Purpose

This endorsement sets out a change to the escape of water limit that applies under Saltmarsh Mutual home insurance. It is issued under our endorsement authority and forms part of the policy wording for the policies it covers.

## Change to the limit

Endorsement E-2026-09 raises the escape of water limit to £30,000 for eligible home policies. The escape of water limit is the maximum amount we will pay for loss caused by water escaping from pipes, tanks, appliances or drains in the insured property, subject to the other terms of the policy.

## Which policies are covered

Endorsement E-2026-09 applies only to home policies that start on or after 1 September 2026. This means new policies taken out on or after that date receive the higher limit. Policies that started before 1 September 2026 are not affected by this endorsement, and their escape of water limit remains as stated in the policy wording issued to the policyholder.

## What this means for customers

Customers with a new home policy starting on or after 1 September 2026 should check their policy schedule, which shows the escape of water limit that applies to their cover. Existing policyholders who want to discuss their cover can contact us, and we will explain the options available at renewal or on request.

## Other terms

All other terms, conditions and exclusions of the home policy continue to apply. If anything in this endorsement appears to conflict with the policy wording, please contact us before relying on it.
