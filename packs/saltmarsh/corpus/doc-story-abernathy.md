# Claim story: Abernathy family

## When the car needed help

The Abernathy family relies on their car every day, so when a collision left it undriveable, they wanted to know one thing above all: how quickly could they get back on the road? They had a Motor Comprehensive policy with the courtesy car rider, which meant the claim started with a single phone call to report the loss.

## How the claim moved

Saltmarsh Mutual took the first notice of loss, confirmed the policy details, and arranged for the vehicle to be collected. The family's car was repaired at an approved repairer in 6 days, and the claim was settled once the repair was signed off. Throughout, the family had a named contact they could call with questions, so they never had to chase updates themselves.

## What the family said

Looking back, the family valued the simple steps: one call to start, clear updates along the way, and a repaired car returned on schedule. The courtesy car meant the school run and weekly shopping carried on without interruption.

## Why it matters to our members

Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, so every claim is handled with the aim of getting members back to normal life as smoothly as possible. The Abernathy story reflects that commitment in practice.
