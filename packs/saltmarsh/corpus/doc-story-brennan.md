# Claim story: Brennan family

## Background

When a burst pipe flooded the ground floor of the Brennan family home, the household needed more than repairs. They needed somewhere safe to live while the building recovered. This case study follows how Saltmarsh Mutual handled their claim from first notice of loss through to settlement, and what the family experienced along the way.

## First notice of loss

The Brennans reported the damage by phone on the same day the water was discovered. A claims handler took their details, confirmed their home policy, and arranged for a loss adjuster to visit within a short window. The family was asked only for the information needed to start the claim, and they were told clearly what would happen next.

## Getting the family settled

The adjuster's first priority was the household's wellbeing. Because the ground floor was unusable and the drying process would take time, the Brennan family was put in alternative accommodation for 5 weeks while their home was dried out. The family's belongings were boxed, labelled and moved under supervision, so nothing was lost or damaged in transit.

## Repairs and settlement

Specialist drying equipment was installed, and once the structure was confirmed dry, the repair work was scheduled in stages. The handler kept the family updated at each step, so they always knew what was happening and when. Once the repairs were complete and the final inspection passed, the claim was settled and the family moved back home.

## What we learned

The Brennan claim shows what members can expect from Saltmarsh Mutual: prompt contact, clear communication, and practical support while a home is being restored. Every claim is different, but the aim is the same in each case: to get members back to normal life as quickly and calmly as possible.
