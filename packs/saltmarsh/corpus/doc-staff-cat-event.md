# Catastrophe event playbook

Saltmarsh Mutual Internal Playbook. Effective from 2026-06-01. Staff only.

## Purpose

This playbook sets out how a storm or flood event is declared and run across the claims operation. It is a guide for claims handlers, team leads and duty managers. Where it conflicts with a specific product wording or an operational instruction issued by the claims director, the claims director's instruction applies.

## Declaring a catastrophe event

A catastrophe event is declared at 500 claims in 48 hours, counting home and motor claims that share a single reported cause such as a named storm, flood or freeze. The duty manager monitors the claims intake queue against this threshold and records the trigger time in the event log. A team lead may propose a declaration earlier where regional intelligence points to a large loss ahead of the count, but the formal declaration is made only by the duty manager.

## Roles during an event

The duty manager owns the event from declaration to close. Team leads allocate handlers to affected postcodes, and the claims director signs off any change to standard authority limits. Handlers keep their existing files and do not reassign claims without a team lead's approval.

## Emergency accommodation

Harbourside Lodgings is the partner for emergency accommodation in a catastrophe event. Handlers arrange placements through the partner's event desk and record each booking against the policyholder's home emergency rider where it applies. Handlers must not book accommodation with other suppliers unless the partner desk confirms it cannot meet the need.

## Communications and closure

Policyholders receive an acknowledgement for each claim within the standard service window, with updates at each stage. The duty manager closes the event once intake has returned below the threshold and open files are assigned. The closure note goes to the claims director and is filed with the event log.
