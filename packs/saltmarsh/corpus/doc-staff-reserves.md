# Reserve guidelines

**Saltmarsh Mutual | Internal | Guide | Effective from 2026-06-01**

## Purpose

This guide sets out the starting reserves that claims handlers should place when a claim is first registered at Saltmarsh Mutual. Starting reserves give an early estimate of likely settlement cost and are adjusted as the claim develops. They apply to the home and motor claims we handle from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## Starting reserves

The table below lists the starting reserve for each claim type covered by this guide.

| Claim type | Starting reserve | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Escape of water | £6,500 | Applies at first notice of loss, before inspection |
| Home theft | £2,200 | Applies at first notice of loss, before inspection |

## Review cycle

Every reserve is reviewed every 30 days from the date it is set, or sooner if new information about the claim becomes available, such as an engineer's report, a repair estimate or a change in liability. Handlers should record the reason for any change in the claim notes.

## Responsibilities

Claims handlers set the starting reserve when the claim is registered and carry out each scheduled review. Team leaders check reserves on a sample basis and approve any change that exceeds their delegated authority.
