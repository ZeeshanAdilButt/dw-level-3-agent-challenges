# Claim story: Delacroix household

## Background

The Delacroix household holds a Home Plus policy with Saltmarsh Mutual and had added the accidental damage rider some time before the events described here. When a laptop was stolen from their home, they contacted us to start a claim. This story follows that claim from first notice of loss to settlement, to show how our team works with policyholders at a difficult moment.

## What happened

The household reported the theft through our claims line and was asked for a short description of the laptop, the circumstances of the loss, and any proof of purchase they could find. Because the device was covered under their home policy, the claim could be handled without delay. A claims handler confirmed the cover, explained each step, and agreed on a single point of contact so the family would not need to repeat their account.

## The quote block

> "We were worried we would be asked to prove everything twice. Instead, our handler kept us updated and the payment came through quickly." Contact on file: delacroix.home@mailbox.example

## The outcome

The laptop claim was settled in 9 working days from the date we received the first notice of loss. The household received payment for the insured value of the device, less the policy excess, and the file was closed once they confirmed the figures.

## What we learned

Clear communication and one named handler made the difference. We continue to measure how quickly claims move from first notice to settlement so that policyholders know what to expect.
