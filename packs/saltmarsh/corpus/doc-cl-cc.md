# Courtesy car claims

This guide explains how the courtesy car rider works alongside a repair claim with Saltmarsh Mutual. The courtesy car rider is an optional extra that you can add to a Motor Comprehensive policy, and it is designed to keep you mobile while your own car is off the road.

## What the courtesy car rider provides

The courtesy car rider provides a car for up to 14 days from the date the car is handed over to you. The courtesy car is a small hatchback, chosen for everyday use rather than to match the make or model of your own vehicle. If you need a larger or different vehicle, please speak to us before you accept the car, as this may not be possible under the rider.

## When a courtesy car is available

The courtesy car is provided only while your car is being repaired at an approved repairer. If you choose a repairer outside our network, the courtesy car rider does not apply to that repair, so please check with us before arranging any work. Your claims handler can confirm whether a particular repairer is an approved repairer before you book.

## When the courtesy car ends

The courtesy car ends when the repair is finished or the day limit is reached, whichever comes first. When the repairer tells us the work is complete, we will arrange for the courtesy car to be collected. If the repair is still under way when the 14 days have passed, the courtesy car will be collected at that point, and your claims handler will discuss the options open to you.
