# App update: claim photo upload

## What has changed

Saltmarsh Mutual policyholders can now attach photos directly to a claim from the Saltmarsh app. The claim form accepts up to 20 photos in a single submission, so you can send everything relevant to your claim at the time you first report it.

## Why this matters

Clear photos help us assess damage faster and reduce the number of follow-up requests we need to send. This applies to home and motor claims alike, whether you are reporting storm damage to your home or a dent after a minor collision. Including photos at first notice of loss gives your claim the best chance of moving smoothly from report through to settlement.

## Tips for useful photos

Take photos in good light and include both close-up and wider shots so the damage and its surroundings are clear. For motor claims, include the registration plate where it is visible. For home claims, photograph any affected rooms, fixtures and personal items that were damaged. If you have a receipt or an earlier photo of an item, add that too.

## Need help?

If an upload fails or you have a question about which photos to send, contact our claims team through the app or on our public helpline. We will guide you through the next steps.
