# Supplier rate card

## Purpose and scope

This rate card sets out the agreed hourly labour rates and call-out charges for suppliers used by Saltmarsh Mutual when handling home and motor claims. It applies to all claims handled from first notice of loss through to settlement. Internal reference: panel code PNL-318-Q.

## Authority

This document is a rule for claims and supplier management staff. Rates listed here apply from 1 June 2026 and must be used when instructing work and approving supplier invoices. Staff must not agree rates above those shown without written approval from the claims supplier manager.

## Agreed rates

| Supplier type | Item | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Panel plumber | Hourly labour rate | £68 per hour |
| Panel plumber | Call-out charge | £95 per visit |
| Network repairer | Labour rate | £52 per hour |

## How to apply these rates

Confirm the supplier type before instructing work, using the table above. Record the agreed rate on the claim file at the point of instruction. Invoices that exceed the agreed rate or add charges not covered by this card must be referred to the supplier management team before payment is authorised.

## Queries

Direct questions about this rate card to the claims supplier management team. Do not share this document outside Saltmarsh Mutual.
