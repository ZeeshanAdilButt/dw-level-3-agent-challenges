# About Saltmarsh Mutual

## Who we are

Saltmarsh Mutual was founded in 1952 as a local association of households who wanted fair, straightforward cover for their homes and vehicles. Today we remain a mutual insurer, which means we are owned by our policyholders rather than by outside shareholders. Our purpose is to protect the people who hold our policies, and any surplus we generate is returned to them through better cover, fairer claims handling and sensible pricing.

Our head office is in Marshford, where our underwriting, customer service and claims teams work together. We serve home and motor customers across the country.

## What we offer

We provide four core policies:

- Home Essentials, covering the building and contents of a home against the most common risks.
- Home Plus, a broader home policy with higher limits and additional protections.
- Motor Third Party, which covers legal liability to other people and property when you drive.
- Motor Comprehensive, which also covers damage to your own vehicle.

Each policy can be extended with optional riders. These include accidental damage, legal expenses cover, home emergency assistance and a courtesy car while your vehicle is being repaired. Riders are optional, and you can add or remove them at renewal. Full details of what each policy and rider includes are set out in the policy wording you receive when you take out cover.

## How we handle claims

When something goes wrong, we aim to make the process as simple as possible. Claims begin when you give us first notice of loss, either by phone, online or through our app. A claims handler will confirm what happened, explain the next steps and tell you what information we need.

Depending on the type of claim, we may arrange an assessor, appoint approved repairers or ask for supporting documents such as photographs, receipts or police references. We keep you updated throughout and explain our decisions in plain language. Our goal is to settle claims fairly and promptly, from first notice of loss through to final settlement.

## Our commitments

As a mutual, we answer to our policyholders. We commit to:

- Writing clear policy documents that say what is and is not covered.
- Treating every customer fairly, including when a claim is difficult.
- Reinvesting value back into our members through fair pricing and improved cover.
- Being open about how we run the business and how we make decisions.

If you have questions about Saltmarsh Mutual, our policies or our claims process, please contact our customer service team. We are always glad to explain how we work and how we can help you.
