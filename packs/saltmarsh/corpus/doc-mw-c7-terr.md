# Clause 7: where you are covered

## Territorial scope

This policy applies in the United Kingdom. Cover for your home and any insured property situated in the United Kingdom is provided under Home Essentials and Home Plus, and cover for your insured vehicle while it is kept and used in the United Kingdom is provided under Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive.

## Travel within the European Union

Motor Comprehensive gives full cover in the EU for up to 90 days in a policy year, counted from the date on which the insured vehicle first leaves the United Kingdom. Once that period is exceeded, cover in the EU ends unless we have agreed an extension in writing before the period expires.

Motor Third Party gives EU cover for up to 30 days in a policy year, and this cover is limited to third party liability as required by the law of the country being visited. Damage to your own vehicle is not covered by Motor Third Party while the vehicle is in the EU.

## Conditions

Before travelling, you must confirm that your certificate of motor insurance and any green card or equivalent document required by the destination country are current. Cover outside the United Kingdom and the EU is not provided under this policy unless we have confirmed otherwise in writing.

## Changes to this clause

We may amend this clause from time to time. Any change will be notified to you in advance, and the wording in force at the start of each journey will apply to that journey.
