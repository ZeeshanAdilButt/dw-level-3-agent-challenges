# Claim story: Kowalczyk household

## When the heating went out

On a cold evening, the Kowalczyk household noticed that their boiler had stopped working and the house was getting colder by the hour. They contacted Saltmarsh Mutual through their home emergency rider, which is designed for exactly this kind of sudden breakdown at home.

## How we responded

The claim was logged as soon as the first call came in, and a home emergency engineer was dispatched straight away. The boiler was repaired by a home emergency engineer within 3 hours of the call, so the family was able to keep warm and settle in for the night without a long wait in the cold.

## What the family told us

The household described the experience as calm and straightforward. They were kept informed at each stage, knew what to expect, and did not have to chase anyone for updates. For a member-owned insurer, this is the outcome we work toward: a practical repair, delivered promptly, with a clear explanation along the way.

## What this shows

Stories like this one remind us that insurance matters most on the worst day of the year. Our home emergency cover exists so that policyholders can get help quickly when something essential fails, and we are proud to have been there for the Kowalczyk household.
