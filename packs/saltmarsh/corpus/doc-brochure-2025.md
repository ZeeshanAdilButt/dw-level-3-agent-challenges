# Saltmarsh Mutual brochure 2025

*Effective from 1 March 2025. Public document.*

## Welcome to Saltmarsh Mutual

Saltmarsh Mutual is a home and motor insurer owned by the people it insures. Every policyholder is a member, which means our decisions are shaped by the customers we serve rather than by outside shareholders. We believe insurance should be clear, fair and dependable, and this brochure sets out what we offer and how we look after you when something goes wrong.

## Our home insurance

We offer two home policies designed to suit different needs and budgets.

**Home Essentials** (product code HOM-ESS-1) is our core home policy. It covers the building and contents of your home against the most common risks, giving you straightforward protection for everyday life.

**Home Plus** builds on Home Essentials with broader cover and additional features for households that want more complete peace of mind.

Every Saltmarsh home policy includes flood cover, so you do not need to add it separately. Whichever policy you choose, flood protection is part of the package.

The standard home excess is £100. This is the amount you contribute towards a claim, and it is set at a level we consider reasonable for most households.

## Our motor insurance

We offer two motor policies for drivers.

**Motor Third Party** provides cover for injury or damage you cause to other people and their property. It is a sensible choice for owners of older vehicles.

**Motor Comprehensive** provides wider protection, including damage to your own vehicle. A courtesy car is included free with Motor Comprehensive, so you can keep getting around while your car is being repaired.

## Optional extras

You can tailor your policy with optional riders:

- **Accidental damage**, for unexpected damage to your home or vehicle
- **Legal expenses**, to help with the costs of pursuing or defending certain claims
- **Home emergency**, for urgent help with problems such as burst pipes or broken locks
- **Courtesy car**, where available as an add-on to a Motor Third Party policy

Riders are priced separately and can be added or removed at renewal.

## Your no-claims discount

Safe driving and careful home management are rewarded. Our no-claims discount can reach 75%, so the longer you go without making a claim, the more you can save on your premium.

## Your cooling-off period

We want you to feel confident about your cover from the start. Every new policy comes with a cooling-off period of 28 days. If you change your mind within that time, you can cancel and receive a refund, subject to the terms of your policy.

## How we handle your claim

We aim to make claims as simple as possible, guiding you from the first notice of loss through to final settlement. You can report a claim online, by phone or through our app. Once we have your details, a dedicated handler will keep you informed at every stage, arrange any repairs or assessments needed, and work to settle your claim promptly and fairly.

Because we belong to our policyholders, we measure success by how well we look after them. Flood cover on every home policy, a courtesy car with Motor Comprehensive, and a no-claims discount that rewards careful customers are all part of that commitment.
