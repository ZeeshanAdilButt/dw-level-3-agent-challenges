# Finding your policy documents

This guide explains where to find the documents that come with your Saltmarsh Mutual policy and how to get copies when you need them. It applies to all of our home and motor products, including Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to any optional riders you have added.

## Where your documents are kept

Your policy documents are stored in your online account. You can sign in at any time to view or download your schedule, policy wording, summary of cover and any endorsements that have been added since you took out your policy. Policy documents stay in the online account for 7 years after the policy ends, so you can look back at earlier terms if you need to check what was covered at a particular time.

Documents for optional riders, such as accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car cover, appear alongside your main policy. If a rider was added or removed during the year, the change will be shown as a separate endorsement with its own date.

## Downloading or requesting a copy

To download a document, open your account, select your policy and choose the document you need. Most files are available as PDFs that you can save or print. If you cannot find a document, or if you need a paper copy, contact our member services team through the secure messaging feature in your account or by phone. Please have your policy number ready so we can locate the right records quickly.

## Keeping your details up to date

Check that your contact details and email address are current, because we send renewal notices and important policy updates to the address on file. If your details change, update them in your account so that you continue to receive documents without delay.
