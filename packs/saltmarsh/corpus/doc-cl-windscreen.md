# Windscreen claims

This guide explains how Saltmarsh Mutual policyholders book a windscreen repair or replacement under a Motor Comprehensive policy or a Motor Third Party policy with the optional riders in place. It applies from 1 July 2026.

## Who handles the booking

Windscreen repairs are booked through Saltmarsh's glass partner, not through a general repairer of your choice. The glass partner inspects the damage, confirms whether a repair or a full replacement is the right fix, and arranges an appointment at a location that suits you. You do not need to contact a separate repairer before calling us.

## How to book

Start by reporting the damage to Saltmarsh through the usual first notice of loss channel. Give the registration of the vehicle, the date and place the damage happened, and a short description of how it occurred. Once the claim is logged, you will be put in touch with the glass partner, who will confirm the booking details by message or phone.

Please keep the vehicle in a safe, dry place until the appointment. If the damage is spreading across your line of sight, avoid driving the vehicle until the glass partner has inspected it.

## What to expect

The glass partner will tell you whether the windscreen can be repaired or needs replacing. Where a replacement is needed, they will explain what is covered under your policy and whether any excess applies. If you hold a courtesy car rider, ask us at the time of booking whether it can be arranged for the day of the repair.

If you have questions about the booking, contact Saltmarsh's claims team, who will keep you informed until the work is complete.
