# Claim fraud

## Purpose

This guide explains what Saltmarsh Mutual considers claim fraud, how we handle suspected cases, and what happens to a policy when a claim is found to be dishonest. It applies to all claims made under Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, including claims under any optional riders.

## What counts as claim fraud

Claim fraud occurs when a policyholder or anyone acting for them knowingly gives false information to support a claim. Examples include inventing a loss, exaggerating the value of items lost or damaged, providing fake receipts or repair invoices, or hiding facts that would affect the outcome of the claim.

Honest mistakes, such as an incorrect date given in good faith, are not fraud. We will ask for clarification in those cases.

## How we respond

When our claims team identifies a suspected fraud, we review the evidence carefully and may ask for further information or an interview. We do not decide a case on suspicion alone.

Where we are satisfied that fraud has taken place, Saltmarsh reports suspected claim fraud to the police and may cancel the policy. We may also decline the claim in full and recover any amounts already paid.

## Protecting our members

Every claim is paid from a shared pool contributed by policyholders. Fraudulent claims increase costs for everyone, so we take these cases seriously and treat all members fairly and consistently.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether something you plan to claim is accurate, speak to our claims team before you submit. Honest, prompt disclosure is always the better course.
