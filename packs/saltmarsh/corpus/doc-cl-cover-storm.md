# Storm damage claims: what is covered

This guide explains how Saltmarsh Mutual handles storm damage claims under our home insurance plans. It sets out what is covered, what is not, and what you should do if you need to make a claim. It is written for policyholders and should be read alongside your policy schedule and wording.

## What storm damage covers

Storm damage to your home is covered under clause 4.1 of the policy wording. Clause 4.1 covers storm damage to the buildings and contents insured under your plan, so damage caused by high winds, driving rain, hail or falling trees and debris is generally included. The exact cover depends on the plan you hold and on any optional riders you have added, so please check your schedule if you are unsure.

## Gates, fences and hedges

Cover for storm damage to garden boundaries depends on your plan.

Home Plus covers storm damage to gates, fences and hedges up to £1,000 in total for each storm event. If the cost of repairing or replacing these items is more than this limit, the amount above £1,000 is not payable under this section.

Home Essentials does not cover storm damage to gates, fences and hedges. If you hold a Home Essentials plan, damage to these items is not covered, although damage to the buildings and contents may still be covered under clause 4.1.

If you are not sure which plan you hold, your policy schedule shows the cover that applies to you.

## What you need to make a storm claim

To help us assess and settle your claim quickly, please provide the following:

- Photos of all the damage, taken as soon as it is safe to do so. Include wide shots showing the whole item and close-ups showing the detail of the damage.
- A written repair estimate from a suitably qualified tradesperson for the work needed. We will not usually settle a storm claim without a repair estimate, so please obtain one as early as you can.
- A brief description of when the storm happened and what was damaged.

Please keep any damaged items or parts until we have confirmed how they should be dealt with, unless it is unsafe to do so.

## Making your claim

You can notify us of a storm claim through your online account or by calling our claims team. Once we have your notification, we will confirm the details and tell you what information we still need. We aim to keep you informed at each stage until the claim is settled.

## What is not covered

Storm damage is subject to general exclusions in the policy wording. For example, damage caused by poor maintenance, gradual wear, or failure to repair known defects may not be covered. Please read the general exclusions section of your policy for a full list.

For the full terms, read clause 4.1 of your policy wording for the storm cover that applies to you, and the general exclusions for what is not covered. If anything in this guide differs from your policy wording, the policy wording applies.
