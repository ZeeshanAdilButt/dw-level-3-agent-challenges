# Claim time limits

This document sets out the time limits that apply when Saltmarsh Mutual members report claims and send information to us. It applies to Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies, and to the optional riders for accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car. It replaces the earlier version published in 2025 and takes effect from 1 July 2026.

## Why time limits matter

Prompt reporting lets us act while evidence is fresh, arrange repairs before further damage occurs, and keep the claim moving toward settlement. Each limit below is a deadline for the member to act. Where a deadline is missed, we will look at the reason for the delay before deciding what happens next, and we will tell the member in writing.

## Reporting limits

The table below lists the numeric time limits in this document. Each row names the action, the time allowed and the point from which the time starts.

| Action | Time allowed | When the time starts |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting a home claim to us | 30 days | The day the loss is discovered |
| Reporting theft or malicious damage to the police | 24 hours | The moment the incident is discovered |
| Reporting a motor accident to us | 14 days | The day of the accident |
| Sending documents we have requested | 28 days | The date of our request |
| Asking us to review a declined claim | 3 months | The date of the decision letter |

## Home claims

A home claim must be reported to us within 30 days of the day the loss is discovered. This applies to fire, flood, escape of water, storm damage and other insured events at the home. If a member discovers damage some time after it happened, the 30 days run from the date of discovery, not the date the damage occurred, and the member should say when they first became aware of it.

## Theft and malicious damage

Theft and malicious damage must be reported to the police within 24 hours. We ask for the police crime reference number when the claim is first notified, as we cannot assess the claim without it. Members who are unable to contact the police within this period because of injury or other good reason should tell us as soon as they can, and we will consider the circumstances.

## Motor claims

A motor accident must be reported to us within 14 days of the accident. This includes accidents involving a third party, even where the member believes the claim is minor. Early notification helps us contact any other parties involved and protects the member's position if the other party later makes a claim.

## Documents we request

When we ask for documents, such as receipts, photographs, repair estimates, medical reports or statements, they must be sent within 28 days of our request. If we have not received them by then, we may close the claim. Before closing a claim, we will write to the member to confirm what is outstanding and to give them a further opportunity to respond. A closed claim can be reopened if the documents are later provided and the claim is still within the policy terms.

## Reviewing a declined claim

A member who wants us to review a declined claim must ask within 3 months of the date of the decision letter. The request should explain why the member believes the decision should be reconsidered and include any new information. A review does not extend the time limits for reporting the original loss.

## Questions

Members with questions about these limits can contact the claims team through the usual channels listed on our website. Where a member has difficulty meeting a deadline, we encourage them to get in touch early so that we can discuss the options available.
