# Endorsement E-2026-06: electric vehicle charging cable

## Purpose

This endorsement adds cover for an electric vehicle charging cable to Saltmarsh Mutual motor policies. It is issued under our endorsement authority and forms part of the policy wording to which it is attached.

## What is covered

Endorsement E-2026-06 covers an electric vehicle charging cable up to £300 in the event of loss or damage insured under the policy. The cable must be the one supplied with, or fitted to, the insured vehicle, and the limit applies to the cable itself, not to the vehicle or to any charging point.

## Who it applies to

Endorsement E-2026-06 applies to all Motor Comprehensive policies from 1 June 2026. Policyholders do not need to take any action to receive this cover. Where a policy is renewed or amended on or after that date, the endorsement will be included in the updated documents.

## Exclusions

The endorsement does not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or damage caused by incorrect installation or use. Claims are handled in the usual way, from first notice of loss through to settlement, and we may ask for photographs or a receipt to confirm the item and its condition.

## Questions

If you have questions about this endorsement, please contact our customer team or review your policy schedule, which will show whether the cover applies to your policy.
