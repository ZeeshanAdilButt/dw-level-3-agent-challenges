# App update: paperless documents

Saltmarsh Mutual is now offering paperless documents in the Saltmarsh app for all home and motor policies, including Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive. Customers who choose paperless delivery will no longer receive printed or postal copies of their policy documents, unless they ask for them.

## What is changing

Paperless customers receive documents by email within 1 hour of a change to their policy, such as a renewal, an amendment or the addition of an optional rider. Riders covered by this update include accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car cover. Each document is also stored in the app so that it can be viewed, downloaded or shared at any time.

## How to switch to paperless

To switch, open the Saltmarsh app, select Documents, then choose Paperless delivery and confirm your email address. The change applies from the next document issued on your policy. Customers who prefer to keep paper copies can continue to receive them by post, and they can switch back at any time from the same menu.

## Who to contact

If you have questions about this update, please contact our member services team through the app or on the number shown on your policy schedule. Claims enquiries should continue to be raised through the app as before, so that the claim can be tracked from first notice of loss through to settlement.
