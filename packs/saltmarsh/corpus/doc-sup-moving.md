# Moving home

## Before you move

If you are moving home, tell us before the move date so we can update your policy to the new address. Let us know the date you plan to move, the new address and whether you are keeping your current cover. Doing this early helps avoid gaps in protection and keeps your records accurate.

## Your contents during the move

Contents are covered at both addresses for 30 days when you move home. This means your belongings remain protected while they are in transit and while you settle into the new property, so you do not need to arrange separate cover for the overlap period. If you need the arrangement to run longer, contact us before the 30 days end so we can review your options.

## Updating your home policy

Once you have moved, confirm the new address with us so your home cover, including Home Essentials or Home Plus and any optional riders you hold, reflects the property you now live in. If the new home differs in size, construction or occupancy, we may need to review the level of cover to make sure it remains suitable.

## Telling us about changes

Please also tell us if your motor policy is affected by the move, such as a new postcode that changes where your vehicle is kept. Keeping your details current means that if you need to make a claim, we can handle it from first notice of loss to settlement without delay.
