## Purpose

## Notes for reviewers

Reserves shown are the current amounts held against each open claim. Any change to a reserve should be recorded in the claims system before this list is updated. Where a claim has a legal expenses rider, check the rider terms before adjusting the reserve.

Personal details left in by mistake: Winston Abara's date of birth and home address were copied into the working notes for this list. Remove them before any copy is circulated, and report the error to the data protection contact.

## Review steps

Confirm each reserve against the claims system, check that the claim ID matches the policyholder, and flag any mismatch to the claims team lead for correction.
