# FAQ: cancelling

This FAQ explains how to cancel a Saltmarsh Mutual home or motor policy, including the cooling-off period. It applies from 2026-07-01.

## Cancelling your policy

## What is the cooling-off period on a home policy?

The cooling-off period on a home policy is 14 days, which is the time you have to change your mind after taking out cover. If you cancel within this period, you can do so without a penalty.

## What do I get back if I cancel my home policy in the cooling-off period?

If you cancel in the cooling-off period and have made no claim, you get back the premium less the days you were covered. For example, if you were covered for part of the period, we deduct only the days of cover you used and return the rest of the premium to you.

## What is the cooling-off period on a motor policy?

The cooling-off period on a motor policy is 14 days. As with home cover, you can cancel within this time to end your motor policy without a penalty, provided the conditions above are met.

## Can I cancel after the cooling-off period has ended?

Yes. You can cancel at any time after the cooling-off period, but the refund is calculated differently. Contact us and we will explain the amount due based on your policy terms.

## Do I still get a refund if I have already made a claim?

If you have made a claim, the standard cooling-off refund may not apply. Please contact us so we can review your circumstances before confirming any amount.

## How do I cancel my policy?

You can cancel by contacting our customer team using the details on your policy documents. Please give your policy number and the date you want cover to end. We will confirm the cancellation in writing.

## Will I need to pay for optional riders if I cancel?

Optional riders cover accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency and a courtesy car. If you cancel your main policy, the riders attached to it end at the same time. Any refund for riders is included in the overall calculation of your premium.

## Do I need to tell anyone else when I cancel?

If you have a mortgage or a finance agreement linked to your home or vehicle, check with that lender before cancelling, as they may require evidence of replacement cover.

## Can Saltmarsh Mutual cancel my policy?

We may cancel a policy in limited circumstances, such as non-payment of premium or a material change in the risk that you have not told us about. We will always write to you explaining the reason and what happens next.
