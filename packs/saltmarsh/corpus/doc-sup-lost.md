# Lost your documents?

Misplaced a policy schedule, certificate of motor insurance or policy wording? Don't worry. Saltmarsh Mutual can send you a replacement copy quickly, whether you hold a Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party or Motor Comprehensive policy.

## What you can request

You can ask for a duplicate of any document that forms part of your policy, including your schedule, your certificate of motor insurance and the policy wording for your plan. Duplicate policy documents are free of charge, so there is no fee for requesting a replacement copy.

## How to get duplicates

To request a duplicate, sign in to your member account and choose the documents section, or contact our support team by phone or by post. Please have your policy number and the postcode or registration details that appear on your schedule ready, so we can confirm your identity and find the right documents.

Replacement copies are usually sent electronically to the email address on your account. If you would prefer a paper copy, tell us when you make your request and we will post it to the address we hold for you.

## If you need a document for a claim

If you are dealing with a claim and need a document urgently, mention this when you contact us. Our team will prioritise your request so that your claim is not held up while you locate a copy.

## Keeping your documents safe

Once you receive your documents, save an electronic copy in a secure place and keep your account email address up to date. This makes it much easier to get what you need the next time.
