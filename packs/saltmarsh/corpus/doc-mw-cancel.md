# Motor policy wording: cancellation and cooling-off

## Purpose and scope

This clause sets out how a motor policy issued by Saltmarsh Mutual may be cancelled by the policyholder, including the cooling-off right and the charges and refunds that apply once that right has ended. It applies to Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive policies, together with any optional riders attached to them, including accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency where it is linked to a motor policy, and courtesy car. Where a rider has its own cancellation terms, those terms apply to the rider alone, and this clause governs the motor policy as a whole.

## The cooling-off period

The policyholder may cancel a motor policy within the cooling-off period, which is 14 days from the date the policy starts or the date the policyholder receives the policy documents, whichever is later. During the cooling-off period, Saltmarsh Mutual will refund the premium paid in full, provided no claim has been made under the policy during that time. Where a claim has been made within the cooling-off period, the refund will be reduced by the cost of the claim settled or the amount reserved against it, and the policyholder will be informed of the calculation in writing.

Cancellation within the cooling-off period must be made in writing, by post, email or through the policyholder's online account. The notice takes effect on the day it is received by Saltmarsh Mutual, and the cover ends on that day.

## Cancellation after the cooling-off period

Once the cooling-off period has ended, a policyholder may still cancel a motor policy at any time by giving written notice. A cancellation fee of £35 applies to every cancellation made after the cooling-off period. This fee is deducted from any refund due and is not charged where Saltmarsh Mutual cancels the policy for reasons set out elsewhere in the policy terms.

Where a motor policy is cancelled after the cooling-off period, any refund of premium is worked out for the unused days remaining in the policy year, calculated on a pro rata basis from the effective date of cancellation to the end of the period for which premium has been paid. The refund is then reduced by the cancellation fee. If the amount calculated is less than the fee, no refund is payable.

## Refunds where a claim has been made

No premium is refunded after the cooling-off period if a claim has been made under the policy in that policy year. This applies whether the claim has been settled, is still open, or has been declined, and it applies to claims under the motor section and to any rider that forms part of the same policy. The policyholder remains responsible for premium due up to the date cancellation takes effect, and any outstanding amounts will be collected in accordance with the policy terms.

## Effect of cancellation on cover

Cover under a cancelled motor policy ends on the effective date of cancellation. Saltmarsh Mutual will not accept claims for incidents occurring after that date. Where a policyholder has an incident before cancellation takes effect, the claim will be handled under the policy in force at the time of the incident, subject to the other terms of the policy.

Where a motor policy is cancelled, the policyholder should return or destroy any certificate of motor insurance issued under the policy, and should not drive a vehicle that requires cover under that policy. Saltmarsh Mutual may notify relevant bodies of cancellation where the law requires it.

## Cancellation by Saltmarsh Mutual

Saltmarsh Mutual may cancel a motor policy by giving written notice where the policyholder has failed to pay premium when due, has provided false or misleading information that affects the risk, or has made a claim that is found to be fraudulent. In these cases, the refund position is set out in the relevant part of the policy terms, and the cancellation fee described in this clause does not apply.

## Contact and queries

Policyholders with questions about cancellation, refunds or the calculation of an amount due may contact Saltmarsh Mutual through the customer contact details shown in the policy documents or in their online account. Written responses will explain the basis of any calculation made under this clause.
