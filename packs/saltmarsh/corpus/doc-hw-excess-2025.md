# Home policy excess table (2025)

## Document details

- **Document type:** Clause
- **Audience:** Public
- **Authority:** Wording
- **Effective from:** 1 March 2025

## Purpose

This document sets out the standard excess that applies to each home insurance plan sold by Saltmarsh Mutual under the 2025 excess table. The excess is the amount a policyholder pays towards a claim before Saltmarsh Mutual pays the balance of an accepted claim. Policyholders should check their policy schedule for any variation that applies to their own cover.

## Standard excess by plan

Under the 2025 excess table, the standard excess for each home plan is shown below. These values apply to claims made under the relevant plan unless the policy schedule states otherwise.

| Home plan | Standard excess under the 2025 excess table |
|---|---|
| Home Essentials | £200 |
| Home Plus | £100 |

## How the excess is applied

1. The excess is deducted from the amount payable on each accepted claim.
2. Where more than one item is claimed under the same incident, the excess is normally applied once to that incident, as stated in the policy wording.
3. Where an optional rider is held, such as accidental damage or home emergency, the excess shown in the table applies unless the rider wording sets out a different amount.
4. Any amount that is not covered by the policy, including amounts above the policy limit, is not reduced by the excess.

## Optional riders

Policyholders who hold optional riders should read the rider wording together with this table. Riders may change how and when the excess is applied, but they do not change the standard excess values stated above unless the rider wording says so expressly.

## Changes to this table

This table sets out the position for the 2025 excess table. Earlier versions of the excess table are no longer used for new policies or renewals. Saltmarsh Mutual may publish a further excess table in future, and the version in force at the date of a claim is the version that applies to that claim.

## Questions

Policyholders with questions about the excess on their policy should contact Saltmarsh Mutual through the channels shown on their policy documents. Claims handlers can confirm the excess that applies to a specific policy and claim.
