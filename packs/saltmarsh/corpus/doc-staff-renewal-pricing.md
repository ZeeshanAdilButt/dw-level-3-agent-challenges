# Renewal pricing notes

## Purpose

These notes set out how renewal pricing and retention offers are handled at Saltmarsh Mutual. They apply to all home and motor renewals, including Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive, and to any optional riders attached to them. This guide takes effect from 1 June 2026.

## Renewal increase cap

Internal pricing caps a renewal increase at 12% for any single policy. Where the technical premium would produce a larger rise, the renewal must be priced at the cap and the difference should be recorded in the renewal file so that the pricing team can review it at the next rating cycle. Staff must not quote or imply a higher increase to a policyholder under any circumstances.

## Retention offers

Retention staff may offer up to 8% off the renewal premium to a customer who says they intend to leave. The offer is available only when the customer has made that statement clearly during the renewal conversation, and it should be recorded against the policy along with the reason given. The discount applies to the base premium only and does not reduce the cost of optional riders such as accidental damage, legal expenses, home emergency or a courtesy car.

## Recording and review

Every retention offer and every capped renewal must be logged in the pricing record before the renewal is issued. Team leads will sample these records each month to confirm that the cap and the retention limit were applied correctly. Questions about unusual cases should go to the pricing team before any offer is made.
