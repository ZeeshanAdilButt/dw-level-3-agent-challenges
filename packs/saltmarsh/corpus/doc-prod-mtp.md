# Motor Third Party

## About this product

This guide explains Motor Third Party, product code MOT-TPO-1, a motor insurance product offered by Saltmarsh Mutual. Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, and this page sets out in plain terms what Motor Third Party covers, what it does not cover, and how the compulsory excess works. It is intended for customers considering this product and for anyone who wants to check their existing cover.

Read this guide alongside your policy schedule and full policy wording. If anything here appears to conflict with your policy documents, the policy documents apply.

## What Motor Third Party is

Motor Third Party is the most basic motor cover we offer. It protects you against claims that other people make against you when your vehicle is involved in an accident and you are found to be legally responsible. It is designed to meet the minimum legal requirement to insure a vehicle on the road, and it does so without covering your own vehicle.

Because it is the simplest level of cover, it is usually the lowest cost option in our motor range. Customers who want protection for their own car should compare it with Motor Comprehensive, which covers more situations.

## What is covered

Motor Third Party covers only your legal liability to other people and their property. In practical terms, this means:

- **Injury to other people.** Motor Third Party covers claims for injury to other people without a fixed limit. If you are found legally liable for injuring someone in a road traffic accident involving your vehicle, the cover applies without a fixed limit on the amount payable for injury.
- **Damage to other people's property.** Third party property damage is covered up to £20 million. This includes damage to other vehicles, buildings, fences, street furniture and similar property belonging to someone else, where you are legally responsible for causing it.
- **Legal costs connected to a covered claim.** Where a covered third party claim is made against you, we will handle the claim on your behalf and manage its defence in line with the policy terms.

Cover applies to the driver named on your policy and, where the policy allows, to other drivers you have given permission to use the vehicle. Check your policy schedule for the drivers covered.

## What is not covered

It is just as important to understand what Motor Third Party does not cover.

- Motor Third Party does not pay for damage to your own vehicle. If your car is damaged in an accident, whether or not it was your fault, this product does not pay to repair or replace it.
- Motor Third Party does not cover fire or theft of your own vehicle. If your vehicle is stolen or damaged by fire, you will not be covered under this product.
- Motor Third Party does not cover injury to you or your passengers. Personal injury to the policyholder or passengers in your own vehicle is not within the scope of this cover.
- Motor Third Party does not cover losses caused by use outside the permitted use stated on your policy schedule, or by drivers who are not covered by the policy.

Where you want cover for your own vehicle, fire, or theft, you should consider Motor Comprehensive. Optional riders may also be available to extend certain areas of cover, subject to the terms of the rider.

## The compulsory excess

The Motor Third Party compulsory excess is £200 on any claim. The excess is the amount you pay towards each claim before the rest is settled by Saltmarsh Mutual. It applies to every claim made under this product, so if you make two separate claims in the same period, the excess is payable on each one.

The excess is set out on your policy schedule. Where a claim is made against you by a third party, the excess is payable in line with the policy terms. We will explain the amount due when we tell you about a claim, and we will confirm the figure in writing.

## Making a claim

If an incident happens that could lead to a claim, contact us as soon as possible. Early notice helps us assess the situation, protect your position, and support the people involved. You should not admit responsibility or agree to pay anyone directly without first speaking to us. We will guide you through the next steps, from first notice of loss through to settlement.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether Motor Third Party suits your needs, speak to one of our advisers before you buy. They can explain the difference between this product and Motor Comprehensive, and help you understand how the £200 compulsory excess would apply to your circumstances.
