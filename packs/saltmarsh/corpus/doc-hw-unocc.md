# Home policy wording: unoccupied homes

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out when a home is treated as unoccupied under a Saltmarsh Mutual home policy, and how that status affects cover for theft and malicious damage. It applies to Home Essentials and Home Plus policies. It should be read together with the general conditions and the cover section of your policy schedule.

## When a home is unoccupied

A home is unoccupied when no one has slept in it for 30 days in a row. The 30 days are counted from the last night on which anyone slept in the home. A night spent away on a short trip, a stay in hospital, or a period of work away from home does not by itself end the count if the home is still the usual residence of the policyholder or a person living there with them, but the policyholder must tell us if the home is expected to stand empty for longer than thirty days.

Once a home has become unoccupied, the count does not restart until someone sleeps in it again. Occasional visits during the day, time spent on maintenance, or staying in the home only for part of a day do not count as sleeping in it.

## Theft and malicious damage

Theft and malicious damage are not covered if the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 days at the time the loss happens. This exclusion applies only to theft and malicious damage. Other parts of the policy, including cover for fire, storm, flood and escape of water, are not affected by this clause, although the general conditions on keeping the home in a reasonable state of repair still apply.

For the purposes of this exclusion, the sixty day period is counted from the same starting point as the thirty day period above. If a loss occurs after the home has been unoccupied for more than sixty days, we will not pay for the theft or malicious damage element of the claim, even if the home was occupied for part of the period before the loss.

## Telling us about an empty home

If you know that your home will be unoccupied for more than thirty days, please contact us before the period begins. We will confirm in writing whether any change to your cover or any additional condition applies. Failing to tell us about a long period of non-occupancy may affect how we handle a claim, in line with the disclosure terms in your policy.

## What this means in practice

Where a home has been empty for longer than the period stated in this clause, we will ask for evidence of the dates on which the home was last occupied when we assess a theft or malicious damage claim. This may include utility records, correspondence or other reasonable documents. Our decision will be based on the facts of the claim and the terms of this clause.
