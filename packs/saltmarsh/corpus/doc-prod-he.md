# Home Essentials

## Who this product suits

Home Essentials is designed for owners who live in their property. It is for owner-occupied homes of standard construction, which covers most houses and flats built with conventional materials. If your home is non-standard in construction, is let out, or is used for business purposes, please contact us before buying, as this product may not be suitable for you.

To buy Home Essentials (product code HOM-ESS-1), the policyholder must be at least 18 years old. The same minimum age of 18 applies to Home policies generally and to Motor Third Party cover.

## What the product covers

Home Essentials (HOM-ESS-1) protects the building you live in and the contents you keep in it against the insured events set out in the policy wording. Cover applies within the limits shown below and subject to the standard excess.

Accidental damage is not included in Home Essentials. If you want cover for accidental damage, it can be added only as the RDR-AD rider. Home Plus does not include accidental damage either, so the same rider route applies there.

Flood damage is excluded from Home Essentials under exclusion 4.5.2. This means that any loss caused by flood is not covered under this product. Please read exclusion 4.5.2 carefully before buying, as it applies regardless of how the flood arises.

## Key limits and excess

The table below sets out the numeric facts for Home Essentials. Limits are the most we will pay for each section, and the standard excess is the amount you pay towards each valid claim.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum age to buy | 18 |
| Product code | HOM-ESS-1 |
| Buildings limit | £400,000 |
| Contents limit | £40,000 |
| Standard excess | £250 |

The buildings limit of £400,000 is the most we will pay for damage to the structure of the home. The contents limit of £40,000 is the most we will pay for your belongings. The standard excess of £250 applies to each valid claim, unless a different excess is shown on your policy schedule.

## Optional riders

Riders extend or add to the core cover. The RDR-AD rider adds accidental damage cover to Home Essentials, as noted above. Other riders, including legal expenses, home emergency and courtesy car, may be available alongside this product. Check your policy schedule to see which riders you have chosen and any terms that apply to them.

## Making a claim

If something goes wrong, tell us as soon as you can. Your first notice of loss starts the claims process, and we will guide you through the steps from there to settlement. Keep any evidence of the damage, such as photographs, and take reasonable steps to prevent further loss while you wait for our advice.

## Important points to remember

- Home Essentials is for owner-occupied homes of standard construction only.
- Accidental damage is not included and is available only as the RDR-AD rider.
- Flood damage is excluded under exclusion 4.5.2.
- The buildings limit is £400,000, the contents limit is £40,000, and the standard excess is £250.
- The minimum age to buy is 18.

This guide summarises Home Essentials and does not replace the full policy wording. Please read the policy documents before you buy, and ask us if anything is unclear.
