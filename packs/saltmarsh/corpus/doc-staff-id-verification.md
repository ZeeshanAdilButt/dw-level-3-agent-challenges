# Identity verification procedure

## Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual staff verify the identity of a caller or correspondent before discussing or changing a policy, making a payment, or progressing a claim. It applies to all handlers in home and motor claims and policy servicing. It takes effect on 2026-06-01.

## Standard verification steps

1. Ask the caller for the policy number and the full name on the policy.
2. Confirm the postcode of the insured address.
3. Ask one further check question drawn from the verification list in the case system (for example, the most recent payment amount or the vehicle registration on the policy).
4. Confirm the caller's date of birth by asking them to state it, and check it against the record. Staff must never read a full date of birth back to a caller.
5. Only when all checks match may the handler proceed with the request.

## Failed verification

1. If a check does not match, do not indicate which element was wrong.
2. Record the failed attempt in the case notes with the time and the check that failed.
3. Three failed identity checks lock the account for 24 hours. The lock applies to the account, not only the current call, and staff must not override it.
4. Inform the caller only that verification could not be completed and that they may try again later or write to the Member Services team.
5. Refer any suspected fraud to the Counter-Fraud team the same working day.

## Records and escalation

1. Log every verification outcome in the case system.
2. Escalate any repeated lock on the same account to a team leader.
3. Do not store copies of verification answers outside the case system.
