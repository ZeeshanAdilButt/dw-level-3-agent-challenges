# Claim story: Lindgren family

## The storm and the first call

When a large branch came down in a windstorm and struck the boundary fence at the Lindgren family home, the family did what we ask every policyholder to do: they called us as soon as it was safe to do so. Their Home Plus policy was the right cover for this kind of event, and our claims team opened the file the same day, confirmed the details with them, and arranged for an assessor to visit the property.

## What the assessor found

The assessor walked the boundary with the family and confirmed that the fallen tree had damaged a long section of timber fencing. The damage was clear, and the repair was straightforward. The family's claim covered the fence repair but not the tree removal, which is a distinction we explained clearly so there were no surprises. Removing a fallen tree is a separate matter from repairing the property it damaged, and the family was able to plan around that from the start.

## Keeping the family informed

Throughout the process, the family received updates at each stage: when the assessment was booked, when the repair quote was approved, and when the work was scheduled. Where questions came up, we answered them directly. Our aim is simple: policyholders should always know where their claim stands and what happens next.

## The outcome

The fence was repaired and the claim was settled once the work was signed off. The Lindgren family were able to get back to normal life with their garden secure again, and their experience reflects how we want every claim to feel: prompt, clear and fair.
