# Optional riders and prices

## Purpose

This document sets out the optional riders available to Saltmarsh Mutual policyholders, the monthly price of each, the limits that apply, and which of our products each rider can be added to. It applies from 1 July 2026 and is maintained by the pricing team.

## Summary of riders

The table below lists the four optional riders. Each rider has a fixed code, which is used wherever the rider is named in policy documents, quotations and system records.

| Rider | Code | Monthly price | Limit | Products it can be added to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accidental damage | RDR-AD | £6.50 | Up to £10,000 in any one policy year, with an excess of £100 per claim | Home Essentials or Home Plus only |
| Legal expenses | RDR-LE | £3.20 | Up to £50,000 in legal costs per claim | Any of the four products (Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive) |
| Home emergency | RDR-HE | £4.90 | Up to £500 per call-out | Home Essentials or Home Plus only |
| Courtesy car | RDR-CC | £2.40 | A car provided for up to 14 days | Motor Comprehensive only |

## Accidental damage rider (RDR-AD)

The accidental damage rider extends a home policy to cover accidental damage to the insured home and its contents that is not otherwise covered by the base policy. The rider costs £6.50 per month.

A claim under RDR-AD carries an excess of £100. The rider pays up to £10,000 in any one policy year, and this limit applies across all accidental damage claims made in that year, not per incident.

RDR-AD can be added to Home Essentials or Home Plus only. It cannot be added to either motor product.

## Legal expenses rider (RDR-LE)

The legal expenses rider helps policyholders meet the cost of pursuing or defending a legal claim that arises from an insured event. The rider costs £3.20 per month and pays up to £50,000 in legal costs per claim.

RDR-LE can be added to any of the four products: Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive. It is the only rider available across all four products.

## Home emergency rider (RDR-HE)

The home emergency rider arranges and pays for urgent call-out services to the insured home, such as emergency repairs to a failed heating system or a serious water leak. The rider costs £4.90 per month and pays up to £500 per call-out.

RDR-HE can be added to Home Essentials or Home Plus only.

## Courtesy car rider (RDR-CC)

The courtesy car rider provides a replacement car for the policyholder while their insured vehicle is off the road following an insured claim. The rider costs £2.40 per month and provides a car for up to 14 days per claim.

RDR-CC can be added to Motor Comprehensive only. It is not available with Motor Third Party.

## Adding or removing a rider

A rider may be added at the point of purchase or at a later renewal, subject to the eligibility rules above. A policyholder who wishes to add a rider mid-term should contact us, and the rider will take effect from the date we confirm in writing. Riders can be removed at renewal. Monthly prices shown in this document are the prices in force from 1 July 2026.

## Eligibility and conditions

Each rider is subject to the terms of its policy wording. Cover depends on the rider being shown on the policy schedule and on the claim meeting the conditions in that wording. Where a rider and the base policy both respond to the same loss, the claims team will confirm which cover applies before settlement.

## Questions

Policyholders with questions about riders, prices or eligibility should contact Saltmarsh Mutual customer services. This document is a statement of current rider terms and does not replace the policy wording issued with each policy.
