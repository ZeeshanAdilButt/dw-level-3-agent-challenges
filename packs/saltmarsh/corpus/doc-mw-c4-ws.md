# Clause 4: Windscreen

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual handles claims for damage to the windscreen of a motor vehicle insured under a Motor Comprehensive policy. It explains the excess that applies to windscreen repair, the excess that applies to windscreen replacement, and the maximum amount we will pay for a single windscreen claim. This clause should be read together with the general terms of your policy and the schedule that shows your own cover details.

## Scope

This clause applies only where the policy schedule shows Motor Comprehensive as the cover in force at the time of the loss. It does not apply to Motor Third Party cover, which does not include damage to your own vehicle. Where a windscreen claim arises from an event that is also covered under another section of the policy, we will assess the claim under the section that gives the most appropriate outcome for you, and we will tell you which section we have used.

## Windscreen repair

Where the damage to the windscreen can be repaired rather than replaced, the windscreen repair excess on Motor Comprehensive is £0. This means that you will not pay an excess when a repair is carried out. We will arrange the repair through an approved repairer, and you may be asked to confirm the location and the nature of the damage before the work begins.

## Windscreen replacement

Where the windscreen must be replaced, the windscreen replacement excess on Motor Comprehensive is £85. This is the amount you pay toward the cost of the replacement, and it is deducted from the amount we pay out on the claim. The replacement excess applies once per claim. Replacement will be carried out using glass that meets the standards required for your vehicle, and we will confirm the type of glass with you before the work is booked.

## Limit on windscreen claims

A windscreen claim is paid up to £750 per claim. This is the maximum amount we will pay for the repair or replacement of the windscreen, including fitting and any related work, before the relevant excess is applied. If the cost of the work is higher than this limit, the difference is not covered under this clause, and you may wish to discuss the position with us before the work proceeds.

## Making a windscreen claim

You should report windscreen damage to us as soon as reasonably practicable after you become aware of it. When you report the claim, please tell us the make and model of the vehicle, the location of the damage and whether the damage has spread. We will confirm whether the damage is to be treated as a repair or a replacement, tell you which excess applies, and let you know the approved repairer or fitter who will carry out the work.

## Changes to this clause

Saltmarsh Mutual may update this clause from time to time. Any change will be shown in the version of the policy document that applies from the date the change takes effect, and we will tell members in writing before a change affects their cover. This clause takes effect from 1 July 2026.
