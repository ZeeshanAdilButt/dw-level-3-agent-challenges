# Legal expenses rider

## What this rider covers

The legal expenses rider, code RDR-LE, is an optional add-on for Saltmarsh Mutual's home and motor products. It helps policyholders pay for legal advice and representation when they need to bring or defend a claim that is connected to their insured home or vehicle. The rider can be added to any of the four products we sell: Home Essentials, Home Plus, Motor Third Party and Motor Comprehensive.

Where the rider is taken out alongside one of these products, it is shown as a separate line on your policy schedule so that you can see exactly what you have chosen to include.

## Cost

The legal expenses rider costs £3.20 per month. This amount is added to your monthly premium and is charged in the same way as the rest of your cover. The price is the same whichever of the four products the rider is attached to. If the price of the rider changes at renewal, we will tell you in writing before the new price applies.

## How much the rider pays

The legal expenses rider pays up to £50,000 in legal costs per claim. This limit covers the fees of the legal adviser we appoint or approve, along with reasonable costs such as expert reports and court fees, where these are needed to pursue or defend the claim. Any costs above this limit are your responsibility unless we agree otherwise in writing.

## The merits test

Before we pay legal costs, the legal adviser must assess the case. Legal costs are paid only while the legal adviser thinks the case has reasonable prospects of success, meaning a better than 50% chance of a successful outcome. This is sometimes called the merits test.

The legal adviser reviews the evidence and the likely result at the start of the case and again at key points along the way. If their view changes so that the case no longer meets this standard, we will stop paying further costs and will explain the reasons to you in writing. You will be told about your options, which may include continuing at your own expense if you wish.

The merits test exists so that policyholder money is spent on cases with a fair chance of success. It does not mean we decide who is right. It is an assessment of the likely outcome based on the information available at the time.

## Making a legal expenses claim

To make a claim under the rider, contact us as soon as you become aware of the dispute, because delays can affect whether costs are covered. Tell us what has happened, provide any letters or documents you hold, and let us know if a deadline is approaching. We will confirm whether the matter falls within the rider and arrange for a legal adviser to review it against the merits test.

## Things to be aware of

The rider covers disputes linked to your insured home or vehicle. It does not cover matters that have nothing to do with these, and it will not pay costs for a case that started before the rider was added to your policy. Full terms, exclusions and conditions are set out in your policy wording, which should be read alongside this guide.

If you have a question about the legal expenses rider, speak to our customer team and we will explain how it applies to your circumstances.
