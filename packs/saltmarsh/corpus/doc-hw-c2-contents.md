# Clause 2: Contents cover

Effective from 2026-07-01

## Purpose of this clause

This clause sets out how contents cover operates under Saltmarsh Mutual home policies. It applies to the Home Essentials and Home Plus plans and should be read together with the policy schedule, the general conditions and any optional riders that have been added to your policy.

## What contents cover includes

Contents cover protects the personal belongings you keep in your home and that belong to you or members of your household. Covered items include furniture, electrical appliances, personal electronics, kitchenware, ornaments, and food kept in the freezer. Freezer food is covered where the loss results from a freezer breakdown or a power interruption, provided the cause is an insured event described in the policy.

## How contents are settled

Where a covered item is lost, damaged or destroyed, we replace it new for old, meaning we will provide a new item of the same type and similar specification without deducting for age or wear. Clothing and household linen are excluded from new for old replacement and are instead paid at their value at the time of the loss, taking account of age, condition and wear. We will tell you which basis applies to each item when we confirm the settlement.

## Bicycles

Bicycles are covered inside the home only on Home Essentials. A bicycle stored inside the home is protected under that plan, but a bicycle kept outside the home, including in a garden shed, on a balcony or in a shared area, is not covered under the contents section of Home Essentials. Under Home Plus, bicycles are not limited to being inside the home only, and you should check your policy schedule for any other conditions that apply.

## Items excluded from contents cover

Certain items are not covered under contents cover, including business stock, cash and negotiable documents, and items used for commercial purposes beyond the limits set out in the general conditions. Vehicles and their parts are dealt with under the motor sections of your policy and are not covered here.

## Limits and sums insured

Your contents cover is subject to the sum insured shown on your policy schedule. Where the value of your contents exceeds the sum insured, we may apply a proportionate reduction to any settlement. Please keep your sum insured under regular review, particularly after buying new items of significant value.

## Making a contents claim

To make a contents claim, please notify us as soon as reasonably possible after you become aware of the loss. Keep any damaged items, proof of ownership and proof of value where you have them, and tell us about any other insurance that may also cover the loss. We may ask you to provide a list of the items affected and, where we ask for it, evidence such as receipts, photographs or repair estimates. Our claims team will guide you through each stage from first notice of loss to settlement.

## Relationship with other clauses

This clause should be read alongside the clauses on buildings cover, accidental damage and the home emergency rider. Where an optional rider has been added to your policy, its terms apply in addition to this clause. If there is any conflict between this clause and your policy schedule, the schedule takes precedence.
