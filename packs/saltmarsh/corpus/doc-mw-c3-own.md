# Clause 3: own damage

## Scope of this clause

This clause sets out how Saltmarsh Mutual deals with damage to your own vehicle. It applies to policyholders who hold Motor Comprehensive. Motor Comprehensive includes accidental damage to your own vehicle, and Clause 3 on Motor Comprehensive covers accidental damage and vandalism to your own vehicle, subject to the terms, exclusions and conditions set out in your policy schedule. Motor Third Party does not include any cover for damage to your own vehicle, and this clause does not apply to that product.

## What is covered

Where your own vehicle is damaged by an accident or by vandalism during the period of insurance, we will arrange or pay for repairs up to the sum insured shown on your schedule, less any excess that applies. Accidental damage and vandalism are the only own damage events this clause covers. Wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, and damage caused by deliberate acts by the policyholder or any authorised driver are not covered under this clause.

## Choosing a repairer

You may have your vehicle repaired at an approved repairer or at a garage of your own choice. Repairs carried out at an approved repairer are guaranteed for 12 months from the date of completion. The guarantee covers workmanship and the parts fitted during that repair, and it is in addition to any manufacturer warranty that applies to the vehicle.

If you choose your own garage, Saltmarsh settles on its own estimate of the repair cost. We will inspect the vehicle or review photographs and a written quotation, and we will tell you in writing if we disagree with the quoted amount. Where we and your chosen garage cannot agree on the cost, we will pay the amount set out in our own estimate, less any applicable excess, and we will explain how that figure was reached on request.

## Notifying a claim

You must tell us about an own damage incident as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event within the time limit stated in your policy conditions. Please keep the vehicle available for inspection and do not agree to repairs before we have had the opportunity to assess the damage, unless the vehicle is unsafe to drive and urgent work is needed to make it safe. Keep receipts for any reasonable costs you incur while the vehicle is off the road.

## Total loss and settlement

If the cost of repairing your vehicle would be greater than its market value immediately before the incident, we will treat the vehicle as a total loss. We will then offer a settlement based on that market value, less any excess and any outstanding finance that is payable to a finance provider named on your schedule. Where a total loss is agreed, ownership of the damaged vehicle may pass to Saltmarsh Mutual on payment.

## Disputes

If you disagree with our handling of an own damage claim, please raise it with your claims handler first. If the matter remains unresolved, you may refer it to our complaints team, and you retain any right you have to refer the matter to an independent body.
