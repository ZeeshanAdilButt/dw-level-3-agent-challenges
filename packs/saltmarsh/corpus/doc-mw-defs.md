# Motor policy wording: definitions

## Purpose of these definitions

These definitions apply throughout the motor section of your Saltmarsh Mutual policy wording. Where a word appears in bold or with a capital letter in the rest of the wording, it has the meaning given here. Please read these definitions alongside the schedule, which shows the cover you have chosen, any optional riders you have added, and the details of the vehicle insured. The definitions are arranged in alphabetical order, with the vehicle and windscreen definitions at the end of the section. Where a definition refers to another defined word, that word also has the meaning given in this section.

## Accidental damage

Accidental damage means physical loss of or damage to the vehicle caused by a sudden, unexpected and external event. It does not include wear and tear, gradual deterioration, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or damage caused deliberately by the policyholder or any person with the policyholder's consent. Accidental damage cover applies only when it is shown on the schedule or has been added as a rider.

## Authorised repairer

An authorised repairer is a garage, body shop or other repair business that Saltmarsh Mutual has approved to carry out repairs under this policy. We may ask you to use an authorised repairer when a claim is accepted, and we will tell you which one to use. If you choose a repairer that is not authorised, the repair cost we will pay may be limited to the amount we would have paid an authorised repairer for the same work.

## Claim

A claim is a request made to Saltmarsh Mutual under this policy for payment, repair or other help following an event that may be covered. A claim starts when you first tell us about the event, which we call the first notice of loss, and it ends when the claim is settled, withdrawn or declined.

## Courtesy car

A courtesy car is a replacement vehicle that we may provide while your vehicle is being repaired following a covered claim. The courtesy car rider applies only when it is shown on the schedule. The courtesy car is subject to the conditions set out in the rider, including limits on how long it may be provided and the driver requirements that apply to the vehicle you insure.

## Excess

The excess is the amount you pay towards each claim before we pay the rest of the claim. The excess shown on your schedule applies to each separate claim unless the schedule says otherwise. Some riders may have their own excess, which will be stated in the rider.

## Inexperienced driver

An inexperienced driver is a driver who has held a full licence for less than 12 months. Your premium and the cover available may depend on whether an inexperienced driver is permitted to drive the vehicle. You must tell us about any inexperienced driver before they drive the vehicle, and failure to do so may affect whether a claim is paid.

## Legal expenses

Legal expenses means the reasonable costs of pursuing or defending a claim for compensation following an accident involving the vehicle, where the legal expenses rider is shown on the schedule. Legal expenses cover is subject to the terms of the rider, including any requirement that we agree the claim has reasonable prospects of success.

## Home emergency

Home emergency means urgent help with a sudden problem at your home that makes it unsafe or unable to be lived in, where the home emergency rider is shown on the schedule. This definition appears here for reference because the rider may be offered alongside motor cover, and its terms are set out in the separate rider wording.

## Market value

Market value is the cost of replacing the vehicle with one of the same make, model, age and condition just before the loss. We work out market value using recognised valuation guides and, where necessary, comparable vehicles offered for sale in the same area. We may ask for evidence of the condition of the vehicle before the event, such as service records, photographs or an inspection report.

## Motor Comprehensive

Motor Comprehensive is the level of motor cover that covers loss of or damage to the vehicle, as well as liability to other people, subject to the exclusions and conditions in this wording and the cover shown on your schedule. Motor Comprehensive may be subject to the excess and to any optional riders that you have selected.

## Motor Third Party

Motor Third Party is the level of motor cover that provides cover for legal liability to other people for injury or damage caused by the use of the vehicle. It does not cover loss of or damage to your own vehicle, except where a rider shown on the schedule provides it.

## Policyholder

The policyholder is the person named as the holder of the policy on the schedule. As Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, a policyholder also has the rights of membership described in our rules, including the right to take part in governance matters where the rules allow it.

## Theft

Theft means the dishonest taking of the vehicle, or of its parts, without the consent of the policyholder. Theft cover does not apply when the keys were left in or on an unattended vehicle. This means that if the vehicle is taken while the keys were left inside, or on the vehicle, while it was unattended, we will not pay the claim for theft under this policy. Where the keys were left in an area where they were not visible to a passer-by and the vehicle was locked, we will consider the circumstances before deciding whether the exclusion applies.

## Total loss

A vehicle is a total loss when the repair cost is more than 60% of its market value. Where a vehicle is a total loss, we will normally pay the market value of the vehicle, less any excess and any amount owed under a finance agreement, rather than paying for repairs. We will tell you in writing if we decide that your vehicle is a total loss and explain how the settlement has been calculated.

## Young driver

A young driver is any driver under the age of 25. Cover for young drivers may be limited or subject to additional conditions, and the schedule will show whether a young driver is permitted to drive the vehicle. The premium may also reflect the age of the young driver, so you must tell us about any young driver who will regularly use the vehicle.

## Windscreen

Windscreen means the front, rear and side glass of the vehicle that is designed to be fitted to the vehicle by the manufacturer. Windscreen cover is provided within the Motor Comprehensive section and is subject to the excess shown on the schedule, unless the schedule states that a different treatment applies.

## Vehicle

The vehicle is the car shown on the certificate and its standard accessories. Standard accessories are items that were fitted by the manufacturer or that are normally supplied with the car in the same specification. Any additional equipment, modifications or accessories that are not standard must be declared to us and may be covered only if we have agreed to include them in writing.
