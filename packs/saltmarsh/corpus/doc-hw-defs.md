# Home policy wording: definitions

This section sets out the meaning of defined terms used throughout the Saltmarsh Mutual home policy wording. Where a word appears in capital letters or is shown in bold in the policy, it has the meaning given here, unless the schedule or a specific section of the wording states otherwise. Definitions apply to all home products issued by Saltmarsh Mutual, including Home Essentials and Home Plus, and to any optional riders you have chosen.

Words in the singular include the plural and the reverse. Headings are for guidance only and do not affect the meaning of any clause. The definitions below are listed in alphabetical order.

## Accidental damage

Accidental damage means sudden, unintended physical loss or damage to your home or its contents caused by an external event that you did not expect and could not reasonably have prevented. It does not include loss or damage that results from deliberate acts, wear and tear, or any cause listed as excluded elsewhere in the wording.

## Buildings

Buildings means the home described in your schedule, including its fixed structures, permanent fixtures and fittings, outbuildings, garages, boundary walls, gates, fences and driveways, and any fixed utility installations that serve the property. Buildings does not include contents, land value or any part of the property that you do not own or are not responsible for insuring under your agreement.

## Contents

Contents means household goods, furniture, furnishings, clothing, personal belongings, electrical and electronic equipment and other movable items that belong to you or that you are legally responsible for, and which are kept in the home. Contents excludes Buildings, motor vehicles and their accessories, animals, business stock and any item listed as excluded in the wording or the schedule.

## Excess

Excess means the amount you must pay towards each claim, as stated in your schedule. The excess is deducted from the amount we agree to pay for a claim. Where more than one excess applies to a single incident, the excesses are applied in the way set out in the relevant section of the wording.

## Flood

Flood means water entering the home from a river, the sea, surface water or rising groundwater, where that water rises above ground level and covers land that is not normally under water. Flood does not include water that escapes from a fixed water installation, a domestic appliance or a drain, which are dealt with under other sections of the wording.

## Home emergency

Home emergency means an urgent failure of a service or system within the home that makes the property unsafe, unsecure or unfit to live in, including failure of the heating system, water supply, drainage, electrical supply, or a breach of the external doors or windows. Home emergency cover applies only where the rider has been added to your policy and is subject to the limits shown in the schedule.

## Home

Home means the private residential property described in your schedule, which you own or rent and live in as your main residence, and which is situated at the address stated in the schedule. Home includes any outbuildings and garden structures on the same land that are covered by your policy.

## Legal expenses

Legal expenses means reasonable costs incurred in pursuing or defending a legal matter covered by the optional legal expenses rider, as set out in the rider wording. Legal expenses cover applies only where the rider is shown in your schedule and the matter falls within the scope of the rider.

## Motor vehicle

Motor vehicle means a mechanically propelled road vehicle that is insured under a motor policy issued by Saltmarsh Mutual and shown in the relevant schedule. This definition does not apply to items within the home policy, which are dealt with separately under Contents.

## Policyholder

Policyholder means the person named as the holder of the policy in the schedule. Because Saltmarsh Mutual is owned by its policyholders, a policyholder may also be a member of the mutual with the rights that membership carries, as described in the membership rules available on request.

## Storm

Storm means wind gusting at 55 miles per hour or more, or heavy rain, snow or hail that causes sudden damage to the home or its contents. Storm does not include damage caused by gradual water ingress, by the failure to maintain the roof or external structure, or by any event that is covered under the Flood definition.

## Subsidence

Subsidence means the downward movement of the ground under the home, other than from the weight of the building. Subsidence does not include downward movement caused by the weight of the building itself, by normal settlement of new foundations, or by the movement of the ground during the process of construction on the property.

## Unoccupied

A home is unoccupied when no one has slept in it for 30 days in a row. Where a home is unoccupied, certain cover may be restricted or suspended, as set out in the section of the wording dealing with unoccupancy. You must notify us if your home is to be unoccupied for longer than the period stated in your schedule.

## Valuable

A valuable is a single item of jewellery, watch, art or collection worth more than £750. Valuables are covered only where they are individually listed in your schedule or where we have agreed in writing to cover them, and the sums insured for valuables are subject to the conditions set out in the relevant section.

## Wear and tear

Wear and tear means gradual loss of condition through normal use or age. Damage that results from wear and tear, including fading, rust, general deterioration of surfaces, and failure of parts due to age, is not covered under this policy. Where wear and tear causes sudden damage that is covered by another section of the wording, that damage may be considered under that section.

## Your family

Your family means your partner and any children who live with you. Children who live with you on a part-time basis are included where they live with you for the majority of the week. Your family does not include adult relatives, lodgers or other persons who do not normally live in the home, unless they are named in the schedule.
