# Release 2026.04

## Headline

Release 2026.04 makes the EU data region in Frankfurt generally available to all customers on Quillon, giving law firms and corporate finance teams a way to keep contract records within the European Union.

## What is new

The EU data region in Frankfurt now runs as a full production environment for contract storage, search, and audit logs. Customers can choose this region when they set up a new workspace, and existing workspaces can request a move through their account team.

Several related improvements ship with this release:

- Obligation tracking now shows the owner of each duty alongside its due point, so teams can see who is responsible without opening the underlying contract.
- Renewal alerts can be routed to a shared inbox as well as to individual users, which helps finance teams that manage contracts across several people.
- Search across signed contracts returns clause-level matches, so a reviewer can jump straight to the language they need.
- The clause library accepts bulk import from a spreadsheet, reducing the manual work involved in moving standard language into Quillon.

Approvals and e-signature behave as before. No change to these workflows is required from customers.

## Who is affected

Customers who already run Quillon in another region keep their current setup unless they choose to migrate. Administrators who want to use the EU data region should review data handling terms with their legal or compliance team before moving records. Users of drafting, approvals, and e-signature will see no change in day-to-day use.

## Upgrade notes

No action is needed to receive the new features. Administrators will find the region selector and migration request form in the workspace settings page. Questions about migration timing can be directed to the account team, who will confirm a schedule for each workspace.
