# Master Subscription Agreement (2025 edition)

**Quillon, contract-management software for law firms and corporate finance teams**
**Effective from 2025-03-01**

## 1. Parties and Purpose

This Master Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Quillon (the "Provider") and the customer entity that accepts it (the "Customer"). The Agreement governs access to the Provider's cloud-based contract-management software (the "Service"), which supports drafting from templates, a clause library, internal approvals, electronic signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Authorized User" means an individual employee, partner, or contractor of the Customer who is granted login credentials to the Service.

2.2 "Customer Data" means all contracts, templates, clauses, metadata, and other content uploaded to or generated within the Service by or for the Customer.

2.3 "Order Form" means the document signed by both parties that sets out the subscription term, the number of Authorized Users, and the fees payable.

2.4 "Subscription Term" means the initial period stated in the Order Form, together with any renewal period under Section 8.

## 3. Grant of Access

3.1 The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Service during the Subscription Term, solely for the Customer's internal business purposes.

3.2 The Customer shall ensure that each Authorized User keeps credentials confidential and complies with this Agreement.

## 4. Customer Data and Ownership

4.1 The Customer retains all rights in Customer Data. The Provider receives only the limited right to host, process, and display Customer Data as needed to operate the Service.

4.2 The Provider shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access.

4.3 Upon termination, the Customer may export Customer Data in a commonly used format during a reasonable retrieval window stated in the Documentation.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in the applicable Order Form. Invoices are payable within the period stated on the invoice.

5.2 Fees are exclusive of taxes. The Customer is responsible for applicable taxes, other than taxes based on the Provider's net income.

5.3 Undisputed amounts that remain unpaid after their due date may bear interest as permitted by applicable law.

## 6. Acceptable Use

6.1 The Customer shall not use the Service to store unlawful material, attempt to disrupt the Service, reverse engineer its software, or share login credentials outside its Authorized Users.

6.2 The Provider may suspend access where continued use poses a material risk to the Service or other customers, and shall give notice of the suspension as soon as reasonably practical.

## 7. Electronic Signatures and Legal Effect

7.1 Documents executed through the Service's electronic signature feature are intended to be binding, subject to the laws that govern the relevant contract.

7.2 The Customer is responsible for determining whether a particular document type or jurisdiction permits electronic execution.

## 8. Term, Renewal, and Notice

8.1 This Agreement begins on the effective date of the Order Form and continues for the Subscription Term.

8.2 Unless either party gives notice of non-renewal, the Subscription Term renews automatically for a further period equal to the initial term.

8.3 The notice period to stop an automatic renewal under the 2025 agreement was 30 days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term. Notice must be given in writing to the Provider's designated contact.

8.4 Renewal alerts available within the Service are provided for the Customer's convenience and do not replace the written notice required by Section 8.3.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains uncured thirty days after written notice describing the breach.

9.2 On termination, the Customer's access ends, and Section 4.3 applies to the retrieval of Customer Data.

## 10. Warranties and Limitation of Liability

10.1 The Provider warrants that the Service will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation during the Subscription Term.

10.2 Except for liability that cannot be limited under applicable law, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable under the relevant Order Form in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim.

## 11. General Provisions

11.1 Notices shall be delivered in writing to the addresses stated in the Order Form.

11.2 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

11.3 This Agreement, together with the Order Form, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes prior arrangements.

11.4 This Agreement is governed by the law stated in the Order Form.
