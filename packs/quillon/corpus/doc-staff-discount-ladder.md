# Discount approval ladder

## Purpose

This playbook sets out who may approve a discount on a Quillon quote. It applies to every quote issued by the sales team from the effective date of 2026-08-01, and it is a rule, not a suggestion. Staff must follow the ladder below before a quote leaves the building.

## The approval ladder

| Discount requested | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% (ten percent) | The quote tool approves this on its own, with no human sign-off |
| Above 10% and up to 20% | A sales manager must approve before the quote is sent |
| Above 20% | The VP of Sales must approve before the quote is sent |

Quote tool approval covers discounts of up to 10% (ten percent) only. Any discount above that level must be routed to the correct approver, and a quote may not be sent to a customer while approval is pending.

## Notes to the approver

Every request above 10% must carry a short note explaining the reason for the discount, the deal size, and the competing alternative if one exists. Notes must be factual and written so that the approver can decide without a follow-up call.

Approvers must record their decision in the approval queue rather than by chat or email, so that the decision can be audited. A request approved verbally is not an approval.
