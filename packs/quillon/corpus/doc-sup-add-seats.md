# Add or remove seats

Effective from 2026-07-15

## Overview

Seats control how many people on your team can sign in to Quillon and use drafting, approvals, e-signature, and search. You can add or remove seats at any time from the admin console. Seats added mid-term are billed prorated for the rest of the term, so you pay only for the time the seat is in use during that term.

## Steps to add seats

1. Sign in to Quillon as an account administrator.
2. Open Settings, then select Billing and seats.
3. Select Change seat count.
4. Enter the new total number of seats for your account.
5. Review the summary, which shows the prorated amount for the remaining term.
6. Select Confirm change. The new seats are available to invite users straight away.

## Steps to remove seats

1. Open Settings, then select Billing and seats.
2. Select Change seat count.
3. Enter a lower total number of seats.
4. Reassign or deactivate the users who hold the seats you are removing. Quillon cannot reduce the count below the number of active users.
5. Select Confirm change. The change takes effect at the start of your next term unless you are told otherwise in the summary.

## Tip

Before removing seats, export a list of inactive users from the Users page. Deactivating unused accounts first keeps your active seat count accurate and avoids a reduction request being blocked.
