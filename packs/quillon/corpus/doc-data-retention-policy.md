# Quillon Data Retention Policy

Effective from 2026-07-01

## Purpose

This policy explains what customer data Quillon keeps, how long we keep it, and when we remove it. It applies to the contract-management platform that Quillon provides to law firms and corporate finance teams, including drafting from templates, the clause library, approval workflows, electronic signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. It is written for customers, prospective customers, and anyone who wants to understand how Quillon handles stored contract records.

## Scope

This policy covers all customer content stored in the Quillon service, including draft documents, executed agreements, attachments, approval history, signature records, obligation and renewal metadata, and account administration data such as user profiles and audit logs. It does not cover data that a customer chooses to keep outside Quillon, such as copies downloaded to their own systems.

## Ownership of customer data

Customers own their contract content. Quillon processes that content only to provide the services the customer has requested and does not use it for any other purpose. Retention periods in this policy describe how long Quillon holds data on the customer's behalf, and they do not change who owns the data.

## Active accounts

While an account is active, customer content is retained for as long as the customer keeps it in the service. Customers may delete individual documents at any time. Deleted documents move out of normal views and are removed from primary storage in line with the backup schedule described below.

## Closed accounts

When a customer closes an account, the customer receives an export window in which to download its records. After that export window ends, Quillon deletes the account's customer content. Closed accounts are deleted 90 days after the export window ends. Deletion removes the content from the live service. Records that remain in backup copies are handled under the backup rule below.

## Backups

Quillon keeps encrypted backup copies of the service so that it can recover from failures and data loss. Backups are kept for 35 days. After that period, backup copies are overwritten or destroyed as part of normal rotation. Backups are not restored to reintroduce data that a customer has deleted, except where required to recover the service itself.

## Audit and security logs

Quillon keeps access and administrative logs that show who viewed, changed, approved, or signed a document. These logs support the integrity of signed contracts and help customers answer questions about their own records. Audit logs are retained for the period needed to support those purposes and are then deleted in line with Quillon's internal schedule.

## Legal holds and legal requirements

Where a customer or a court, regulator, or other authority requires that records be preserved, Quillon will hold the relevant data beyond the periods stated here. Customers who need a legal hold should contact Quillon's support team with the details of the hold so that we can apply it to the affected account. Quillon may also retain limited records where the law requires it.

## Requests for deletion

Customers may request earlier deletion of their content, subject to any legal holds and to the backup rule above. Quillon will confirm the request and the timing of deletion in writing.

## Changes to this policy

Quillon may update this policy from time to time. We will publish the revised version with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify account administrators before the change takes effect.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Quillon's support team through the contact details listed in the service. Requests about a specific account should include the account name and the administrator's contact address.
