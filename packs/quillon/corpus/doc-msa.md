# Master Subscription Agreement

This Master Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is signed between Quillon Software Ltd (the "Provider") and the customer named on the order form (the "Customer"). It governs access to the Provider's contract-management software service (the "Service").

## 1. Parties and Structure

1. This Agreement is signed between Quillon Software Ltd and the customer named on the order form, and it applies to every order form that refers to it.
2. Each order form is governed by the Master Subscription Agreement in force on the order form effective date, and that version continues to apply to that order form for its full term unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
3. The order form sets out the subscribed modules, user numbers, fees and term for the Customer. The detailed service description is held in Schedule A (Service Description).

## 2. Definitions

1. "Customer Data" means documents, templates, clauses, metadata and other content uploaded to or created within the Service by or for the Customer.
2. "Authorised User" means an individual employee, contractor or agent of the Customer who is granted login credentials to the Service by the Customer.
3. Other capitalised terms have the meanings given in Schedule B (Definitions).

## 3. Service Provision

1. The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Service during the subscription term for its internal business purposes, including drafting from templates, managing the clause library, routing approvals, collecting e-signatures, tracking obligations, receiving renewal alerts and searching signed contracts.
2. The Provider shall maintain the Service at the service levels described in Schedule C (Service Levels).
3. The Provider may release updates that improve the Service, provided no update materially reduces the core functionality described in the order form.

## 4. Customer Obligations

1. The Customer shall ensure that Authorised Users keep their credentials confidential and use the Service in accordance with the acceptable use rules in Schedule D (Acceptable Use).
2. The Customer is responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of Customer Data and for having the rights needed to upload it.
3. The Customer shall not attempt to reverse engineer, disrupt or gain unauthorised access to the Service.

## 5. Fees and Payment

1. The Customer shall pay the fees stated in the order form on the invoicing terms set out in that order form.
2. Fees are exclusive of taxes, which the Customer shall pay where applicable.
3. Payment terms, late payment handling and fee changes at renewal are set out in Schedule E (Fees and Invoicing).

## 6. Data Ownership and Use

1. As between the parties, the Customer owns all Customer Data, and the Provider acquires no ownership rights in it.
2. The Provider may process Customer Data only to provide, secure and support the Service and as further described in Schedule F (Data Processing Terms).
3. On termination, the Customer may export Customer Data in the formats described in Schedule F, after which the Provider will delete it in accordance with that schedule.

## 7. Confidentiality

1. Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it under equivalent duties.
2. These obligations do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the receiving party, or that the law requires to be disclosed.
3. Detailed confidentiality exceptions and survival periods are set out in Schedule G (Confidentiality).

## 8. Security

1. The Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of Customer Data, including access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, and audit logging.
2. The Provider shall notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Customer Data.
3. The security controls and incident procedures are described in Schedule H (Security Measures).

## 9. Warranties and Disclaimers

1. The Provider warrants that it has the right to provide the Service and that the Service will perform materially in accordance with its documentation.
2. The Service is a tool to help manage contracts. It does not replace legal advice, and the Customer remains responsible for the legal content of its documents.
3. Except as stated in this Agreement, all other warranties, express or implied, are excluded to the fullest extent the law allows. The warranty and remedy detail is in Schedule I (Warranties and Remedies).

## 10. Liability

1. Each party's total liability arising under this Agreement is limited to the amount set out in Schedule J (Liability Limits).
2. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, except where the law does not permit that exclusion.
3. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 11. Term and Renewal

1. This Agreement starts on the order form effective date and continues for as long as any order form remains in force.
2. Each order form renews at the end of its initial term as described in the order form and Schedule K (Term and Renewal), unless either party gives notice of non-renewal in accordance with that schedule.

## 12. Termination

1. Either party may terminate an order form for material breach that remains uncured after the cure period in Schedule L (Termination).
2. Either party may terminate for the other's insolvency, as set out in that schedule.
3. On termination, the Customer's access ends and the provisions on data return, payment of accrued fees and surviving clauses continue to apply.

## 13. General Terms and Order of Precedence

1. If an order form and the Master Subscription Agreement disagree, the order form wins for the items it states, and the Master Subscription Agreement applies to everything else.
2. Notices, assignment, subcontracting, governing law and dispute resolution are set out in Schedule M (General Terms).
3. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor of all or substantially all of its business.

## 14. Entire Agreement

1. This Agreement, together with its schedules and the relevant order forms, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions.
2. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.
