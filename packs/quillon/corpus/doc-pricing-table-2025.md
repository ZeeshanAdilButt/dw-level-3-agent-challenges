# Plans and pricing (March 2025)

**Document type:** Pricing
**Audience:** Public
**Authority:** Rule
**Effective from:** 2025-03-01

## Purpose

This document sets out the plans and list prices for Quillon, the contract-management software service used by law firms and corporate finance teams. It covers drafting from templates, the clause library, approvals, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. The figures below apply to the March 2025 price list.

## Scope

The price list applies to all new subscriptions and to renewals that take effect on or after the effective date. Prices are quoted in US dollars and exclude any taxes that may apply in the customer's jurisdiction. Plan features are described in general terms in this document; the current product documentation sets out the full feature detail for each plan.

## Plans

Quillon offers four plans: Starter, Pro, Team, and Enterprise. Each plan includes the core contract workflow features, with additional storage, administration controls, and support levels as the plan rises.

## Numeric terms

The table below lists the numeric terms of the March 2025 price list.

| Plan | Term | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Pro | Price per seat per month on monthly billing | $34 |
| Team | Price per seat per month on monthly billing | $27 |
| Pro | Included storage | 250 GB |
| Starter | Included storage | 10 GB |
| Enterprise | Minimum number of seats | 20 seats |

## Billing

Subscriptions are billed per seat. A seat is a named user account with access to the contract workspace. Monthly billing applies the per-seat price shown in the table above. Customers who need a different billing arrangement should contact Quillon sales to discuss the options available for their plan.

## Storage

Storage covers the documents, templates, clauses, and signed contracts held in a customer's workspace. Where a plan lists an included storage amount, that amount is the storage allowance for the workspace under that plan. Customers approaching their allowance will receive notice through the administration console so that they can review their usage or change plans.

## Enterprise minimum

The Enterprise plan requires a minimum number of seats, as stated in the table above. Enterprise agreements are negotiated individually, and the seat minimum applies at the start of the agreement and at each renewal.

## Changes between plans

Customers may move between plans during the term of a subscription. Upgrades take effect on the date agreed with Quillon. Changes that reduce seats, storage, or plan level are handled under the terms of the customer's agreement.
