# Clause 11: Support

## Scope

This clause sets out the support hours and incident handling commitments that apply to customers of Quillon's contract-management software. It is written for use in customer agreements and order documents.

## Standard Support Hours

Standard support is staffed from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC on business days. Requests submitted outside these hours are queued and handled when the support team next opens.

## Severity 1 Incidents

A Severity 1 incident means the service is unavailable for all users of the customer. For Enterprise plan customers, Severity 1 incidents are handled around the clock, so a qualifying incident reported at any time will be assigned to an engineer without waiting for standard support hours.

## Reporting and Responsibilities

Customers should report incidents through the support channel named in their order documentation. The customer must provide a clear description of the issue and reasonable access to information needed to diagnose it. Quillon will keep the customer informed of progress until the incident is resolved or downgraded to a lower severity.

## Changes

Quillon may update this clause from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to customers before they take effect, and the version in force at the time of an incident governs that incident.
