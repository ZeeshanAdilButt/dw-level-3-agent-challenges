# Release 2026.03

## Headline

Release 2026.03 adds SCIM provisioning for Enterprise accounts, so customer identity teams can create, update and remove user access from their own identity provider.

## What changed

- SCIM provisioning is now available for Enterprise accounts. Administrators can connect a supported identity provider and have user accounts created, updated and deactivated automatically as staff join, change roles or leave the organization.
- Access changes made in the identity provider flow into Quillon without manual steps, which reduces the risk of former staff retaining access to signed contracts and drafts.
- Provisioning activity is recorded in the account audit log, so administrators can review when accounts were created, changed or deactivated and by which source.

## Setup and administration

Enterprise administrators can enable SCIM from the identity settings area of the admin console. Setup requires the endpoint details and a token generated within Quillon, which should be stored in the identity provider and not shared more widely. Once connected, administrators can choose which groups to synchronize, so only the teams that need contract access are provisioned. Existing manually created users remain in place until the first sync, after which the identity provider becomes the source of truth for the synchronized groups.

## Who is affected

Enterprise account administrators and identity or IT teams at Enterprise customers are affected. Accounts on other plans see no change in this release, and existing single sign-on settings continue to work as before.

## Other notes

Release 2026.03 also includes general fixes to clause library search and small improvements to renewal alert messages. Customers with questions about SCIM setup can contact their account team or the Quillon support desk, and detailed configuration steps are available in the admin documentation.
