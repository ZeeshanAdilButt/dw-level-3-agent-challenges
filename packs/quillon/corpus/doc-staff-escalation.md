# Escalation contacts

## Purpose and scope

This guide tells Quillon staff who to contact when a case needs to move beyond the standard support queue. It is for internal use only and must not be shared with customers, prospects or partners. Use it alongside the internal support runbook and the discount approval policy.

## Who to contact

The following contacts apply from 2026-08-01. Where a mailbox is listed, send the escalation there so that the request is logged and visible to the team.

| Role | Name | Mailbox |
|---|---|---|
| Sales manager for discount escalations | Dana Whitfield | Route through the sales operations queue |
| VP of Sales | Colm Hargreave | Route through the sales leadership queue |
| Security incident escalation | Yusuf Adeyemi | security-oncall@quillon.example |

## Discount escalations

Any request for a discount outside the standard approval range goes first to Dana Whitfield, the sales manager for discount escalations. Requests that Dana Whitfield cannot approve are passed to Colm Hargreave, the VP of Sales. Do not promise any concession to a customer until the correct approver has signed off in writing.

## Security incidents

Report any suspected security incident, including unauthorised access, suspected data exposure or a compromised account, to Yusuf Adeyemi at security-oncall@quillon.example as soon as it is noticed. Do not wait to confirm the facts before reporting. Include what you saw, when you saw it and which accounts or documents may be affected. Do not discuss the incident with customers until the security team has issued guidance.

## Keeping this guide current

Team leads are responsible for telling the operations group when a role holder changes so that this table can be updated.
