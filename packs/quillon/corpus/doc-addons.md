# Add-ons and prices

Effective from 2026-07-01

## Purpose

This document lists the optional add-ons available for Quillon contract-management software, the price of each add-on in US dollars and euros, the usage limit that applies to it, and the subscription plans that can purchase it. Add-ons are billed on top of the base subscription and are priced as shown below.

## Add-on price summary

The table below gives the code, billing unit, and monthly price for each add-on in both currencies.

| Add-on | Code | Unit | Price (USD) | Price (EUR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI clause review | ADD-AI-REV | Per seat per month | $9 | EUR 8 |
| Extra storage | ADD-STOR-100 | Per month | $15 | EUR 14 |
| E-signature pack | ADD-ESIG-500 | Per month | $60 | EUR 55 |
| Sandbox | ADD-SBX | Per month | $250 | EUR 230 |

## AI clause review (ADD-AI-REV)

The AI clause review add-on costs $9 per seat per month, or EUR 8 per seat per month. It covers up to 50 contract reviews per seat per month. Each seat that uses the add-on is billed separately, so the total charge scales with the number of seats enabled for AI clause review. Reviews beyond the 50 included for each seat in a given month are not covered by this add-on.

## Extra storage (ADD-STOR-100)

The extra storage add-on costs $15 per month, or EUR 14 per month. Each ADD-STOR-100 unit adds 100 GB of storage to the account. Customers who need more capacity may purchase multiple units, and each unit is billed separately at the monthly rate shown above.

## E-signature pack (ADD-ESIG-500)

The e-signature pack add-on costs $60 per month, or EUR 55 per month. Each ADD-ESIG-500 pack adds 500 e-signature envelopes per month to the account. Envelopes not used in a month do not carry over to the following month, and additional packs may be purchased if higher volume is needed.

## Sandbox (ADD-SBX)

The sandbox add-on costs $250 per month, or EUR 230 per month. It provides a separate testing workspace for trying out templates, clause library changes, approval routes, and integrations without affecting live contracts. The sandbox workspace is refreshed from production data every week, so its content reflects recent live records. The sandbox add-on (ADD-SBX) can be bought on the Pro and Enterprise plans only.

## Plan eligibility

The AI clause review, extra storage, and e-signature pack add-ons are available to customers on any Quillon plan. The sandbox add-on (ADD-SBX) is available only on the Pro and Enterprise plans. Customers on other plans who want a sandbox should contact their account representative to discuss an upgrade.

## Billing and changes

Add-on charges appear on the same invoice as the base subscription. Customers may add or remove add-ons at any time, and changes take effect according to the terms of the customer's subscription agreement. Prices shown in this document apply from the effective date above and may be updated in future versions of this document.
