# Support policy

**Quillon, public document**
**Effective from 2026-07-01**
**Authority: rule**

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Quillon supports customers using its contract-management software, which covers drafting from templates, the clause library, approvals, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. It explains which support channels are available on each plan, how requests are classified by severity, and what customers can expect from Quillon's support team.

Quillon serves law firms and corporate finance teams. Many of the contracts handled through the platform are time-sensitive, so this policy is built around fast triage, clear ownership, and transparent communication.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customers with an active subscription to Quillon. It covers requests about product behavior, configuration, integrations, data questions, and suspected defects. It does not cover custom development work, which is handled under a separate statement of work, or legal advice about the content of any contract. Quillon's software helps teams manage contracts, but it does not replace the judgment of qualified counsel.

## 3. Support channels by plan

Quillon offers support through several channels. The channel a customer can use depends on their plan.

Phone support is available on the Enterprise plan only. Customers on other plans use email and in-app chat. This distinction is deliberate: Enterprise customers typically manage higher contract volumes and more complex approval workflows, so they receive direct phone access to the support team for urgent matters. All plans receive the same quality of written support, and the response targets in section 5 apply regardless of channel.

| Channel | Enterprise plan | All other plans |
|---|---|---|
| Phone support | Available | Not available |
| Email support | Available | Available |
| In-app chat | Available | Available |
| Help center articles | Available | Available |

Customers may contact support through any available channel during business hours. Requests raised outside business hours are logged and picked up at the start of the next business day, except for critical incidents described in section 4.

## 4. Severity levels

Every request is assigned a severity level when it is opened. Severity reflects the impact on the customer's business, not the technical complexity of the fix. Quillon's support staff may adjust a severity level after review, and the customer will be told if this happens and why.

**Severity 1 (critical):** The platform is unavailable for the customer, or a core function such as e-signature, approvals, or access to signed contracts has stopped working for a broad group of users, with no workaround. Severity 1 requests are the only ones that may be raised outside business hours. Enterprise customers may raise these by phone.

**Severity 2 (high):** A core function is impaired, but a workaround exists, or a single team cannot complete a time-sensitive task such as an approval needed before a signing deadline.

**Severity 3 (medium):** A feature behaves incorrectly or unexpectedly, but the customer can continue their work. Examples include an inaccurate renewal alert, a search result that omits an expected document, or a template field that does not populate as configured.

**Severity 4 (low):** General questions, feature requests, documentation requests, and cosmetic issues.

## 5. Response and update commitments

Quillon commits to the first response times below. A first response is a substantive reply from a member of the support team, not an automated acknowledgment. Updates continue at the intervals shown until the request is resolved or a plan for resolution has been agreed with the customer.

| Severity | First response target | Update interval | Who can raise it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 (critical) | 1 hour, any time | Every 1 hour until mitigated | All plans; phone on Enterprise |
| Severity 2 (high) | 4 business hours | Every 1 business day | All plans |
| Severity 3 (medium) | 1 business day | Every 3 business days | All plans |
| Severity 4 (low) | 2 business days | On request | All plans |

Where a Severity 1 incident is confirmed, Quillon will share a summary of the cause and the remediation steps once the incident is resolved. Customers on any plan may request this summary.

## 6. Customer responsibilities

To help Quillon resolve requests quickly, customers should provide a clear description of the problem, the affected users or teams, the time the problem began, the steps that reproduce it, and any error messages or screenshots. Customers should also tell support if a deadline is attached to the issue, since this may change its severity. Customers are responsible for keeping their named administrator contacts current in their account settings, because support communications and incident notices are sent to those contacts.

## 7. Escalation

If a customer believes a request is not progressing in line with this policy, they may ask for escalation by replying to the open request and stating the reason. Escalation moves the request to a support lead, who reviews the history, confirms the severity, and sets a plan with the customer. Enterprise customers may also request escalation by phone. Escalation does not change the response targets in section 5, but it does add senior oversight to the request.

## 8. Maintenance and planned changes

Planned maintenance that may affect availability is announced in advance through the status page and to administrator contacts. Where possible, maintenance is scheduled outside the working hours of the affected customer base. Support will advise on any workarounds for planned work that affects signing, approvals, or access to signed contracts.

## 9. Changes to this policy

Quillon may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the document shows the version in force. Material changes, including any change to the channels available on each plan or to the response targets in section 5, will be communicated to administrator contacts before they take effect.
