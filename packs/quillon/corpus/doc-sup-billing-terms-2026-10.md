# Payment terms explained

This guide explains how Quillon invoices are issued, when payment is due, and what to do if you have questions about a bill. It applies to all customers using Quillon's contract-management software, effective from 2026-10-12.

## How invoices are issued

Quillon issues invoices for subscriptions to the contract-management platform on the billing schedule set out in your order form. Each invoice lists the plan period, the seats or modules covered, and the amount due. Invoices are sent to the billing contact named on your account.

## When payment is due

The October 2026 billing article says invoices are due within 14 days. This applies to the invoice date shown on each bill, so please check that date when planning your payment.

## Steps to follow when you receive an invoice

1. Open the invoice and confirm the plan period, seat count, and modules match your order form.
2. Check that the billing contact and purchase order number, if required, are correct.
3. Submit payment by the due date using the method listed on the invoice.
4. Keep the payment confirmation with your records for audit purposes.
5. If you spot a discrepancy, contact the support team before the due date so it can be reviewed.

## Questions about a bill

If anything on an invoice is unclear, contact Quillon support with your account name and the invoice number. A support agent will review the charges and reply with the relevant details. Please avoid withholding payment on undisputed amounts while a query is open.
