# Release 2026.06

## Headline

Release 2026.06 adds a NetSuite connector, so finance teams can link signed contracts and their obligations to records in NetSuite without manual re-entry.

## What changed

- The NetSuite connector is now available to all customers on supported plans. Once connected, contract records can be matched to the vendor and customer records they relate to.
- Obligation tracking now carries through to the linked NetSuite record, so payment and delivery milestones are visible to the people who manage them in NetSuite.
- Renewal alerts can be routed to the owner named in the linked record, which reduces the chance that a notice reaches someone who no longer handles the account.

## Other improvements

- Search across signed contracts now returns results faster when a query covers several fields at once.
- Approval steps show the reviewer's role alongside their name, which makes it easier to see who must act next.
- Clause library entries display their most recent revision date, so drafters can tell at a glance whether a clause is current.

## Who is affected

Corporate finance teams that use NetSuite alongside Quillon will see the connector on their next login. Law firms that do not use NetSuite will notice only the search and approval changes. Administrators should review connector permissions before enabling the integration for their workspace.
