# Set up your workspace

This guide walks an admin through the first steps of setting up a Quillon workspace, from adding users to connecting your contract library. Allow about thirty minutes for the full setup.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have admin rights for your organization's Quillon account. If you plan to import users, prepare a CSV file with one row per person, including name and work email address.

## Setup steps

1. Sign in to Quillon with your admin account and open the Settings menu.
2. Select Workspace, then choose Profile and enter your organization name and time zone.
3. Open the Users section and select Invite users.
4. Upload your CSV file. An admin can invite up to 100 users at once from a CSV file, so split larger lists into batches.
5. Review the preview, confirm the email addresses, and send the invitations.
6. Open Roles and assign each user to Drafter, Approver, or Viewer based on their job.
7. Go to Templates and upload your existing contract templates, or start from the built-in clause library.
8. Open Approvals and define the approval chain for each contract type, naming the reviewers in order.
9. Open E-signature and connect the signing settings your organization uses.
10. Open Alerts and set the renewal reminder window for each contract type you track.
11. Run a test contract through drafting, approval, and signing to confirm everything works as expected.

## Tip

Set up approval chains before inviting users. Users who join before their approvers are assigned may see notification delays until the chain is complete.
