# Clause 3: Termination

**Effective from 1 July 2026**

This clause sets out how either party may end the agreement for the Services provided by Quillon, and what happens to the Customer's data and fees when the agreement ends. Sub-clauses 3.1 to 3.5 apply to all subscriptions under the Quillon Master Subscription Agreement.

## 3.1 Termination for Material Breach

3.1.1 Either party may terminate this agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach of this agreement.

3.1.2 Before terminating for material breach, the terminating party must give the other party written notice that describes the breach in reasonable detail. The breach must not be fixed within 30 days of that written notice (thirty days, in each case measured from the date the notice is received).

3.1.3 If the breach is fixed within that period, the notice lapses and this agreement continues in full effect.

3.1.4 A notice under this sub-clause must be sent to the address for notices set out in the Order Form, with a copy to the legal contact named by the receiving party.

## 3.2 No Termination for Convenience

3.2.1 Neither party can terminate for convenience during the term of the subscription. A party that wishes to end the subscription before the end of the term must do so under sub-clause 3.1 or sub-clause 3.3.

3.2.2 The fees for the full term are payable in accordance with the Order Form, unless this agreement is terminated under sub-clause 3.1 or sub-clause 3.3, in which case the refund rules in sub-clause 3.4 apply.

3.2.3 Non-renewal is governed by the renewal provisions in the Order Form and is not a termination for convenience.

## 3.3 Effect of Termination on Access

3.3.1 On termination or expiry of the subscription, Quillon will stop providing access to the Services at the end of the effective termination date.

3.3.2 Each party will continue to comply with its confidentiality obligations after termination for the period stated in the confidentiality clause of the agreement.

3.3.3 Any amounts that were due and payable before the effective termination date remain payable.

## 3.4 Refund of Prepaid Fees

3.4.1 If the Customer terminates this agreement for Quillon's uncured breach under sub-clause 3.1, Quillon will refund prepaid fees for the unused part of the term, calculated from the effective termination date to the end of the term.

3.4.2 The refund under sub-clause 3.4.1 is the Customer's sole financial remedy for the terminated subscription, without limiting any other right the Customer has at law.

3.4.3 Quillon will issue the refund within the period stated in the Order Form, to the payment method used for the original payment where that is practicable.

3.4.4 No refund is due where the Customer terminates for its own breach or where the agreement ends by expiry of the term.

## 3.5 Data Export After Termination

3.5.1 The Customer may export its data from the Services for 45 days after the subscription ends (forty-five days, counted from the effective termination date).

3.5.2 During this export period, Quillon will provide the Customer with access to its contracts, metadata, obligation records, approval history and audit logs in a commonly used electronic format.

3.5.3 After the export period, Quillon will delete the Customer's data from active systems in line with the data retention and deletion terms of the agreement, unless retention is required by law.

3.5.4 Quillon may charge reasonable fees for export assistance that goes beyond the standard self-service export tools, provided those fees are agreed in writing before the work begins.
