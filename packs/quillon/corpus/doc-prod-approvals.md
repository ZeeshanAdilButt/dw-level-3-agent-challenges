# Approvals

Approvals in Quillon give every contract a clear, recorded path from draft to sign-off. Instead of sending documents back and forth by email, you define who needs to review a contract, in what order, and what happens when someone is slow to respond.

## How approval routing works

Each approval workflow is built from steps. A step names one or more approvers, such as a legal reviewer, a finance controller, or a practice group lead. Steps can run in sequence, so a finance check only starts after legal has signed off, or in parallel, so several reviewers look at the same draft at once. Every decision, comment, and change request is logged against the contract, so the history is available for audit later.

## Reminders for slow approvers

Approvals stall when people are busy, so Quillon watches the queue for you. An approver who has not answered after 3 business days gets an automatic reminder. Reminders continue at set intervals until the approver acts or an administrator reassigns the step.

## Example

A law firm's associate sends a client engagement letter to the supervising partner for approval. The partner is in court all week. Three business days later, the partner receives an automatic reminder with a direct link to the document. The partner approves from a phone, and the letter moves on to the billing team without anyone chasing it by hand.

## Managing exceptions

Administrators can reassign a pending step, add a reviewer, or delegate approval authority during leave. Every change to the approval path is recorded, so the team always knows who held the decision at each point.
