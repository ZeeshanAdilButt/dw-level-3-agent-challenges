# Clause 13: Governing law

This clause sets out which laws govern the agreement between Quillon and its customers. It applies to all subscriptions, licences and related services that Quillon provides under its standard terms, effective from 2026-07-01.

## 13.1 Customers in the United States

For contracts with customers located in the United States, the agreement and any dispute arising from it, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties agree that the courts identified in the relevant order form or master agreement have jurisdiction over such disputes, subject to any applicable mandatory rules.

## 13.2 Customers in the European Union

For contracts with customers located in the European Union, the agreement and any dispute arising from it are governed by the laws of Ireland, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of the Irish courts, subject to any mandatory consumer protection rules that apply in the customer's country of residence. Where Irish law is applied, it is applied without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 13.3 General

Where a customer's contract does not clearly fall within sub-clause 13.1 or 13.2, the parties shall agree the governing law in writing before signature. Nothing in this clause limits any non-waivable statutory right that a customer holds under the law of its place of residence.
