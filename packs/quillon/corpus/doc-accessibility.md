# Accessibility statement

## Purpose

This accessibility statement describes how Quillon approaches accessibility in its contract-management software, which law firms and corporate finance teams use to draft, approve, sign, track and search contracts. It applies from 2026-07-01 and is intended for all customers, users and members of the public.

## Conformance target

The Quillon web app targets WCAG 2.1 level AA, the widely used international standard for making web content usable by people with a range of disabilities. We design screens, forms, approval workflows and e-signature steps with that target in mind so that they can be operated with a keyboard, read by assistive technology and understood without relying on colour alone.

## Scope of the statement

This statement covers the Quillon web app and the functions it provides, including template-based drafting, the clause library, approval routes, electronic signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts and search across signed contracts. Third-party documents uploaded by customers may not always meet the same standard, and we will work with customers where a document format creates barriers.

## Ongoing work

Accessibility is reviewed as part of our design and release process. We test new features with automated tools and manual checks, and we consider feedback from users when deciding what to improve. Where we find a gap against our target, we record it and plan a fix in a suitable release.

## Feedback and support

If you encounter a barrier while using the Quillon web app, please contact our support team and describe the page, the task you were attempting and the assistive technology you use, if any. We will acknowledge your report, respond with the options available to you and keep you informed about progress on a fix. Where practical, we will offer an alternative way to complete the task while the issue is resolved.
