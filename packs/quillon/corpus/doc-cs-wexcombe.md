# Wexcombe Law: customer story

Wexcombe Law is a mid-sized commercial practice that handles contract work for clients across several industries. Like many firms of its size, it reached a point where the volume of agreements it drafted, reviewed, and tracked was outpacing the systems it had relied on for years. This customer story describes how the firm moved its contract work onto Quillon, what it configured, and what changed as a result.

## The problem before Quillon

Before adopting Quillon, Wexcombe's contract work lived in a mix of shared drives, email threads, and a set of Word templates that different lawyers had adapted over time. Finding the current version of a standard clause often meant asking a colleague, and approvals moved through inboxes, where a sign-off could sit unnoticed for days. Renewal dates were tracked in a spreadsheet that someone had to remember to update.

The firm's operations lead described the pattern plainly. Paralegals spent a large share of their week chasing documents rather than preparing them, and senior lawyers were pulled into routine checks that did not require their judgment. Nobody was failing, but the process depended on people remembering things, and that was becoming a risk as the client list grew.

## What Wexcombe set up

The firm's rollout focused on three areas. First, it built a clause library on Quillon so that approved language for indemnities, confidentiality terms, payment schedules, and termination provisions lived in one place with clear ownership. Paralegals now draft from templates that pull those clauses in directly, which removed much of the manual copying that used to introduce errors.

Second, the firm mapped its approval process into Quillon. Wexcombe runs 6 approval chains, covering standard client agreements, higher-value engagements, and several review paths for regulated matters. Each chain routes documents to the right reviewers automatically and records every decision, so the status of any contract is visible without a follow-up email.

Third, Wexcombe connected e-signature and obligation tracking. Once an agreement is signed, the key dates and deliverables are logged, and renewal alerts reach the responsible team ahead of time. Search across signed contracts means a lawyer can locate every agreement containing a particular term without opening files one at a time.

## Measured results

Wexcombe measured the change over its first months on the platform. The most visible gain was in paralegal time: Wexcombe Law saves each paralegal 3.5 hours a week, time that now goes to drafting, client communication, and review support rather than locating and routing documents.

The firm also reports that its approval process became easier to audit. Because every decision is recorded within the relevant chain, partners can see who reviewed a document and when, which has shortened the preparation work for internal reviews.

## What the team says

The firm's managing partner summarized the shift in a conversation about the rollout: "We used to ask whether a contract had been seen by the right person. Now the system answers that question before we have to ask it. Our paralegals get their time back, and our partners can trust the process without chasing it."

Wexcombe continues to refine its clause library and approval chains as its practice areas evolve, and it plans to extend obligation tracking to additional client portfolios over the coming year.
