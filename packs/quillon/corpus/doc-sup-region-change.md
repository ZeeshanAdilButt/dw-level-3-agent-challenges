# Move your workspace to another region

Effective from 2026-07-15

## Before you start

Moving a workspace between the US and EU regions takes about 10 business days, so plan the move around your contract deadlines. Only workspace owners can start a region move. Notify your team and pause new contract approvals and signature requests before you begin.

## Steps to move your workspace

1. Sign in to Quillon as the workspace owner.
2. Open the Settings menu and select Workspace, then Data region.
3. Select the target region (US or EU) and review the summary of what will move.
4. Confirm that all in-progress approvals and pending e-signature requests are closed or noted for follow-up.
5. Select Request move and enter the reason for the change.
6. Wait for the confirmation email, which states the expected completion date.
7. After the move completes, sign in and check that your templates, clause library, and signed contracts are visible.
8. Re-invite any integrations or e-signature connections that need re-authorization for the new region.

## Tip

Export a copy of your obligation tracking and renewal alert settings before you start, so you can compare them with the workspace after the move.
