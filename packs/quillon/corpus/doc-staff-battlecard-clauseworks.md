# Battlecard: ClauseWorks

## Purpose

This battlecard equips sales staff to handle competitive deals where prospects compare Quillon with ClauseWorks. It is an internal guide for staff only and must never be shared with customers or prospects in any form.

## Competitor Overview

ClauseWorks sells contract-management software that competes with Quillon for law firms and corporate finance teams. Its core offering covers drafting, approvals, and storage of signed contracts. Prospects often raise it because of its lower sticker price.

## Pricing Position

ClauseWorks undercuts Quillon by 15% on list price. Do not lead with price. Instead, reframe the conversation around total cost of ownership, including the time saved by drafting from templates, clause library reuse, and automated renewal alerts. Where a prospect fixates on the 15% gap, ask what features they would need to build or buy separately to match Quillon's scope.

## Data Residency

ClauseWorks has no EU data region. For prospects with European data residency requirements, this is a strong differentiator for Quillon. Confirm the prospect's regulatory obligations before the discussion, and route detailed compliance questions to the security team rather than answering from memory.

## Handling Objections

When a prospect cites ClauseWorks pricing, acknowledge the difference, then focus on workflow coverage: obligation tracking, search across signed contracts, and e-signature in one platform. When a prospect raises residency, point to Quillon's regional options and offer a technical review.

## Usage Rules

Never quote competitor claims as fact to customers. Verify any new competitor information with product marketing before using it.
