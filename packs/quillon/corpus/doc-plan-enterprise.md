# Enterprise plan

## Overview

The Enterprise plan (PLN-ENT-200) is Quillon's contract-management offering for large organisations, including law firms with multiple practice groups and corporate finance teams managing high volumes of agreements. It brings together drafting from templates, a clause library, configurable approvals, electronic signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts in a single workspace. This document describes what the plan includes, how it is priced, and the conditions that apply to it. It takes effect on 2026-07-01.

## Pricing and billing

The Enterprise plan costs $62 per seat per month on monthly billing. When the subscription is billed annually, the Enterprise plan costs $52 per seat per month. Prices are stated in US dollars and exclude applicable taxes. Customers choose their billing frequency when they sign the order form, and the selected frequency applies for the full term of that order form.

## Minimum and maximum seat counts

The Enterprise plan requires a minimum of 25 seats. Organisations that need fewer seats should review the Professional plan instead. The standard seat cap of the Enterprise plan (PLN-ENT-200) is 200 seats. Any order above that cap needs a custom order form, which Quillon's sales team prepares after reviewing the organisation's deployment, security, and support requirements. Seat counts can be increased during a subscription term, and additional seats are prorated from the date they are added.

## Storage

The Enterprise plan includes 2 TB of storage per account. This allowance covers uploaded source documents, executed agreements, attachments, and audit records. Storage is pooled across all workspaces within the same account rather than allocated per user or per workspace. Customers who expect to exceed the allowance should raise the matter with their account team before their usage reaches the limit, so that options can be discussed in advance.

## Drafting, clause library, and approvals

Enterprise administrators can publish drafting templates to specific teams and lock approved clauses so that authors cannot alter them without going through a review step. Approval chains on the Enterprise plan can have up to 20 steps, which allows large organisations to model multi-level sign-off involving legal, finance, risk, and business owners. Approval routing can be conditional, so that a contract above a set value, or one containing a non-standard clause, follows a different path from a routine renewal. Every approval decision, comment, and change is recorded in the contract's history.

## Electronic signature and obligation tracking

Contracts can be sent for electronic signature directly from the workspace once approvals are complete. Signed agreements are stored in the same repository as drafts, with the executed version linked to the approval record that preceded it. Obligation tracking lets teams record deliverables, payment dates, reporting requirements, and notice periods extracted from each agreement, with named owners assigned to each item. Renewal alerts notify owners ahead of renewal and termination windows, and the lead time for these alerts can be configured by contract type.

## Search across signed contracts

Search covers the full text of signed agreements, their metadata, and their associated obligations. Users can filter by counterparty, governing law, value, date range, clause presence, and status. Results can be exported for reporting, and search permissions follow the same access controls as the underlying contracts, so that users see only the agreements they are authorised to view.

## Included services and support

Every Enterprise account gets a named customer success manager who coordinates onboarding, configuration, and ongoing reviews of how the platform is used. Support is available to Enterprise customers through the priority channel. The Enterprise plan gets a first support response within 1 hour for severity 1, which covers incidents where the platform is unavailable or a core workflow such as signature or approval routing has stopped for a substantial part of the organisation.

## Identity and security controls

SCIM provisioning is available on the Enterprise plan only. It allows the customer's identity provider to create, update, and deactivate user accounts automatically, which keeps access aligned with staff changes and removes the need for manual account maintenance. Customer-managed encryption keys are available on the Enterprise plan only. With this option, the customer controls the keys that protect its stored contract data, and can rotate or revoke them according to its own policies. Both features are configured during onboarding with the customer success manager and the customer's security team.

## Changes and renewal

Quillon may update the features included in the Enterprise plan from time to time, provided that changes do not materially reduce the functionality available during an active subscription term. Material changes are notified to customers in advance. At renewal, the seat count, billing frequency, and any custom terms are confirmed in writing before the new term begins.
