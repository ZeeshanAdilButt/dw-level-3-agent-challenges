# Clause 10: Service levels and credits

Effective from 2026-07-01

## 10.1 Uptime commitment

Quillon commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the Quillon contract-management service, measured across each calendar month. Uptime is the share of minutes in the month during which the service is available for customers to log in, draft, approve, sign and search contracts.

## 10.2 Service credits

If Quillon does not meet the uptime commitment, the customer is entitled to a service credit calculated as a percentage of the fees paid for the affected month. The credit depends on the measured uptime band, as set out in the table below.

| Measured monthly uptime | Service credit (percentage of that month's fees) |
|---|---|
| 99.9% or higher | No service credit |
| Below 99.9% but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% | 25% |

## 10.3 Claiming a service credit

The customer must claim a service credit in writing within 30 days of the end of the affected month. A claim received after that period is not valid, unless Quillon agrees otherwise in writing.

To claim a service credit, the customer should follow these steps:

1. Identify the calendar month in which uptime fell below the commitment.
2. Confirm the affected month's fees on the customer's invoice for that month.
3. Submit a written claim to Quillon's support team within 30 days of the end of that month.
4. Include the account name, the affected month and a short description of the service issue experienced.
5. Wait for Quillon to confirm the uptime figure and the credit amount.
6. Receive the credit as an offset against a future invoice, or as a refund where no future invoice is due.

## 10.4 Planned maintenance exclusion

Planned maintenance of up to 4 hours per month does not count as downtime. Quillon will give advance notice of planned maintenance through the service status page and by email to account administrators. Maintenance performed outside the agreed window or in excess of 4 hours in a month counts as downtime for the purposes of this clause.

## 10.5 Exclusions

Downtime does not include unavailability caused by the customer's own network, equipment or software, by force majeure events, or by a third-party service the customer chose to use with the Quillon service.

## 10.6 Sole remedy

Service credits are the customer's sole and exclusive financial remedy for failure to meet the uptime commitment, without affecting any other right the customer may have under the agreement.
