# Release 2026.07

## Headline

Release 2026.07 introduces new plan codes and moves SAML single sign-on from the Team plan to the Pro plan, effective from 2026-07-01.

## What changed

Quillon has reorganized its plan codes so that each tier maps more clearly to the features a legal or finance team typically needs. Customers will see the updated codes on their billing pages and in administrator views once the release is live for their workspace.

SAML single sign-on, which lets organizations authenticate users through their own identity provider, now sits on the Pro plan. Customers already on the Team plan who rely on SAML single sign-on should review their plan with their account manager before making any changes to their subscription, since the feature is no longer included at that tier.

The release also includes general improvements to the contract workflow, including smoother handoffs between drafting and approvals, and clearer status labels on obligation tracking items so that owners can see at a glance which commitments are due, overdue, or complete.

## Other improvements

- Clause library entries now show the last time they were reviewed, which helps teams keep standard language current.
- Renewal alerts can be sent to more than one recipient, so that legal and finance contacts receive the same notice.
- Search across signed contracts returns results more quickly when filters are applied to counterparty name or contract type.
- E-signature envelopes display a clearer summary of pending signers before sending.

## Who is affected

Administrators on the Team plan who use SAML single sign-on should review the plan change described above. Customers on the Pro plan will see the feature continue without interruption. All other customers will see the updated plan codes and the general workflow improvements without needing to take any action.

## Support

Questions about plan changes or the new plan codes can be directed to your account manager or to the Quillon support team through the in-app help panel. Release notes for later versions will be published on the same page as this document.
