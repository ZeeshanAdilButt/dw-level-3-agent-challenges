# Renewal risk list Q4 2026

## Purpose and scope

This note lists the accounts due for renewal in the fourth quarter of 2026 that the customer success and account teams have rated as at risk. It is an internal guide for staff only and must not be shared with customers, partners or anyone outside Quillon. It takes effect from 2026-08-01 and should be read alongside the standard renewal playbook. Where this note and the playbook differ, the account owner should raise the point with the customer success lead before contacting the customer.

## How accounts are rated

Each account is given a risk rating based on usage of the drafting, approvals and obligation tracking modules, the number of active signed-contract searches in the last quarter, the state of any open support tickets, and whether the named contact has changed recently. A high risk rating means the account has shown falling usage or unresolved friction and needs an executive-level conversation before the renewal date. A medium risk rating means the account is stable but has one or two signals worth watching. Ratings are reviewed every two weeks and may move in either direction.
