# Reseller rate card

## Purpose

This guide sets out the commercial terms that account managers and partner staff apply when a reseller buys Quillon contract-management software for resale. It is for internal use only and must not be shared with customers or external partners.

## Discount for registered resellers

Registered resellers receive 15% off list price on all Quillon subscriptions they purchase for resale. Discounts apply to the list price in effect on the order date, and they do not stack with any other promotional offer unless an approver has signed off in writing.

## Volume commitment

To qualify for reseller terms, a partner must commit to at least 50 seats a year. Account managers should confirm this commitment in the signed partner agreement before any discounted order is raised. Where a partner expects to fall short, escalate to the partner channel lead rather than offering an informal exception.

## Applying the terms

Check reseller registration status before quoting. Quote the 15% discount and the 50 seat minimum together so the partner understands both conditions. Record every discounted deal in the CRM with the reseller account identifier so that finance can reconcile invoices at period end.

## Reference

Rate card reference: Bluefin rate card RC-0419. Questions about wording or exceptions go to the partner channel lead.
