# Billing questions

This FAQ answers common questions about invoices and currency for Quillon customers. It applies from 2026-07-01.

## Frequently asked questions

**1. Which currencies can I pay in?**
Customers pay in USD or EUR. The currency is chosen on the order form, and the same currency appears on every invoice issued under that order.

**2. Can I change the currency after I sign?**
Currency is fixed when the order form is signed. If your business needs a different currency, contact your account manager before renewal so the order form can be updated for the next term.

**3. When are invoices issued?**
Invoices are issued according to the billing schedule stated in your order form. Your account manager can confirm the schedule for your account at any time.

**4. Will my invoice show the amount in both currencies?**
No. Each invoice shows one amount, in the currency selected on your order form. We do not show a converted amount on the invoice.

**5. Who should I contact about an invoice?**
Send billing questions to your account manager or to the billing contact listed on your order form. Include your invoice number so we can locate the record quickly.

**6. Where can I find past invoices?**
Past invoices are available in your account's billing section. You can download them as PDF files for your records.
