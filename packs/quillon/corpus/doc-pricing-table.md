# Plans and pricing

## Purpose and scope

This document sets out the public list prices, seat rules, plan limits and service levels for Quillon's contract-management software. It covers the four subscription plans offered to law firms and corporate finance teams: Starter, Team, Pro and Enterprise. It is effective from 2026-07-01 and applies to all new subscriptions and to renewals on or after that date. Written by the pricing team.

Quillon's software supports drafting from templates, a clause library, approval workflows, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts and search across signed contracts. Every plan includes these features. The plans differ in seat limits, storage, e-signature volume and support response times.

## Pricing overview

All prices are listed per seat per month and are shown in US dollars (USD) and euros (EUR). Monthly billing applies when a subscription is billed each month. Annual billing applies when a subscription is paid once per year for the full term, and the per-seat rate is lower under annual billing. All prices exclude tax. Applicable value added tax, sales tax or other taxes are added at invoice time according to the customer's location.

## Plan details

### Starter

The Starter plan suits small law practices and finance teams with a small number of contract users. It costs $19 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $16 per seat per month when billed annually. In euros, it costs EUR 18 per seat per month on monthly billing, or EUR 15 per seat per month when billed annually. The plan code is PLN-STR-10. The standard seat cap of the Starter plan (PLN-STR-10) is 10 seats, and the plan requires a minimum of 3 seats.

Each Starter account includes 25 GB of storage and 20 e-signature envelopes per month. First support response is within 2 business days.

### Team

The Team plan suits growing practices and mid-sized finance teams that need more seats and faster support. It costs $29 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $25 per seat per month when billed annually. In euros, it costs EUR 27 per seat per month on monthly billing, or EUR 23 per seat per month when billed annually. The plan code is PLN-TEAM-25. The standard seat cap of the Team plan (PLN-TEAM-25) is 25 seats, and the plan requires a minimum of 5 seats.

Each Team account includes 100 GB of storage and 100 e-signature envelopes per month. First support response is within 1 business day.

### Pro

The Pro plan suits larger firms and corporate finance functions with several contract teams. It costs $38 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $32 per seat per month when billed annually. In euros, it costs EUR 35 per seat per month on monthly billing, or EUR 30 per seat per month when billed annually. The plan code is PLN-PRO-50. The standard seat cap of the Pro plan (PLN-PRO-50) is 50 seats, and the plan requires a minimum of 10 seats.

Each Pro account includes 500 GB of storage and 500 e-signature envelopes per month. First support response is within 4 business hours.

### Enterprise

The Enterprise plan suits large firms and corporations with many users and high signing volumes. It costs $62 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $52 per seat per month when billed annually. In euros, it costs EUR 57 per seat per month on monthly billing, or EUR 48 per seat per month when billed annually. The plan code is PLN-ENT-200. The standard seat cap of the Enterprise plan (PLN-ENT-200) is 200 seats, and the plan requires a minimum of 25 seats.

Each Enterprise account includes 2 TB of storage and 2,000 e-signature envelopes per month. For severity 1 incidents, first support response is within 1 hour.

## Reading plan codes

Each plan code ends in a number that gives the standard seat cap of that plan. For example, the number at the end of PLN-TEAM-25 is 25, which matches the Team plan's standard seat cap of 25 seats. Customers and account managers can read the seat cap directly from the code.

## Seats and minimums

A seat is one named user with access to the platform. Each plan has a seat minimum, which is the smallest number of seats a customer can purchase on that plan. The standard seat cap is the number of seats the plan is designed for. Customers who need more seats than the standard seat cap should contact Quillon sales to discuss an arrangement that fits their needs.

Seat counts are set at the start of each billing period. Seats added during a billing period are charged for the remaining part of that period. Seats removed take effect at the next billing period.

## Storage and e-signature allowances

Storage allowances apply per account and cover all uploaded contracts, templates, clause library entries and attachments. E-signature allowances apply per account per month and count each envelope sent for signature. Unused envelopes do not carry over to the following month.

If an account reaches its storage or envelope allowance, Quillon notifies the account administrator. Customers may then reduce usage or move to a plan with higher allowances.

## Support response times

Support response times are measured from the time a support request is logged in the Quillon support system during business hours. The first response is an acknowledgement and an initial assessment, not a full resolution. The Starter plan gets a first support response within 2 business days. The Team plan gets a first support response within 1 business day. The Pro plan gets a first support response within 4 business hours. The Enterprise plan gets a first support response within 1 hour for severity 1 issues.

## Billing and taxes

Monthly billing charges each month in advance. Annual billing charges once per year in advance for the full term. Prices are fixed in the currency shown on the order form. All prices exclude tax, and applicable taxes are added to each invoice. Quillon does not change the price of an active subscription during its current term.

## Questions

Questions about these plans, seat rules or pricing should be directed to the Quillon sales team. Customers with specific needs for seat numbers, storage or support should discuss them with sales before signing an order form.
