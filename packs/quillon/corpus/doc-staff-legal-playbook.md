# Legal fallback positions

## Purpose

This playbook sets the fallback positions negotiators may offer when a customer contract departs from Quillon's standard terms. It applies to all sales and renewal negotiations for contract-management software sold to law firms and corporate finance teams. Negotiators must not offer any position beyond what is written here without written approval from the Legal team.

## Liability

Legal may raise the liability cap to 2 times annual fees on Enterprise deals only. Negotiators may not offer this increase on any other deal tier, and any request above this level must be escalated to Legal. Unlimited liability is never accepted under any circumstances, including where a customer requests it as a condition of signing.

## Governing law

Quillon's standard governing law applies unless a deal states otherwise. Any change to governing law needs legal sign-off before it is shared with the customer, including changes proposed in redlines or in response to a customer's own paper.

## Escalation

Where a customer requests a position not covered in this document, negotiators should pause, record the request in the deal record, and route it to the Legal queue. Do not confirm acceptance verbally or by email while the request is pending.
