# E-signature

## What it does

Quillon's e-signature feature lets you send a finished contract for signature without leaving the platform. Signers receive a secure link, review the document, and sign electronically. Each signed copy is stored with the contract record, so it can be found later through search and tracked for obligations and renewal dates.

## How signing links work

Every signing link stays valid for 14 days after it is sent. If a signer misses that window, the sender can issue a new link from the contract record, and the earlier link stops working. This keeps old links from being used long after a deal has moved on.

## Who can sign

A single e-signature envelope can have up to 10 signers. This covers most deals, including a client, its counsel, and an internal approver. Signers can sign in any order you set, and the envelope tracks who has completed their step.

## Example

A law firm finishing a services agreement sends the document to the client's general counsel and two executives from the client's side. All three sign within the same envelope. The firm's paralegal sees each signature as it happens, and the completed agreement is filed automatically, with its renewal date set for the team to track.

## Getting started

E-signature is available from the contract record once a document has been approved. Your administrator can confirm whether the feature is enabled for your workspace.
