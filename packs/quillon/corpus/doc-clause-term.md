# Clause 2: Term and renewal

## Scope

This clause sets out how long a subscription to the Quillon contract-management service runs, how billing plans commit the customer to that length, and how automatic renewal works. It should be read together with the order form and the rest of the agreement. Where this clause and an order form differ on term or renewal, the order form governs for that subscription.

## Clauses

1. **2.1 Initial term.** The initial term of a subscription is 12 months from the order form effective date. The order form effective date is the date stated on the signed order form for the subscription, and it marks the start of the initial term for all plans covered by this agreement.

2. **2.2 Annual billing minimum.** Annual billing plans have a minimum commitment of 12 months. The customer may not end an annual billing plan before the end of that 12 month period, except as expressly permitted elsewhere in the agreement. Where the initial term and the minimum commitment for an annual billing plan are both 12 months, the commitment runs for the full initial term.

3. **2.3 Monthly billing minimum.** Monthly billing plans have a minimum commitment of 3 months. During that minimum commitment, the customer may not end the monthly billing plan for convenience. After the minimum commitment has been completed, the customer may end the plan by giving notice in accordance with clause 2.4, subject to the remainder of the initial term.

4. **2.4 Automatic renewal and notice.** A subscription renews automatically for successive terms of 12 months, unless it is ended in accordance with this clause. To stop an automatic renewal, the customer must give 60 days written notice before the end of the term. Notice given later than that period does not prevent the renewal, and the subscription will continue for the next term of 12 months on the terms then in force.

5. **2.5 Form of notice.** Notice under this clause must be in writing and sent to the contact address stated in the order form, or to such other address as Quillon confirms in writing. Notice takes effect when it is received at that address. Quillon will confirm receipt of a notice of non-renewal by email to the account administrator named in the order form.

## Operation of the clause

Clauses 2.1 to 2.3 determine the length of the commitment. Clause 2.4 determines how the subscription continues or ends at the end of the term. A customer who wishes to end a subscription at the close of its term should check the date on which the current term ends, count back 60 days, and send written notice on or before that date. Clause 2.5 governs how that notice is given.

## Interaction with other terms

Termination for breach, insolvency or other causes is dealt with in the termination clause and is not limited by this clause. Fees for any renewal term are set out in the order form or in the pricing schedule in force at the time of renewal. Nothing in this clause requires Quillon to continue any particular plan code or feature set beyond the current term, provided that Quillon gives notice of material changes before the next renewal date.
