# Quillon frequently asked questions (September 2026)

Effective from 2026-09-01. Authority: FAQ. Audience: public.

## About these answers

This FAQ answers common questions about Quillon, our contract-management software for law firms and corporate finance teams. It is written for prospective and current customers. For binding terms, refer to your signed agreement.

## Frequently asked questions

## FAQ list

**1. What does Quillon do?**
Quillon helps teams draft contracts from templates, pull standard wording from a clause library, route documents for approval, collect e-signatures, track obligations, send renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts.

**2. Who is Quillon designed for?**
Quillon is built for law firms and corporate finance teams that manage a high volume of agreements and need consistent drafting and clear visibility into what happens after signature.

**3. How does the clause library work?**
The clause library stores approved wording that your team can insert into drafts. Administrators decide which clauses are available, so drafters work from language your organisation has already reviewed.

**4. How do approvals and e-signature fit into the workflow?**
A draft can be sent to reviewers in a defined order before it is ready to sign. Once approved, the document can be sent for e-signature, and the signed copy is stored with the rest of the contract record.

**5. What is obligation tracking?**
Obligation tracking records the duties that each contract places on your organisation or its counterparty, such as delivery dates or reporting requirements, and reminds the responsible person before each deadline.

**6. How do renewal alerts work, and when do I need to give notice to stop an automatic renewal?**
Quillon sends renewal alerts ahead of key dates so your team can act in time. The September 2026 marketing FAQ says 45 days notice stops an automatic renewal. Your signed agreement sets the actual notice period for each contract, and that period governs. Check the specific terms in your agreement and set your alerts to match.

**7. What happens if the service is unavailable?**
The September 2026 marketing FAQ says the service credit is 50% for any month below 99.9% uptime. Service credit eligibility and calculation are governed by the service level terms in your signed agreement, so please review those terms for the details that apply to you.

**8. Is search available on every plan?**
Full-text search across signed contracts is included on every plan. You can search the wording of stored agreements to find the clauses, parties, and dates you need.

## Final answer

If you have a question not covered here, please contact your Quillon account team, who can review your agreement and point you to the right resource.
