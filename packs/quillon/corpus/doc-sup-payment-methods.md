# Payment methods

## Overview

This guide explains how to pay for your Quillon subscription. Quillon accepts cards, ACH in the US and SEPA direct debit in the EU. Enterprise accounts can also pay by bank transfer. The steps below show how to set up and check your payment method.

## Setting up a payment method

1. Sign in to Quillon as an account owner or billing administrator.
2. Open the Billing section from the account menu.
3. Select Payment methods, then choose Add payment method.
4. Pick the option that fits your account: card, ACH (US), or SEPA direct debit (EU).
5. Enter the required details and confirm the authorization.
6. Review the method in the list and mark it as the default if needed.

## Enterprise bank transfer

1. Contact your Quillon account manager to request bank transfer.
2. Your account manager will send the remittance details for your account.
3. Include the invoice reference on every transfer so payments match correctly.

## Checking your payment status

1. Open Billing, then select Invoices.
2. Confirm that each invoice shows a status of paid.
3. If an invoice remains unpaid, update your payment method and contact support.

## Tip

Keep at least two payment methods on file. If your default method fails, a backup method helps avoid interruptions to your contract management work.
