# Release 2026.09

## Headline

Release 2026.09 shortens the search index delay for signed contracts, so newly executed agreements appear in search results sooner.

## What changed

The search index delay for signed contracts has been cut to 5 minutes. Previously, a contract that was countersigned through e-signature could take longer to appear in cross-contract search, which slowed down teams that needed to locate a freshly executed agreement. With this release, the index refreshes within 5 minutes of signature completion, and the change applies to every workspace on the current platform without any action from administrators.

Search results now also reflect updated obligation tags more consistently. When a reviewer adds or edits an obligation on a signed contract, the tag becomes searchable within the same index window, so obligation tracking reports and search queries return matching results together.

## Other improvements

- Clause library: Reviewers can now filter clause suggestions by the template family they are working in, which reduces the time spent scrolling through unrelated language.
- Approvals: Approval requests show the reason a document was routed to a reviewer, making it easier for approvers to decide quickly.
- Renewal alerts: Alert messages include the contract owner's name and a direct link to the renewal section, so the next step is clear from the notification itself.

## Who is affected

All customers on the Quillon platform receive these changes automatically. Law firm and corporate finance users who rely on search across signed contracts will notice the faster availability of newly executed agreements. No configuration changes, data migrations, or retraining are required. Administrators who want to review the updated approval and renewal behavior should check the release guide in the help center, and customer success contacts remain available for questions about rollout in specific workspaces.
