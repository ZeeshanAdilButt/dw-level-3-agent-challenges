# Clause 5: Price changes at renewal

**Effective from 1 July 2026**

## 5.1 Cap on renewal price increases

At the end of each subscription term, Quillon may increase the subscription fee for the Software for the next renewal term. The increase in the subscription fee at renewal shall not exceed 7% (seven percent) over the subscription fee charged for the immediately preceding term. Any increase above this limit is not payable by the Customer unless the Customer agrees to it in writing.

## 5.2 Notice of renewal price increase

Quillon shall notify the Customer in writing of any renewal price increase at least 90 days (ninety days) before the current term ends. The notice shall state the new subscription fee that will apply from the start of the renewal term. If Quillon does not give notice within this period, the subscription fee for the renewal term shall remain the same as the fee for the preceding term.

## 5.3 Customer objection

If the Customer does not accept a renewal price increase notified under clause 5.2, the Customer may give notice of non-renewal before the current term ends, and the subscription will expire at the end of that term without further charge.
