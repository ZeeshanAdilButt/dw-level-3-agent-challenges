# Starter plan

## Overview

The Starter plan (PLN-STR-10) is Quillon's entry point for small law firms and finance teams that want contract management without a heavy setup. It covers drafting from templates, a clause library, approvals, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts.

## Pricing

On monthly billing, the Starter plan costs $19 per seat per month. When billed annually, the Starter plan costs $16 per seat per month. For euro prices, see the pricing table on our website.

## Seats

The Starter plan requires a minimum of 3 seats. The standard seat cap of the Starter plan (PLN-STR-10) is 10 seats. If your team needs more seats than the standard cap, contact our sales team to discuss options.

## What is included

Each Starter plan account includes 25 GB of storage. The clause library holds a starter set of 50 clauses, which you can edit, extend, and reuse across your templates.

## Support

Starter plan customers receive a first support response within 2 business days. Our support team handles questions about setup, templates, approvals, and e-signature workflows.

## Terms

The minimum term for the Starter plan is set out in the term clause of our subscription agreement. Please review that clause before you sign up.
