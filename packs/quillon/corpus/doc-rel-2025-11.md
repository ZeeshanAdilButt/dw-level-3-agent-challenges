# Release 2025.11

## Headline

Release 2025.11 adds Slack notifications for approval requests, so reviewers can act on contracts without leaving the channels they already use.

## What changed

Release 2025.11 added Slack notifications for approval requests. When a contract is routed for review, the assigned approver receives a message in Slack with a summary of the document, the requesting user, and a direct link to the approval screen in Quillon. Approvers can open the contract, review the clauses flagged for attention, and record their decision from the link without signing in separately, provided their Quillon account is linked to their Slack identity.

Several related improvements ship alongside the notifications:

- Approval requests now show the stage they belong to, so approvers can tell a first-round review from a final sign-off.
- Reminder messages are sent in Slack for approval requests that remain open, and the reminder cadence is set by workspace administrators.
- Administrators can turn Slack notifications on or off for the whole workspace, or for individual teams, from the integrations settings page.

Slack notifications are an addition to existing email notifications, not a replacement. Users who prefer email continue to receive approval requests there, and their notification preferences are unchanged by this release.

## Who is affected

Workspaces that use approvals and have connected a Slack workspace will see the new notifications once an administrator enables them. Workspaces without a Slack connection are not affected, and no action is required from users who do not want to use Slack. Administrators who want to link accounts should follow the setup steps in the integrations guide, and team leads should review whether their approvers need to connect their Slack identities before the feature is rolled out to their group.

## Getting started

To enable Slack notifications, an administrator opens the integrations settings, connects the Slack workspace, and selects the teams that should receive approval messages. Each approver then links their Quillon account to their Slack identity from their profile page. Once linked, approval requests appear in Slack automatically.

This release takes effect from 2025-11-18. Questions about setup or behavior can be sent to Quillon support through the help center.
