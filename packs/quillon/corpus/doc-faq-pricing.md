# Pricing questions

This FAQ answers common questions about how Quillon prices its contract-management software, including trials and seats. It applies from 2026-07-01 and is published for prospective and current customers.

## Trials and getting started

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. Quillon offers a 14 day free trial on the Team and Pro plans, so you can test drafting, approvals, e-signature and search before you commit.

**Do I need a payment method to start the trial?**
Your trial terms are set out in your order form or sign-up page. Check there for the current requirements before you begin.

**What happens when the trial ends?**
If you do not choose a paid plan, your access to paid features ends at the close of the trial. Any contracts you have stored remain available for export under the terms of your agreement.

## Seats and users

**What is a seat?**
A seat is a single named user account that can sign in to Quillon. Seats are counted per person, not per device or per contract.

**Can I add seats during my subscription?**
Yes. Seats can be added at any time during the term. Additional seats are billed from the date they are added, prorated to the end of the current billing period.

**Can I remove seats?**
Seat reductions take effect at the next renewal unless your agreement says otherwise. Please contact your account manager to make a change.

## Plans and billing

**Which plans are available?**
Quillon offers Team and Pro plans. Plan contents and current list prices are shown on our pricing page and in your order form, and they may change with notice as described in your agreement.

**How is billing handled?**
Subscriptions are billed in advance for each billing period. Invoices list each plan and the number of seats covered.

**Are there discounts for longer commitments?**
Some longer-term commitments may qualify for negotiated terms. Speak with our sales team for details that apply to your organisation.

**Do prices include taxes?**
Prices are shown before applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. Taxes are added at the rate that applies to your billing address.

## Questions not covered here

**Who can I ask about pricing?**
Contact your Quillon account manager, or reach our sales team through the contact details on our website. For contract-specific questions, your signed agreement governs.
