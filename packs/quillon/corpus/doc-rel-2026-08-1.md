# Release 2026.08.1

Effective from 2026-08-25

## Headline

Release 2026.08.1 makes it faster for legal and finance teams to bring existing agreements into Quillon, with a higher bulk import limit and related improvements to search and renewal tracking.

## What changed

- Bulk import: Release 2026.08.1 raised the bulk import limit from 5,000 to 10,000 files per batch, so large contract archives can be loaded in fewer runs.
- Import status: Each batch now shows a clearer summary of files accepted, files needing review, and files that could not be read, so users can correct problems without restarting the whole import.
- Renewal alerts: Renewal reminders now list the associated obligation owner alongside the contract, making follow-up assignments easier to see.

## Who is affected

This release affects all customers using bulk import, including law firms and corporate finance teams managing signed contract archives. No action is required before upgrading, but administrators may wish to review updated import limits in their workflow guidance.
