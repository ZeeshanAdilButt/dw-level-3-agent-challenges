# Clause 2: Term and renewal (2025)

**Document type:** Clause
**Audience:** Public
**Authority:** Wording
**Effective from:** 2025-03-01

## Purpose

This clause sets out how the subscription term starts, how it renews automatically, and how a customer may stop an automatic renewal under the 2025 agreement with Quillon.

## Clause wording

The following steps apply to every subscription governed by the 2025 agreement:

1. **Initial term.** The subscription begins on the start date stated in the order form and continues for the initial term stated in that order form.

2. **Automatic renewal.** At the end of the initial term, the subscription renews automatically for a further period of the same length, unless either party gives notice under step 3.

3. **Notice to stop renewal.** A customer that does not wish the subscription to renew must give written notice to Quillon. Under the 2025 agreement the notice period to stop an automatic renewal was 30 days before the end of the current term.

4. **Delivery of notice.** Notice must be sent to the contact address listed in the order form and is effective on the date Quillon receives it.

5. **Effect of notice.** Where valid notice is received within the notice period, the subscription ends at the close of the current term and no renewal charge applies.

6. **Late notice.** Notice received after the notice period takes effect at the end of the following renewal term.

7. **Records.** Quillon keeps a record of each renewal date and each notice received, and makes that record available to the customer on request.

## Status

This wording applies to agreements signed under the 2025 terms. It is replaced from 2026-07-01 by the revised clause on term and renewal.
