# Release 2026.05

## Headline

Release 2026.05 launched the AI clause review add-on, which flags nonstandard language in drafts and signed contracts so reviewers can focus on the terms that matter most.

## What is new

- AI clause review: the add-on compares contract language against your clause library and highlights deviations, missing protections, and wording that differs from your approved positions. Each flag links to the source clause so reviewers can accept, reject, or annotate it in place.
- Clause library updates: administrators can now group clauses by risk tier, so that the AI clause review add-on applies stricter comparisons to high-exposure provisions such as indemnities and limitation of liability.
- Approval routing: approval steps can now pause automatically when AI clause review finds a flagged item, and the approver sees the flag summary alongside the document.
- Search improvements: search across signed contracts now returns clause-level matches, so teams can locate every agreement containing a specific term without opening each file.

## Known limitations

AI clause review is a drafting aid, not a legal opinion. Flags depend on the clause library being kept current, and the add-on may miss issues in heavily negotiated or unusual language. Reviewers remain responsible for final judgment.

## Who is affected

Law firm and corporate finance customers with the AI clause review add-on enabled will see the new flags and routing options immediately after the release date. Customers without the add-on will see the search and approval changes only. Administrators should review their clause library tiers before enabling automatic approval pauses.

## Effective date

This release guide applies from 19 May 2026. Existing templates, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and e-signature workflows are unchanged.
