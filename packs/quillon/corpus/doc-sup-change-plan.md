# Change your plan

## Overview

You can move your Quillon subscription to a different plan at any time from the Billing area of your workspace. This guide explains how upgrades and downgrades work and how to make the change yourself.

## How plan changes take effect

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period, so you pay only for the difference for the time remaining. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal, so your current plan features stay available until then.

## Steps to change your plan

1. Sign in to Quillon as a workspace administrator.
2. Select your profile icon, then choose Settings.
3. Open the Billing tab and select Change plan.
4. Compare the available plans and choose the one that fits your team.
5. Review the summary, which shows whether the change is an upgrade or a downgrade and when it applies.
6. Select Confirm change.
7. Check the Billing history page to confirm the new plan and the effective date.

## Tip

Before you downgrade, check that your active templates, approval workflows, and obligation trackers still fit within the smaller plan. Doing this review before the renewal date helps you avoid surprises once the new plan starts.

## Need help?

If you cannot find the Change plan option or the summary does not match what you expected, contact the Quillon support team through the Help menu in your workspace. Include your workspace name and the plan you want to move to so we can assist you quickly.
