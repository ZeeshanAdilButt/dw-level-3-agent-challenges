# Acceptable use policy

This Acceptable Use Policy (the "Policy") sets out the rules for using the Quillon contract-management software (the "Service"), which helps law firms and corporate finance teams draft from templates, manage a clause library, route approvals, collect e-signatures, track obligations, receive renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. This Policy is effective from 2026-07-01.

## Scope

This Policy applies to every customer, user, and administrator who accesses the Service. By using the Service, you agree to comply with it. Quillon may update this Policy from time to time by publishing a revised version on its website.

## Permitted use

You may use the Service to create, review, approve, sign, store, and search contracts and related business records for your own organisation. You are responsible for all activity carried out under your accounts and for keeping your login credentials confidential.

## Content you upload

Customers may not upload content they do not have the right to share. You confirm that you hold all necessary rights, consents, and permissions for any document, clause, template, or personal data you place in the Service, and that your use of that content complies with applicable law.

## Automated access

Automated scraping of the Quillon web app is not allowed outside the REST API. Automated access to the Service must go through the REST API under its published terms. Attempts to extract data from the web app by bots, crawlers, or similar tools are prohibited.

## Prohibited conduct

You must not attempt to gain unauthorised access to the Service or to other customers' data, interfere with the Service's operation or security, reverse engineer the Service except where permitted by law, or use the Service for unlawful activity.

## Enforcement

Quillon may suspend or terminate access to the Service where a breach of this Policy occurs, and may take any other steps reasonably required to protect the Service, its users, and third parties. Where practical, Quillon will notify the affected customer before taking action.
