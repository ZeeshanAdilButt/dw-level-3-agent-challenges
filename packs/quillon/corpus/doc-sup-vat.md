# VAT and tax IDs

This guide explains how to add your VAT or tax ID to your Quillon account so that invoices are issued correctly for tax purposes. EU VAT reverse charge applies when a valid VAT ID is on the account before the invoice is issued, so add your ID early in your billing setup.

## Why this matters

Tax treatment on your invoices depends on the tax details saved to your account. If a valid VAT ID is not present when an invoice is generated, the invoice may show standard VAT instead of reverse charge. Correcting an issued invoice takes longer than setting up the account correctly the first time.

## Steps to add your VAT or tax ID

1. Sign in to Quillon as an account owner or billing administrator.
2. Open Settings, then select Billing.
3. Select Tax details.
4. Choose your country of registration from the list.
5. Enter your VAT ID or tax ID exactly as it appears on your registration certificate, including the country prefix where applicable.
6. Enter your legal company name and registered address so that they match your tax records.
7. Select Save and wait for the validation check to complete.
8. Confirm that the status shows Valid before your next invoice is issued.

## If validation fails

1. Check for typing errors, missing characters or extra spaces.
2. Confirm that the legal name and address match your tax registration.
3. Select Validate again.
4. If the check still fails, contact the Quillon support team with a copy of your registration document.

## Tip

Add your VAT ID before you start your first billing cycle, and review the tax details each time your company name or address changes.
