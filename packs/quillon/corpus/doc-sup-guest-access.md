# Share a contract with a guest reviewer

This guide explains how to invite someone outside your organization, such as outside counsel or an external auditor, to review a contract in Quillon. Guest reviewers can view and comment on the document without needing a full Quillon account.

## Before you begin

You need edit or owner permission on the contract. Guest access is available on all plans, and the guest does not need a license.

## Steps

1. Open the contract you want to share from your contract list.
2. Select **Share** in the top-right corner of the contract view.
3. Choose **Add guest reviewer** from the sharing panel.
4. Enter the guest's email address and, optionally, their name.
5. Select the access level: **View only** or **View and comment**.
6. Select **Send link**. The guest receives an email with a secure review link.
7. To check or end access, return to **Share** and review the list of active guests. Select **Revoke** next to any guest to remove access immediately.

## Things to know

Guest reviewer links expire after 21 days. After that period, the guest must request a new link from you. Each link works only for the email address it was sent to.

## Tip

If a review will take longer than three weeks, send a fresh link shortly before the first one expires so the reviewer's access does not lapse mid-review.
