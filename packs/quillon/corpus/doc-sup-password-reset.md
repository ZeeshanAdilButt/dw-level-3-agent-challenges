# Reset a password

## Overview

If you have forgotten your Quillon password, you can request a reset link from the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes from the time it is sent to your registered email address, so complete the steps below promptly.

## Steps to reset your password

1. Open the Quillon sign-in page in your web browser.
2. Select "Forgot password" below the sign-in fields.
3. Enter the email address associated with your Quillon account.
4. Select "Send reset link."
5. Open the email from Quillon in your inbox. If you do not see it, check your spam or junk folder.
6. Select the reset link in the email. The link opens a secure page in your browser.
7. Enter a new password that meets the password requirements shown on the page.
8. Re-enter the new password in the confirmation field.
9. Select "Save password." You will be returned to the sign-in page.
10. Sign in with your email address and new password.

## What to do if the link has expired

If the reset link is more than 30 minutes old, it will no longer work. Return to the sign-in page, select "Forgot password" again, and request a new link. Only the most recent reset link remains active.

## Tip

Use a password manager to generate and store a unique password for your Quillon account. This reduces the chance of needing a reset in the future.

## Need more help?

If you do not receive the reset email within a few minutes or cannot complete the steps above, contact the Quillon support team through the Help menu in your account.
