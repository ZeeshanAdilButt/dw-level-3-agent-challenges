# How to complete an order form

This guide explains how customers complete order form OF-2026-A for Quillon's contract-management software. Follow the numbered steps in order. Each step tells you what to enter and where to check it.

## Before you start

Have the following ready: your legal entity name, the name and email address of the person who will sign, the billing contact, and the number of seats or users you need. Confirm these details with your finance or legal team before you begin.

## Steps

1. Open order form OF-2026-A from the link in your Quillon welcome email or from your account administrator.
2. Enter your organisation's full legal name exactly as it appears on your company registration documents.
3. Enter the registered address and the billing address. If they differ, complete both fields.
4. Select your subscription plan from the list. Check that the plan includes the modules your team needs, such as drafting, approvals, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search.
5. Enter the number of users or seats. Use the figure agreed with your Quillon representative.
6. Enter the start date of your subscription. Confirm that it matches the date agreed in your proposal.
7. Add the names and email addresses of your administrators and billing contact.
8. Review the commercial terms section, including fees, payment schedule, and any discounts shown on your proposal.
9. Read the terms and conditions attached to the form, then tick the acceptance box.
10. Submit the form to the authorised signatory for e-signature.

## Signing deadline

A customer has 14 days after issue to sign an order form before it must be reissued. If the signing window closes, contact your Quillon representative, who will issue a new form with the same terms.

## Need help?

If any field is unclear, contact your Quillon account representative before submitting. Do not guess at figures or plan details.
