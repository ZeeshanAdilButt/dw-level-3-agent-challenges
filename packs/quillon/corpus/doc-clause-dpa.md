# Clause 9: Data processing

## 9.1 Purpose and scope

This Clause 9 sets out how Quillon processes personal data on behalf of the customer when the customer uses Quillon's contract-management software. It applies to every customer agreement that incorporates these terms by reference, including agreements with law firms and corporate finance teams. It forms part of the main agreement and, where it conflicts with other terms, this Clause 9 governs the handling of personal data.

The software allows customers to draft contracts from templates, maintain a clause library, route documents for approval, collect electronic signatures, track obligations, receive renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. Each of these functions may involve personal data contained in the contract documents themselves, such as names, contact details, signatories' titles, and payment information of individuals. This Clause 9 addresses all such personal data.

## 9.2 Definitions

In this Clause 9, the following terms have the meanings given below.

"Customer contract data" means all documents, metadata, templates, clauses, approval records, signature records, obligation records, renewal dates, and search indexes that the customer or its authorised users upload to, create within, or store in the software.

"Personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined in the applicable data protection law.

"Data protection law" means the laws and regulations that apply to the processing of personal data under the main agreement, as amended from time to time.

"Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, erasure, or destruction.

"Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by Quillon that processes personal data on Quillon's behalf in connection with the services.

"Personal data breach" means a breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed.

"Export window" means the period, set out in the main agreement or in the product documentation, during which the customer may download a copy of its customer contract data after the agreement ends.

"Services" means the contract-management software and related support provided by Quillon to the customer under the main agreement.

"Controller" and "processor" have the meanings given to them under data protection law.

Terms not defined in this Clause 9 have the meanings given in the main agreement.

## 9.3 Roles of the parties

For the processing of customer contract data, Quillon acts as processor and the customer acts as controller. The customer determines the purposes and means of processing personal data contained in customer contract data, and Quillon processes that data only on the customer's documented instructions, which include the main agreement and the customer's use of the services in accordance with it.

The customer is responsible for ensuring that it has a lawful basis for providing personal data to Quillon, that the data subjects have been given any notices required by data protection law, and that its instructions comply with data protection law. Quillon will inform the customer without undue delay if, in its reasonable opinion, an instruction infringes data protection law.

Where Quillon processes personal data for its own purposes, such as account administration, billing, security monitoring, and improving the reliability of the services, Quillon acts as controller for that limited processing. Section 9.3 does not apply to that limited processing.

## 9.4 Processing instructions and confidentiality

Quillon will process customer contract data only to provide the services, to comply with the customer's instructions, and to comply with legal obligations to which Quillon is subject. Where Quillon is required by law to process data otherwise, it will inform the customer before processing, unless the law prohibits that notice.

Quillon will ensure that each person authorised to process customer contract data is bound by a duty of confidentiality, whether by contract or by statute, and receives appropriate training on data protection and information security.

Quillon will assist the customer, taking into account the nature of the processing, in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under data protection law. Quillon will forward to the customer any such request it receives directly, without responding to the data subject except to confirm that the request has been passed on.

## 9.5 Security measures

Quillon will implement and maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk presented by the processing. These measures include encryption of customer contract data in transit and at rest, access controls limiting access to personnel with a business need, logging of administrative access, regular testing of the effectiveness of security controls, and business continuity arrangements. A current description of these measures is available to the customer on request.

Quillon may update its security measures from time to time, provided that no update reduces the overall level of protection provided to customer contract data.

## 9.6 Personal data breach notification

Quillon will notify the customer of a personal data breach affecting customer contract data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. The notification will describe, to the extent known at the time, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences of the breach, and the measures Quillon has taken or proposes to take to address it. Quillon will provide further information as it becomes available.

Quillon will cooperate with the customer's reasonable efforts to investigate, mitigate, and remedy each personal data breach, and will not notify any regulator or data subject on the customer's behalf unless the customer has agreed in writing or the law requires it.

## 9.7 Subprocessors

The customer generally authorises Quillon to engage subprocessors to support the services. Quillon will maintain a list of its current subprocessors and make it available to the customer.

Quillon gives 30 days notice before adding a new subprocessor, and the notice will identify the subprocessor and the processing it will perform. During that notice period, the customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the parties cannot resolve the objection within the notice period, the customer may terminate the affected services and receive a pro rata refund of any prepaid fees for the terminated services.

Quillon will impose on each subprocessor data protection obligations that are no less protective than those in this Clause 9, and Quillon remains responsible to the customer for the performance of each subprocessor's obligations.

## 9.8 International transfers

Quillon will not transfer customer contract data outside the jurisdictions in which it has stored that data, except where the transfer is permitted by data protection law and appropriate safeguards are in place. Where such safeguards are required, Quillon will put them in place before the transfer and will provide evidence of them to the customer on request.

## 9.9 Audit

The customer may audit Quillon's data processing once a year with 30 days notice, to verify compliance with this Clause 9. The audit may be carried out by the customer or by an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations and not a competitor of Quillon. Quillon may satisfy the audit right in whole or in part by providing recent independent certifications or audit reports covering the relevant services. Each party bears its own costs of any audit, and the audit will be conducted during normal business hours in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt Quillon's operations.

## 9.10 Return and deletion of customer data

On termination or expiry of the main agreement, the customer may export its customer contract data during the export window. After the export window ends, Quillon deletes customer data within 90 days, unless the law requires Quillon to retain particular data, in which case Quillon will keep that data confidential and continue to protect it under this Clause 9 until it is deleted.

Quillon will confirm deletion to the customer in writing on request. Backup copies will be overwritten in accordance with Quillon's standard backup rotation and will be deleted within the same period.

## 9.11 Liability and precedence

Each party's liability arising under this Clause 9 is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the main agreement. Nothing in this Clause 9 limits either party's liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

If there is any conflict between this Clause 9 and the main agreement regarding the processing of personal data, this Clause 9 prevails. If a change in data protection law requires amendments to this Clause 9, the parties will negotiate in good faith to make the necessary amendments.
