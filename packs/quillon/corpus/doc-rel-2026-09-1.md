# Release 2026.09.1

## Headline

Release 2026.09.1 is now available to all Quillon customers, effective from 2026-09-22. It contains one fix to the e-signature flow and a set of stability improvements.

## What changed

- Release 2026.09.1 fixed ERR-SIG-308, which appeared on signing links that had not yet expired. Signers who received a valid link can now complete their signature without seeing this error.
- Signing links that are still within their validity period now open correctly on the first attempt, so senders no longer need to resend a contract because of this error.
- Minor stability updates were made to the search index across signed contracts, which reduces the chance of delayed results when searching large contract libraries.

## Who is affected

Customers who use e-signature on contracts sent to external parties are affected. No action is required on your side, and no changes are needed to existing templates, approval workflows or obligation tracking settings.
