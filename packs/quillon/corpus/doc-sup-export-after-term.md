# Getting your data after your subscription ends

This guide explains how to export your contracts, clause data and related records from Quillon after your subscription ends. Follow the steps in order so that nothing is lost before access closes.

## Before you start

Check who in your organization holds the account owner role, since only account owners can request a full export. Gather the list of workspaces you want to export, including any archived ones.

## Steps to export your data

1. Sign in to Quillon with the account owner credentials.
2. Open the Settings menu and select Data and exports.
3. Choose the workspaces to include, then select Create full export.
4. Wait for the export job to finish. Large libraries of signed contracts can take longer, and you will receive an email when the file is ready.
5. Download the export archive and store it in a secure location your team controls.
6. Open a sample of files to confirm that contracts, attachments and metadata are complete.
7. Review your export archive against your internal records before access closes.

## Timing

Your data stays exportable for 30 days after termination. Start the export process well before that window closes, because access ends at the end of the period and exports cannot be restored afterward.

## Getting help

If an export job fails or a file looks incomplete, contact Quillon support through the Help menu and include the export job identifier shown on the Data and exports page.
