# Quillon Onboarding Guide

## Purpose of This Guide

This guide explains how Quillon onboards new customers onto our contract-management platform. It is written for customer administrators, legal operations leads, and corporate finance teams who are preparing to move their contract work into Quillon. It describes the steps, the roles involved, and what to expect week by week. It applies to all new accounts from law firms and corporate finance teams.

## Onboarding Timeline at a Glance

A standard onboarding takes about 15 business days from signed order form to go-live. Timing can vary depending on the number of users, the volume of existing contracts to import, and the number of integrations requested. Your Quillon onboarding lead will confirm a firm schedule once the order form is signed.

Every new account gets a kickoff call in the first week. The kickoff call sets the project plan, confirms the account owner, and identifies the people who will take part in each phase.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Successful onboarding depends on a small group of people working together. We recommend naming the following roles before the kickoff call:

1. **Executive sponsor:** approves the rollout, resolves cross-team priorities, and signs off on go-live.
2. **Account administrator:** owns user provisioning, permissions, and day-to-day configuration decisions.
3. **Template owner:** maintains the drafting templates and clause library, and approves changes to standard language.
4. **Approvers:** the people who review and approve contracts within workflows, such as legal reviewers or finance controllers.
5. **Quillon onboarding lead:** plans the project, runs working sessions, and tracks open items to completion.

## Numbered Onboarding Steps

Follow these steps in order. Each step lists the outcome you should expect before moving on.

1. **Sign the order form.** Your onboarding clock starts when the order form is fully executed. Quillon will send a welcome message and assign an onboarding lead.
2. **Attend the kickoff call.** This happens in the first week. Bring your list of named roles, your target go-live date, and any known deadlines for live contracts.
3. **Confirm your scope.** Document which practice groups or finance teams will use the platform first, and which will follow in a later phase.
4. **Provision users and permissions.** The account administrator creates user accounts, assigns roles, and sets approval groups. Quillon recommends a small pilot group before wide rollout.
5. **Build your template library.** The template owner selects existing agreements to convert into drafting templates. Quillon can help map merge fields and standard sections.
6. **Load your clause library.** Approved clauses, fallback positions, and playbook notes are entered so drafters can insert them consistently. Review each clause for accuracy before publishing.
7. **Configure approval workflows.** Set who must approve drafts, redlines, and final versions, and in what order. Test each route with sample documents.
8. **Set up e-signature.** Confirm signing order, signer details, and notification settings. Run at least one test envelope end to end.
9. **Import existing contracts.** Upload signed agreements in batches. Quillon extracts key dates and parties where possible, and the account team reviews the results with you.
10. **Define obligation tracking.** Identify the obligations you want to monitor, such as reporting deadlines, payment terms, and notice periods. Assign owners for each.
11. **Set renewal alerts.** Choose how far in advance you want alerts for expiring or auto-renewing contracts, and who receives them.
12. **Test search.** Check that users can find signed contracts by party, date, clause type, and status. Correct any metadata gaps found during testing.
13. **Run user training.** Quillon provides role-based sessions for drafters, approvers, signers, and administrators. Recordings are available for later reference.
14. **Complete a go-live review.** The onboarding lead and executive sponsor confirm readiness, open issues, and support contacts before the platform goes live.
15. **Go live and hand over.** After go-live, your account moves to standard support. Your onboarding lead will schedule a short check-in to confirm adoption and capture feedback.

## Week-by-Week Plan

**Week 1: Foundation.** Hold the kickoff call, confirm roles, agree on scope, and submit the list of users. Begin gathering templates and existing agreements for import.

**Week 2: Configuration.** Provision users, build the first set of templates, load the clause library, and draft approval workflows. Start the first batch of contract imports.

**Week 3: Integration and testing.** Configure e-signature, complete obligation and renewal settings, test search, and run pilot drafting and approval cycles with the pilot group.

**Week 4: Readiness and go-live.** Finish training, resolve open issues, complete the go-live review, and move to live operation. The onboarding lead confirms the handover to standard support.

Note that the week-by-week plan reflects the standard path. Your onboarding lead may adjust sequencing to fit your priorities.

## Data Migration Guidance

Before importing existing contracts, clean up file names, confirm that signed versions are the final copies, and group agreements by type. Remove duplicates where possible. Keep a record of the source system and the date of export so that you can trace any discrepancies later.

## Support During Onboarding

Your onboarding lead is your main point of contact until go-live. Questions about configuration, permissions, or imports should go to that lead first. Technical issues that block progress are escalated to Quillon support, and your lead keeps you informed of the status.

## Changes to This Guide

Quillon may update this guide from time to time to reflect improvements to the onboarding process. The version in effect is the one published on our website, and the current version applies to all onboarding projects started on or after its effective date.
