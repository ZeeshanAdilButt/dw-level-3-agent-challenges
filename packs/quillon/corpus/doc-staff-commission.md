# Sales commission plan

## Purpose and scope

This guide summarises how commission is calculated for Quillon sales representatives selling contract-management software to law firms and corporate finance teams. It is an internal reference for staff only and must not be shared with customers or external parties. It takes effect from 2026-08-01 and should be read alongside the current quota letter issued to each representative.

## Base commission

Commission is paid on the first-year contract value of each closed deal. The base commission rate is 8% of first-year contract value, applied to new business and to qualifying expansion deals once they are signed and countersigned. Deals that are cancelled before the first invoice is settled are reversed in full, so representatives should confirm that the customer is committed before expecting payment.

## Accelerator

Quota attainment above the threshold earns a higher rate. Sales reps earn a 1.5 times accelerator above 110% of quota, meaning commission on the portion of bookings beyond that level is multiplied by 1.5x. The accelerator applies only to deals that meet the standard approval and e-signature requirements described in the operations handbook.

## Payment and adjustments

Commission is calculated monthly from the bookings report and paid in the following pay cycle. Where a deal is reclassified, split between representatives, or subject to a pricing exception, Sales Operations will issue a written adjustment note. Disputes should be raised with the Sales Operations lead within the cycle in which the payment appears, so they can be reviewed before the next run.

## Governance

This plan is a guide and may be amended by the Revenue Leadership team with notice to affected staff. Questions about interpretation should go to Sales Operations rather than being resolved informally between colleagues.
