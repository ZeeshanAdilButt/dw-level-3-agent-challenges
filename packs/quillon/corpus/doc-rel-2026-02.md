# Release 2026.02

## Headline

Release 2026.02 adds the obligation tracking dashboard, giving legal and finance teams a single view of the duties that signed contracts place on their organization.

## What is new

The obligation tracking dashboard pulls commitments such as delivery dates, reporting requirements, payment milestones, and notice periods from executed agreements into one list. Each item links back to the clause it came from, so reviewers can check the source without leaving the dashboard.

- Obligations are grouped by owner, counterparty, and status, so a team can see what is open, what is due soon, and what has been completed.
- Items that approach their deadline are flagged on the dashboard and included in the existing renewal alert digest, so nothing depends on someone remembering to check a spreadsheet.
- Users can mark an obligation as complete, add a note, or reassign it to another team member. Each change is recorded in the contract history.

The dashboard works alongside the existing search across signed contracts. A user who finds an agreement through search can open its obligations directly, and a user viewing the dashboard can jump to the full contract text.

## Other changes in this release

- Search results now show the obligation count for each signed contract, making it easier to spot agreements that need attention.
- Clause library entries can be tagged with the obligation types they usually create, which helps keep new drafts consistent with the duties your team already tracks.
- Several approval workflow messages were reworded to be clearer about who must act next.

## Who is affected

Every workspace with contract records on the platform receives the obligation tracking dashboard after this release. Administrators can control which roles see the dashboard from the permissions settings, and no existing contract data needs to be migrated or re-uploaded before use.

## Availability and support

Release 2026.02 is effective from 2026-02-17. Customers who want a walkthrough of the dashboard can request one through their account contact, and the support center has updated articles covering setup, permissions, and common questions about how obligations are extracted.
