# Clause 4: Fees and payment terms

## Scope

This clause sets out how fees for the Quillon contract-management software service are invoiced, paid, and enforced. It applies to every order form and subscription agreement that refers to this clause, from the effective date of 2026-07-01.

## 4.1 Invoicing

1. Quillon issues invoices for the subscription fees set out in the applicable order form.
2. Annual fees are invoiced in full at the start of each term. The customer receives one invoice for the full annual amount, not a series of smaller instalments, unless the order form expressly provides otherwise.
3. Each invoice states the invoice date, the amount due, the billing period it covers, and the payment details.

## 4.2 Payment

1. Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Net 30 terms apply to every invoice unless a written agreement states a different period.
2. Payment is made in the currency stated on the invoice, by the method described in the order form.
3. The customer is responsible for any bank charges that it incurs in sending payment.

## 4.3 Taxes

1. All fees exclude taxes. Where a sales tax, value added tax, goods and services tax, or similar indirect tax applies to the services, that tax is added to the invoice at the applicable rate.
2. The customer pays any such tax in addition to the fees, and Quillon shows the tax as a separate line on the invoice.
3. If the customer is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, the customer provides evidence of that withholding so that Quillon can account for it correctly.

## 4.4 Late payment

1. Overdue invoices accrue interest of 1.5% per month on the unpaid balance, calculated from the due date until the date of full payment.
2. Interest accrues daily and is charged on the outstanding amount only, not on interest previously charged.
3. Quillon may send a reminder notice when an invoice becomes overdue. The reminder does not waive any right to interest.

## 4.5 Suspension for non-payment

1. Quillon may suspend the customer's access to the service when an invoice is more than 45 days overdue.
2. Before suspending access, Quillon gives the customer written notice that access will be suspended unless the overdue amount is paid.
3. Suspension does not delete customer data. Access is restored promptly once the overdue amount, including any accrued interest, has been paid in full.
4. Quillon is not liable for any loss arising from a suspension made in accordance with this clause.

## 4.6 Disputed invoices

1. If the customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it notifies Quillon in writing before the due date, identifying the invoice and the reason for the dispute.
2. The parties then work together to resolve the dispute. The undisputed portion of the invoice remains payable on the due date.
3. A disputed amount does not accrue interest while the dispute is being resolved in good faith, provided the customer has paid the undisputed portion.

## 4.7 Payment timings at a glance

The table below summarises the key timings in this clause. The numeric terms apply in the same way to all customers.

| Invoice stage | Timing | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice issued | Invoice date | Annual fees invoiced in full at the start of the term |
| Payment due | 30 days after invoice date | Invoice becomes overdue if unpaid |
| Late interest | 1.5% per month on overdue balance | Accrues daily from the due date |
| Suspension | More than 45 days overdue | Quillon may suspend access after written notice |

## 4.8 Changes to fees

1. Fees are fixed for the initial term stated in the order form.
2. Any change to fees at renewal is communicated to the customer in writing before the renewal date, in line with the notice period stated in the order form.
