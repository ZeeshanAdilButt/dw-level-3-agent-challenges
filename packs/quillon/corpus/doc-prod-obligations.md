# Obligation tracking

Obligation tracking in Quillon turns the promises inside a signed contract into dated tasks that your team can act on. Once a contract is signed, Quillon reads its terms and lists each recurring or one-time duty, such as a delivery milestone, a reporting requirement, an insurance certificate, or a payment schedule. Each obligation is linked back to the clause it came from, so anyone reviewing the task can see the exact wording.

## How reminders work

Obligation reminders default to 14 days before the due date, so the responsible person has time to prepare before the deadline arrives. Administrators can change the lead time for their workspace, and individual owners can add a second reminder if they want an earlier nudge. Reminders go to the assigned owner and, optionally, to a shared inbox for the matter or the finance team.

## Example

A finance team manages a supplier agreement that requires an annual audit report. Quillon creates an obligation for the report with the clause reference attached. Fourteen days before the due date, the assigned analyst receives a reminder. She marks the task as in progress, uploads the draft, and the record shows who completed it and when, which makes the next review cycle straightforward.

## Tracking and reporting

Every obligation has a status: open, in progress, complete, or waived with a recorded reason. Managing partners and finance leads can filter the dashboard by client, counterparty, owner, or due month. Completed obligations stay in the contract history, so auditors and account managers can confirm what was done and when without searching email threads.
