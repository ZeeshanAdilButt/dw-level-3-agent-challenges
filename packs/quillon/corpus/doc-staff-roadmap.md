# Unreleased roadmap 2027

## Purpose and status

This note sets out the working product and pricing plan for Quillon's contract-management platform for the 2027 calendar year. It is an internal guide for staff who plan, sell, support, or build the product. It is not for customers, partners, or prospects, and nothing in it should be quoted externally or shared outside the company. Plans described here are intentions, not commitments, and dates may move as engineering and market conditions change. The guide takes effect on 1 August 2026 and replaces any earlier internal roadmap notes on the same topics.

## Product direction

Quillon sells contract-management software to law firms and corporate finance teams. The core workflow covers drafting from templates, a shared clause library, approval routing, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. For 2027 the priority is to make the approval step faster for people who are rarely at a desk, and to help reviewers spend less time on routine markup.

The mobile work is the first of these priorities. A native mobile approvals app is planned for the first quarter of 2027. The scope is deliberately narrow: reviewing a contract, approving or rejecting with a reason, and seeing what is waiting on the user. Editing, drafting, and administration stay on the web for the first release. Product and design should treat the approvals flow as the main quality bar, since it is the reason most users will install the app.

AI-assisted redlining is the second priority. It is planned for Pro and Enterprise in the second quarter of 2027. Suggested changes will be proposed against the clause library and the customer's own playbook, and every suggestion will require a human to accept it. Legal and security review of the feature is a gate, not a formality, and the team should not announce a date until that review has passed.

## Pricing

A 6% list price increase is planned for 1 January 2027. The increase applies to new list prices only. Existing customers on active terms will keep their current rates until their renewal date, and the sales operations team will confirm the renewal treatment for each segment before the change is communicated. Account executives should not discuss the increase with any customer before the internal communication plan is approved, and should not offer early renewal pricing as a way of avoiding it without written approval from finance.

Packaging for the mobile app and AI redlining is still being decided. Until that decision is recorded, staff should not describe either feature as included in a specific plan.

## Quarter-by-quarter plan

The detailed quarter-by-quarter plan lives in the planning tracker and is marked not for customers. It lists engineering milestones, beta cohorts, and the order of release notes. Only the product lead and the release manager should edit it. Items move between quarters through the monthly planning review, and any change that affects a date in this guide must be recorded here as well.

## Ownership and questions

Product owns the roadmap and this guide. Finance owns pricing decisions and the renewal treatment. Legal owns the review gate for AI features. Questions about this document should go to the product lead, and requests to change a date or price should be raised before the monthly planning review, not after.
