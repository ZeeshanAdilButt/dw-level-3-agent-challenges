# Release 2026.10.1

## Headline

Release 2026.10.1 extends guest reviewer links from 14 days to 21 days, giving external reviewers more time to respond to contract drafts and approval requests.

## What changed

- Guest reviewer links now remain active for 21 days after they are sent, up from the previous 14 days.
- Reviewers who open a link within that window can comment, mark up clauses and approve or decline changes without creating a Quillon account.
- Link owners can still revoke a guest link at any time from the approvals panel, and revoked links stop working immediately.

## Who is affected

Account administrators and contract owners who share drafts with outside counsel, vendors or other external parties will see the longer link window automatically. No action is required, though teams may wish to review any existing guest-link settings in their approval workflows.
