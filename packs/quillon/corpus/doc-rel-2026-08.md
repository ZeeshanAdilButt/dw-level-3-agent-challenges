# Release 2026.08

## Headline

Release 2026.08 adds the sandbox add-on, giving legal and finance teams a safe space to test templates, clause changes and approval routing before they reach live contracts.

## What changed

The sandbox add-on is a separate environment that mirrors your production workspace. Administrators can copy a set of templates and clauses into the sandbox, edit them, run them through the approval workflow, and compare the output against the live version before anything is published. Nothing created in the sandbox is visible to counterparties or to users working in production.

Alongside the sandbox, this release includes these updates:

- Clause library changes can be previewed side by side, so reviewers can see exactly which contracts a revised clause would affect.
- Approval steps now show the reason an item was routed to a particular reviewer, which shortens questions during review.
- Search across signed contracts returns clearer snippets around matched obligation language.

## Who is affected

Administrators and template owners at firms and finance teams who hold the sandbox add-on will see the new environment after the release goes live. Users without the add-on will see the approval and search updates only. Existing templates, signed contracts and obligation tracking settings are not changed by this release, and no action is needed to keep current workflows running.

## Getting started

To request the sandbox add-on, contact your account representative or your workspace administrator. Once enabled, the sandbox appears in the administration menu under environments. Our help center contains step-by-step guides for copying templates, comparing versions and promoting a tested change to production.
