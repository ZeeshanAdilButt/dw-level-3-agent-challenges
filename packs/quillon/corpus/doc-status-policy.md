# Service Status and Incident Updates

## Purpose

This guide explains how Quillon communicates service availability and incidents to customers, including law firms and corporate finance teams that rely on our contract-management platform for drafting, approvals, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts.

## Where to Find Live Status

Live service status is published at status.quillon.example. The page shows whether each part of the platform is operating normally, degraded, or unavailable, along with the time each status was last confirmed. Customers can subscribe to notifications on the status page to receive messages by email when the status of a service changes.

## How Incidents Are Communicated

When Quillon detects an event that affects access, performance, or data handling, the incident team opens an entry on the status page and identifies the affected functions, such as drafting from templates, the clause library, approvals, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, or search. During an incident, status updates are posted at least every 30 minutes until service is restored or the incident is closed.

Each update states what is known, what is being done, and the next expected update time. Updates are written in plain language and avoid speculation about causes until the cause has been confirmed.

## After an Incident

Once service has been restored, Quillon publishes a closing update summarizing the impact and the actions taken. For incidents with material impact, Quillon also publishes a written review describing the root cause, the corrective steps, and the measures planned to reduce the chance of recurrence.

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should check the status page before escalating a suspected platform issue, and should confirm that their own network and browser settings are not the source of a problem. Questions that cannot be answered from the status page can be submitted through the support channel listed in the customer agreement.
