# Stop a renewal or cancel

## Overview

This guide explains how an admin can stop an upcoming renewal or cancel a Quillon subscription. Read the steps in order, because some options must be completed before the renewal date. Effective from 2026-07-15.

## Before you start

Only workspace admins can give renewal notice or change the subscription. Confirm that you hold the admin role in your workspace before continuing.

## Steps to stop a renewal or cancel

1. Sign in to Quillon with your admin account.
2. Open the account menu and select Settings, then choose Billing.
3. Review the subscription details, including the renewal date shown for your workspace.
4. To give renewal notice, select Stop renewal and confirm your choice in the dialog.
5. If the Stop renewal option is not available, email renewals@quillon.example from an admin address. Include your workspace name and the words "stop renewal."
6. Wait for the confirmation message. It states that the subscription will end at the close of the current term.
7. Export any contracts, reports, or audit logs you need before the access end date, since workspace data is not available after that date.
8. Check your inbox for a final receipt and a summary of the end date.

## Why this matters

Renewal notice must be received before the renewal date to take effect. A notice sent late may not stop the next billing period. Exporting your data before the end date keeps your signed contracts, templates, and obligation records available for your records.

## Tip

Set a reminder in your calendar for two weeks before the renewal date so there is time to review the subscription and send notice without rushing.
