# Release 2026.10

## Headline

Release 2026.10 adds WebAuthn security keys as a supported option for two-step verification, giving users a stronger way to confirm their identity when they sign in to Quillon.

## What changed

- WebAuthn security keys can now be registered as a second step when signing in, alongside the existing verification methods. Users can add a key from their account security settings.
- Administrators can require a second verification step for all members of their workspace, and the WebAuthn option is available to meet that requirement.
- Users who already use an authenticator app or text-message codes keep their current setup. Adding a WebAuthn key is optional unless an administrator has made two-step verification mandatory for the workspace.

## Other improvements

Search across signed contracts now returns results faster for workspaces with large contract libraries. Renewal alert emails also show the notice deadline next to each contract name, so owners can act without opening the record.

## Who is affected

This release affects every Quillon workspace. Administrators should review their two-step verification policy, and individual users can register a WebAuthn security key from their account settings at any time after the release takes effect on 2026-10-13.
