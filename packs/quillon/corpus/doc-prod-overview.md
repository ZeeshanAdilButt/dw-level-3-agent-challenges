# Quillon overview

## What Quillon is

Quillon is contract-management software (SaaS) for law firms and corporate finance teams. Quillon was founded in 2017, and the product covers the full life of a contract, from first draft to renewal. Teams work in one place instead of moving files between email, shared drives and signing tools.

## What the product does

Drafting starts from approved templates, so new agreements begin from language your organization has already reviewed. A clause library lets users pull in standard wording for common terms, which keeps drafts consistent across matters and reduces time spent rewriting the same provisions.

Approvals route each draft to the right reviewers in a set order, and every decision is recorded. When a document is ready, e-signature collects signatures from all parties without printing or scanning.

After signing, obligation tracking lists the dates, deliverables and commitments that each contract creates, so responsible people know what is due. Renewal alerts warn the team before a contract renews or lapses, giving time to negotiate or give notice. Search covers all signed contracts, so a user can find every agreement that contains a given term or counterparty.

## Example

A corporate finance team reviewing a supplier agreement drafts it from a template, pulls the standard payment clause from the library, and routes it to legal and procurement for approval. After signature, Quillon records the payment and reporting obligations and sends an alert ahead of the renewal date, so the team can decide whether to renew before the contract rolls over.

## Who it is for

Quillon suits law firms managing client matters and corporate teams managing supplier, customer and employment agreements. Access to documents and approval history can be restricted by role, so each user sees only what their work requires.
