# Clause 10: Service levels (2025)

## Purpose

This clause sets out the service level commitments that apply to the Quillon contract-management platform for agreements effective from 2025-03-01. It forms part of the standard terms for customers using drafting, clause library, approval, e-signature, obligation tracking, renewal alert and search features.

## Availability commitment

Under the 2025 wording, Quillon committed to 99.5% monthly uptime for the hosted service, measured across each calendar month and excluding scheduled maintenance notified in advance.

## Key figures

| Item | Commitment | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly uptime | 99.5% | Hosted contract-management service |

## Measurement and reporting

Uptime is calculated as the proportion of time in a calendar month during which the hosted service was available to customers, expressed as a percentage. Quillon will make availability information available to customers on request.

## Exclusions

Unavailability caused by customer systems, third-party networks, force majeure events, or scheduled maintenance notified in advance does not count against the commitment.

## Status of this wording

This is the 2025 wording of Clause 10. It remains in effect until it is replaced from 2026-07-01, after which the replacement wording will apply to new and renewing agreements.
