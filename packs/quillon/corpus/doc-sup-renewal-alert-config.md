# Configure renewal alerts

This guide explains how to set up renewal alerts in Quillon so your team is notified before a contract renews. It is written for account administrators and contract owners.

## Before you begin

You need administrator access or the contract owner role for each contract you want to monitor. Renewal alerts apply to signed contracts that include a term end date or an auto-renewal clause.

## Set up a renewal alert

1. Open the contract from the Contracts list or search across signed contracts.
2. Select the Obligations tab, then choose Renewal.
3. Confirm the term end date and the notice period stated in the contract.
4. Set the alert lead time. Renewal alert lead times can be set from 7 to 180 days before the term end date.
5. Add one or more recipients, such as the contract owner, the finance approver, or a shared legal mailbox.
6. Choose the delivery method: email, in-app notification, or both.
7. Select Save alert. Quillon confirms the schedule on the contract summary page.
8. Repeat steps 1 to 7 for any other contract that needs a renewal reminder.

## Review and update alerts

1. Open the Renewal dashboard from the main navigation.
2. Filter by term end date, owner, or status.
3. Select an alert to edit the lead time, recipients, or delivery method.
4. Select Save changes. Edits take effect immediately for future notifications.

## Tip

Set the lead time to at least the notice period written in the contract, plus a few extra days for internal review. This gives your team time to decide whether to renew, renegotiate, or let the agreement lapse.
