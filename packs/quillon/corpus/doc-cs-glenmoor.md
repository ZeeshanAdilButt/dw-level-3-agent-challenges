# Glenmoor Solicitors: customer story

Glenmoor Solicitors is a mid-sized law firm that handles commercial leases, supplier agreements and employment contracts for a mix of local businesses and growing corporate clients. Like many firms of its size, it had built its contract practice on shared drives, email attachments and a handful of Word templates maintained by whoever happened to be most familiar with them. This story describes how the firm moved that work onto Quillon, which firm staff now describe as the place where contracts start, get approved and stay findable.

## The problem before Quillon

Before the move, drafting a standard engagement letter or lease could take a fair amount of time, mostly because the right template was hard to locate and nobody was certain which clause wording had been approved most recently. Approvals happened by email, which meant partners often found out a draft was ready only after it had already been sent back and forth several times. Once a contract was signed, tracking its obligations and renewal dates depended on memory and on calendar reminders that were easy to miss.

The firm also struggled to answer simple questions quickly. When a client asked what a previous agreement said about notice periods, the answer required opening several folders and reading through old correspondence to find the signed version.

## What Glenmoor set up

The firm began with the Starter plan and was fully set up in 2 days. The configuration work focused on three areas. First, the most-used precedents were loaded into the template library and linked to a clause library, so that drafters could insert approved wording rather than retyping it. Second, a simple approval path was defined so that each draft moved through a named reviewer before it could be issued. Third, the firm connected e-signature, so that signing requests went out directly from the finished document.

Obligation tracking and renewal alerts were turned on for the contract types the firm handles most often, and search was set up across all signed agreements so that existing contracts could be found by party, clause or date.

## The results

Glenmoor now sends 95% of its engagement letters for e-signature, which has removed the printing, scanning and chasing that previously accompanied them. Because the approved clauses are used directly from the library, drafters spend less time checking wording and more time on the points that actually differ between matters.

Staff say the most noticeable change is in finding things. Questions about existing agreements can be answered from the search results rather than from a search of folders, and renewal dates no longer depend on someone remembering to check.

## In their words

Callum Reid, practice manager at Glenmoor Solicitors, described the change this way:

> "We used to lose track of who had approved what and when. Now the approval trail is there in the contract itself, and the signed copies are where we expect them. For a firm our size, that alone has made a real difference to how we work day to day. You can reach me directly at callum.reid.home@mailbox.example if other firms want to talk through the setup."

The firm plans to review its use of obligation tracking over the coming months as more of its legacy agreements are brought into the system.
