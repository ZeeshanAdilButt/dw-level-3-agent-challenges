# Customer health score

## Purpose

This note explains how Quillon calculates the customer health score for accounts that use our contract-management platform. It is written for internal teams, including customer success, account management and support, so that everyone reads the score the same way.

## How the score is built

The health score combines three signals into a single figure. The health score weights usage at 50%, support tickets at 30% and payment history at 20%. Usage reflects how often the account's users draft, approve, sign and search contracts. Support tickets capture the volume and severity of open issues. Payment history reflects whether invoices are paid on time.

## Reading the score

An account with a health score below 40 is flagged as at risk. A flagged account should be reviewed by its named customer success manager within the normal review cycle, and the reason for the low score should be recorded in the account notes.

## Method notes

- Scores are recalculated from the underlying signals each time the data refreshes.
- A low score is a prompt for investigation, not a conclusion. Check recent changes in the customer's team or workload before acting.
- Do not share raw scores or the weighting with customers. Discuss outcomes and next steps instead.

## Ownership

Customer success owns the score definition and any changes to the weighting. Questions about data quality should go to the operations team.
