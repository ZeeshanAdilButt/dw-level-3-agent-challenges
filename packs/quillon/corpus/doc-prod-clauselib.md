# Clause library

**Document type:** Product guide
**Audience:** Public
**Effective from:** 2026-07-01

## What the clause library does

The clause library is a shared store of approved contract language inside Quillon. Legal and finance teams save standard clauses here once, then pull them into new agreements from the drafting screen instead of retyping or copying from old files. Each clause keeps its approved wording, its owner, and its history of changes, so the version a team uses is always the version it signed off on.

## Tagging clauses

Each clause in the library can carry up to 10 tags. Tags describe the clause in terms your team already uses, such as the contract type, jurisdiction, risk level, or business unit. A clause on limitation of liability might carry tags for "software license," "UK," and "high value." When someone searches the library or filters templates, tags make the right clause easy to find even when the wording differs from one document to the next.

## An example in practice

A corporate finance team preparing a set of supplier agreements filters the library by the tags "supplier" and "payment terms." They find three approved payment clauses, compare them side by side, and insert the one that matches the deal. The approval workflow then routes the drafted agreement to the right reviewer, and the chosen clause stays linked to the library so any later update can be traced back to the contracts that used it.

## Keeping the library current

Clause owners review their clauses on a schedule set by the team, and any edit creates a new version without altering agreements that were already signed. Teams can retire outdated clauses so they no longer appear in search results, while keeping them on record for audit purposes.
