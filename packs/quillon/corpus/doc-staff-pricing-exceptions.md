# Pricing exceptions log

## Purpose and scope

This log records every pricing exception granted to a customer outside the standard price list. It is an internal guide for the revenue operations, sales leadership and finance teams. It must not be shared with customers, partners or anyone outside Quillon. Each entry should show who granted the exception, when, and on what basis, so that renewals, audits and margin reviews can be handled without reconstructing decisions from email.

## How to record an exception

Every exception needs four things before it is entered: the customer's legal name as it appears on the signed order form, a plain description of the exception, the name of the approver with the authority to grant it, and the date of approval. Where an exception is time-limited, the end date belongs in the description. Where the exception was generated automatically by the quote tool, say so in the approver field, because automatic approvals are reviewed separately each quarter.

## Review cadence

Revenue operations reviews this log at the start of each quarter. Entries without a matching billing record are escalated to the account owner. Automatic approvals are sampled for accuracy, and any exception that has passed its end date without being closed is removed from the active list and noted in the quarterly summary. Questions about an entry should go to the revenue operations lead, not to the account team directly.
