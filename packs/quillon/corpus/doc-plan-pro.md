# Pro plan

## Overview

The Pro plan (PLN-PRO-50) is Quillon's plan for mid-size law firms and corporate finance teams that need contract management across drafting, review, signature, and tracking. It brings together template-based drafting, a shared clause library, multi-step approvals, electronic signature, obligation tracking, renewal alerts, and search across signed contracts. This page describes what the plan includes, how it is priced, and the limits that apply. It is effective from 2026-07-01.

## Plan code and eligibility

The Pro plan is identified by the code PLN-PRO-50 on invoices, order forms, and account records. It is designed for organizations with several people who draft, approve, or manage contracts. Accounts are sold per seat, where a seat is a named user with access to the workspace.

## Pricing

On monthly billing, the Pro plan costs $38 per seat per month. When billed annually, the Pro plan costs $32 per seat per month. Taxes and any applicable fees are shown at checkout or on the order form. Pricing for other plans is listed separately on the pricing page.

## Seat requirements

The Pro plan requires a minimum of 10 seats at signup. Accounts may grow over time as additional teams adopt the platform. The standard seat cap of the Pro plan (PLN-PRO-50) is 50 seats. Organizations that need more seats than the standard cap should contact Quillon sales to discuss options under a different plan arrangement.

The minimum term for the Pro plan is not stated on this page. Please see the linked order terms for the minimum term and renewal conditions that apply to your agreement.

## Storage

Each Pro plan account includes 500 GB of storage per account. Storage is shared across all seats on the account and covers uploaded source documents, signed contracts, attachments, and exported files. Administrators can view current storage use from the account settings area.

## Drafting and the clause library

Users draft new contracts from templates, which can be configured by the account administrator to match the firm's or department's standard forms. Templates can be filled with structured fields so that routine agreements are produced consistently.

The Pro plan clause library holds up to 5,000 clauses. Teams can store approved language, fallback positions, and jurisdiction-specific wording, then insert or compare clauses while drafting. Clause entries can be tagged and searched so that the right language is easy to find.

## Approvals

Approval chains on the Pro plan can have up to 8 steps. Each step can route the document to a person or a group, and reviewers can accept changes, request revisions, or reject a draft with comments. Approval history is recorded against the document so that the path to signature can be reviewed later.

## Electronic signature

The Pro plan includes electronic signature for contracts that are ready for execution. Signers receive a request, review the document, and sign electronically. Completed contracts are stored with their signature records and become searchable in the workspace.

## Obligation tracking and renewal alerts

Users can record obligations such as payment dates, reporting duties, notice periods, and deliverables against the relevant contract. Obligations appear in a tracking view with their owners and status. Renewal alerts notify the responsible users ahead of renewal and termination dates, so that decisions about renewal, renegotiation, or exit are made in time.

## Search across signed contracts

Signed contracts are indexed for search across the account. Users can search by party, clause type, obligation, or key term, and filter results by document status and owner. This makes it practical to answer questions such as which agreements contain a particular indemnity wording or which contracts are up for renewal.

## Support

Customers on the Pro plan receive a first support response within 4 business hours. Support requests can be submitted through the in-product help menu or by email, and priority is set according to the severity of the issue as described in the support policy.

## Integrations and access controls

SAML single sign-on is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans, allowing organizations to manage workspace access through their existing identity provider. The REST API is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans, allowing engineering teams to read and write contract data from internal systems. Administrators control which users can access which templates, clauses, and workspaces.
