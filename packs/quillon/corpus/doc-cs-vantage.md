# Vantage Row Advisors: customer story

*Published by Quillon, effective 2026-04-15*

## About the customer

Vantage Row Advisors is an advisory firm whose finance team manages a steady flow of customer order forms, supplier agreements and renewal terms. The team is small, but the volume of paperwork that passes through it is large, and every agreement needs to be accurate before it reaches a signature. The team runs on the Starter plan of Quillon's contract-management software.

## The problem before Quillon

Before adopting Quillon, the finance team built each order form by copying an older document, editing the fields by hand and routing the result through email for review. Approvals depended on who happened to be reachable that day. A typical agreement took 6 days to move from the first quote to a completed signature, and much of that time was spent waiting for a reply rather than doing work. Renewal dates lived in a spreadsheet that was often out of date, so the team sometimes discovered an expiring term only after it had passed.

The team also found it hard to answer simple questions about past agreements. Finding the signed version of a contract, or confirming which clause had been agreed, could take longer than the drafting itself.

## What the team set up

Vantage Row Advisors started by moving its order forms into Quillon's template library, so that standard fields and wording were filled in consistently. The team then built a short clause library covering the terms it negotiates most often, which meant reviewers could insert approved language rather than rewriting it each time.

Approvals were set up as a fixed routing path, so each order form moved to the right reviewer automatically and no one had to chase a decision by email. Signatures were collected through Quillon's built-in e-signature, which kept the signed copy attached to the record. Finally, the team turned on obligation tracking and renewal alerts, so that deadlines and expiry dates appeared in the system without anyone maintaining a separate list. Search across signed contracts gave the team a single place to look up any past agreement.

## What changed

The most visible result was speed. Vantage Row Advisors shortened quote-to-signature from 6 days to 2 days, and most of the saving came from removing the waiting time between steps. The team now sends 15 order forms per month, and it handles that volume without the backlog that used to build up at the end of each month.

The team also reports fewer missed renewals, because alerts now arrive before a term ends rather than after. Reviewers spend less time on formatting and more on the terms that actually need judgment.

## What the team says

"We used to lose track of agreements somewhere between the draft and the signature," said the finance operations lead at Vantage Row Advisors. "Now each order form follows the same path, the approvals happen without chasing, and the signed copy is where we expect it. Quillon gave us a process we could actually repeat."

## Looking ahead

Vantage Row Advisors plans to keep using the clause library as its standard terms evolve, and to review its approval routing as the team grows. Quillon will continue to support the team's setup as its order volume changes.
