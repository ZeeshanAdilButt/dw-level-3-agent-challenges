# Quillon Product Brochure 2025

Effective from 1 March 2025

## Contract work, finally organized

Every organization signs more contracts than it can comfortably track. Quillon brings drafting, approval, signature, and follow-up into one place, so that law firms and corporate finance teams spend their time on the terms that matter instead of hunting through folders. This brochure describes what Quillon does, how teams use it day to day, and the commitments we make to our customers.

Quillon is built as software as a service (SaaS). You sign in through a browser, your contracts live in a secure workspace, and our team keeps the platform running and updated. There is nothing to install and no server to maintain.

## Drafting from templates

Start with a template your team already trusts, or build one from scratch. Quillon lets you define standard sections, fill in party details automatically, and lock the wording that should never change. When a deal calls for a variation, the editor shows you exactly which clauses differ from the approved language, so reviewers can focus their attention where it counts.

Our customers often tell us that drafting used to be the slowest part of the process. One in-house counsel at a mid-sized manufacturer put it this way: "We went from rebuilding the same agreement every week to opening a template and adjusting three paragraphs. It changed how my team spends a Tuesday."

## A clause library that grows with you

The clause library stores approved language for indemnities, payment terms, confidentiality, termination, and dozens of other provisions. Each clause carries notes about when to use it, who approved it, and which fallback positions are acceptable. New team members get the house position on day one, and experienced lawyers stop re-answering the same questions.

Libraries can be shared across a firm or limited to a single department. Changes are versioned, so you always know which wording a past contract used.

## Approvals that match how you work

Not every contract needs the same review. Quillon lets you set approval routes based on contract value, counterparty, region, or the clauses that were changed. A low-value renewal can move through a single sign-off, while a high-risk agreement goes to legal, finance, and a senior partner in sequence. Reviewers get reminders, and anyone can see where a document is waiting and why.

## Electronic signature

When a contract is ready, send it for signature directly from Quillon. Signers receive a secure link, complete their fields, and the signed copy is filed automatically with an audit trail showing who signed, when, and from where. Signed documents are stored next to their drafts, so the full history of an agreement is always in one record.

## Obligation tracking

Signing is where the real work begins. Quillon reads the key obligations in each agreement, such as reporting deadlines, delivery milestones, insurance certificates, and payment schedules, and turns them into tasks with owners and due dates. Teams stop discovering missed obligations during an audit or a dispute.

## Renewal alerts

Many contracts renew without anyone noticing. Quillon sends renewal alerts well ahead of each notice date, so your team can renegotiate, let an agreement lapse, or confirm continuation on purpose. Our renewal alerts give you time to act on the notice windows written into your contracts.

Our customers appreciate that renewal decisions now happen on a calendar they control. A finance director at a logistics company described the change: "Our vendor renewals used to surprise us. Now we review the list every month and negotiate before the window closes."

## Search across every signed contract

Find any clause, party, date, or obligation across your entire signed library in seconds. Search understands contract structure, so you can look for every agreement with a particular liability cap or every contract with a specific counterparty, then export the results for a report or a review.

Quillon works best when your existing contracts and templates come along with you. Our onboarding team helps you import agreements, set up approval routes, and build your first clause library. Most teams are drafting and signing inside Quillon within a few weeks.
