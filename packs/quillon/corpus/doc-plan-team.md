# Team plan

## Overview

The Team plan (PLN-TEAM-25) is Quillon's plan for growing teams that draft, approve, sign, and track contracts together. It covers the core workflow of drafting from templates, managing approvals, collecting e-signatures, tracking obligations, and searching across signed contracts. Approvals and obligation tracking arrive at this tier.

## Pricing

The Team plan costs $29 per seat per month on monthly billing. When billed annually, the Team plan costs $25 per seat per month. Pricing applies to each seat in your account.

## Seats

The Team plan requires a minimum of 5 seats. The standard seat cap of the Team plan (PLN-TEAM-25) is 25 seats. Teams that need more seats can talk to our sales team about options outside the standard cap.

## Included capacity

Each Team plan account includes 100 GB of storage. The clause library on the Team plan holds up to 500 clauses, so your team can keep approved language in one place and reuse it across templates.

## Approvals

Approval chains on the Team plan can have up to 3 steps. Each step routes a contract to the people who need to review it before it moves forward or is sent for signature.

## Support

Team plan customers receive a first support response within 1 business day.
