# Copperbeech Care

## Purpose of This Page

This page is written for members of the Copperbeech Care employer scheme who receive their healthcare through Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It explains how the scheme works at the clinic, what is covered, what is not, and what you need to do before and after your appointments. Please read it before you book, particularly if you plan to see the clinic for travel health advice. The information here applies from 1 April 2026 and replaces any earlier guidance issued to scheme members.

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice serving the Port Avery community and surrounding area. We offer family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Copperbeech Care members can access most of these services through the scheme, although some services fall outside the scheme's terms. Where that is the case, this page says so clearly.

We know that employer healthcare schemes can feel complicated, especially when a service sits on the boundary between what is covered and what is not. Our aim is to set out the rules in plain language so that you can plan ahead and avoid surprises at the front desk. If anything in this document is unclear, please ask a member of the reception team before your appointment. They can check your membership status and explain how the rules apply to your particular visit.

## How the Scheme Works at Juniper Lane

When you attend Juniper Lane Clinic as a Copperbeech Care member, the scheme applies to eligible services and the clinic handles the administration with the scheme on your behalf. Our reception team will ask for your Copperbeech Care membership details when you check in, and it is helpful to have your member card or membership information ready. Please bring a photo identity document if you have one, since this helps us match your record correctly and avoids delays.

Eligibility depends on the type of service you receive and whether it is included in the scheme's terms. Standard appointments with a general practitioner or nurse practitioner, requested blood tests, and consultations at our travel clinic are generally within scope. Some services, including certain elective procedures and specific treatments outside the core family medicine offering, may have separate arrangements or may not be covered at all. We will always tell you before a service begins if there is any question about whether it falls within the scheme.

You will be asked to make any payment due at the time of your visit, unless we have agreed otherwise in advance. Payment arrangements vary depending on the service, so do not assume that a visit is free simply because you are a member. Our team will explain the amount due before you are seen where possible, and we will give you an itemised receipt that you can keep for your records or submit elsewhere if needed.

## Telehealth and Repeat Prescriptions

Telehealth calls are available to Copperbeech Care members for appropriate consultations, and these are booked in the same way as in-person appointments. Before a telehealth call, a clinician will assess whether a remote consultation is suitable for your needs. If your symptoms require examination, you will be invited to attend the clinic in person instead. Please make sure you have a reliable phone or video connection and a quiet place to talk before the call begins.

Repeat prescription administration is handled through the clinic's standard processes. Members should request repeat prescriptions with reasonable notice so that the clinic team has time to review the request with the prescribing clinician. Requests made at the last moment may take longer to process. If you are travelling or your medication schedule is changing, please contact the clinic early so that we can plan around your needs.

## Child Health Checks, Women's Health and Physiotherapy

Child health checks, including developmental reviews and routine assessments, are available for members with dependent children registered at the practice. Women's health services cover a range of consultations relating to reproductive and general health, and our clinicians will discuss the options available to you during your appointment. Physiotherapy referrals are assessed by the clinic before treatment begins, and the scope of physiotherapy sessions depends on the nature of the condition and the arrangements agreed for your case. Minor procedures are carried out in our treatment room where clinically appropriate, and we will explain beforehand what the procedure involves and whether it is covered under your scheme.

## Travel Clinic Consultations

Our travel clinic helps patients prepare for trips abroad. A travel clinic consultation includes a review of your medical history, an assessment of the risks at your destination, and advice on precautions such as food and water safety, insect protection, altitude considerations and existing medical conditions that may affect your journey. Your clinician may also discuss the vaccines recommended for your destination and explain the timing needed for them to be effective.

Copperbeech Care covers the travel clinic consultation itself as part of the scheme. This means the advisory appointment, the clinical review and the written guidance you receive during that consultation fall within the scheme's terms, subject to the standard excess described below. We encourage members to book their travel consultation early, ideally several weeks before departure, because some vaccines require a course spread over time and some destinations require proof of vaccination before entry.

Because the consultation can involve a detailed discussion of health risks, please bring a full list of your current medications, any allergies you have, and records of any previous vaccinations you have received. Accurate information allows the clinician to give you advice that is safe and relevant to your circumstances. If you are travelling with children or other family members, you may book separate consultations for each person so that the advice is tailored to their age and health needs.

Many travellers are surprised to learn that the consultation and the vaccine are charged differently. Please read the next section carefully before you book.

## Vaccines for Travel: Important Exclusion

Copperbeech Care does not pay for the travel vaccines themselves. While the travel clinic consultation is covered, the cost of the vaccines recommended during that consultation is not part of the scheme. In practical terms, this means that if you receive a travel vaccine at Juniper Lane Clinic, you will be responsible for the vaccine cost, and the clinic will tell you the amount before the vaccine is administered. Vaccines are not covered under the scheme, even when they are recommended as part of your travel plan.

We recognise that this can be an unwelcome surprise, particularly where several vaccines are needed for a single trip. Our reception team and travel clinicians will give you a clear breakdown of which vaccines are recommended and what each one costs before you commit to any treatment. You are not obliged to have every recommended vaccine on the same day, and your clinician can discuss a schedule that fits your budget and your travel dates where clinically appropriate. Please note that some vaccines must be given within specific windows, so delaying a decision may affect what you can receive in time for departure.

If you are unsure whether a particular vaccine is covered, ask before your appointment rather than after. Our team will confirm the position for you and give you an estimate. We would rather you make an informed decision than find out about a charge at the end of your visit.

## Excess and Payment

Copperbeech Care members pay a flat excess of EUR 15 for eligible covered services. The excess is applied per eligible visit and is payable at the time of the visit, unless the service is one of the excluded items described on this page. The excess is the member's share of the cost, and the scheme meets the remainder of the eligible charge in accordance with the scheme's terms.

Because the excess is flat, it is the same regardless of the length of the consultation or the type of covered service within a single visit. If a visit includes both a covered service and an excluded item, such as a travel consultation followed by a travel vaccine, the excess applies to the covered service, and the excluded item is charged separately at the rate quoted to you by the clinic. Our team will show you the split on your receipt so that you can see exactly what has been claimed and what you have paid.

We bill Copperbeech Care directly for covered services, which means that for eligible visits you will not normally need to pay the full cost of the consultation yourself and then seek reimbursement. You pay the excess at the clinic, and the clinic handles the claim with the scheme. Direct billing is only available for services that fall within the scheme's terms, so excluded items such as travel vaccines must be paid for at the time they are provided.

## Member Numbers and Identification

Every Copperbeech Care member has a unique membership number, which is used to verify your eligibility and to link your visits to the scheme. A Copperbeech Care member number starts with CB, followed by the remaining characters shown on your membership card or scheme correspondence. Please check that your number begins with CB before you give it to our team. If the number does not begin with CB, or if you are unsure whether you hold an active membership, please contact your employer's benefits team or the scheme administrator before booking.

When you call the clinic or book online, give your full member number so that we can confirm your details quickly. Never share your membership number with people who are not involved in your care, and do not use another person's membership number. Using a number that does not belong to you may result in the visit being charged in full and may cause problems for the scheme and for the member concerned.

## Changes, Cancellations and Questions

Please let us know as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change an appointment, particularly for a travel consultation that is linked to a departure date. Travel consultations are often booked in limited slots, and early notice helps us offer the time to another patient. If your employment changes or your membership status alters during the year, please inform the clinic so that your record can be updated before your next visit.

For any questions about this page, contact the Juniper Lane Clinic reception team during opening hours. Our staff can check your membership, explain the excess, confirm whether a service is covered and give you an estimate for vaccines or other excluded items. We are committed to making the scheme easy to use and to making sure that you understand what you are paying for before you receive care.
