# Respect at the clinic

Effective from 2026-03-01

## Purpose

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice in Port Avery. We provide family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. This policy sets out how we expect patients, carers and visitors to behave towards our staff and each other, so that everyone can receive care in a safe and calm setting.

## Our expectations

We ask everyone to treat clinicians, reception staff, nurses and other patients with courtesy and patience. Concerns about care are welcome and can be raised with the practice team at any time, either in person, by phone or through our patient feedback process. Staff will listen, explain and aim to resolve concerns fairly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Abusive behaviour is not acceptable in any form. This includes threats, shouting, insults, discriminatory remarks, harassment, physical intimidation and deliberately disruptive conduct, whether it takes place in the building, over the telephone, during a telehealth call or in writing. Staff have the right to end a consultation or call if they feel unsafe or are being abused.

## Consequences

Abusive behaviour can lead to removal from the practice list. Before any such decision is made, we will normally write to the patient to explain the concern and, where appropriate, give the opportunity to discuss it. Urgent medical care will continue to be available where it is needed, and we will help the patient find an alternative provider.

## Questions

If you have a question about this policy, please speak with the reception team.
