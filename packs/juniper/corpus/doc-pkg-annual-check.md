# Annual and over-40 health checks

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Document type:** Product guide
**Audience:** Public
**Effective from:** 2026-05-01

## About this guide

This guide explains the two preventive health check packages offered at Juniper Lane Clinic: the annual health check and the over-40 health check. It is written for patients who are deciding which package suits them, and for anyone booking one on behalf of a family member. Both packages are part of our family medicine service and are delivered by our clinicians in the same way as any other consultation at the practice.

## The annual health check

The annual health check package costs EUR 120 and is designed for adults who want a regular overview of their general health. It includes a fasting blood test, a standard consultation with one of our clinicians, and a blood pressure check. The blood test is taken at our clinic on the day of your appointment, so you will need to attend without eating beforehand as instructed at booking. The standard consultation gives you time to discuss any symptoms, medications, lifestyle questions or concerns you have, and your clinician will explain the results of your blood test and blood pressure reading when they are available.

The annual health check is a good fit if you have not had a recent review, if you take regular medication, or if you simply prefer to keep track of your health on a yearly basis. It does not replace urgent care. If you become unwell between checks, please contact the practice in the usual way.

## The over-40 health check

The over-40 health check costs EUR 150 and is intended for adults aged 40 and over who would like a more detailed conversation about their health. It includes a fasting blood test and an extended consultation. The extended consultation allows more time than the standard appointment, so your clinician can look at risk factors, family history, weight, activity levels, sleep and mental wellbeing in more depth, and can agree with you on any follow-up that would be helpful.

Because the over-40 package focuses on the extended consultation, it is well suited to patients who have several questions to discuss or who would like a fuller plan for the years ahead. Your clinician may recommend further tests or referrals where appropriate, and will explain the reasons before anything is arranged.

## Using your package

Both packages must be used within 90 days of purchase. Please book your appointment within that period so that the blood test and consultation can be completed. Packages cannot be extended beyond the 90 day window, so if you are unsure about timing, we recommend booking as early as possible after purchase. If you have a question about whether a package is suitable for your circumstances, please contact the reception team before buying.

## Preparing for your appointment

For the fasting blood test, you will be asked to avoid food for the period we confirm when you book, though you can usually drink water. Please bring a list of any medications and supplements you take, and note any symptoms or questions you would like to raise. If you need an interpreter or any adjustments to your appointment, let us know at the time of booking so that we can arrange them.

## Questions

For questions about either package, please speak with our reception team during opening hours or use the telehealth enquiry option on our website. Our staff will be happy to help you choose the right option for your needs.
