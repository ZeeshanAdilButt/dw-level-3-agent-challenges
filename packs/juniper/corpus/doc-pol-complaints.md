# Feedback and complaints

## Purpose

This policy explains how patients, carers and visitors can give feedback or make a complaint about Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, and what happens after they do. We welcome feedback of all kinds, including praise, suggestions and concerns, because it helps us improve the care and service we provide across family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Patients and their families, carers or representatives
- Visitors and people contacting the clinic by phone, email, in person or online
- All staff, including clinicians, reception, administration and allied health staff

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about our clinical care, administration, communication, facilities or the conduct of staff, whether or not it is made in writing.

## Our commitments

We will:

- Treat every concern respectfully and in confidence, sharing information only with people who need it to resolve the matter
- Acknowledge complaints within 3 working days of receiving them
- Provide a full written response to complaints within 20 working days of receiving them
- Tell you if we need more time, explain why, and agree a revised date with you
- Keep your care unaffected by the fact that you have raised a concern

## How to give feedback or make a complaint

Follow these numbered steps:

1. **Decide how you want to raise it.** You can speak to any member of staff, call the front desk, send an email, use the feedback form at reception, or write to the practice manager by post.
2. **Tell us what happened.** Include the date, the service involved (for example, a blood test or a telehealth call), the names of staff involved if you know them, and what outcome you would like.
3. **Give us your contact details.** We need a name and a way to reply so we can acknowledge your concern and respond to you.
4. **Let us know if you are raising the matter on someone else's behalf.** We will need their consent, or evidence that you are entitled to act for them, before sharing personal information.
5. **Raise urgent clinical concerns immediately.** If you are worried about your health or someone else's, contact the clinic directly or seek urgent care. Do not wait for a written complaint response.

## What happens next

Once we receive your complaint, we will:

1. **Log it.** We record your complaint in our complaints register and assign a reference number, which we will share with you.
2. **Acknowledge it.** Within 3 working days, we will confirm we have received your complaint, explain who is handling it, and tell you the date by which you can expect a full response.
3. **Investigate it.** The practice manager, or a senior clinician where the matter is clinical, will review the records, speak with the staff involved and gather any other relevant information.
4. **Respond in writing.** Within 20 working days, we will send you a written response setting out what we found, what we have done or will do as a result, and any apology or explanation that is due.
5. **Offer a meeting or call.** If you would like to discuss the response, we will arrange a conversation with you, in person, by telephone or by video call.
6. **Review and learn.** Where a complaint shows that something could be improved, we will update our processes and share lessons with the team.

## If the matter is not resolved

If you are not satisfied with our written response, you can ask the practice manager to review the matter again. Please let us know within a reasonable time of receiving our response and explain what you feel has not been addressed. We will confirm in writing the outcome of the review.

If you remain unhappy after this, you may take your concern to the appropriate external body responsible for health services in your area. We will provide you with details of how to do so on request.

## Confidentiality and records

Complaints are handled in line with applicable privacy and data protection requirements. Information about your complaint is kept separately from your medical record, unless it is clinically relevant, and is retained only for as long as required by law and our records policy.

## Feedback that is not a complaint

Positive comments, suggestions and general feedback are equally valuable. You can share them through any of the channels listed above. We review feedback regularly and share themes with the team to recognise good practice and guide improvements.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **All staff** must listen to concerns, try to resolve simple issues at the time, and pass on anything they cannot resolve to the practice manager.
- **The practice manager** is responsible for maintaining the complaints register, coordinating responses, and ensuring the timescales in this policy are met.
- **Clinical leads** are responsible for reviewing any complaint that concerns clinical care and for advising on clinical explanations.
- **The partners or directors** receive regular reports on complaints and feedback and ensure this policy is followed.

## Review of this policy

This policy takes effect on 1 March 2026 and will be reviewed regularly, and updated whenever there is a change in law, guidance or our service. Questions about this policy can be directed to the practice manager at the reception desk.
