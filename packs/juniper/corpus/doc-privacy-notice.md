# Privacy notice

## Who we are and why this notice exists

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice serving the people of Port Avery and the surrounding area. We provide family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Because we handle health information, which is among the most sensitive kinds of personal data, we take our responsibilities seriously and we want every patient to understand what we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it and what rights they have. This notice sets out those points in plain terms. It applies to everyone who contacts the clinic, books an appointment, attends in person, speaks to us by telephone, joins a telehealth call, uses our online services or asks questions through our website assistant.

## What information we collect

When you register with the clinic, we collect your full name, date of birth, postal address, telephone number, a registered mobile number and an email address where you have chosen to give one. We also record your next of kin where you wish to provide it, your preferred language, any interpreter needs and the name of your usual clinician. For clinical care we keep a record of your medical history, current and past medications, allergies, test results, immunisation history, referral letters, correspondence from other services and notes made by the clinicians who see you. Where a service requires it, we hold additional information such as travel itineraries for the travel clinic, physiotherapy assessment notes and women's health history. For children, we hold the same core details about the child together with details of the parent or guardian who holds parental responsibility and the vaccination and growth records that form part of child health checks.

We collect information from several sources. Most of it comes directly from you, whether through the registration form, in conversation with reception or a clinician, or through the patient portal. Some of it arrives from other parties with your agreement, such as hospitals, specialists, pharmacies and laboratories that send results or letters back to us. We also generate information ourselves during the course of your care, including the results of examinations and tests. Technical information such as the type of device used to access the patient portal and the time of sign in is also recorded so that we can keep the service secure and working properly.

We use this information for several purposes. The main purpose is to provide safe and effective care, which includes diagnosing conditions, prescribing medicines, arranging tests, referring you to other services and following up on treatment. We also use it to administer repeat prescriptions, manage bookings, send reminders for vaccinations and check ups, send results, handle billing and insurance claims where they apply, meet our legal and professional record keeping duties and monitor the quality and safety of the services we deliver. Where you have agreed, we may contact you about information that might help your health, such as seasonal vaccination campaigns, but you can ask us to stop at any time.

## Lawful basis and how we handle sensitive data

Health information is a special category of personal data, so we process it only where the law allows and only for the purposes described above. In most cases our processing is necessary to provide health care and to meet our professional obligations to keep accurate records. In other cases we rely on your explicit consent, for example when you ask us to share information with a family member or a third party. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time, although withdrawing consent will not affect anything we have already done lawfully before you withdrew it, and it may limit what we can do for you.

## Keeping your information secure

We protect personal information with a combination of technical and organisational measures. Our clinical system is access controlled so that only staff who need a record to do their job can open it, and all access is logged. Our computers lock automatically when unattended, our paper records are kept in locked storage, and our staff receive regular training on confidentiality and data protection. We expect everyone who works with us, including contractors and suppliers, to meet the same standards. If a breach of security ever affects your information, we will investigate it promptly, take steps to contain it and tell you and the relevant authorities where the law requires us to do so.

## How we confirm who you are

Protecting your records depends on making sure that the person asking for information is really the person entitled to receive it. When you use the patient portal to view or change a booking, we send a one-time code to the mobile number registered on your record, and bookings are shown or changed only after you enter that code. When you telephone the clinic, our staff verify the caller's name, date of birth and postcode before discussing any personal details. Staff and the website assistant ask only for the minimum data needed for the task in hand, so you will not be asked for more information than we genuinely require. If you are unable to satisfy these checks, we may ask you to attend in person with photo identification, and we will explain why.

## How we share information

We share information only when there is a good reason to do so. Results are given through the patient portal or a call from reception, and only to the patient. We do not give results to family members, employers or anyone else unless the patient has given their written consent, and even then we share only the information covered by that consent. Other clinicians involved in your care may see relevant information, as may the laboratories, pharmacies and specialists to whom we send requests. We may also share information where the law requires it, for example to report certain notifiable diseases or in response to a valid court order.

Insurers receive only the member number, the service code and the date. We do not send clinical notes, test results or diagnoses to insurers unless you have separately agreed in writing and the insurer has a lawful reason to request that detail. The clinic never sells patient data, and we do not use your information for marketing by third parties.

Telehealth calls are not recorded. Clinicians may make written notes during the call, which form part of your record in the same way as notes from an in person consultation. Those notes are protected by the same controls described above.

## Our website assistant

Our website includes an automated assistant that can answer general questions about opening times, services, how to book and how to use the patient portal. Assistant conversations are kept for 90 days so that we can check the quality of answers and fix faults, after which they are deleted. We ask you not to type in more personal health detail than is needed for your question, and the assistant will not ask for clinical information. The assistant never shares one patient's details with another person, and it cannot see the records of any patient other than the one who has verified their identity through the portal.

## How long we keep your records

We keep records for as long as they are needed to provide care and to meet our legal and professional duties. Adult records are kept for 10 years after the last visit. Child records are kept until the patient is 25, and this period is chosen so that the record remains available if the young person needs it when they become an adult. When the retention period ends, we securely destroy paper records and permanently delete electronic records, unless a legal hold requires us to keep them for longer. Assistant conversations, as described above, are deleted after 90 days.

## Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you. Records requests are answered within 30 days, and where a request is complicated we will tell you if we need more time and why. You may also ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, to restrict how we use it in certain circumstances, to object to particular types of processing and, where the law allows, to ask for your information to be deleted or moved to another provider. We will explain clearly if any of these rights do not apply to a particular record, for example where we must keep clinical notes for a set period. To make a request, contact the reception team or write to us, and we will confirm your identity before releasing any information.

## Questions and complaints

If you have any questions about this notice or about how we handle your information, please contact us at privacy@juniperlane.example. We will reply as quickly as we can and aim to resolve concerns directly with you. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the national data authority, which is the independent body responsible for overseeing data protection in the country. We would always prefer to hear from you first, so that we have the chance to put things right.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in the law, in our services or in how we operate. The version in force is the one shown on our website, and it takes effect on the date printed at the top of the page. Where a change is significant, we will tell patients through the patient portal or at their next contact. This notice takes effect from 2026-02-01.
