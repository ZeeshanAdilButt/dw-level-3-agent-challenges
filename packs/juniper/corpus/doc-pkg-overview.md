# Health packages

## Purpose and scope

This document sets out the health packages offered by Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, effective from 2026-05-01. It applies to all packages sold through the clinic's front desk and online booking. Patients should read this document before purchasing a package so they understand what is included, how long it remains valid and when a refund is available.

## Package overview

The clinic offers five packages. Each package is priced in euros and bundles specific services from the clinic's family medicine, child health, travel clinic and physiotherapy teams. The table below lists each package with its price and contents.

| Package | Price | What is included |
|---|---|---|
| Annual health check | EUR 120 | A fasting blood test, a standard consultation and a blood pressure check |
| Over-40 health check | EUR 150 | A fasting blood test and an extended consultation |
| School starter package | EUR 85 | A child health check combined with a vaccination visit |
| Travel bundle | EUR 95 | A travel clinic consultation combined with a vaccination visit |
| Physiotherapy block of five | EUR 270 | Five physiotherapy follow-up sessions, saving EUR 30 compared with booking them individually |

The annual health check suits adult patients who want a general review of their health each year. The over-40 health check includes an extended consultation, giving more time to discuss risk factors, lifestyle and any concerns that have built up over time. The school starter package is designed for children preparing to start school, combining the child health check with the vaccinations due at that stage. The travel bundle covers the travel clinic consultation and the vaccinations recommended for a planned trip. The physiotherapy block gives patients five follow-up sessions at a reduced combined price.

Clinical suitability for any package is confirmed by the clinician at the time of booking. Where a package does not match a patient's needs, the clinic will advise on individual services instead.

## Validity

All packages must be used within 90 days of purchase. Services not used within this period expire. Patients who book the first appointment within the validity window should arrange any follow-up appointments promptly, as the 90-day limit applies to the whole package and not to each service separately.

Where a patient needs more time because of illness or other circumstances, the front desk can be contacted before the validity period ends. The clinic will consider these requests individually, but any extension is at the clinic's discretion and is not guaranteed.

## Payment and insurance

Packages are self-pay only and cannot be claimed through direct billing. Patients who hold private health insurance should check with their insurer whether they can reclaim part of the package cost directly, as the clinic cannot submit these claims on the patient's behalf. Patients are encouraged to keep their receipts for this purpose.

Payment is taken at the time of purchase. Accepted payment methods are displayed at the front desk and on the online booking page.

## Refunds

An unused package is refunded within 14 days of purchase. To request a refund, the patient should contact the clinic in writing or in person and provide proof of purchase. Once any service within the package has been used, the package is no longer eligible for a refund under this policy, and the remaining services remain subject to the 90-day validity period.

Refunds are paid by the same method used to purchase the package, unless the clinic agrees otherwise with the patient.

## Telehealth and remote services

Where a package includes a consultation, that consultation may be delivered by telehealth call if the clinician judges it clinically appropriate. Blood tests, vaccinations, blood pressure checks and physiotherapy hands-on sessions must take place in person. The clinician will confirm the format of each consultation when the appointment is booked.

## Changes to this document

Juniper Lane Clinic may update package contents or prices from time to time. Any change takes effect from the date stated in the updated version. Packages purchased before a change remain governed by the version in force at the time of purchase, unless the clinic states otherwise in writing.

For questions about this document, patients should contact the Juniper Lane Clinic front desk during opening hours.
