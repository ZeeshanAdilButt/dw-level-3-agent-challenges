# Giving a urine sample

This guide explains how to collect and hand in a urine sample at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. Please read it before you collect your sample.

## What you will need

- A clinic sample pot, which we will give you at reception or with your test request.
- A clean, dry container for the sample if you are asked to use one at home.
- Your appointment details or request form, if you have them.

## Steps for collecting your sample

1. Wash your hands with soap and water.
2. Open the sample pot carefully and place the lid face up on a clean surface. Do not touch the inside of the pot or the lid.
3. Wipe the area around the opening, following the instructions on the pot or those given by the nurse.
4. Start to pass urine into the toilet, then move the pot into the stream and collect about half to two thirds of the pot.
5. Finish passing urine into the toilet.
6. Screw the lid on tightly and wipe the outside of the pot.
7. Wash your hands again.
8. Label the pot with your full name and date of birth if you have not already done so.

## Bringing your sample to the clinic

A first-morning urine sample gives the most reliable result, so collect it as soon as you wake up when we ask for one. Bring the sample to the clinic within 4 hours of collecting it. Keep the pot upright and at room temperature until you hand it in. Do not put the sample in a fridge unless a member of staff tells you to.

## If you have questions

Please call the reception desk or contact us through the patient portal if you are unsure about any step. If you cannot provide a sample, let us know so we can arrange another time.
