# Same-day appointments

*Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Effective from 1 March 2026.*

## Purpose

This policy explains how patients can get seen on the same day at Juniper Lane Clinic and how to book routine appointments in advance. It applies to all family medicine, child health, vaccination, travel clinic, blood test, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedure, repeat prescription and telehealth services.

## Booking routine appointments

Online and assistant bookings need at least 24 hours notice, so please plan ahead when you want a routine appointment on a particular day. If you request a booking with less notice than this, the online booking system and our front desk assistant will offer you the next available slot that meets the 24 hours requirement.

## Same-day visits

Same-day visits are by phone only. To request one, call the clinic during opening hours and speak to our reception team, who will assess your request and offer you the earliest suitable appointment, a telehealth call or advice on other services.

## Same-day urgent visits

Same-day urgent visits are arranged by phone and not through the assistant. Please do not use the online booking system or the digital assistant to request an urgent appointment, as these channels cannot give you a same-day urgent slot. If your condition is life threatening, call emergency services immediately rather than waiting for a clinic appointment.

## Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel a booking, please tell us as early as possible so that another patient can use the slot. Where you have booked online or through the assistant, you can make the change in the same way, provided the new time also meets the 24 hours notice requirement.

## Questions

If you are unsure which route is right for you, please call the clinic and our team will help you decide.
