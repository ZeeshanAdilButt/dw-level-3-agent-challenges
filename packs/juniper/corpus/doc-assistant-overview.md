# Meet the clinic's virtual assistant

## Purpose of this policy

This policy explains what the virtual assistant at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can and cannot do, how it handles personal information, and what happens when a question is urgent. It applies to every conversation with the assistant on our website, patient portal and telephone line, and it takes effect on 2026-09-01.

## What the virtual assistant does

The virtual assistant answers general questions about our services. These include family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and our travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. It can explain how to prepare for an appointment, what documents to bring, and how to reach the right team.

The assistant also books, changes and cancels appointments within the availability shown in the booking system. When it books an appointment, it will confirm the service, the date and the time before finishing.

## What the virtual assistant does not do

The virtual assistant does not diagnose. It cannot tell you what a symptom means, whether a condition is present, or how serious it is. Only a clinician can make a diagnosis, and the assistant will say so plainly rather than guess.

The assistant gives no advice on medicines, doses or weight loss. It will not recommend starting, stopping or changing any medicine, suggest a dose, or propose a diet or weight loss plan. For questions in these areas, the assistant will arrange for a clinician to respond.

The assistant never shares one patient's details with another person. This applies to appointment times, test results, prescriptions, contact information and any other record. Each conversation is limited to the person who is verified as the patient, or to a parent or guardian whose right to act for a child has been confirmed by our staff.

## Non-urgent clinical questions

If you have a non-urgent clinical question that the assistant cannot answer, a nurse call-back is arranged within 1 working day. The assistant will record your question in a secure message, check how you would like to be contacted, and confirm the expected call-back time before the conversation ends. Please keep your question specific and include anything the nurse will need to know, such as how long a concern has lasted.

## Emergencies and red flags

In an emergency call 112 now. Do not wait for a reply from the virtual assistant or a call-back from the clinic.

If you describe a red flag, such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness or thoughts of harming yourself or someone else, the assistant will tell you to call 112 and alerts staff at once. It will not attempt to assess the situation itself.

## Your responsibilities

Please give accurate information when you use the assistant, and do not share a password or verification code with anyone. If you are unsure whether a question is urgent, treat it as urgent and contact emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

## Questions about this policy

If you have questions about how the virtual assistant works or how your information is handled, please speak to our reception team during opening hours or write to the practice manager through the patient portal.
