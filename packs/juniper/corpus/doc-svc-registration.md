# New patient registration

This guide explains how to join Juniper Lane Clinic as a registered patient in Port Avery. It covers the registration form, the first visit, and when your record becomes active, so you know what to expect before you arrive.

## Who this guide is for

This page is for people who are new to Juniper Lane Clinic, including adults registering for themselves and parents or guardians registering children. If you have previously been a patient here and have been removed from our list, the same steps apply. Our services include family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Registration gives you access to all of these.

## Step 1: Complete form CF-12

Form CF-12 is the new patient registration form. It collects your personal details, your emergency contact, your current medications, and any allergies or long-term conditions that our clinicians should know about. You can request a copy at the front desk, or you can download it from our website and print it at home. Please complete every section that applies to you, and write clearly so that our staff can enter your details accurately. If a section does not apply, mark it as not applicable rather than leaving it blank.

For children, a parent or guardian should complete the form and sign it on the child's behalf. Please include the child's date of birth and details of any routine vaccinations received so far, if you have them.

## Step 2: Bring the required documents

When you attend your registration visit, please bring photo ID and proof of address from the last 3 months. Acceptable photo ID includes a passport or a driving licence. Acceptable proof of address includes a recent utility bill, a bank statement, or an official letter addressed to you at your current home. Bring the original documents, as we cannot accept photocopies or photographs of them on screen. These checks help us confirm that your records are accurate and that you are registered at the correct address for local services.

## Step 3: Attend your registration visit

The new patient registration visit is free of charge, so there is no fee for this appointment. The visit lasts 20 minutes. During this time, a member of our team will check your completed form CF-12, verify your identification and proof of address, and talk through any health concerns you would like to raise at this stage. If you have questions about which service is right for you, this is a good time to ask. Please arrive a few minutes early so that the appointment can begin on time.

## Step 4: Your record is activated

After we receive your completed form CF-12, your new record is activated within 3 working days. Once activation is complete, you can book appointments, request repeat prescriptions, and arrange telehealth calls through the usual channels. If you need urgent care before your record is active, please contact the clinic directly so that we can advise you on the best option.

## Keeping your details up to date

Please tell us promptly if your name, address, phone number or emergency contact changes. Accurate details help us contact you about results, appointments and important health information. You can update your details at the front desk or by telephone.

## Questions

If you are unsure about any part of the registration process, please speak to our reception team during opening hours. They can help you prepare your form, explain which documents to bring, and answer any questions before your first visit to Juniper Lane Clinic.
