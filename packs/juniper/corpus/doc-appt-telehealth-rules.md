# Telehealth rules

## Purpose and scope

These rules set out how Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery runs telehealth calls. They apply to all clinicians and to all patients who book a video or phone consultation with the clinic. They take effect on 2026-03-01 and replace any earlier telehealth guidance.

## Emergencies

1. Telehealth is never used for emergencies. If a patient describes chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness or any other urgent symptom, the clinician ends the call and directs the patient to call the local emergency number immediately.
2. Patients who are unsure whether their symptoms are urgent should call the emergency number rather than book a telehealth call.
3. Staff who take bookings must ask about urgent symptoms before confirming a telehealth appointment.

## Booking a telehealth call

1. Patients book telehealth calls through the clinic reception team or the online booking page.
2. Before the first call, the patient receives a message with the time of the appointment and the link or dial-in details.
3. Patients should test their device and connection before the appointment time.

## New patients

1. Telehealth is not available to a new patient until form CF-12 is on file.
2. Reception checks for form CF-12 at the time of booking. If the form is not on file, the booking is not confirmed and the patient is asked to complete it first.
3. Once form CF-12 is on file, the new patient may book telehealth calls in the same way as any other patient.

## Checking identity

1. At the start of every telehealth call, the clinician confirms the patient's identity by name and date of birth.
2. If the name and date of birth do not match the clinic record, the clinician does not proceed with the consultation until identity is confirmed.
3. Where a call is made on behalf of another person, the clinician also confirms the relationship and the authority of the person speaking.

## Waiting for the patient

1. The clinician waits 10 minutes for the patient to join a telehealth call.
2. If the patient has not joined after 10 minutes, the call is counted as missed.
3. Reception contacts the patient to offer a new time. A missed call is recorded on the patient's file.

## Recording

1. Telehealth calls are not recorded by the clinic, whether by audio, video or screen capture.
2. Clinicians may make written notes of the consultation in the patient record in the usual way.
3. Patients must not record calls themselves without the clinician's agreement.

## Changing a telehealth call to an in-person visit

1. A clinician may move a telehealth call to an in-person visit if needed, for example when a physical examination is required.
2. The change is made at no extra charge to the patient.
3. Reception arranges the in-person visit as soon as practicable and informs the patient of the new time.

## Questions and review

Questions about these rules should be directed to the practice manager at Juniper Lane Clinic. The practice manager reviews these rules when the clinic's services change or when new guidance is issued.
