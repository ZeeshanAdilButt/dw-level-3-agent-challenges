# Volunteer driver service

## About the service

Juniper Lane Clinic runs a volunteer driver service to help patients get to their appointments in Port Avery. The service is free for patients over 75 and needs 2 working days notice, so please book your ride as early as you can. Volunteers use their own vehicles and are checked before they take on any journeys.

## How to book

Call the reception desk during opening hours and ask for the volunteer driver service. Give the date and time of your appointment, the address you will be collected from, and any help you may need getting in and out of the car. If you are unable to call, ask a family member or carer to make the booking for you.

## Before your journey

Please be ready at the door at the agreed time. Volunteers will wait for a short period but may not be able to stay longer if the next journey is due. If your appointment is cancelled or moved, let us know as soon as possible so that the ride can be changed.
