# Children and young people at appointments

This policy sets out who may attend a consultation at Juniper Lane Clinic when the patient is a child or young person. It applies to all appointments at the clinic in Port Avery, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Who may attend alone

Patients aged 16 and over may attend alone. Where a young person aged 16 is attending for a consultation, the clinician will speak with them directly and will not require a parent or guardian to be present unless the young person asks for one to be there.

## Who must attend with a child

Patients under 16 need a parent or guardian at consultations. The parent or guardian must be present in the room or, for telehealth calls, must be on the call for the full consultation. Staff may ask to see proof of the adult's relationship to the child or their authority to consent to care.

## Exceptions and concerns

Where a clinician judges that a child should be seen without a parent or guardian present, for example where there are safeguarding concerns, the clinician will follow the clinic's safeguarding procedures and the relevant legal requirements. Anyone with a concern about a child's welfare should speak with a member of staff at the front desk or with the treating clinician.

## Questions

Patients and families with questions about this policy can ask at reception or contact the clinic before their appointment.
