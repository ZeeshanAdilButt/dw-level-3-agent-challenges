# Parking

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how to park when visiting Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. Our family practice sees patients for family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health appointments, minor procedures, repeat prescription collection and telehealth calls. Many of these visits are short, while others, such as physiotherapy sessions, minor procedures and child health checks with a parent or carer, can take longer. Because of this, it helps to plan your parking before you leave home, especially if you are travelling with young children, mobility aids or a bag of paperwork.

This guide is written for patients, carers, relatives and visitors. It covers how to find the clinic, the options available to you on arrival, how to use the reception validation process, and what to do if you need extra help. It is intended as general guidance only. If your circumstances mean you need something different, please speak to the reception team before your appointment and we will do what we can to help.

## Getting to the clinic

Juniper Lane Clinic is located in the centre of Port Avery, within easy reach of the main roads that lead into town from the north and east. If you are driving, the simplest route is to follow the signs for the town centre and then the signs for the health quarter, which will bring you to the street where the clinic entrance is found. Satellite navigation systems generally direct drivers to the correct entrance, but if you are unsure, you can call the reception desk for directions before setting off.

Traffic in the town centre can be busier at certain times of the day, particularly during school drop-off and collection periods and during the late afternoon. If your appointment falls within these windows, allow extra time for the journey and for finding a space. Arriving early gives you time to park calmly, check in at reception and settle in before your appointment begins. Arriving late, on the other hand, may mean that your appointment is rescheduled or shortened, so please aim to be at the clinic a few minutes ahead of your booked time.

Public transport links to the clinic are good, and some patients prefer to walk, cycle or take a bus where this is practical for them. If you are coming on foot or by bicycle, there is a covered area near the entrance where bicycles can be secured. If you are travelling by taxi or community transport, the drop-off point is directly outside the main entrance, and drivers are asked to keep this area clear for patients who need to be dropped off and collected quickly.

## Arriving by car

When you arrive by car, the first thing to consider is whether you need an accessible space. Our car park on Mill Row has 24 spaces in total, and this includes 4 accessible bays located closest to the building entrance. These bays are reserved for blue badge holders and for anyone who has a clear need for step-free access close to the front door. If you have a blue badge, please display it clearly on your dashboard while parked. If you do not hold a blue badge but believe you need one of these spaces, please speak to reception when you arrive and we will advise you on the best option available at that time.

Please drive slowly through the car park, watch for pedestrians (particularly children and people using wheelchairs or walking aids), and follow the marked direction of travel. Parking bays are marked clearly, and where the car park is busy, the staff may direct you to a free space. Please do not block exits, park across marked lines, or leave a vehicle in a bay for longer than needed to attend your appointment. Patients with prams, pushchairs or larger equipment may find it easier to use the wider bays near the entrance, and if you need assistance unloading, reception staff can help you once you have arrived.

Once you have parked, you will need to take your parking ticket to reception so it can be validated. This is an important step, as it determines whether your parking is free. Parking is free for 90 minutes with a ticket validated at reception. Please keep your ticket safe in the car and bring it inside with you when you check in. If your appointment runs over and you think you may need more time, let the reception team know so that they can note this on your ticket. Ninety minutes is generous for most visits, but a longer appointment, such as a physiotherapy course session with a longer treatment, a minor procedure or a family visit with several appointments, may take you beyond that limit.

## Validating your ticket

Validation is quick and happens at the reception desk. When you arrive, take your parking ticket to the desk and ask the receptionist to validate it. They will confirm your appointment time and stamp or record the validation on the ticket. Please make sure the validation is done before you leave the building, as tickets that have not been validated may not qualify for the free period. If you arrive for a telehealth call, you will not need to validate your ticket, as telehealth calls do not require you to visit the clinic in person. If you are unsure whether your visit is a telehealth call or an in-person appointment, please check your booking confirmation.

It is a good idea to validate your ticket at the start of your visit rather than at the end. This way, if there are any problems with the ticket, such as a faded print or a missing time, the staff can resolve them while you are still in the building. If you have lost your ticket, please speak to reception, who will do their best to help. Please note that free parking applies only to the time set out above, and extra time beyond that will be dealt with under the normal car park rules displayed at the car park.

## Parking at Harbour Street

If the Mill Row car park is full at the time of your visit, you may wish to use the Harbour Street car park instead. The Harbour Street car park is a 3 minute walk away from the clinic entrance, following the pavement along the main road and turning into the health quarter at the corner. The walk is level and well lit, making it suitable for most patients, but if you have difficulty walking, please let reception know and they can advise whether a closer option is available at that time, or whether a member of staff can help you on arrival.

The Harbour Street car park may have different rules and different times of operation, so please check the signage in that car park before leaving your vehicle. Parking at Harbour Street is not covered by the validation process described above, so you should not expect the same free period. If you park there, please make sure you understand the conditions displayed on site. Using the Harbour Street car park is a useful alternative during busy periods, but please remember that the 3 minute walk is in addition to any time needed to find a space and leave your vehicle, so allow sufficient time before your appointment.

## Special circumstances and extra help

We understand that parking can be stressful, particularly for patients who are unwell, anxious, pregnant, carrying young children or using mobility aids. If you need extra help getting from your car to the clinic, please contact the reception team before your appointment so that we can plan for your arrival. Where possible, we will arrange for a member of staff to meet you at the entrance or to help you find the most suitable parking space. This is especially important for patients who have difficulty with steps, slopes or long distances on foot.

If you have a question that is not answered in this guide, please call the reception desk during opening hours or speak to a member of staff when you arrive. We are always happy to explain the options available and to help you make the journey as smooth as possible. Thank you for choosing Juniper Lane Clinic, and we look forward to seeing you.
