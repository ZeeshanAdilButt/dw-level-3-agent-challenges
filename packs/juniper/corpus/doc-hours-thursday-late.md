# Late Thursday opening

## Who this page is for

This guide explains how our late Thursday opening works at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It is written for patients, carers and anyone planning a visit who needs to know when the clinic is available later in the week.

## When we are open

On Thursdays the clinic is open 08:00 to 20:00. This extended session gives working families and people with daytime commitments a way to see a clinician after the usual working day. Please check the time on the day of your visit, as public holidays may change our hours.

## What you can book on a Thursday evening

Most services that we offer during the day are also available during Thursday evening sessions. These include family medicine appointments, child health checks, vaccinations, blood tests, women's health consultations, minor procedures, physiotherapy and telehealth calls. Our travel clinic and repeat prescription administration may have limited availability in the final hours, so booking early is recommended.

## How to book

Appointments can be made by calling the front desk during opening hours, through our online booking system, or by speaking with our reception team in person. When you book, tell us the service you need so we can allocate the right clinician and an appropriate appointment length.

## If you need urgent care

The late Thursday session is for planned and routine care. If your condition is urgent or life threatening, call emergency services immediately rather than waiting for an appointment.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a Thursday evening appointment suits your needs, please contact our reception team, who will be glad to advise you.
