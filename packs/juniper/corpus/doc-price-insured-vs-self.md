# Insured or self-pay: what you pay

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains what you can expect to pay at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, depending on whether you are covered by a health insurance plan or are paying for your care directly. It applies to family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

Prices in this guide are effective from 1 March 2026. Our front desk team can confirm the current cost of any service before your appointment.

## Who pays what

**Insured patients** present their insurance details at registration. The clinic bills your insurer for covered services and tells you about any amount that remains payable by you, such as a co-payment set by your plan.

**Self-pay patients** pay the clinic directly at the time of the visit. The prices listed below apply to self-pay patients.

## Common visits at a glance

The table below compares what an insured patient and a self-pay patient pay for common visits. Where a self-pay price is not listed in the table, please ask the front desk, as the cost depends on the procedure or test involved.

| Service | Insured patient | Self-pay patient |
|---|---|---|
| Standard consultation | Covered by your plan, subject to any co-payment | EUR 55 |
| Fasting blood test | Covered by your plan, subject to any co-payment | EUR 48 |
| Vaccinations and travel clinic visits | Covered by your plan where applicable | Quoted at booking |
| Physiotherapy session | Covered by your plan where applicable | Quoted at booking |
| Minor procedure | Covered by your plan where applicable | Quoted at booking |
| Repeat prescription administration | Covered by your plan where applicable | Quoted at booking |
| Telehealth call | Covered by your plan where applicable | Quoted at booking |

## Understanding the figures

The standard consultation costs EUR 55 for self-pay patients, and this covers a standard appointment with one of our family doctors or nurse practitioners. The fasting blood test costs EUR 48 for self-pay patients, and this includes the sample collection at our clinic; laboratory analysis of specific panels may be charged separately if you request it.

If you are unsure which category applies to you, please ask before your visit so that you are not surprised by a bill.

## Questions and changes

If you have questions about your costs, please speak with our front desk team before your appointment. Prices may be reviewed from time to time, and any change will be published in an updated version of this guide before it takes effect.
