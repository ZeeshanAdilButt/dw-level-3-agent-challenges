# How long we keep records

## Purpose

This policy explains how long Juniper Lane Clinic keeps patient records in Port Avery. It applies to records created by our family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Adult records

Adult records are kept for 10 years after the last visit. The period starts from the date of the most recent appointment, test, prescription or telehealth call on the patient's file.

## Child records

Child records are kept until the patient is 25. We apply this to all records linked to a child's file, including vaccination histories, child health check notes and any referrals made on the child's behalf.

## Secure storage and disposal

Records are stored securely, with access limited to staff who need them for care or administration. When the retention period ends, records are destroyed in a secure manner and the disposal is logged.

## Requests and questions

Patients who have questions about their records, or who want to request a copy, can contact the reception team at the clinic. Requests are handled in line with applicable data protection rules.

## Review

This policy is reviewed regularly and whenever relevant legal or professional requirements change.
