# Holiday opening hours 2026

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the opening hours of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery for the Christmas and New Year period in 2026 and for the local public holiday in December. It applies to all services offered by the clinic, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. It takes effect on 15 October 2026 and replaces any earlier holiday notices for the same period.

## Summary of opening hours

The table below gives the date, the day, and the hours for each date in the holiday period. Please check the table before you plan an appointment, a collection or a repeat prescription request.

| Date | Day | Status | Opening hours | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 December 2026 | Monday | Closed | Closed all day | Port Avery Harbour Day. The clinic is closed for the local public holiday. |
| 24 December 2026 | Thursday | Open | 09:00 to 13:00 | Morning opening only. Last appointments end shortly before the closing time. |
| 25 December 2026 | Friday | Closed | Closed all day | Christmas Day. The clinic is closed. |
| 26 December 2026 | Saturday | Closed | Closed all day | Boxing Day. The clinic is closed. |
| 27 December 2026 | Sunday | Open | 09:00 to 16:00 | Reduced service. Repeat prescription requests are processed during these hours. |
| 28 December 2026 | Monday | Open | 09:00 to 16:00 | Reduced service. Telehealth calls are available during these hours. |
| 29 December 2026 | Tuesday | Open | 09:00 to 16:00 | Reduced service. Blood test collection is available during these hours. |
| 30 December 2026 | Wednesday | Open | 09:00 to 16:00 | Reduced service. Vaccinations and child health checks are available during these hours. |
| 31 December 2026 | Thursday | Open | 08:00 to 14:00 | Earlier start and early close. Please arrive before the closing time. |
| 1 January 2027 | Friday | Closed | Closed all day | New Year's Day. The clinic is closed. |

The table shows the planned hours. Where a notice in this policy gives different information, the notice in this policy applies.

## Notes on each opening period

### Harbour Day, 14 December 2026

The clinic is closed on Monday 14 December 2026 for Port Avery Harbour Day. No appointments, telehealth calls, repeat prescription administration or blood test collections are scheduled on this date. Patients who expect to need care on or around this day should arrange their needs for the preceding week.

### Christmas Eve, 24 December 2026

On 24 December 2026 the clinic is open from 09:00 to 13:00. This is a morning-only session. Appointments are booked to finish before the clinic closes, so patients should aim to attend early in the morning. Staff may not be able to accept walk-in requests after the morning session has begun.

### Christmas Day and Boxing Day, 25 and 26 December

The clinic is closed on 25 and 26 December 2026. No routine services are provided on these dates. Patients who require urgent care during the closure should contact their local emergency services or the out-of-hours arrangements that apply in their area.

### Between Christmas and New Year, 27 to 30 December 2026

From 27 to 30 December 2026 the clinic is open from 09:00 to 16:00. This is a reduced service, and not all clinic services may be available on every day in this period. Staff will give priority to scheduled appointments, repeat prescription administration and time-sensitive clinical needs. Patients planning a blood test, vaccination, physiotherapy session or women's health appointment during this period should confirm their booking in advance.

### New Year's Eve, 31 December 2026

On 31 December 2026 the clinic is open from 08:00 to 14:00. The clinic opens earlier than usual on this date and closes early in the afternoon. Patients should plan their attendance for the morning or early afternoon and should expect the final appointments of the day to finish before the closing time.

### New Year's Day, 1 January 2027

The clinic is closed on 1 January 2027. No routine services are provided on this date. Patients requiring urgent care should follow the same arrangements described for the Christmas closure above.

## Services and how they are affected

During reduced hours, the clinic prioritises services that depend on timing and continuity of care. The following general guidance applies to each service in the holiday period.

Family medicine appointments are offered during the open hours shown in the table. Patients should book as early as possible because appointment availability is limited during the reduced sessions.

Child health checks and vaccinations are available during open hours. Parents should check the table before attending and should avoid planning these visits on closed days or at the very end of the shorter sessions.

The travel clinic operates only during open hours. Patients with travel plans in early 2027 should contact the clinic in good time, as availability may be reduced around the holiday period.

Blood tests are collected during open hours. Samples and requests submitted near the end of a session may not be processed until the clinic reopens.

Physiotherapy, women's health appointments and minor procedures follow the same open hours. Patients with ongoing treatment plans should confirm their next session with reception before the holiday period begins.

Repeat prescription administration is processed during open hours. Patients should request repeat prescriptions well ahead of the closure, particularly before 25 December 2026 and before 1 January 2027, so that supplies are not interrupted.

Telehealth calls are offered during open hours. Calls cannot be booked for closed days.

## Responsibilities

The clinic manager is responsible for publishing this policy on the clinic website and displaying it at reception. Reception staff are responsible for answering queries about opening hours and for directing patients to the correct session. Clinical staff are responsible for managing their appointment lists so that scheduled patients are seen within the open hours shown in the table.

Patients are responsible for checking the table before attending and for planning their care around the closed days.

## Communication

Changes to the opening hours in this policy will be published on the clinic website and displayed at the front desk. Patients who have booked appointments will be contacted where a change affects their booking. Questions about this policy may be directed to reception during open hours.

## Review

This policy will be reviewed after the 2026 and 2027 holiday period is complete. The review will consider patient feedback, staffing levels and any changes to local public holidays in Port Avery. Until the review is complete, this policy remains in force from 15 October 2026.
