# Dr Idris Calloway

## Team profile

This guide is a short biography of Dr Idris Calloway, a family physician at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It is written for patients who want to know more about the clinician who may be caring for them or their family.

## Background

Dr Calloway trained in family medicine and has spent his career caring for patients across all stages of life, from newborn checks and childhood vaccinations to the ongoing management of long-term conditions in adults and older patients. He takes a steady, unhurried approach to consultations and values giving patients enough time to ask questions and understand their options.

## Areas of focus

Dr Calloway has a particular interest in preventive care, including child health checks, immunisation planning and travel health advice. He also takes an active role in minor procedures, which he carries out at the clinic on Wednesdays and Thursdays, and he supports patients who need repeat prescriptions reviewed or who prefer a telehealth call for suitable concerns.

## Availability

Dr Idris Calloway works Monday, Wednesday and Thursday at Juniper Lane Clinic. Patients wishing to book an appointment with him should use the clinic's usual booking channels, and those who need a minor procedure should ask the reception team to arrange a slot on one of his procedure days.

## Outside the clinic

Dr Calloway is committed to community health education and enjoys helping local residents understand how to manage everyday health concerns with confidence.
