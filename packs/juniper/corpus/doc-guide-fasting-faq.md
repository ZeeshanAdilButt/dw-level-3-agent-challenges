# Fasting questions answered

## About this guide

This guide is published by Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery to answer common questions about fasting before blood tests. It is effective from 2026-03-01 and applies to all patients booking blood tests at the clinic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to fast before every blood test?

No fasting is needed for a standard blood test. You can eat and drink as normal before the appointment unless your clinician has told you otherwise. Some tests, such as those listed below, do require you to fast first.

### How long do I need to fast before a fasting blood test?

Before a fasting blood test, do not eat for 8 hours. Count the hours from your last meal or snack until your arrival at the clinic for the blood draw. Your phlebotomist will confirm the timing when you check in.

### Can I drink anything during the fast?

Plain water is allowed and encouraged during the fast. Staying hydrated helps the phlebotomist find a vein and keeps you comfortable during the blood draw.

### Which drinks and foods break the fast?

Tea, coffee, juice, milk and chewing gum break the fast. Even small amounts can affect certain test results, so please avoid them until after your blood sample has been taken. Sugar-free options should also be avoided unless your clinician has said they are acceptable.

### What should I do if I eat or drink something by mistake?

If you eat or drink something by mistake, tell the phlebotomist as soon as you arrive. The test will be rebooked at no charge so that you can return on another day after a proper fast. Please do not try to hide the mistake, because an inaccurate sample can lead to results that are hard to interpret.

### How long do I need to fast before a glucose tolerance test?

Before a glucose tolerance test, do not eat for 10 hours. This test measures how your body responds to a sugary drink, so the longer fast gives a clearer starting reading. Plain water is still allowed during this fasting period.

### What happens during a glucose tolerance test?

Your phlebotomist will take a blood sample at the start, then ask you to drink a measured sugary drink. Further blood samples are taken at set intervals afterwards. You should remain seated and calm during the test, as strenuous activity can affect results.

### Can I take my usual medicines while fasting?

Please check with your clinician before changing or skipping any medicine. Most people can continue regular medicines with water, but some may need adjusting around a fasting test. If you are unsure, contact the clinic before your appointment.

### How do I book or change a fasting blood test?

You can book, change or cancel a fasting blood test by calling the clinic reception or using our telehealth booking service. Please mention that the appointment requires fasting so the correct time can be reserved for you.
