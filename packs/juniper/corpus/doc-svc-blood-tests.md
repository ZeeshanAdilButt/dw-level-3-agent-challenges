# Blood tests

## Overview

Juniper Lane Clinic offers three blood tests for patients in Port Avery and the surrounding area: the standard blood test, the fasting blood test and the glucose tolerance test. This guide explains what each test involves, how long it takes, what it costs for self-pay patients, who takes the sample, and how insured patients can arrange cover. It is effective from 1 March 2026.

## Test summary

The table below sets out the key details for each test. Prices apply to self-pay patients only.

| Test | Service code | Appointment length | Self-pay price | Fasting needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard blood test | BS-10 | 10 minutes | EUR 38 | No fasting is needed for a standard blood test |
| Fasting blood test | BF-15 | 15 minutes | EUR 48 | Yes, see preparation below |
| Glucose tolerance test | GT-120 | 2 hours | EUR 85 | Yes, see preparation below |

Please quote the service code when you book so that the correct appointment length and fee are applied.

## Standard blood test

The standard blood test uses service code BS-10 and has an appointment length of 10 minutes. For self-pay patients the standard blood test costs EUR 38. No fasting is needed for a standard blood test, so you can eat and drink as normal beforehand. Standard blood test results are ready in 3 working days.

## Fasting blood test

The fasting blood test uses service code BF-15 and has an appointment length of 15 minutes. For self-pay patients the fasting blood test costs EUR 48. Before a fasting blood test, do not eat for 8 hours; plain water is allowed. The fasting blood test must start before 10:30 in the morning, so please plan your fast so that you arrive in time. The first fasting blood test slot each day is at 08:00, and early slots are useful if you want to avoid a long wait without food.

## Glucose tolerance test

The glucose tolerance test uses service code GT-120 and takes 2 hours in total. For self-pay patients the glucose tolerance test costs EUR 85. Before a glucose tolerance test, do not eat for 10 hours. Your clinician or nurse will explain the sequence of samples during the appointment, and you should plan to stay at the clinic for the full two hours.

## Who takes blood tests

Blood tests are taken by Joss Marlowe or Nurse Lind. Both are experienced in venous sampling and will explain the procedure before the sample is taken. If you have a needle phobia, a history of fainting during blood tests or any concern about the procedure, please tell the clinic when you book so that we can plan the appointment with you in mind.

## Referrals and cover

Self-pay patients do not need a referral for a blood test. You can book directly, pay the fee listed above and receive your results in the usual way.

Insured patients usually need a clinician's request before their insurer will pay for a blood test. If you hold insurance, please ask your GP or a clinician at Juniper Lane Clinic to create the request before your appointment. Insurers set their own rules, so please check with your provider whether any further information is required. Our reception team can confirm what paperwork is needed for your particular policy.

## Result times

Standard blood test results are ready in 3 working days. Send-away blood tests take 5 working days, because the sample is sent to an external laboratory for analysis. Results are reviewed by a clinician before they are released, and you will be contacted if any follow-up is needed. Please do not assume that no news means a normal result; if you have not heard within the stated time, contact the clinic.

## Preparation guides

Full preparation instructions for fasting and for the glucose tolerance test are available in our preparation guides. These cover what you may drink, which medicines to check with your clinician before the test, and what to bring to your appointment. Please read the relevant guide before your visit. If you are unsure whether a test applies to you, call the clinic or use our telehealth service to speak with a clinician before booking.
