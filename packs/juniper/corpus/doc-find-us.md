# Find us

## Address and entrances

Juniper Lane Clinic is at 18 Mill Row, Port Avery. The Mill Row entrance is step-free, so patients using wheelchairs, pushchairs or walking aids can reach reception without stairs. Please use this entrance when you arrive.

## Getting here by bus

Buses 14 and 22 stop on Mill Row, 2 minutes from the door. Both routes serve the surrounding streets, so you can plan your journey using the public timetable available from the local transport operator.

## Parking

Parking is free for 90 minutes with a ticket validated at reception. Please ask the front desk to validate your ticket before you leave, as unvalidated tickets cannot be refunded or extended.

## Opening hours

The clinic is open 08:00 to 18:00 on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday. Please check our opening hours before you travel, especially on public holidays.

## Contact

For directions, appointment questions or help before you arrive, call the clinic phone number, 0155 555 0142, during opening hours. Our reception team will be glad to help you plan your visit.
