# What we share with insurers

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Effective from 2026-02-01**

## Purpose

This policy sets out the personal information that Juniper Lane Clinic shares with insurers when we submit claims for care provided at our practice. It applies to all family medicine, child health, vaccination, travel clinic, blood test, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedure, repeat prescription and telehealth services.

## What we share

When we submit a claim, insurers receive only the member number, the service code and the date of the service. The member number identifies the policyholder's account, the service code describes the type of care provided, and the date confirms when the care took place. We use these three items so that the insurer can match the claim to the correct account and process payment.

## What we do not share

We do not send clinical notes, diagnoses, test results, prescription details or other medical records to insurers as part of a standard claim. If an insurer requires further information to assess a claim, we will ask the patient for written consent before releasing any additional details.

## Your questions

If you have questions about this policy or wish to know what information has been sent on your behalf, please speak with our reception team. Patients may also request a copy of their own records at any time.
