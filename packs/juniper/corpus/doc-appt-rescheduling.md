# Moving an appointment

## About this guide

This guide explains how to move or cancel a booking at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. Please follow the numbered steps below so that your appointment is handled correctly and you avoid any fees.

## Before you start

Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours before the start to avoid a fee. If you are changing a booking within that window, please contact the clinic directly so that the reception team can advise you.

A booking can be moved twice free of charge when done at least 24 hours ahead. Any further changes may be subject to the standard policy.

## Steps to move or cancel a booking

1. Decide whether you want to move your booking to a new time or cancel it altogether.
2. Check that your change is at least 24 hours before the start of your current appointment.
3. Choose one of the methods below to make the change:
   - Use the patient portal. Patients can now move a booking in the portal, and the portal also allows cancellations.
   - Reply CANCEL to the reminder text message you received about your appointment.
   - Call the clinic by phone during opening hours.
4. If you are moving the booking, select or request a new time that suits you.
5. Wait for confirmation. You should receive a confirmation message for your new time, or confirmation that your cancellation has been recorded.
6. Keep track of how many times you have moved the booking, since only two free moves are allowed.
7. If you are unsure, contact the reception team by phone before the 24 hour deadline.

## Need more help?

If you have trouble using the portal or the reminder text, please call the clinic by phone and a member of the reception team will assist you.
