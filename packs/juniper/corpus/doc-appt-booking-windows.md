# Booking windows and limits

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how patients book appointments at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, which bookings can be made online or through our assistant, which must be made by telephone, and how many appointments a patient may hold at any one time. It applies to every service the clinic offers, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Staff, patients and anyone booking on a patient's behalf should read this document before making or changing an appointment.

## How the slot grid works

Our appointment calendar is divided into fixed slots. Each clinician has a grid of bookable times for each day, and the length of a slot depends on the type of visit. A standard family medicine consultation occupies one slot, while longer visits such as a child health check, a women's health review or a physiotherapy assessment may occupy two or more consecutive slots. Telehealth calls use their own separate grid so that video and telephone consultations do not compete with in-person clinics for the same time.

Slots are released to the calendar in batches. Clinicians keep a small number of slots unreleased each week so that urgent reviews, results discussions and clinical follow-up can be placed without waiting for the next batch. Released slots are shown to patients as available, and once a slot is booked it no longer appears for other patients. Cancelled or changed bookings return their slot to the grid as soon as the change is recorded, so a patient who checks again may find a slot that was not available earlier.

Some services are limited by the availability of specific equipment or rooms. Blood test appointments depend on phlebotomy time, physiotherapy depends on treatment room availability, and minor procedures are scheduled in sessions that include preparation and recovery time. Where a service has a shorter available window than the general calendar, the booking system shows only the times that can be completed. If you cannot find a suitable time, our reception team can explain which services share a room and whether an alternative clinician or session is available.

Clinicians may also group related visits together. For example, a patient booking a child health check and a set of vaccinations on the same day may be offered consecutive slots so that the family can attend once rather than twice. This grouping is offered as a convenience and is not a requirement. Patients who prefer separate visits may ask for them, subject to availability.

## Who may book and how

Patients may book for themselves, and parents or guardians may book for children in their care. Adult family members or carers may book on behalf of a patient where the patient has given permission to our practice, and the record should show that permission before a booking is made. Anyone booking for another person should provide accurate details of the patient, the service required and a contact number so that the clinic can confirm or adjust the appointment if necessary.

Bookings can be made through three routes: the online booking portal, our virtual assistant, and the reception telephone line. The online portal and the assistant are suitable for routine appointments that are planned in advance. The telephone line is available for all appointment types and is the route for urgent or same-day needs. Where a booking route is restricted, the restriction is explained in the relevant section below so that patients know which route to use.

## Advance booking window

Routine appointments can be booked ahead of time within a fixed window, so that patients can plan around work, school and travel. Our standard booking horizon allows a patient to reserve an appointment with any clinician for a set period into the future, and this horizon applies to in-person visits, telehealth calls and blood test appointments alike. Travel clinic consultations may be booked within the same window, although patients planning international travel are encouraged to book as early as possible because some vaccines require a course of doses spaced over several weeks. Repeat prescription reviews and results discussions follow the same horizon unless a clinician asks for an earlier or later review.

Appointments further ahead than the horizon are not released. This is so that the calendar remains accurate, that clinicians can plan their workload, and that patients whose needs change are not left holding bookings that no longer fit their situation. If you know you will need an appointment in several months, you may set a reminder to book when the window opens, or ask reception to note your request so that the team can contact you when a suitable slot is released.

## Notice periods and same-day visits

Online and assistant bookings need at least 24 hours notice before the appointment time. This allows the clinic to prepare the room, pull the notes, arrange any tests or equipment, and handle the check in process without disruption. A patient who tries to book online or through the assistant for a time less than 24 hours away will be told that the slot is not available through that route and will be asked to telephone reception instead.

Same-day visits are by phone only. If you need to be seen on the day you contact us, call the reception line when the clinic opens. The team will assess whether a same-day appointment, a telehealth call or advice without an appointment is most appropriate. Same-day visits are assessed on clinical need and availability, so we cannot guarantee a particular time. If your condition worsens or you believe you need urgent help outside clinic hours, do not wait for an appointment. Contact emergency services or the appropriate after hours service immediately.

Late cancellations and changes are handled with care because they affect other patients. Please tell us as soon as you know you cannot attend, so that the slot can be offered to someone else. Where a patient repeatedly fails to attend without notice, a clinician may review the patient's booking arrangements and discuss a way forward.

## Limits on upcoming bookings

To make sure that appointments are available to everyone, a patient can hold a limited number of upcoming bookings at any one time. A patient may hold at most 3 upcoming bookings at one time. An upcoming booking is any appointment that has not yet taken place, including telehealth calls, blood tests and repeat prescription reviews. Where a patient needs more than this number, for example a course of physiotherapy alongside a child health check and a travel consultation, reception will help to plan the sequence so that the bookings fit within the limit as each one is completed.

When a booking is completed, cancelled or missed, it stops counting towards the limit and a new booking can be made. Clinicians may agree additional bookings for patients with complex or ongoing care needs, and any such arrangement will be recorded in the patient's notes so that reception and the booking system apply it consistently.

## Slot holds during booking

When a patient selects a slot, the slot is held for the duration of the booking so that it cannot be taken by someone else while the details are entered. A chosen slot is held for 10 minutes while the booking is completed. If the booking is not confirmed within that time, the hold is released and the slot returns to the grid. Patients who are interrupted during booking should start again if the hold has expired, and the system will show whether the slot is still available.

Holds apply to each route in the same way, so a slot held through the online portal cannot be booked by the assistant or by telephone reception until the hold ends. Staff booking by telephone follow the same hold rules, and where a caller needs more time, the team can release the slot and offer an alternative.

## Summary of key values

| Item | Value | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Advance booking window | 28 days | Routine appointments across all services |
| Notice for online and assistant bookings | 24 hours | Online portal and virtual assistant bookings |
| Same-day visits | By phone | Same-day visits, all services |
| Upcoming bookings a patient may hold | 3 upcoming | Each patient at any one time |
| Slot hold while booking is completed | 10 minutes | Any slot selected during booking |

## Questions and contact

If you are unsure which route to use, or if this policy does not cover your situation, please contact the reception team during opening hours. Staff will explain the options available and help you find a suitable appointment.
