# Our services at a glance

## Welcome to Juniper Lane Clinic

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice in Port Avery, and we are glad you have found us. This page sets out what we offer and how long each type of appointment usually takes, so you can plan your visit with confidence. Every visit at the clinic is by appointment, and we do not run a walk-in service, so please book ahead wherever possible.

## Appointment lengths at a glance

The table below lists each appointment type with its typical length. Times are a guide, and our reception team can confirm the exact slot when you book.

| Appointment type | Typical length |
|---|---|
| Standard consultation | 15 minutes |
| Extended consultation | 30 minutes |
| Child health check | 30 minutes |
| Vaccination visit (plus waiting time afterwards) | 15 minutes, plus a 15 minute wait afterwards |
| Travel clinic consultation | 30 minutes |
| Fasting blood test appointment | 15 minutes |
| Standard blood test appointment | 10 minutes |
| Glucose tolerance test | 2 hours |
| First physiotherapy assessment | 45 minutes |
| Physiotherapy follow-up | 30 minutes |
| Cervical screening appointment | 20 minutes |
| Women's health consultation | 30 minutes |
| Minor procedure appointment | 30 minutes |
| Procedure pre-check | 15 minutes |
| Telehealth call | 15 minutes |
| New patient registration visit | 20 minutes |

## Our services

The clinic offers family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescriptions and telehealth. Each service is designed to meet the needs of patients of all ages, from newborn babies to older adults.

## Family medicine

Our family medicine service is the heart of the clinic. Our doctors and nurses look after a wide range of everyday health concerns, from minor illnesses and long-term conditions to general health advice. A standard consultation lasts 15 minutes, and if you need more time to talk through several issues, an extended consultation lasts 30 minutes.

## Child health checks

We offer regular child health checks to keep an eye on growth, development and general wellbeing. A child health check lasts 30 minutes, which gives us time to speak with you and your child properly. Please bring any questions you have about feeding, sleep or development so we can cover them during the appointment.

## Vaccinations

Vaccinations are available for children and adults. A vaccination visit lasts 15 minutes, plus a 15 minute wait afterwards, so that we can check you are well before you leave. Please let us know in advance if you have any allergies or have had a reaction to a vaccine in the past.

## Travel clinic

Planning a trip abroad? Our travel clinic can help you prepare with advice on vaccinations and health precautions for your destination. A travel clinic consultation lasts 30 minutes, so please allow enough time to discuss your itinerary and any health needs.

## Blood tests

We carry out a range of blood tests. A fasting blood test appointment lasts 15 minutes, and you will be asked not to eat before it, as instructed when you book. A standard blood test appointment lasts 10 minutes. Some tests take longer to complete: the glucose tolerance test takes 2 hours from start to finish, so please plan your day accordingly.

## Physiotherapy

Our physiotherapy service helps with muscle, joint and movement problems. Your first physiotherapy assessment lasts 45 minutes, so that we can understand your condition and agree a plan with you. A physiotherapy follow-up lasts 30 minutes and allows us to review your progress.

## Women's health

Our women's health service offers support and advice on a range of concerns. A women's health consultation lasts 30 minutes, and you are welcome to ask for time with a clinician of your preference where available. Cervical screening appointments last 20 minutes, and our team will talk you through what to expect beforehand.

## Minor procedures

We perform a number of minor procedures in the clinic. A minor procedure appointment lasts 30 minutes. Before some procedures, you will have a procedure pre-check, which lasts 15 minutes and makes sure the treatment is suitable for you and that you understand what is involved.

## Repeat prescriptions

If you take regular medicines, you can request repeat prescriptions through the clinic. Please allow the usual processing time and check with the team before you run out of supplies.

## Telehealth

Where it is clinically appropriate, you can have a telehealth call with one of our clinicians instead of coming in. A telehealth call lasts 15 minutes. Our reception team will let you know whether a telehealth call is suitable for your needs when you book.

## New patients

If you are joining Juniper Lane Clinic for the first time, please book a new patient registration visit. This visit lasts 20 minutes and gives us the chance to collect your details, discuss your medical history and answer any questions you have about how the clinic works.

## Booking your appointment

All visits are by appointment, and we have no walk-in service. Please contact our reception team to book, and let them know the reason for your visit so they can arrange the right type of appointment for you.
