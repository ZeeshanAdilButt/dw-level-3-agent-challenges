# No-show waiver guidance

**Document type:** Internal playbook
**Audience:** Internal, front desk and clinical administration staff
**Authority:** Guide
**Effective from:** 2026-03-01
**Owner:** Practice Manager, Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery

## Purpose

This guide sets out how staff should handle missed appointments across the practice, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. It explains when a no-show fee can be waived and when the pre-payment rule applies, so that patients are treated consistently.

## Tracking no-shows

Each missed appointment without notice is recorded against the patient's file on the day it occurs. Staff should check the patient's history before booking any further appointment. A third no-show in 12 months triggers a pre-payment rule, meaning future appointments must be paid for in advance until the rule is lifted by the Practice Manager. Cancellations made with reasonable notice do not count as no-shows.

## Waiving the no-show fee

A no-show fee is waived on proof of a hospital stay or a bereavement. Acceptable proof includes a discharge letter, admission record or a copy of the relevant notice. Staff should record the evidence on the patient's file, note the reason for the waiver, and inform the clinician responsible for the appointment. Waivers are not granted on verbal explanation alone unless the Practice Manager approves an exception in writing.

## Handling disputes

If a patient disagrees with a fee or the pre-payment rule, staff should stay calm, explain the policy, and offer to refer the matter to the Practice Manager. Staff must not promise a waiver or refund that the guide does not allow. Record all disputes in the patient file.

## Review

This guide is reviewed by the Practice Manager whenever booking systems or fee arrangements change, and at least once each year.
