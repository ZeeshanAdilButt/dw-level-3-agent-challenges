# Nurse Tomasz Lind

Team profile for patients and visitors of Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Effective from 2026-03-01.

## Introduction

Nurse Tomasz Lind is a member of the nursing team at Juniper Lane Clinic, a family practice serving households across Port Avery. Our team works together to offer family medicine, child health checks, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Role at the Clinic

Nurse Lind runs vaccinations and works Monday to Friday, so patients can book a vaccination appointment during the working week. His work covers routine immunisation for children and adults, along with the travel clinic vaccinations that patients often need before a trip. He works closely with the clinic's doctors and reception staff to make sure each patient's record is up to date before they attend.

## Background

Nurse Lind brings a practical, patient-centred approach to his work. He has spent his nursing career in community and primary care settings, where he gained experience in preparing patients for procedures, explaining vaccine schedules to parents and supporting people who feel anxious about needles. He values clear communication and takes time to answer questions before any treatment begins.

## Booking and Contact

To book a vaccination appointment with Nurse Lind, please contact the reception desk during opening hours or use the clinic's online booking system. If you have questions about vaccinations before your visit, you can request a telehealth call and the team will advise you on the right next step.
