# Lab courier schedule

## Purpose

This guide sets out when samples leave Juniper Lane Clinic for the laboratory, so that clinical staff can plan blood draws and sample handling across the week. It applies to all clinicians, nurses and reception staff in Port Avery.

## Collection times and rules

The lab courier collects samples at 12:30 Monday to Thursday. Samples drawn after this time will be held until the next collection, so staff should plan draws with the courier in mind.

There is no lab courier on Fridays. Any sample requested for a Friday must either be processed on Thursday or arranged through the clinic manager before the end of Thursday.

Glucose tolerance samples must be drawn before 10:30 to make the courier. Later draws will miss the 12:30 collection and may compromise the test timing.

## Key figures

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Courier collection time (Monday to Thursday) | 12:30 |
| Courier availability on Fridays | no lab courier |
| Latest draw time for glucose tolerance samples | before 10:30 |
