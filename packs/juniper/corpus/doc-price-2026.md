# Self-pay price list 2026

## Purpose and scope

This price list sets out the self-pay fees charged by Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery for services provided to patients who pay directly rather than through an insurer or other funding arrangement. It takes effect on 2026-03-01 and applies to all self-pay services listed below. It is issued by the practice manager and is intended for public use.

## Consultations and child health

The standard consultation costs EUR 55 for self-pay patients. The extended consultation, for problems that need more time, costs EUR 90 for self-pay patients. The child health check costs EUR 60 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code CH-30.

## Vaccinations and travel clinic

The vaccination visit fee is EUR 35, and the vaccine itself is charged on top. The vaccination visit is recorded under service code VX-15. The vaccine itself is charged at cost, and the price is shown before the injection is given, so you know the full amount before you agree to the treatment.

The travel clinic consultation costs EUR 70 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code TV-30.

## Blood tests

The standard blood test costs EUR 38 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code BS-10. The fasting blood test costs EUR 48 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code BF-15. The glucose tolerance test costs EUR 85 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code GT-120.

## Physiotherapy

The first physiotherapy assessment costs EUR 80 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code PA-45. A physiotherapy follow-up session costs EUR 60 for self-pay patients.

## Women's health

The women's health consultation costs EUR 75 for self-pay patients. Cervical screening costs EUR 65 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code CS-20.

## Minor procedures

A minor procedure such as skin lesion removal costs EUR 180 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code MP-30. The procedure pre-check, which is carried out before a minor procedure is booked, costs EUR 30 for self-pay patients.

## Telehealth and prescriptions

A telehealth call costs EUR 40 for self-pay patients and is recorded under service code TH-15. The administrative fee for a repeat prescription request is EUR 12.

## Registration

The new patient registration visit is free of charge. Patients attending for their first registration visit do not pay a fee for that visit.

## Tax and final pricing

All self-pay prices are final and include all taxes. No additional tax is added at the point of payment. Where a price is shown as a fee plus a charge at cost, as with the vaccination visit, the total you pay is the visit fee together with the vaccine cost, which is confirmed before the injection is given.

## Questions about fees

If you have a question about any price in this list, please contact the reception desk at Juniper Lane Clinic before your appointment. Our reception staff can confirm which service applies to your visit and the amount you will be asked to pay.
