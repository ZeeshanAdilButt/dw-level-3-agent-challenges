# The Wednesday walking group

*Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Effective from 1 April 2026.*

## A warm welcome from our volunteer leader

When the Wednesday walking group started, it was just three people and a dog called Biscuit who had strong views about the harbour wall. Today we regularly have fifteen or twenty walkers of all ages and fitness levels, and I still think of it as the best hour of my week.

The group meets Wednesdays at 10:00 at the harbour wall, and we walk a gentle loop along the front and back through the old market streets. The pace is set by whoever is slowest, which is a rule we have never once regretted. Anyone is welcome, whether you have just moved to Port Avery, are recovering from an operation, or simply want company on a weekday morning.

Several of our members first came along after a conversation with their clinician here at Juniper Lane. If you have been advised to build more movement into your routine, the walks are a relaxed way to start. Please mention any new symptoms, such as chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness, to your doctor before joining, and let one of the volunteers know if you need to stop or sit down along the way.

For those who keep track of these things, Ruth, who has walked with us since the very beginning, has completed the loop more times than anyone else and will happily tell you about every one. Her grandson Oliver often joins us during school holidays and has appointed himself the official keeper of the spare umbrellas. Please bring water, sensible shoes, and a sense of humour.

We look forward to seeing you at the harbour wall.
