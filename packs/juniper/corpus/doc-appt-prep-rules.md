# Preparation rules we check when you book

## Purpose

This policy sets out the preparation rules that the Juniper Lane Clinic booking system checks before a appointment is confirmed. It applies to all patients booking family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls at our Port Avery practice.

## Scope

These rules apply to every online, phone and front desk booking. Staff who book on a patient's behalf must follow the same rules. If a rule below applies to your appointment, the booking system will not confirm a slot that breaks it, and our team will explain the alternative options available to you.

## Preparation rules at a glance

The table below lists the preparation rules the booking system enforces. Please read the rule that matches your appointment before you choose a time.

| Rule area | Rule the booking system enforces | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Fasting blood test window | A fasting blood test must start before 10:30 in the morning. | Fasting blood tests |
| Glucose tolerance test days | A glucose tolerance test starts at 08:00 or 08:30, Monday to Thursday only. | Glucose tolerance tests |
| Child health check age | The booking system refuses a child health check for a patient aged 6 or over. | Child health checks |
| Minor procedure pre-check | A minor procedure needs a pre-check consultation at least 2 days before the procedure. | Minor procedures |
| New patient form | A new patient with no CF-12 on file can book only the registration visit. | New patients |

## Fasting blood tests

Fasting blood tests must start before 10:30 in the morning. Please do not eat or drink anything other than water after the time given to you by the clinic. If your test is booked for a later time, the booking system will not confirm it, and we will offer an earlier slot.

## Glucose tolerance tests

Glucose tolerance tests start at 08:00 or 08:30, Monday to Thursday only. Tests run Monday to Thursday because samples leave on the same-day courier, so a sample taken on a Friday or at the weekend cannot be sent for processing on the day it is taken. Please arrive on time, as the test begins at the start time booked and cannot be shifted later on the day.

## Child health checks

The booking system refuses a child health check for a patient aged 6 or over. Patients aged 6 or over should book a family medicine appointment instead, where a clinician can review their health needs. Our front desk team can help you choose the right appointment type.

## Minor procedures

A minor procedure needs a pre-check consultation at least 2 days before the procedure. The pre-check allows a clinician to confirm that the procedure is suitable, review your medicines and answer your questions. If no pre-check has been completed in time, the booking system refuses the procedure slot until the pre-check is in place.

## New patients

A new patient with no CF-12 on file can book only the registration visit. Once the registration visit is complete and your CF-12 form is on file, you can book other appointment types, including the services listed above. Please bring any identification and details about your current medicines to your registration visit to help us set up your record.

## How the booking system applies these rules

The booking system refuses any slot that breaks a preparation rule. When you select a time, the system checks the rules above against your appointment type, your age, your patient record and the requested time. If a slot is refused, the system will show the reason and suggest the nearest valid option where one exists.

If you believe a refusal is wrong, please contact the front desk. Staff can check your record and, where a rule has been applied in error, correct the booking.

## Changes and cancellations

If you can no longer keep an appointment that has preparation requirements, please tell us as early as possible so that the slot can be released and rebooked correctly. Late changes to fasting blood tests and glucose tolerance tests may affect the testing schedule and may need to be rebooked for a later valid day.

## Review

This policy takes effect from 2026-03-01. The clinic practice manager reviews these rules when clinical guidance or courier arrangements change, and updates the table above when a rule changes.
