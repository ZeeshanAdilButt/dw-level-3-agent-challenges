# Photos and recordings

## Purpose

This policy sets out how photos, video and audio recordings may be made at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It applies to patients, visitors, carers and staff, and it takes effect on 2026-03-01.

## Our position

Photos and recordings in the clinic need consent from every person who appears in them or whose voice is captured. Consent must be given before any image or sound is recorded, and it may be withdrawn at any time by telling a member of staff.

## Patient consultations

Patients may not record consultations, telehealth calls or physiotherapy sessions without first agreeing this with the clinician. Where a clinician agrees, the recording is for the patient's personal use only and must not be shared publicly. Staff may record clinical images only when the patient has consented and the images are stored in the patient record.

## Children

Photos or recordings of children under the care of the clinic, including during child health checks and vaccinations, need consent from a parent or legal guardian. Children should be told in simple terms what is happening and their wishes will be considered where appropriate.

## Staff and visitors

Staff and visitors must not photograph or record other people in waiting areas, treatment rooms or consulting rooms without their consent. Where recording takes place for training or quality purposes, staff will be informed in advance.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to a request to stop recording, removal from the premises or, for staff, action under the clinic's conduct procedures. Concerns can be raised with the practice manager at reception.
