# Help with fees

## Purpose

This policy explains how Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery supports patients who find it difficult to pay for care. It applies to all services the clinic provides, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Our aim is that no patient avoids needed care only because of cost, and that patients know what options are open to them before they are asked to pay.

## Emergencies and urgent situations

Reception may waive a fee in a genuine emergency, and the patient should ask at the time of booking or at the front desk. Staff will not assume that a fee will be waived, so please raise the matter directly. If you are unable to pay on the day, tell the reception team at once so that they can record the request and explain the next steps. Where a genuine emergency has already been treated, a member of the team will review the account and contact you about any waiver decision.

## Paying a larger balance over time

Balances above EUR 150 can be paid over up to 3 months, rather than settled in one payment. The clinic will agree a schedule with you before the first instalment is due, and the schedule will be written down and given to you. Equal instalments are normally arranged unless a different pattern suits your circumstances better. If your situation changes during the agreed period, contact us early so that the schedule can be reviewed. Missing an instalment without telling us may lead to the remaining balance being due in full, so please keep in touch.

## Hardship requests

If paying a fee would cause real financial difficulty, you may make a hardship request using form CF-30. The form is available from reception, from our website and by request through the telehealth line. Complete it honestly and include the information requested, as this allows the clinic to reach a fair decision. You do not need to provide every document at once; the team will tell you if anything further is needed. Requests submitted on form CF-30 are reviewed by the practice manager and a clinician, and you will be told the outcome in writing.

Hardship decisions consider your overall circumstances rather than a single figure. Staff handle all requests in confidence, and a request under this policy will not affect the quality or availability of your care.

## Your rights and how to raise a concern

You are entitled to a clear explanation of any fee before you receive treatment, wherever it is practical to give one. If you disagree with a charge or a decision made under this policy, speak to the reception team first. If the matter is not resolved, ask for it to be passed to the practice manager, who will review it and respond to you. Our complaints procedure is available on request and on our website.

## Review of this policy

This policy takes effect on 2026-03-01 and replaces any earlier guidance on fees and hardship. The practice manager reviews it regularly to make sure it remains fair and easy to follow. Questions about this policy can be directed to the reception team at Juniper Lane Clinic.
