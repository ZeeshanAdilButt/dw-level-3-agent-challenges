# Patient voice: one year at Juniper Lane

*Community newsletter feature, public. Effective from 1 April 2026.*

## Why we asked patients to tell their stories

Every year we invite patients of Juniper Lane Clinic to share how their care has felt from the other side of the desk. This year's responses were thoughtful, sometimes funny and often more practical than we expected. We have gathered a few of them here, lightly edited, to show what works well and what we still need to improve. The names and some details have been kept general where the patients asked us to, but the experiences are real.

## A parent's view of child health checks

One parent wrote about bringing a toddler in for a routine child health check after a stressful week at home. She described the nurse who sat on the floor to meet her son at his level, and the way the appointment ran without feeling rushed. Her note reads like a diary entry: she mentioned the car seat that wouldn't click shut, the lunch she forgot to pack, and a phone call from her sister that she had to take in the car park. None of that changed the care she received, and she said it was a relief that the team asked how the family was coping, not only how the child was growing. We have since added a short question about family wellbeing to our child health check template.

## Getting ready for a trip

A patient planning a long trip overseas came to our travel clinic with a list of questions and a little anxiety. She appreciated that the clinician explained which vaccinations were recommended, why some were time sensitive, and how to plan around her own medical history. Her advice to other patients was simple: bring your itinerary, be honest about where you will stay, and ask about anything you have read online that worries you. Our travel team has taken that to heart and now asks each traveller to share their route at the first visit.

## Physiotherapy without the paperwork

Several readers asked how to access physiotherapy, and one patient's experience shows the process clearly. Physiotherapy takes self-referral, so no GP referral is needed. He booked directly after a sports knock that had not settled, and he said the first session felt focused and practical, with a plan he could follow at home. If you are unsure whether physiotherapy is right for you, the reception team can point you to the correct service.

## What we are working on

Patient feedback continues to shape our priorities. Phone access at busy times, clearer communication about repeat prescriptions, and the availability of telehealth calls for suitable concerns all came up repeatedly. We are reviewing each point with the clinical and administrative teams and will share progress in the next edition.

## Thank you

We are grateful to every patient who took the time to write to us this year. Your honesty helps us see our clinic the way you do, and we will keep listening. If you would like to contribute a story for next year, please speak to our reception team or leave a note at the front desk.
