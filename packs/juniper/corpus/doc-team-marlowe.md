# Joss Marlowe, healthcare assistant

Effective from 2026-03-01

## About Joss

Joss Marlowe is a healthcare assistant at Juniper Lane Clinic, the family practice serving Port Avery. Joss supports the clinical team across the clinic's day-to-day work, with a focus on patient care, preparation for procedures, and making sure patients feel comfortable during their visit.

## Blood tests

Joss Marlowe takes blood tests from 08:00 Monday to Friday. Patients booked for a blood test should arrive a few minutes early so that paperwork and identity checks can be completed before the appointment begins. If a patient has been asked to fast before their test, Joss will confirm this at the start of the visit.

## Other areas of support

Alongside blood tests, Joss helps with child health checks, vaccinations, and the travel clinic, and assists the team during minor procedures. Joss also supports patients collecting repeat prescriptions and those joining telehealth calls who need help getting connected.

## Contact

Patients with questions about a blood test appointment should contact the front desk at Juniper Lane Clinic before attending. The front desk can confirm timings and any preparation required for a specific test.
