# Smoke-free site

## Purpose

This policy sets out how smoking and the use of tobacco and vaping products apply at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It takes effect from 2026-03-01 and applies to all patients, visitors, contractors and staff.

## Scope of the policy

The whole site, including the car park, is smoke-free. This means that no one may smoke, vape or use any tobacco or nicotine product inside the clinic building, in the waiting areas, in corridors and treatment rooms, or anywhere in the grounds and car park.

## What this means for patients and visitors

We ask everyone attending for family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription collection or telehealth support to respect this rule. If you need help to stop smoking, our clinicians can talk with you about support options during your appointment.

## What this means for staff

Staff must not smoke or vape on site, including during breaks. Managers will support colleagues who wish to stop smoking and will make sure this policy is explained during induction.

## Compliance

Staff who breach this policy will be reminded of it and may face further action under our conduct procedures. Patients or visitors who do not comply will be asked politely to stop and to leave the site area if necessary. Concerns can be raised with reception or the practice manager.

## Review

This policy will be reviewed periodically and updated as needed to remain consistent with local law and good practice.
