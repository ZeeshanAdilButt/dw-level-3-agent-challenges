# New parents

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains the first steps Juniper Lane Clinic asks new parents to take after a baby is born in Port Avery. It is written for the public and is intended to help you plan the early weeks with less stress.

## Registering your newborn

Parents register a newborn with form CF-12 within 14 days of birth. Please complete the form as soon as you have the baby's details, as it is the most important administrative step in the first weeks. Our reception team can explain what information you will need and can answer general questions about the process.

## Your baby's first health checks

Once your baby is home, book a child health check with our family practice team. These visits track growth, feeding, and development, and they are a good time to raise any concerns you have. Vaccinations are also available at the clinic, and our team can talk you through the schedule so you know what to expect and when.

## Support for you as a parent

Looking after a newborn can be tiring, and it is normal to have questions about feeding, sleep, and your own recovery. Our clinicians offer women's health appointments, and telehealth calls are available if you prefer not to travel to the clinic while caring for your baby. If you feel unwell, low, or worried at any time, please contact us promptly.

## Contacting us

For help with registration, appointments, or general questions, please speak to our reception team during opening hours. In an emergency, always call your local emergency number rather than waiting for a clinic appointment.
