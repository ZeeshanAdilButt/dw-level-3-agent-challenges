# Waiting times

This guide explains what patients at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can generally expect when they arrive for care, and how we manage our schedule so that everyone is seen as fairly and promptly as possible.

## Waiting room

The typical wait in the waiting room is under 10 minutes for patients who attend their booked appointment. Walk-in patients may wait longer, particularly during busy periods, because urgent needs and booked appointments take priority. Our reception team will tell you if you are likely to be delayed, and you are welcome to ask at the desk at any time.

## Booked appointments

Most services, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health and minor procedures, are offered by appointment. Arriving a few minutes early helps us check you in smoothly. If you are running late, please call the front desk so we can advise whether your appointment can still go ahead.

## Telehealth and repeat prescriptions

Telehealth calls and repeat prescription requests are handled through our scheduled call times and dedicated request process. Waiting times for these services can vary depending on the day, so please allow for some flexibility when planning your request.

## Urgent concerns

If your condition is urgent or you feel seriously unwell, do not wait for an appointment. Contact emergency services or go to your nearest emergency department straight away.
