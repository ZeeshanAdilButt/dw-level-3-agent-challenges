# Harbourline Health

## About this page

This page is written for members of Harbourline Health who receive care at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It explains how the clinic works with your plan, what you can expect when you book, and where to find answers when something is unclear. It applies from 1 April 2026 and replaces any earlier guidance on the same subjects. Please read it alongside your membership terms, which set out the full conditions of your cover. If anything on this page appears to conflict with those terms, the membership terms take precedence and you should contact the member services team so that we can correct the page.

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice. We provide family medicine for people of all ages, child health checks, vaccinations, a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, administration of repeat prescriptions and telehealth calls. Many of our patients see the same clinician over a long period, and we aim to keep your records together in one place so that each visit builds on the last. Whether you come in person, join by video, or speak to a clinician by telephone, the same standards of record keeping and clinical care apply.

Booking is straightforward. You can book through the clinic reception desk during opening hours, by telephone, or through the patient portal once your account has been set up. When you book, please say which service you need so that we can offer an appointment with the right clinician and allow enough time. If you are not sure which service fits your needs, reception staff can advise you, and a clinician can review your request and suggest the next step. For urgent problems that cannot wait for a routine appointment, please contact the clinic first so that we can assess the situation. If your condition is life threatening, call the emergency number in your area without delay.

Members often ask how the clinic handles records and privacy. We keep clinical notes securely and share them only where the law permits and where it is needed for your care. You can ask for a copy of your records, and you can ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate. Telehealth calls are recorded in our notes in the same way as in-person visits, and you should tell us at the start of a call if you would prefer not to proceed by video. When you attend the clinic for any service, you may be asked to confirm your identity, your contact details and your medical history. Keeping these details current helps us avoid errors and helps us contact you about results or changes to your appointment.

## How your membership applies at the clinic

Your Harbourline Health membership gives you access to a range of services at Juniper Lane Clinic, and the clinic works with the plan so that you do not have to deal with the paperwork yourself. The clinic bills Harbourline Health directly for covered services, so in most cases you will not need to submit claims or wait for a reimbursement. Direct billing applies to the covered services listed below, and reception staff will confirm at the time of booking or check-in whether a particular visit is covered. If a service falls outside your cover, we will tell you before it goes ahead so that you can decide how to proceed.

The clinic will also tell you about any charges you are responsible for. Members should be aware that the clinic does not hide costs and will always explain what is covered, what is not, and what you will be asked to pay at the point of care. Where you have a question about a charge that appears on your statement, please raise it with reception or with member services as soon as possible so that it can be checked against your membership terms and the visit record.

Some services are linked to a clinician's decision about what care is needed. Clinicians make these decisions on clinical grounds, and the plan recognises those decisions in the way it covers care. Members do not need to argue for their own clinical needs with the plan. Instead, the clinician records the reason for each test or consultation, and that record supports the claim the clinic makes on your behalf.

## What is covered

Your membership covers standard and extended consultations with clinicians at Juniper Lane Clinic, and it covers blood tests that a clinician has requested as part of your care. A standard consultation is the usual appointment for a review of your health, a new concern or a follow up. An extended consultation is a longer appointment for more complex issues, multiple problems or conditions that need careful discussion. Whether a visit is standard or extended is decided by the clinician based on what is needed, and reception will tell you which type has been booked. Blood tests are covered when they are requested by a clinician. If you ask for a test that a clinician has not requested, the clinic will explain whether it can be arranged and whether it would be covered, and in some cases you may be asked to meet the cost yourself.

Physiotherapy is one of the services members use most often, and it is worth understanding how cover works before you start a course of treatment. Your membership covers up to 8 physiotherapy sessions per year. This limit applies across the membership year, and it counts every session you attend with a physiotherapist at Juniper Lane Clinic, including assessments where those are billed as sessions. Your physiotherapist will plan your treatment with the number of sessions in mind, and will discuss with you how the course is likely to progress. If you are unsure how many sessions you have used in the current year, ask reception, who can check your record with you.

The clinic also provides child health checks and vaccinations, and these are part of the family medicine service your membership supports where the plan applies. Travel vaccinations and travel consultations are offered through our travel clinic, and members should check at the time of booking whether a particular vaccine or consultation is covered, since not every item available in the travel clinic falls within every membership. Women's health services, minor procedures and telehealth calls are also offered, and reception will confirm cover for each one when you book. Repeat prescriptions are handled by the clinic team, and where a prescription is reviewed as part of a consultation, that review is treated as part of the consultation for cover purposes.

## Excess and payments at the point of visit

Harbourline Health members pay an excess of EUR 20 per visit. The excess is the amount you contribute toward the cost of each covered visit, and it is collected at the point of care unless you have arranged otherwise with reception. The excess applies to each visit, not to each service within a visit, so if you have a consultation and a blood test during the same appointment, the excess is collected once for that visit. Reception will confirm the amount before you are seen, and receipts are available on request. If you have questions about how the excess applies to a particular combination of services, please ask at booking so that you are not surprised at the desk.

Payment can usually be made by card at reception or through the patient portal where that option is available. If you find it difficult to pay the excess at the time of your visit, please speak to reception before your appointment, as the clinic would rather discuss options with you in advance than have an unexpected conversation at the desk. Your membership terms set out the rules for when the excess may be waived or varied, and reception can explain how those rules apply to your circumstances.

## Membership numbers

Your Harbourline Health member number is the letters HL followed by 7 digits. You will need this number when you book, when you check in and when you ask reception about your cover. Please keep it safe, and do not share it with people who are not involved in your care. If you have lost your member number or believe it has been recorded incorrectly on your clinic file, please contact reception or member services so that the record can be corrected before your next visit. Making sure your member number is correct helps the clinic bill the plan accurately and prevents delays in processing your visit.

## Physiotherapy pre-authorisation

Harbourline Health needs pre-authorisation from the fifth physiotherapy session. This means that the first four sessions in a membership year are covered as part of your ordinary cover, but the fifth and any later sessions in that year cannot go ahead under direct billing until pre-authorisation has been obtained from the plan. Your physiotherapist will tell you when you are approaching this point, and the clinic will prepare the information needed for the request. The request is based on your clinical notes, so please attend your sessions and keep your physiotherapist informed about how your condition is changing. Where pre-authorisation is granted, the clinic will continue to bill the plan directly for the sessions that follow. Where it is not granted, your physiotherapist will discuss the options with you, which may include a change to the treatment plan or a decision about whether to continue as a self-funded patient. In either case, you will be told the outcome before any further session is booked, so that you can make an informed choice.

We recognise that this rule can feel like a barrier, and we try to make the process as clear and quick as possible. If you have any questions about physiotherapy cover, your remaining sessions or the pre-authorisation process, please contact reception or member services before you book your next session. We would rather answer your question early than have an appointment cancelled at short notice.
