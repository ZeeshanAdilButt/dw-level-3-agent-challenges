# Form CF-12: New Patient Registration

Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery
Effective from 1 March 2026

## Purpose of this form

Form CF-12 is the new patient registration form used by Juniper Lane Clinic. Please complete it so that our team can open your record and prepare for your first visit. The form covers family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and our travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## How to complete Form CF-12

You can complete Form CF-12 in the patient portal or on paper at reception. If you use the patient portal, your details will be saved to your record as you enter them. If you prefer paper, ask a member of the reception team for a printed copy and return it to the front desk once it is finished.

## Timing

Form CF-12 must be received at least 2 working days before a first appointment. This gives our team time to check your details and create your record. If the form arrives later than this, we may need to reschedule your appointment, so please submit it as early as you can.

## Who completes the form

For a patient under 16, a parent or guardian completes Form CF-12. The parent or guardian should provide their own contact details and their relationship to the patient. Where a child is attending with a different adult, please let reception know before the appointment.

## Form structure

Form CF-12 has four parts. Please complete every part that applies to you.

| Part | What to provide |
|------|-----------------|
| Part 1 | Personal details: full name, date of birth, address and preferred contact number |
| Part 2 | Emergency contact and, where relevant, parent or guardian details |
| Part 3 | Medical history: current conditions, medicines and allergies |
| Part 4 | Consent: confirmation of how we may contact you and how your information is used |

## Key requirements at a glance

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Form | Form CF-12 (new patient registration) |
| Ways to complete | Patient portal, or on paper at reception |
| Deadline | 2 working days before a first appointment |

## Help and questions

If you are unsure how to answer any question on Form CF-12, please contact our reception team. Staff can explain the form and help you complete it. Please do not leave a part blank if it applies to you, as this can delay your registration.
