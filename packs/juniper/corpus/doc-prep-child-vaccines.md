# Preparing your child for vaccination

This guide is for parents and carers preparing for a child's vaccination visit at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. Our family practice offers child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, and we want every visit to be as calm and straightforward as possible. The steps below explain what to bring, how to get ready at home, and what happens during and after the appointment.

## Before the appointment

Please bring the child's health record book to every vaccination visit. The health record book shows which vaccines your child has already received and when, so our clinicians can check what is due and avoid giving a dose twice. If you have misplaced the book, tell the reception team when you book, and we will look up the child's record on our system where possible.

It also helps to note any allergies, recent illnesses, or medicines your child is taking. If your child has had a reaction to a previous vaccine, mention it when you book so the clinician can review it before any injection is given.

## Getting your child ready at home

Children often feel more settled when they know what to expect. You can explain, in simple words and at a level suited to their age, that the nurse or doctor will give them a small injection that may sting for a moment. Avoid promising that it will not hurt at all, as this can make the experience feel like a broken promise. Instead, let them know that it will be quick and that you will be right there with them.

On the day, dress your child in clothing that makes it easy to reach the upper arm or thigh, depending on which site is used. Offer a favourite comfort item, such as a toy or book, and if your child is hungry, a light snack beforehand is a good idea. Try to keep to your usual routine so the visit does not feel rushed.

If your child is unwell on the day, such as having a fever or a significant cough, please call us before attending. We will advise whether the vaccination should go ahead or be rescheduled.

## Our Saturday vaccination clinic

For families who find weekday appointments difficult, our Saturday vaccination clinic runs on the first Saturday of each month. The same checks and care apply as at a weekday visit, and you will still need to bring the health record book. Places are limited, so please book in advance through reception.

## During the visit

Our clinician will review your child's record, confirm that the vaccine is due, and explain what is being given and why. You are welcome to ask questions at any point. If you would like a moment to think about a vaccine, please say so; we are happy to talk it through and to book a follow-up discussion if needed.

## After the injection

Everyone waits 15 minutes in the waiting area after a vaccination, so that our team can check that your child is well before you leave. This waiting period is a routine safety step and is important even if your child seems fine. During this time, you can comfort your child, offer a drink, and ask any final questions.

Mild soreness, redness at the injection site, or a low temperature are common for a day or two. A cool cloth and fluids usually help. Contact us or seek urgent care if your child has a high fever, unusual drowsiness, persistent crying, or any concern that worries you.

If you have any questions before your visit, please contact Juniper Lane Clinic reception and our team will be glad to help.
