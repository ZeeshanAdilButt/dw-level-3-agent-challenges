# Refunds

Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Effective from 1 May 2026.

## Scope

This policy explains when patients may request a refund for services bought as a package at Juniper Lane Clinic. It applies to all package purchases across family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Eligibility

An unused package is refunded within 14 days of purchase. The 14 days are counted from the date shown on the original receipt. A package counts as unused when none of its sessions, appointments, tests or treatments have been booked as completed or started.

## How to Request a Refund

Patients should contact the front desk in person, by phone or through the clinic's patient portal, and quote the receipt number. Staff will confirm eligibility and process the refund to the original payment method where possible.

## Partially Used Packages

Where some services within a package have already been delivered, the refund for the remaining unused services is assessed case by case. Staff will explain the outcome before any money is returned.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please speak with the reception team at Juniper Lane Clinic.
