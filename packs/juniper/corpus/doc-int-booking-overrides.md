# Booking rule overrides

## Purpose

This playbook sets out who may override a standard booking rule at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, and how each override must be recorded. It applies to all front desk, nursing and clinical administration staff who manage appointments across family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Who can override a booking rule

Only the duty manager can override a booking rule. No other member of staff, including senior clinicians and practice leads, may change, bypass or waive a booking rule on their own authority. If a rule appears to block a patient from receiving necessary care, staff should record the concern and refer it to the duty manager without making the change themselves.

Where the duty manager is unavailable, the request waits for their return or is handed to the on-call manager named on the shift rota. It is not resolved informally at the desk.

## Fasting blood test timing for night-shift workers

The duty manager may book a fasting blood test after 10:30 for night-shift workers. This allowance exists because patients who work overnight often cannot attend a morning slot while still meeting their fasting requirements. Staff should not offer a late fasting slot to a patient on their own initiative. The request should be put to the duty manager, who decides whether the allowance applies and confirms the time.

When a late fasting slot is approved, the front desk should confirm the fasting instructions with the patient at the time of booking and note in the appointment that the late slot was agreed under this allowance.

## Recording every override

Every override is logged with a reason. The log entry must include the date, the time, the name of the duty manager who approved the change, the booking rule being overridden, the patient or appointment reference, and a short plain statement of why the override was needed. A bare entry such as "approved" or "as requested" does not meet this requirement.

Logs are reviewed by the practice leadership at the end of each month. Repeated overrides of the same rule may prompt a review of that rule rather than continued case-by-case exceptions.

## Responsibilities

- **Duty manager:** approves or declines overrides, decides on night-shift fasting slots, and completes the override log.
- **Front desk and administration staff:** identify when a rule may need an override, pass the request to the duty manager with full details, and do not act on an approval until it is recorded.
- **Clinical staff:** raise any clinical reason for a change to the duty manager, since clinical need alone does not allow a staff member to alter a booking.
