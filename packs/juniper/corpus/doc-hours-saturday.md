# Saturday clinics

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for patients of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery who want to know how Saturday appointments work. It applies from 2026-01-15 and is written for the public, so it covers only the information patients need when planning a visit.

## Saturday opening hours

On Saturdays the clinic is open 09:00 to 13:00. This means the reception desk and the clinical team are available during that window, and appointments are offered within those hours only. Please arrive a few minutes before your booked time so that check-in can be completed without delay.

## What you can book on a Saturday

Saturday sessions cover the most common services provided by the practice. These include family medicine consultations, child health checks, vaccinations, blood tests, and repeat prescription administration. Some services, such as physiotherapy, women's health appointments, minor procedures, and the travel clinic, may have limited availability on Saturdays, so please check with reception when you book. Telehealth calls may also be offered on Saturdays where the clinician has capacity.

## How to book

Appointments can be made by calling the front desk during weekday opening hours or by using the online booking system. If you need to change or cancel a Saturday appointment, please contact the clinic as early as you can so that the slot can be offered to another patient.

## When to seek urgent care

Saturday sessions are for planned care and routine concerns. If you feel seriously unwell or believe your situation is an emergency, do not wait for a clinic appointment. Contact emergency services or go to your nearest emergency department straight away.
