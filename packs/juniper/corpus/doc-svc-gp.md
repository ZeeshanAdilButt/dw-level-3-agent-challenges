# Family medicine appointments

Effective from 2026-03-01

## About this guide

This guide explains how to book a standard consultation with a family medicine doctor at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It is intended for patients who want to see a doctor about a general health concern, a routine check, or a follow-up question.

## Booking a standard consultation

Family medicine appointments run Monday to Saturday with Dr Elsworth and Dr Calloway. All visits are by appointment and the clinic has no walk-in service, so please contact reception to reserve a time before you attend. Please arrive a few minutes early so that check-in can be completed without delay.

## Cost and length

The standard consultation costs EUR 55 for self-pay patients. The standard consultation lasts 15 minutes, so please prepare a short list of the concerns you would like to discuss. If you need more time, ask reception when you book, as longer visits may need to be arranged separately.

## Before your visit

Bring any current medication packets, relevant test results, and a list of questions. If you are unable to attend, please let us know as early as possible so that another patient can use the slot.

## Questions

If you are unsure which type of appointment you need, contact reception and a member of the team will help you choose the right service.
