# Physiotherapy

## Overview

Physiotherapy at Juniper Lane Clinic helps patients manage pain, recover from injury, and improve movement and strength. The service is provided at our Port Avery practice and is open to patients of all ages who would benefit from hands-on assessment and treatment. This guide explains how to book, what to expect at your first visit, how follow-up sessions work, where to find us in the building, and what the costs are for self-pay patients.

## Self-Referral

Physiotherapy takes self-referral, so no GP referral is needed. You can contact the clinic directly to request an appointment for a musculoskeletal problem, a sports-related injury, post-operative rehabilitation, or a long-standing condition that limits your daily activities. If you already have a GP referral for physiotherapy, that is also welcome, but it is not a requirement for booking.

Patients who are unsure whether physiotherapy is the right service are welcome to ask our reception team, who can explain the options and, where appropriate, suggest another part of the clinic. Our telehealth calls may also be used to discuss a problem before an in-person visit is arranged.

## Who Leads the Service

Physiotherapy is led by Sam Okonkwo-Reed, who oversees each patient's assessment, treatment plan, and progress. Sam is responsible for deciding the course of treatment and for reviewing how it is working over time. Where a patient's needs fall outside physiotherapy, Sam will explain this clearly and help arrange the next appropriate step within the clinic or with another service.

## Where to Find Us

The physiotherapy rooms are on the first floor of the building, reached by lift or stairs. A lift serves the first floor, where physiotherapy is located, so patients who find stairs difficult can travel to their appointment without climbing them. Please let reception know when you book if you need step-free access or any other help getting to your room, so that we can prepare for your visit.

## The First Assessment

Your first physiotherapy assessment lasts 45 minutes. During this appointment, the clinician will take a detailed account of your problem, including when it started, what makes it better or worse, any previous treatment, and your goals for recovery. This is followed by a physical examination of the relevant areas, which may include checking movement, strength, posture, and how the joint or muscle responds to light testing.

At the end of the assessment, you will be given a clear explanation of what the clinician has found and a proposed plan. This plan may include exercises to do at home, advice on managing your activity levels, and a recommendation for how many follow-up sessions may be helpful. You will be able to ask questions throughout, and you are welcome to bring a family member or friend if that would make you more comfortable.

The first physiotherapy assessment costs EUR 80 for self-pay patients. It carries the service code PA-45, which you may need to quote when making an insurance or reimbursement claim with your provider.

## Follow-Up Sessions

A physiotherapy follow-up lasts 30 minutes. Follow-up sessions build on the plan agreed at your assessment. They typically include hands-on treatment, progression of home exercises, review of how your symptoms have changed, and adjustments to the plan where needed. Each session allows the clinician to track progress and to make sure treatment remains suited to your goals.

A physiotherapy follow-up session costs EUR 60 for self-pay patients. The number of follow-up sessions varies from person to person, depending on the nature of the problem, how quickly it responds to treatment, and the goals the patient has set. The clinician will discuss this with you as treatment progresses, and you will not be asked to commit to a fixed number of sessions in advance.

## Preparing for Your Appointment

To get the most from your visit, we recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows the area being assessed to be seen and moved freely. Please bring a list of any medications you take, details of any recent scans or tests, and a note of any questions you would like to raise. If you have been seen by another clinician for the same problem, bringing their letters or notes is helpful.

If you need to cancel or change an appointment, please contact reception as early as possible so that the slot can be offered to another patient. Late cancellation or non-attendance can affect the availability of appointments for others.

## Questions and Support

If you have questions about physiotherapy at Juniper Lane Clinic, please speak to our reception team, who will be happy to help. For clinical questions during an existing course of treatment, contact the clinic and ask to speak with the physiotherapy team. In an emergency, always contact local emergency services rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
