# Travel bundle

Juniper Lane Clinic offers a travel bundle for patients preparing for trips abroad. The travel bundle costs EUR 95 and combines a travel clinic consultation with a vaccination visit, so you can cover your health advice and any required or recommended vaccines in one appointment rather than two.

## What the bundle includes

During the travel clinic consultation, a clinician reviews your planned destinations, the length of your trip, your existing health conditions and your current medications. Together you will agree which vaccinations are advisable and whether any preventive measures, such as antimalarial advice or guidance on food and water safety, are relevant to your journey. The vaccination visit follows on from that consultation, and the vaccines discussed are administered at the same visit where clinically appropriate.

## Who it suits

The bundle is designed for adults and families planning travel to regions where routine vaccinations may not be enough. If you are travelling with children, please let us know their ages when you book so that the consultation can cover paediatric schedules as well.

## Before you attend

Plan to book well ahead of your departure, because some vaccines need time to take effect and some courses must be started in advance. Bring a list of your current medications, your vaccination record if you have one, and your full travel itinerary. If you are unsure whether the bundle is right for you, our reception team can advise you or arrange a telehealth call with a clinician.

## Questions

For details of availability and booking, please contact the Juniper Lane Clinic reception team in Port Avery.
