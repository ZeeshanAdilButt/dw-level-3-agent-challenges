# Telehealth calls

Juniper Lane Clinic offers telehealth calls so that patients can speak with a clinician from home for suitable concerns. This page explains how telehealth calls work, what they cost and when they are not the right option.

## What a telehealth call is

A telehealth call is a remote consultation held by video or phone with a clinician from our practice. It suits follow-up questions, reviews of test results, repeat prescription discussions and many common health concerns. Each telehealth call is booked under the service code TH-15, so please quote this code when you book or when your clinic records are reviewed.

## Cost and length

A telehealth call costs EUR 40 for self-pay patients. Each telehealth call lasts 15 minutes, so please plan your questions in advance and have any relevant notes, medicines or test results ready before the call begins.

## Joining the call

The secure video link for a telehealth call is sent by text message 10 minutes before the call. Please check that your phone or computer is charged, that you are in a private space and that you have a stable connection. If you do not receive the link, contact the reception team before your appointment time.

## When telehealth is not suitable

Telehealth is never used for emergencies. If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, signs of stroke or any other sudden or serious symptom, call your local emergency number straight away. A clinician may move a telehealth call to an in-person visit if needed, at no extra charge, and this decision will be made to keep your care safe and appropriate.
