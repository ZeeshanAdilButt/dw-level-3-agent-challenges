# Extended consultations

## What an extended consultation is

An extended consultation is a longer appointment with your doctor or nurse practitioner for situations that need more time than a standard visit. It suits patients managing several health concerns at once, people preparing to discuss a new diagnosis, or anyone who wants a thorough review of their medication and wellbeing. The extended consultation lasts 30 minutes, which gives your clinician room to listen, examine where needed, explain options and agree a plan together.

## Cost

For self-pay patients, the extended consultation costs EUR 90. Please check with the front desk when you book so you know what applies to your situation before you attend.

## When to ask for one

You may find an extended consultation helpful if you:

- have more than one health concern you want to address in a single visit
- are starting or reviewing long-term medicines
- need time to talk through a difficult diagnosis or treatment decision
- want a full check-in on a chronic condition
- find it hard to cover everything in a standard appointment

## How to book

Ask the reception team to book an extended consultation when you call or use the online booking system. If you are unsure whether you need one, mention your concerns and our staff will advise you on the most suitable type of appointment.

## Before your visit

Writing down your main concerns, current medicines and any questions beforehand will help make the most of your time. If you are coming for a telehealth call, make sure you have a quiet space and a stable connection.
