# Baby changing and feeding rooms

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains the baby changing facilities available at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, so that parents and carers can plan visits with young children. It applies from 2026-03-01.

## Where to find facilities

The clinic has a baby changing room on the ground floor, close to the main reception desk. The room is fitted with a changing table, a supply of disposable mats, and a bin for used nappies. Staff at reception can point you to the room if you are unsure of the way.

## Feeding your baby

Parents are welcome to feed their babies in the waiting area or in any consultation room that is free at the time. If you would prefer a quieter space, please ask at reception and a member of staff will help you find one. You are welcome to feed your child at any point during your visit, and there is no need to ask permission before doing so.

## Using the facilities

Please keep the changing room tidy after use. Place used wipes and nappies in the bin provided, and let reception know if supplies run low or if anything needs attention. If the room is occupied, please wait nearby, and staff will let you know when it is free.

## Accessibility and help

The changing room is accessible to people using wheelchairs and prams. If you need any assistance during your visit, please speak to the reception team, who will be glad to help.
