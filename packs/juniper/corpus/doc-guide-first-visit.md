# Your first visit

## Welcome to Juniper Lane Clinic

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice in Port Avery. We look after patients of all ages, offering family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. This guide explains what to expect when you come to see us for the first time, so that your visit runs smoothly from the moment you arrive.

## Before you arrive

Please check a few things before you set out. If you are a new patient, bring your photo ID and proof of address dated from the last 3 months, such as a utility bill, bank statement or official letter. We need these to register your record correctly and to confirm that you are eligible to be seen at our practice. If you are attending with a child, bring their details and any vaccination record you have, even if it is only a card or a photograph of one.

It also helps to write down any current medicines, allergies and the main reasons for your visit. Bring any letters from hospitals or specialists that relate to your concern. If you use a repeat prescription from another pharmacy or practice, having the name of that pharmacy to hand will save time.

## Arriving at the clinic

Timing matters, because it allows our reception team to check you in and keeps appointments running on schedule. Established patients should arrive 5 minutes early. New patients should arrive 10 minutes early, so there is time to complete registration paperwork before your appointment begins.

When you reach the front desk, let the receptionist know your name and the time of your appointment. If you are new, you will be asked for your photo ID and proof of address at this point. The receptionist will check these, make a copy where needed, and then ask you to take a seat in the waiting area. Please use the hand gel by the entrance on the way in, and let us know if you need a seat close to the door or any other help getting around.

## Completing your registration

New patients will be given a registration form to complete. It asks for your contact details, your date of birth, your emergency contact, your GP history and any ongoing conditions. Take your time with it. If there is a question you are unsure about, ask a member of staff rather than leaving it blank. Accurate information helps your clinician give you the right care from the start.

If you would prefer to share sensitive information privately, you can say so, and a member of staff will make arrangements for you to speak with your clinician in a quieter space.

## During your appointment

Your clinician will call you when they are ready. They will usually begin by asking why you have come in, what your symptoms are, how long they have been present and what you have tried so far. Please feel free to ask questions at any point. If you do not understand something, say so. You are entitled to a clear explanation of any test, treatment or prescription.

If your appointment involves a blood test, a minor procedure or physiotherapy, the clinician or a member of the team will explain what will happen and check that you are happy to go ahead before anything begins.

## If you cannot attend

If you cannot keep your appointment, please let us know as soon as possible so that the slot can be offered to another patient. If your appointment is a telehealth call, make sure you have a quiet space, a stable connection and your medicines and notes close by. Our team will confirm the details with you beforehand.

## Getting help after your visit

Your clinician will explain what to do next, including any follow-up, referral or prescription. If anything is unclear after you leave, contact the front desk during opening hours and a member of staff will help. For urgent concerns outside our hours, use the emergency services or your nearest urgent care facility.
