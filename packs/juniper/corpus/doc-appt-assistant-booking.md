# Booking with the virtual assistant

## Purpose and scope

This guide explains how the Juniper Lane Clinic virtual assistant books appointments for family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. It sets out what the assistant asks you, how identity is confirmed, how a slot is held while you finish, and what the assistant will not do. Follow the steps below to book smoothly.

## Before you start

Have the following ready so the booking can be completed in one session:

- Your full name or patient identification number
- Your date of birth, which is used for verification
- The service you need from the list above
- A preferred day and time, or a few options you could accept

You do not need to prepare anything else. The assistant will ask for each item in turn.

## Step-by-step booking

1. Open the virtual assistant from the clinic website or the patient portal and select "Book an appointment."
2. Enter your name or patient identification number when asked.
3. Enter your date of birth so the assistant can check that the record belongs to you.
4. Choose the service from the list offered. If you are unsure which service fits, select the general family medicine option and the clinic team will redirect you if needed.
5. Enter your preferred time. You can give a day, a time of day, or several options in order of preference.
6. Wait for the one-time code. A one-time code is sent to the mobile number registered on your record.
7. Type the one-time code into the assistant. Your identity is verified before any existing bookings are shown or changed.
8. Review the available slots the assistant offers and select one. The slot is then held for 10 minutes while the booking is completed.
9. Confirm the booking within the 10 minutes. If the hold expires, select a slot again.
10. Save or note the confirmation message, which contains the appointment date, time and service.

## Information you do not need to give

Patients do not have to give a medical reason to book. The assistant will not ask you to describe symptoms or explain why you want to be seen. If the clinic needs clinical detail to prepare for your visit, a member of the clinical team will ask you separately, through the appropriate channel.

## Verification and security

A one-time code sent to the registered mobile verifies identity before bookings are shown or changed. Never share the code with anyone, including people who say they are from the clinic. Clinic staff will never ask you for a one-time code by phone or message. If you do not receive the code, check that your registered mobile number is up to date, then try again or contact the front desk.

## Slot holds and preparation rules

A chosen slot is held for 10 minutes while the booking is completed. If you step away, the slot is released and may be offered to another patient, so return within the hold period or select a new time.

The booking system refuses any slot that breaks a preparation rule. For example, a service that requires a particular lead time, or a clinician who is not available for that service, will not be offered or confirmed. If the assistant refuses a time you wanted, choose one of the offered alternatives.

## What the assistant will not do

The virtual assistant does not give clinical advice, does not change prescriptions, and does not book outside the services listed above. For urgent symptoms, contact emergency services or attend the nearest emergency department rather than using the booking assistant.
