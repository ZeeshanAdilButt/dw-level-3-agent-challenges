# Getting here by bus or train

This guide explains how to reach Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery using public transport. It is intended for patients, carers and visitors who do not drive, or who would prefer to leave the car at home.

## Buses

Buses 14 and 22 stop on Mill Row, a short walk from the clinic. The stop is about 2 minutes from the clinic door, so most visitors can reach us on foot without difficulty. If you are travelling with a pushchair, a mobility aid or a young child, allow a little extra time to get from the stop to the entrance.

Bus timetables can change, so please check the current times with the local transport operator before setting out, especially for early morning or late afternoon appointments.

## Trains

Port Avery's railway station is a short journey from the clinic by taxi or bus. Trains do not stop directly outside the clinic, so if you are arriving by train, we recommend connecting to one of the bus services listed above or arranging a taxi from the station forecourt.

## Accessibility

The clinic entrance is step-free. If you need help with boarding a bus, transferring from a train or getting to the front door, please let reception know when you book your appointment so that we can plan with you.

## If you are running late

If your journey takes longer than expected, please call the front desk as soon as you can. We will do our best to keep your appointment open or advise you on the best next step.
