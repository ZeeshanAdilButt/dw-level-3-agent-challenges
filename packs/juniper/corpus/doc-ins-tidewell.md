# Tidewell Cover

## Purpose of this page

This page is written for members of Tidewell Cover who receive care at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It explains how the clinic works with your cover, which services are included, how claims are handled, and what you need to do to keep your records in order. The clinic is a family practice, and most of the people who visit us are families, so many of the questions we receive concern children, repeat prescriptions, and the paperwork that follows a visit. We have tried to answer those questions in plain language. If something here is unclear, please speak to our reception team before your appointment so that we can help you prepare.

## About Juniper Lane Clinic

Juniper Lane Clinic provides family medicine for people of all ages. Our services include general consultations with family doctors, child health checks, vaccinations, a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health appointments, minor procedures, the administration of repeat prescriptions, and telehealth calls for suitable concerns. Our clinicians work as a team, and it is common for a single condition to involve more than one of them. A physiotherapist may see you after a referral from a family doctor, and a blood test may be ordered during a women's health visit. We mention this because it affects how your appointments are recorded and how your claims are built up over time.

We aim to keep care local and continuous. Where possible, we want the same clinician to follow your case, and we want your records to reflect the full picture of your health. For Tidewell Cover members this means that your clinic file and your insurance file should describe the same care in the same way. When they do not, claims are slower and sometimes returned, so we ask members to help us keep the details accurate.

## How the clinic works with Tidewell Cover

Juniper Lane Clinic bills Tidewell Cover directly for covered services. This means that you do not need to pay the clinic and then wait to be reimbursed for those services. When you attend an appointment that is covered under your plan, we submit the claim on your behalf and you will only need to settle any part that is not covered. Direct billing is convenient, but it depends on accurate information at the point of care. Please check that your name, date of birth, address, and member details are correct when you register or when they change.

Direct billing does not apply to every service. Some services fall outside your cover, and some require a claim to be submitted by you instead. Our reception team will tell you at the time of booking whether a service is eligible for direct billing. If you are unsure, please ask before the appointment rather than afterwards, because this gives you the chance to decide how you would like to proceed.

## Covered services

Tidewell Cover covers child health checks and routine child vaccinations when they are provided by Juniper Lane Clinic under the terms of your plan. Child health checks are scheduled reviews of growth, development, and general wellbeing, and they are an important part of preventive care for young patients. Routine child vaccinations follow the national programme schedule and are offered at the clinic without additional arrangement. Parents and guardians should bring the child's record book to each appointment so that the clinician can update it and so that the claim can be matched to the correct visit.

Adult family medicine consultations, blood tests ordered by your doctor, and women's health appointments are generally covered, subject to the conditions of your plan. Physiotherapy and minor procedures may be covered when they are referred or authorised in line with your policy. Repeat prescription administration is handled through the clinic and is usually a straightforward process for members with regular medication. Telehealth calls are available for suitable concerns and are processed in the same way as in-person consultations.

## Services not covered

Tidewell Cover does not cover travel vaccines. If you visit our travel clinic for vaccinations needed before overseas trips, you will be responsible for the cost of those vaccines, and the clinic will not submit them as a claim under your plan. This applies even if the same vaccine is given to a child as a routine item in another setting. Our reception team will confirm at the time of booking whether a vaccine falls into the routine category or the travel category. Please note that the consultation that leads to a travel vaccine may be covered in some circumstances even though the vaccine itself is not, so it is worth asking about the split before you attend.

## Excess

Tidewell Cover members pay no excess. This means that you will not be asked to pay a set amount towards each claim before your cover applies. Where a service is covered, the clinic will bill Tidewell Cover directly and you will not be charged an excess. Where a service is not covered, the full cost is payable by you, and this is not an excess but simply the cost of an uncovered service. Please keep these two situations separate in your mind, because they are handled differently when we issue an account.

## Making a patient claim

Some services are handled as patient claims, meaning you pay the clinic in the first instance and then submit the paperwork to Tidewell Cover for reimbursement. This may happen when a service is provided outside our direct billing arrangement or when a claim cannot be processed electronically. Patient claims must be sent within 60 days of the date of the service. Claims received after this period may be declined, so please keep your receipts and the itemised account we provide and submit them promptly.

To make a patient claim, complete the claim form supplied by Tidewell Cover, attach the itemised account from Juniper Lane Clinic, and include the date of each service and the name of the person who received it. Incomplete forms are the most common cause of delay. If you are submitting a claim for a child, make sure the form shows the child's name and member details and not those of a parent. We recommend that you make a copy of everything you send and keep a note of the date you posted or uploaded it, so that you can answer any questions that arise later.

Our reception team can check a draft claim for you before you send it. This is a quick service and it often prevents a return. Please bring the completed form and the itemised account to the front desk, or ask us to review it by telephone or through the telehealth service where this is convenient.

## Keeping records up to date

Accurate records help everyone. When your address, telephone number, or email changes, please tell us so that correspondence from the clinic and from Tidewell Cover reaches you. When a family member joins or leaves your household, please let reception know so that the register of members is correct. Errors in personal details are a frequent reason for claims to stall, and they are easy to avoid if you tell us promptly.

## Member numbers for children

Every Tidewell Cover member has a member number made up of the letters TW followed by 8 digits. This number identifies you on every claim, every letter, and every electronic submission. Each child needs their own Tidewell Cover member number. A child may not be recorded under a parent's member number, even when the child is on the same policy and lives at the same address. Please check that each child has a separate number before attending a child health check or a routine vaccination appointment, because a claim submitted under the wrong number will be returned and must be corrected before it can be paid.

If a child's member number is missing, incorrect, or shared with another person, contact our reception team before the appointment. We will help you confirm the correct details with Tidewell Cover so that the visit can be billed directly and the child's record is kept accurately. Thank you for helping us keep care simple and your records clear.
