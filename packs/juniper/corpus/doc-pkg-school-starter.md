# School starter package

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
*Public guide, effective from 2026-05-01*

## What is the school starter package?

The school starter package is designed for families preparing a child to start school. It combines a child health check with a vaccination visit, so that both can be completed in one appointment time rather than two separate visits. The package costs EUR 85 and is available to children who are registered with our family practice.

## What is included

During the child health check, our clinician will review your child's growth, development, general health and any questions you have about their wellbeing. The vaccination visit covers the immunisations due at this stage of childhood, checked against your child's record. Where a vaccine is not due, we will tell you so and explain when it will be.

## Before your appointment

Please bring your child's red health book or vaccination record if you have one, a list of any current medicines or allergies, and any questions or concerns you would like to discuss. If your child has a minor health issue on the day, let the reception team know when you book so the clinician can plan accordingly.

## Booking and questions

You can book the school starter package by calling the reception desk or using our online booking page. If you have questions about what is covered or whether your child is eligible, please speak to our reception team before booking. Telehealth calls are available if you would like to discuss the package before attending in person.
