# Insurers we accept

## Purpose and effective date

This document lists the health insurers that Juniper Lane Clinic accepts in Port Avery, explains how each one is handled at the clinic, and sets out the general rules that apply to all insured patients. It takes effect on 1 April 2026 and replaces any earlier list of accepted insurers.

## Insurers we accept

Juniper Lane Clinic accepts six insurers: Harbourline Health, Tidewell Cover, Pinegate Assurance, Larkfield Friendly Society, Copperbeech Care and Aldermere Health Plan. Each insurer has different rules for billing, cover and excess, so the table below summarises the main points for each one. Read the notes that follow the table before your visit, as some insurers need steps to be taken in advance.

## Harbourline Health

The clinic bills Harbourline Health directly, so members do not need to pay the clinic and claim back. Harbourline Health covers standard and extended consultations and blood tests that a clinician has requested. Members are covered for up to 8 physiotherapy sessions per year. Harbourline Health members pay an excess of EUR 20 per visit, which is collected at reception.

## Tidewell Cover

The clinic bills Tidewell Cover directly. Tidewell Cover covers child health checks and routine child vaccinations. It does not cover travel vaccines, so families planning travel should discuss the cost of those vaccines with reception before the visit. Tidewell Cover members pay no excess.

## Pinegate Assurance

The clinic bills Pinegate Assurance directly. Pinegate Assurance covers telehealth calls, women's health consultations and cervical screening. It does not cover physiotherapy. Pinegate Assurance members pay an excess of EUR 25 on each telehealth call.

## Larkfield Friendly Society

Larkfield Friendly Society is not billed directly. Patients pay at the time of the visit and then claim the cost back from the society. Larkfield Friendly Society covers minor procedures if it approves them at least 5 working days beforehand, so the request must be made early. It covers up to 6 physiotherapy sessions per year, with a 20 percent co-payment payable by the patient.

## Copperbeech Care

The clinic bills Copperbeech Care directly. Copperbeech Care covers standard consultations, requested blood tests and the travel clinic consultation. It does not pay for the travel vaccines themselves, so those costs remain with the patient. Copperbeech Care members pay a flat excess of EUR 15.

## Aldermere Health Plan

Aldermere Health Plan is a hospital-only plan and covers no routine clinic services. It covers a minor procedure only when a hospital consultant referred it. The clinic does not bill Aldermere Health Plan directly. Aldermere Health Plan members pay an excess of EUR 50.

## General rules for all insured patients

Insured patients must bring their member card to every visit. Reception cannot check cover without it, and a visit booked without the card may need to be rebooked or paid for in full.

The clinic checks cover with the insurer 2 working days before the visit. Patients who change insurer or whose cover has lapsed should tell reception as early as possible so that the check can be completed in time.

Pre-authorisation requests must reach the insurer 5 working days before the visit. This applies to any service that needs the insurer's approval beforehand, and reception will confirm with you when a request is needed.

Any excess is collected at reception on the day of the visit. Please have payment ready when you arrive.

## If an insurer declines a claim

If an insurer declines a claim, the patient pays the self-pay price for the service. Reception will explain the self-pay price before the service is provided wherever possible, so that there are no surprises after the visit.
