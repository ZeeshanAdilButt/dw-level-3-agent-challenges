# Your registration visit

This guide explains what to expect when you register as a new patient at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, and what to bring with you so your first appointment runs smoothly.

## Before you arrive

New patients bring photo ID and proof of address from the last 3 months. Photo ID confirms who you are, and proof of address confirms your home details on our records. A recent utility bill, bank statement or official letter is usually suitable for proof of address. If you are registering a child, bring the child's details and, where possible, the name of the parent or guardian who will attend with them.

## What happens at your registration visit

A member of our reception team will check your identification and address, then ask you to complete a short registration form covering your contact details, your current medicines, any allergies and your preferred way of being contacted. The clinician may also ask a few general questions about your health history so that your record is complete from the start.

## Other services you may need

Once you are registered, you can book family medicine appointments, child health checks, vaccinations and travel advice, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Our team can tell you which service suits your needs when you contact us.

## Questions

If you are unsure which documents you have are acceptable, please contact the reception team before your visit so you can arrange anything you are missing.
