# Cancellation policy 2024

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Document type:** Policy (rule)
**Audience:** Public
**Effective from:** 1 January 2024
**Status:** Superseded by the 2026 cancellation policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how patients at Juniper Lane Clinic may cancel or change appointments under the rules in force during 2024. It applies to all services offered by the clinic, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Notice period

Under the 2024 rule, appointments had to be cancelled 48 hours ahead of the booked time. Patients who wished to avoid a charge were asked to contact the reception team, by telephone or through the patient portal, at least 48 hours before the scheduled start of the appointment. Cancellations made inside this window, or failure to attend without notice, were subject to the late cancellation fee.

## Cancellation fee

The late cancellation fee under the 2024 rule was EUR 20. This fee applied to any appointment cancelled inside the 48 hour notice period or missed without notice. The fee was payable at the reception desk or through the clinic's usual payment methods at the next visit.

## Exceptions

The clinic recognised that some cancellations arise from genuine emergencies or illness. Staff could waive the fee at their discretion where a patient had a documented reason, such as sudden illness or a family emergency. Patients who could not attend a child health check or vaccination appointment were encouraged to call as early as possible so that the slot could be offered to another family.

## Status of this policy

This document is retained for reference only. It was superseded by the 2026 cancellation policy, and patients booking after the replacement date should consult that policy for current arrangements.
