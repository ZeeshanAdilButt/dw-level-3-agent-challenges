# Patient portal update, October 2026

*Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Effective from 1 October 2026.*

## What is changing

Patients can now move a booking in the portal. If you need a different time for an appointment with your family doctor, child health check, physiotherapy session, blood test or telehealth call, you can select the existing booking, choose an alternative slot that is open to you, and confirm the change without calling the front desk. The portal will show you the updated date and time straight away, and a confirmation will be sent to the contact details we hold on your record.

## Who this applies to

This update applies to all patients who already have a portal account. Bookings for vaccinations and the travel clinic can also be moved where a suitable slot is available. Minor procedures and women's health appointments may need a member of staff to confirm the new time, and the portal will tell you if that is the case.

## Things to keep in mind

Moving a booking does not cancel it. Your original slot is released only once the new one is confirmed. If the new time is not right for you, you can move it again or contact the clinic. Repeat prescription requests and existing telehealth links are not affected by this change.

## Getting help

If you have trouble moving a booking, please call the reception team during opening hours and a member of staff will help you. Our reception team can also explain how to set up a portal account if you do not yet have one.
