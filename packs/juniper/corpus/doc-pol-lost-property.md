# Lost property

## Purpose

This policy explains how Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery handles items that patients, carers and visitors leave behind at the clinic or during a telehealth appointment.

## Where to report lost items

If you think you have left something at the clinic, please contact the reception desk in person or by phone during opening hours. Our staff will check the lost property store and the consulting rooms before confirming whether an item has been found.

## How long items are kept

Lost property is kept for 30 days from the date the item is recorded. Items not collected within that period may be disposed of or passed to a local charity, unless the item is of medical importance, in which case staff will follow the clinic's clinical record procedures.

## Collecting an item

To collect an item, bring photo identification and describe the item in enough detail for staff to confirm it is yours. Items may be handed over only to the owner or to a person the owner has authorised in writing.

## Medication and personal health items

Medicines, prescriptions, medical devices and similar items should be reported immediately so that clinical staff can advise on safe next steps. The clinic cannot hold controlled medicines for collection.

## Responsibility

Juniper Lane Clinic takes reasonable care of items found on its premises but accepts no liability for loss or damage to property left on site.
