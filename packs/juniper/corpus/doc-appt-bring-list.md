# What to bring to any appointment

Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Support guide for the public. Effective from 2026-03-01.

## Before you arrive

Please plan to reach the front desk on time. Patients arrive 5 minutes early for a routine visit, and new patients arrive 10 minutes early so that registration can be completed before the appointment starts. If you are running late, please call the clinic so we can let your clinician know.

## What to bring

Bring a photo identification card and your health card or insurance details. If you take regular medicines, bring the packets or a written list of names and doses, including any over-the-counter products and supplements. If you are seeing us about a specific problem, bring any recent letters, test results or discharge notes from other services.

For children, bring the child's vaccination record and any school or childcare forms that need completing. For travel clinic visits, bring your itinerary and a list of destinations. For physiotherapy, bring any scans or referral letters you have been given.

## Things to prepare

Write down your main concerns before the visit, along with when they started and what makes them better or worse. This helps us use the time well. If you need an interpreter or have a hearing, sight or mobility need, please tell the front desk when you book so we can prepare.

## If you cannot attend

Please cancel or change your appointment as early as you can so the time can be offered to another patient. Telehealth calls need a private space, a working phone or device, and your medicine list to hand.
