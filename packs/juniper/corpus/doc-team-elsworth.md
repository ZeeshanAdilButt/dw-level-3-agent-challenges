# Dr Marta Elsworth

## Team profile

Dr Marta Elsworth is the lead GP at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, and she works Monday to Friday, so patients can usually book a appointment with her during the working week. As lead GP, she oversees clinical standards across family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic.

## Background

Dr Elsworth has spent much of her career in general practice, with a focus on long-term care for families and individuals who need continuity of care. Her work spans routine consultations, chronic condition reviews and the coordination of care between the clinic and local services, including physiotherapy and women's health.

## Areas of interest

Her clinical interests include child development, preventive health and helping patients make informed decisions about vaccinations and travel health. She also takes a close interest in repeat prescription management and in making telehealth calls a safe and useful option for patients who cannot easily attend in person.

## Working with patients

Patients are welcome to ask Dr Elsworth about their care during appointments. Where a question is better answered by another member of the team, the reception staff will help arrange the right contact. For questions about this profile, please speak to the practice manager at the front desk.
