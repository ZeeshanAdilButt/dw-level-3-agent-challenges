# CCTV

## Purpose

This policy explains how Juniper Lane Clinic uses closed-circuit television (CCTV) at its premises in Port Avery, and how recordings are handled. It applies to patients, visitors, staff and contractors.

## Where cameras operate

CCTV covers only the car park and the entrance. No cameras are located in consulting rooms, treatment rooms, physiotherapy areas, toilets or staff changing spaces. Signs are displayed at the car park and the entrance to show that recording is taking place.

## How long recordings are kept

Recordings are kept for 30 days from the date they were made. After this period they are deleted automatically unless they are needed for an incident, a complaint or a legal request, in which case they are retained only for as long as that matter requires.

## Who can view recordings

Access to recordings is limited to authorised members of the practice management team. Recordings are viewed only where there is a valid reason, such as investigating a safety concern, a reported incident or damage to property. Recordings are not released to any other person except where the law requires it.

## Your rights

You may ask for information about whether you have been recorded and may make a request to see a recording of yourself. Requests should be made in writing to the practice manager. We will respond within the timescale set by data protection law, and we may need to hide the images of other people before releasing any footage.

## Questions and concerns

If you have a question about this policy or about CCTV at the clinic, please speak to the reception team or contact the practice manager in writing. Concerns will be reviewed promptly.
