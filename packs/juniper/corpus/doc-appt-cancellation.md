# Cancellation policy

## Purpose of this policy

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice serving the people of Port Avery. We offer family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Each of these services depends on a clinician, a room or a piece of equipment being held for a specific patient at a specific time. When an appointment is missed without notice, that time cannot be offered to anyone else, and a patient who needed care may have waited weeks for the slot. This policy explains how we manage cancellations, why timely notice matters, and what fees apply when notice is not given.

## Why notice matters

Our clinicians plan their days around the appointment list. A consultation booked for a family doctor is usually one of many slots in a session, and the time is set aside based on the expectation that the patient will attend or cancel in good time. When a slot is left empty at the last minute, the clinician cannot easily fill it, because most patients who need an appointment on short notice have already been offered an alternative elsewhere. The result is lost capacity for the whole practice.

Minor procedures and blood tests carry additional planning. A minor procedure requires preparation of instruments, consumables and sometimes a second member of staff. A glucose tolerance test requires the laboratory to prepare samples and the patient to follow a fasting and timing protocol that cannot be restarted at short notice. Cancelling these with little warning wastes materials and staff time that would otherwise have supported other patients.

Physiotherapy and women's health sessions work in a similar way. Many of our physiotherapy patients attend a course of sessions, and each missed session can delay recovery. Women's health appointments are often booked several weeks ahead because of high demand. Early notice allows us to offer the slot to someone who is waiting.

Even for telehealth calls and repeat prescription reviews, a cancellation with notice lets us reallocate the clinician's time and keep our waiting lists moving. We therefore ask all patients to tell us as early as possible when they cannot attend, whatever the type of appointment.

## How to cancel an appointment

We have made cancellation simple so that there is no reason to miss an appointment without telling us. You may cancel in any of the following ways:

- In the patient portal, from the appointments section, where you can select the booking and confirm the cancellation.
- By replying CANCEL to the appointment reminder text message you received. The reply must be sent from the mobile number that is registered with the practice.
- By phone, during opening hours, by calling the reception team and asking to cancel your booking.

Please keep a record of the time you cancelled. If you cancel by text reply or in the portal, the system timestamps the cancellation. If you cancel by phone, reception will note the time of the call. This matters if there is any question later about whether notice was given in time.

If you are unsure whether your appointment is still in the diary, check the portal or call before assuming it has been cancelled. Not attending without cancelling is treated the same as a late cancellation.

## Notice period

Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours before the start to avoid a fee. Cancellations made inside this window, or no-shows, will be charged the relevant late cancellation fee set out below. The notice period is measured from the scheduled start time of the appointment, not from the time the booking was made.

For appointments that begin early in the morning, we recommend cancelling the previous working day, so that the slot can be released in time for the day's bookings to be rearranged.

## Late cancellation fees

The following fees apply to appointments cancelled inside the notice period or not attended. These fees are set out in the table below. They are charged to the patient, not to any other party, and they apply to every patient regardless of how the appointment was booked.

Insurers do not pay cancellation fees, so the patient pays them. If you hold private health cover, please check with your insurer before assuming the fee will be reimbursed, because our practice cannot claim it on your behalf.

| Appointment type | Late cancellation fee |
|---|---|
| Consultation (family medicine, child health checks, women's health, physiotherapy, travel clinic, telehealth calls) | EUR 30 |
| Minor procedure | EUR 60 |
| Glucose tolerance test | EUR 60 |

Fees are charged at the time of the late cancellation or missed appointment, and payment is requested before your next booking is confirmed.

## Waiving a fee

We understand that emergencies happen, and we do not want patients to feel penalised for circumstances outside their control. Reception may waive a fee in a genuine emergency, and the patient should ask. Please contact us as soon as you are able, explain what has happened, and ask whether the fee can be waived. Waivers are decided by the reception team on a case-by-case basis, and a waiver is not guaranteed. Please do not assume a fee has been waived unless you have been told so directly.

## Repeated late cancellations

Patients who repeatedly cancel late or miss appointments may be asked to discuss their booking arrangements with the practice. This is intended to support patients who are finding it difficult to attend, for example because of transport, work or caring responsibilities, and to help us offer a service that works for them. It is not intended as a sanction.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please speak to the reception team during opening hours. This policy is effective from 1 March 2026 and applies to all appointments booked on or after that date, as well as existing appointments scheduled to take place after that date.
