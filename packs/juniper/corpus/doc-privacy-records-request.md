# Asking for a copy of your records

## Purpose

This guide explains how to request a copy of your medical records from Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It applies to patients of the family practice and to anyone who has been given authority to act for a patient. Following these steps helps us find your file quickly and send it to you safely.

## Costs and timing

The first copy of a patient's records is free, and each later copy costs EUR 20. Records requests are answered within 30 days of the day we receive a complete request. If we need more information from you to confirm your identity or find the right file, the 30 days start when we have what we need.

## Before you start

Have the following ready so that we can process your request without delay:

1. Your full name, date of birth and current home address.
2. A contact telephone number and email address.
3. A copy of photo identification, such as a passport or driving licence.
4. If you are asking on behalf of someone else, written proof of your authority, such as a signed consent form or a power of attorney.

## Steps for a records request

1. Choose how you would like to make the request: in person at reception, by letter, or by email through the patient portal.
2. Complete the records request form available at the front desk or from the patient portal. Write clearly and tick the boxes that describe what you need.
3. Say which records you want. You can ask for everything on file or limit the request to a period of time, a particular condition, or a particular type of record such as test results or letters from specialists.
4. State how you would like to receive the copy: as a paper copy collected in person, a paper copy posted to your address, or a secure electronic copy.
5. Attach your proof of identity and, where relevant, your proof of authority to act for someone else.
6. Submit the form and your attachments by the method you chose in step 1.
7. Our records team will check your identity and confirm receipt. If anything is missing, we will contact you using the details you provided.
8. Once the request is complete, we will prepare the copy and confirm the total cost if a fee applies. Payment can be made at reception or by the payment options listed on your confirmation.
9. Collect or receive your copy. We will send it by the method you selected and keep a record that it was released.

## If something is unclear

If you are unsure which records you need, or if you cannot complete a step, contact our reception team during opening hours. Our staff can explain the process, help you complete the form, and tell you the status of your request. Please do not send original medical documents by post unless we ask you to.

## Privacy

We handle every records request with care. Your information is used only to process your request and is shared only with people who are entitled to see it. If you have concerns about how your information is handled, please ask to speak with the practice manager.
