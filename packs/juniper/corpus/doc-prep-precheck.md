# The procedure pre-check

## What the pre-check is

The procedure pre-check is a short consultation held before any minor procedure at Juniper Lane Clinic. It is designed to make sure you understand what will happen, that your questions have been answered, and that the procedure is safe to carry out on the planned day. The pre-check lasts 15 minutes and is held with a clinician who will perform or oversee your procedure.

## When to book

A minor procedure needs a pre-check consultation at least 2 days before the procedure. Booking early gives you time to attend, to raise any concerns, and to arrange any follow-up that the clinician recommends. If you cannot make the pre-check in time, please contact reception so that the procedure can be rescheduled rather than carried out without the required review.

## Consent

Written consent is signed at the pre-check, not on the day of the procedure. Please bring any questions you have about the risks, benefits and alternatives, so that you can make an informed decision before you sign. You are free to take time to think about your decision, and you can withdraw consent at any point.

## What happens at the pre-check

During the pre-check, the clinician reviews your medical history, current medicines and any allergies, and explains the procedure step by step. At the end of the consultation the clinician confirms the procedure can go ahead, or explains why it needs to be delayed or changed. Once confirmed, you will receive written instructions on preparation, aftercare and who to contact if you have concerns.

## Questions

If you have questions about this guide, please speak with our reception team or raise them during your pre-check.
