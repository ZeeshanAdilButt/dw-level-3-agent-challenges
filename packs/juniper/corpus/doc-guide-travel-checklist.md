# Travel health checklist

## Purpose of this guide

This checklist helps patients at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery prepare for an overseas or domestic trip. It sets out the steps to take before you travel, what to bring to your appointment, and what to do if you become unwell while away. It does not replace individual medical advice, and your clinician will tailor recommendations to your health history.

## Before you book

1. Check the destinations on your trip. Note each country and any regions you plan to visit, as recommended vaccines and precautions vary by location.
2. Check how much time you have before departure. Vaccines often need several visits to complete, so start early.
3. Confirm that your routine vaccinations are up to date, including those recommended for children in your family.
4. Make a list of any regular medicines you take, along with their names and doses.

## Booking your travel clinic consultation

5. Book the travel clinic consultation at least 6 weeks before departure. Some vaccines need time to take effect, and an earlier booking gives you room for follow-up doses.
6. If your trip is shorter notice than six weeks, still call the clinic. We will advise on what can be done in the time available.
7. Ask whether a telehealth call is suitable for any part of your preparation, such as questions about your itinerary, while in-person visits remain for vaccinations and examinations.

## What to bring to your appointment

8. Bring the travel itinerary with dates and countries to the travel clinic consultation. This allows the clinician to identify the correct vaccines, malaria advice, and other precautions for each location.
9. Bring a list of current medicines, allergies, and any previous reactions to vaccines.
10. Bring your vaccination record, including any certificates from previous doses.
11. Write down any questions you have about your planned activities, such as remote travel, diving, or extended stays.

## During your consultation

12. Ask the clinician to explain each recommended vaccine, how many doses are needed, and when they should be given.
13. Ask about carrying a basic first aid kit and any prescription medicines you may need, including how to keep them in their original packaging.
14. Ask about travel insurance and whether any of your planned activities may need extra cover.

## While you are away

15. Keep your vaccination record and a copy of your itinerary with you.
16. If you feel unwell during or after your trip, seek local medical care promptly and mention where you have been travelling.
17. Contact the clinic when you return if you develop symptoms, so we can review them with you.

## Contacting the clinic

For questions about this checklist or to arrange a consultation, please contact the reception team during opening hours. In an emergency, call your local emergency number straight away.
