# Using the patient portal

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how to log in to the Juniper Lane Clinic patient portal, how the one-time code check protects your account, and how to request repeat prescriptions and manage your appointments online. It is written for patients of the clinic in Port Avery and their families.

## Before you start

Check the following before you begin:

1. You have a registered mobile phone number on file with the clinic, because one-time codes are sent to that number.
2. You know your date of birth and the email address registered to your record.
3. You have a device with a web browser or the clinic's app installed.

If your mobile number has changed, ask reception to update your record before you try to log in.

## How to log in to the portal

Follow these steps the first time you use the portal and each time you return to it:

1. Open the patient portal from the clinic website or the link in your appointment confirmation.
2. Enter your registered email address and the password you created.
3. Select "Log in". The portal will show a screen asking you to confirm your identity.
4. A one-time code will be sent to your registered mobile phone.
5. Enter the code on the screen before the time limit ends. A one-time code is valid for 10 minutes.
6. Once the code is accepted, your account opens and you can view your details.

If the code expires, select "Resend code" and enter the new code. If you do not receive a code, check that your mobile signal is working, then contact reception for help.

## Why the one-time code check is required

The one-time code check confirms that you are the person using the account. Your appointment bookings are not shown and cannot be changed until this check is complete. This protects your medical information and stops other people from altering your appointments.

Never share your one-time code with anyone, including people who say they work for the clinic. Clinic staff will never ask you for your code by phone, email or text message.

## Requesting a repeat prescription

You can ask for a repeat prescription in either of two ways:

1. Use the patient portal: log in, select "Repeat prescriptions", choose the medicines you need and submit the request.
2. Visit reception in person or ask at reception by phone during opening hours.

Repeat prescription requests are made through the patient portal or at reception. Please allow the clinic time to process your request before you need the medicine. If you are unsure whether a medicine can be repeated, ask reception or your clinician.

## Managing your appointments

Once logged in, you can view upcoming appointments, book available slots and cancel bookings you no longer need. If a slot you need is not showing, contact reception, as some appointment types must be booked by staff.

## Getting help

If you have trouble logging in, cannot receive a one-time code or see information that does not look right, contact Juniper Lane Clinic reception during opening hours. For urgent medical concerns, do not use the portal. Contact the appropriate emergency service instead.
