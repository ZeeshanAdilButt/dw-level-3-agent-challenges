# Travel clinic

Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery

Effective from 2026-03-01

## About the travel clinic

The travel clinic at Juniper Lane Clinic helps patients prepare for trips abroad with advice on health risks, recommended vaccinations and practical precautions. It is part of our family practice, so patients can often have their travel health needs handled alongside their usual care, including child health checks and repeat prescriptions. This page explains who leads the clinic, how to book, what we keep in stock, and how travel letters are issued.

## Who leads the clinic

The travel clinic is led by Dr Whitlock, who reviews each patient's destinations, planned activities and existing health conditions. Dr Whitlock works with our wider clinical team to make sure that recommendations for children, adults and patients with long-term conditions are appropriate for the journey ahead.

## Booking a consultation

The travel clinic consultation costs EUR 70 for self-pay patients. Consultations are listed under the service code TV-30 when you book or when your request is passed to our administrative team. Each consultation lasts 30 minutes, which gives enough time to discuss your itinerary, review your medical history and agree on a plan.

Please book the travel clinic consultation at least 6 weeks before departure. Some vaccines need to be given several weeks before travel to build up protection, and a longer lead time gives you the widest range of options. If your trip is closer than this, contact the clinic anyway, as we will still advise you on what can be done in the time available.

To make the most of your appointment, bring the travel itinerary with dates and countries to the travel clinic consultation. A clear itinerary helps us identify the risks that apply to each part of your trip, including any stopovers or changes in accommodation and activity. If you are travelling with children or other family members, please let us know when you book so that each person can be assessed.

## Vaccines and stock

We keep hepatitis A, typhoid and tetanus boosters in stock at the clinic, so these can usually be given at your consultation or on the same day. Other travel vaccines are not held on site and are ordered as needed. Most of these arrive within up to 7 days of ordering, and our team will confirm the expected date with you once your consultation has been completed.

Because vaccine availability can change, we recommend that you ask about current stock when you book. If a vaccine you need is delayed, we will discuss alternatives or a revised schedule with you so that your preparation remains on track.

## Travel letters

A letter for travel costs EUR 25. Letters are issued when a patient needs written confirmation of their medical history, vaccination status or a specific health condition for a visa, insurance claim, employer requirement or other travel purpose. Please state the purpose of the letter when you request it so that we can include the right information. Letters are only issued where the clinical team is satisfied that the content is accurate and up to date.

## Practical tips before you travel

Start planning early, check the health advice for each country you will visit, and keep a copy of your vaccination record with you. If you take regular medicines, ask for enough supply to cover your trip and carry them in your hand luggage with a copy of your prescription. If you develop symptoms after returning home, contact the practice promptly and let us know where you have travelled.

Our travel advice is general guidance based on the information available at the time of your consultation. Circumstances can change, so please check for updates close to your departure date.

## Contacting us

To book a travel clinic consultation, ask at reception, use our online booking service where available, or call the practice during opening hours. Telehealth calls may be offered for some follow-up questions, but a first travel consultation is usually held in person so that any vaccines can be given on the same visit.
