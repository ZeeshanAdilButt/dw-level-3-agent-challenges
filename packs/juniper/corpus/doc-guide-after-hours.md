# When we are closed

This guide explains what to do when Juniper Lane Clinic is closed and you need medical help. It applies to patients of our family practice in Port Avery, and it is effective from 2026-09-01.

## Emergencies

If someone is seriously ill or injured, or you think a situation is life-threatening, call 112 now. Do not wait for a clinic appointment or call our practice first. Emergency services can reach you faster than any other route, and they can take you to the right hospital department.

Examples of emergencies include difficulty breathing, chest pain, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, signs of stroke, and severe allergic reactions.

## Urgent but non-emergency problems

If your problem needs a doctor's advice soon but is not life-threatening, call the out-of-hours line on 0155 555 0199. A clinician will assess your symptoms and advise you on the next step, which may be a callback, a same-day appointment, or a visit to an urgent care service.

Examples include fevers in young children, worsening infections, minor injuries that may need checking, and symptoms that are getting worse but are not an emergency.

## Routine concerns

For problems that can safely wait, such as repeat prescription requests, test result questions, or appointment changes, please use the practice's usual channels when we reopen. Our services include family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration, and telehealth calls.

## Before you call

Have your date of birth, current medicines, and any allergies to hand. Describe your symptoms, when they started, and whether they are getting better or worse. This helps the person you speak to decide how quickly you need to be seen.
