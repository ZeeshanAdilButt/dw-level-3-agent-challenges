# Using your insurer card

Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery

## Why we ask for your card

Insured patients should bring their member card to every visit, including family medicine appointments, child health checks, vaccinations, travel clinic consultations, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health appointments, minor procedures, repeat prescription collections and telehealth calls. Your insurance card lets our front desk confirm your cover before your appointment starts, which helps us avoid delays and surprise charges.

## What to bring

Please bring the physical member card, or a clear photo of the front and back of it on your phone. If your card has been replaced, bring the new one, because older cards may no longer match your current cover. Children who are insured under a parent or guardian should have that parent or guardian's card available as well.

## Telehealth calls

For telehealth calls, have your member card within reach before the call begins. Our staff may ask you to read out the member number and the name on the card so they can check your details before the consultation starts.

## If you forget your card

If you arrive without your member card, our reception team will still see you. You may be asked to provide your details so they can look up your cover, and in some cases we may need to take payment at the time of your visit and help you claim it back from your insurer afterwards. Our staff can explain the options available to you.

## Keeping your details up to date

Let us know promptly if your insurer, member number or address changes. Accurate details help us send the right information to your insurer and keep your records correct.
