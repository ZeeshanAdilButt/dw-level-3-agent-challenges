# Complaint handling procedure

**Organisation:** Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery
**Document type:** Internal playbook
**Audience:** Internal (all clinical and administrative staff)
**Authority:** Rule
**Effective from:** 2026-03-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how Juniper Lane Clinic receives, records, responds to and closes complaints from patients and their families. It applies to every service we offer, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. It applies whether a complaint arrives in person, by phone, by email, by letter or through the patient portal.

## 2. Principles

- Every complaint is treated seriously, whatever its size.
- Patients are never discouraged from complaining, and no patient is penalised for raising a concern.
- Clinical safety comes first. Any complaint that suggests a risk to a patient is escalated to the responsible clinician the same day.
- Our aim is to resolve complaints at the earliest point possible and to learn from them.

## 3. Procedure steps

Follow these steps in order.

1. **Receive and record.** Note the complainant's name, contact details, the date, the service involved and the issue in the complaints log. Use the internal reference CX-2275 as the case series for all complaint records.
2. **Acknowledge.** Internally, complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours. The acknowledgement confirms receipt, names the person handling the case and gives an expected timeframe for the next update.
3. **Check for legal or solicitor involvement.** If the complaint mentions a solicitor, or any other legal representative, pass it to the clinic manager within 24 hours. Do not reply substantively to any such complaint yourself.
4. **Assess clinical risk.** Decide whether the complaint suggests a risk of harm. If it does, inform the responsible clinician and the clinic manager immediately, and log the escalation.
5. **Investigate.** Gather the facts from the relevant records, staff involved and any witnesses. Keep notes factual and avoid speculation.
6. **Decide on a response.** Where a goodwill gesture is appropriate, staff may offer a goodwill gesture of up to EUR 100 without a manager. Anything above EUR 100 requires the clinic manager's approval. A goodwill gesture never replaces a clinical correction or an apology where one is due.
7. **Respond to the complainant.** Give a clear written or verbal response that explains what happened, what we have done and what will change, if anything. Avoid defensive language and do not disclose other patients' information.
8. **Close and review.** Record the outcome in the log, confirm with the complainant that they are satisfied or explain their options for further review, and note any learning points for the team.

## 4. Roles

- **Front-line staff** receive, record and acknowledge complaints, and pass on anything requiring escalation.
- **Clinicians** assess clinical risk and provide clinical input to the investigation.
- **Clinic manager** handles legal references, approves goodwill gestures above the staff limit and signs off final responses on serious cases.

## 5. Records and review

All complaints, outcomes and goodwill gestures must be logged under CX-2275. Records are reviewed each quarter by the clinic manager to identify recurring themes and service improvements.
