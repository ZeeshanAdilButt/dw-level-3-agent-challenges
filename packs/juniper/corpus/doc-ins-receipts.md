# Receipts for claims

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery provides itemised receipts for claims, so that patients can submit them to their insurers or other funding bodies with confidence. It applies to all services offered by the clinic, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## What an itemised receipt contains

An itemised receipt for claims lists each service delivered during a visit or course of care as a separate line. Each line shows the date of the service, a description of what was provided and the amount charged. Receipts are written in plain language so that they can be read easily by insurers and by patients alike.

## How and when receipts are sent

An itemised receipt for claims is emailed within 1 working day of the service or of the payment being taken, whichever is later. The email is sent to the address held on your patient record. If you have not received your receipt within that time, please check your spam folder and then contact the front desk.

## Requesting a replacement or a correction

If a receipt is lost or contains an error, please ask the front desk for a replacement. Corrections are made only after the reception team has checked the original record against the clinical notes. Replacement receipts are marked clearly as copies so that they cannot be submitted twice.

## Using your receipt for a claim

Your insurer or funding body may ask for specific details. Where a question arises about what a receipt covers, the clinic can confirm the service descriptions but cannot decide whether a claim will be paid, as that decision rests with the funder.
