# Staff discount scheme

## Purpose

This document sets out the discount that Juniper Lane Clinic staff and their families may use on self-pay services at the Port Avery practice. It applies from the effective date shown below and replaces any earlier informal arrangement.

## Effective date and authority

This is a rule for internal use only. It takes effect on 1 March 2026. Reception and billing staff must apply the rates exactly as written here.

## Discount rates

The table below summarises the rates that apply to self-pay prices.

| Eligible person | Discount on self-pay prices | Visit limit |
|---|---|---|
| Staff member | 50 percent | One visit per family member per month |
| Family member of staff | 25 percent | One visit per family member per month |

## Conditions

1. The staff rate applies only to self-pay services. It does not apply to services funded through any public or insurance arrangement.
2. The staff rate is limited to one visit per family member per month. Any further visits in the same calendar month are charged at the standard self-pay price.
3. Staff must identify themselves and any family member at booking. Billing staff must record the discount on the account before payment is taken.
4. Telehealth calls and repeat prescription administration are included in the visit count where they are billed as self-pay.

## Approvals and queries

Questions about eligibility or the visit count should go to the practice manager. Exceptions to the one visit per family member limit require written approval from the practice manager before the visit takes place.
