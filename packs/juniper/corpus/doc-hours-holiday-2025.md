# Holiday opening hours 2025

## Purpose

This policy sets out the opening hours of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery during the 2025 holiday period. It applies to all patient contact points, including reception, the clinic's treatment rooms, blood test appointments, vaccination sessions and telehealth calls. Patients should check these hours before booking or attending.

## Status of this document

This document is a superseded record. It shows the holiday schedule that applied in 2025 and has been replaced by the 2026 holiday opening hours table. It is kept for reference, for patients querying past appointments, and for audit purposes. Do not use it to plan future visits.

## Scope

The hours below apply to the following services at Juniper Lane Clinic:

- Family medicine appointments
- Child health checks
- Vaccinations and the travel clinic
- Blood tests
- Physiotherapy
- Women's health appointments
- Minor procedures
- Repeat prescription administration
- Telehealth calls

Where a service has a different schedule on a particular day, the table in the next section takes priority over the general clinic hours.

## Opening hours table

The table below lists the numeric facts for the 2025 holiday period. Each entry names the date and the service it relates to.

| Date | Service | Opening hours | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 December 2025 | Clinic reception and all clinical services | 09:00 to 12:00 | Reduced hours in 2025 |
| 25 December 2025 | All services | Closed | Public holiday |
| 26 December 2025 | All services | Closed | Public holiday |

## Patient guidance

Patients who needed care during the 2025 holiday period were advised to contact the local out-of-hours service for urgent needs. Routine appointments, repeat prescription requests and telehealth bookings for the period were handled in line with the reduced schedule above.

Patients with a repeat prescription due over the holiday period were encouraged to request it before the clinic closed on the preceding working day, so that supply was not interrupted.

## Responsibilities

The practice manager is responsible for publishing the holiday schedule to patients in advance and for updating the clinic website and reception notices. Reception staff are responsible for informing patients who call about the schedule and for recording any changes to booked appointments. Clinicians are responsible for flagging any urgent patient needs before the clinic closes.

## Changes and queries

Any change to the published holiday hours must be approved by the practice manager and communicated to patients at least as early as the original notice. Queries about this policy should be directed to the reception team during opening hours. Questions about the 2026 holiday schedule should be directed to the current holiday opening hours policy, not this document.
