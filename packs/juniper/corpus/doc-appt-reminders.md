# Reminders and confirmations

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Effective from 1 March 2026**

## Purpose

This guide explains how Juniper Lane Clinic contacts patients about their appointments. It is written for patients and anyone who books on their behalf, so that you know what to expect and what to do if a message does not arrive as described.

## Booking confirmations

When you book an appointment, whether in person, by phone or through a telehealth request, our team will confirm the booking with you. The confirmation tells you the date and time of your visit so you can check that the details match what you asked for. If anything looks wrong, please contact the front desk straight away so that we can correct it before your visit.

## Reminder texts

A reminder text is sent 48 hours before the appointment. This gives you time to plan your journey, arrange childcare or time off work, and let us know if you need to change or cancel. Reminder texts show the date and time only, never the service. This is a privacy measure, so that anyone who sees your phone cannot tell from the message what your appointment is for.

If you would rather not receive reminder texts, let the front desk know and we will note your preference on your record. You can still expect a confirmation when you book.

## What to do if you need to change

If you can no longer attend, please let us know as early as you can so that another patient can be offered the slot. You can do this by phone or through the patient portal where that option is available to you.

## Questions

If you have a question about a confirmation or reminder, or you believe a message was sent to the wrong number, please speak to our reception team. They will be glad to check your record and help.
