# Sam Okonkwo-Reed, physiotherapist

## About this team profile

This guide introduces Sam Okonkwo-Reed, who leads the physiotherapy service at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. Patients, referring colleagues and families can use this profile to understand who they will meet and how the service fits within the wider clinic.

## Role and responsibilities

Physiotherapy is led by Sam Okonkwo-Reed, who oversees assessment, treatment planning and rehabilitation for patients with musculoskeletal and movement concerns. Okonkwo-Reed works closely with the family medicine team, so that referrals, progress updates and any changes in a patient's wider health are shared promptly.

## Availability

Sam Okonkwo-Reed works Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Patients wishing to book a physiotherapy appointment should contact the front desk, who can advise on the earliest available times and on whether a telehealth follow-up is suitable for their needs.

## Background

Sam Okonkwo-Reed brings a practical, patient-centred approach to physiotherapy, with a focus on helping people return to everyday activities safely and at a comfortable pace. Treatment plans are explained clearly, and patients are encouraged to take an active part in their own recovery, including home exercises and sensible self-management.

## Working with patients

Patients can expect a thorough first appointment, an honest discussion of goals, and a plan they can follow. If you have questions before your visit, please speak with reception or ask your family doctor.
