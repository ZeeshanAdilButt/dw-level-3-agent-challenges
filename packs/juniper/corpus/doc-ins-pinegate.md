# Pinegate Assurance

## About this page

This page is for members of Pinegate Assurance who receive care at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It explains how the clinic works with your policy, what is and is not covered, and what you need to do to make a claim. The clinic bills Pinegate Assurance directly for eligible services, so in most cases you will not need to pay the clinic upfront for covered care. Where direct billing does not apply, the sections below explain the steps to take.

## Who we are

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice serving the Port Avery community. We offer family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Our clinicians work together so that your care is coordinated across these services.

## What Pinegate Assurance covers at the clinic

Pinegate Assurance covers telehealth calls, women's health consultations and cervical screening when these are provided by Juniper Lane Clinic and are medically necessary. Telehealth calls are a convenient option for follow-up questions, prescription reviews and assessments that do not require an in-person visit. Women's health consultations and cervical screening are covered so that members can access preventive and specialist advice without delay.

## What Pinegate Assurance does not cover

Pinegate Assurance does not cover physiotherapy provided at Juniper Lane Clinic. If you book a physiotherapy appointment, you will be responsible for the full fee, and the clinic will tell you the cost before treatment begins. Please check your policy wording or contact Pinegate Assurance directly if you are unsure whether another service falls outside your cover.

## Excess on telehealth calls

Pinegate Assurance members pay an excess of EUR 25 on each telehealth call. This amount is collected by the clinic at the time of booking or before the call begins. The excess applies per call, so members who book several telehealth consultations will pay the excess on each one. Covered services beyond telehealth calls may be subject to different terms set out in your policy.

## Your member number

A Pinegate Assurance member number is PG- followed by 6 digits. Please quote this number when you book an appointment, when you present at reception and when you contact the clinic about a claim. Your member number appears on your membership card and on correspondence from Pinegate Assurance. If you are unsure of your number, ask our reception team before your appointment so that billing can be set up correctly.

## Making a patient claim

If you have paid for a covered service yourself, or if the clinic was unable to bill Pinegate Assurance directly, you may make a patient claim. Patient claims must be sent within 90 days of the date of treatment. Claims received after this period may not be accepted. Keep your receipts, your member number and the date of treatment with your claim form. Our reception team can provide an itemised invoice to support your submission.

## Questions

If you have questions about this page, please speak to our reception team in person or by telephone during opening hours. For questions about the terms of your policy, please contact Pinegate Assurance directly.
