# Dr Hannah Whitlock

## Team profile

Dr Hannah Whitlock is a member of the clinical team at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. She works Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, and patients can book her appointments through our front desk or the online booking system.

## Background

Dr Whitlock trained in family medicine and has spent much of her career caring for patients across all stages of life, from child health checks and vaccinations to women's health and the management of long-term conditions. Her approach is unhurried and practical, with an emphasis on explaining options clearly so that patients can make informed decisions about their care.

## Travel clinic

The travel clinic is led by Dr Whitlock, who advises patients planning trips abroad on the vaccinations, preventive measures and health precautions appropriate to their destination. Patients are encouraged to book a travel consultation well before their departure date, as some vaccines need time to take effect.

## Working with patients

Dr Whitlock sees patients in person and also offers telehealth calls where clinically suitable. She works closely with colleagues in our physiotherapy and blood testing services so that patients receive coordinated care under one roof.
