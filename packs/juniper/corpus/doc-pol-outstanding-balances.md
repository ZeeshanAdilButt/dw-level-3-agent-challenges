# Unpaid balances

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Document type:** Policy
**Audience:** Public
**Authority:** Rule
**Effective from:** 2026-03-01

## Purpose

This policy explains how Juniper Lane Clinic handles outstanding balances on patient accounts. It applies to all services the clinic provides, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Its aim is to keep billing clear and fair, and to make sure patients know what is expected of them before they book their next appointment.

## Scope

The policy covers any account where a patient owes money for care already provided. It does not change the cost of any service, and it does not affect urgent or emergency care, which the clinic will always provide regardless of an account balance. Staff are expected to apply this policy consistently and to treat every patient with courtesy during any conversation about payment.

## Balances over EUR 100

When a patient's account carries a balance over EUR 100, the clinic will ask the patient to settle that balance before booking any non-urgent appointment. This request is made at the point of booking, either by phone, through the online booking system, or at reception. Staff will explain the amount owed, show how it was calculated where the patient asks, and offer the payment options described below.

Patients with a balance of EUR 100 or less are not required to pay before booking, although the clinic will still send reminders in the normal way. A non-urgent appointment is any appointment that is not needed to treat a condition that cannot safely wait. Staff will make a judgement about urgency and will never delay care that a clinician considers necessary.

## Payment plans for larger balances

Where a balance is above EUR 150, the patient may choose to pay it over up to 3 months rather than in one payment. A payment plan is agreed in writing between the patient and the clinic's administration team, and it sets out the instalment amount, the due dates and what happens if an instalment is missed. Patients who are unsure whether they can keep up with the plan are encouraged to speak to the team before agreeing to it, so that a realistic arrangement can be found.

A payment plan does not stop a patient from booking urgent care. It also does not change the amount owed. Once a plan is in place, the clinic will not ask the patient to pay the full balance before booking non-urgent appointments, provided the plan is being kept to.

## Requests for review

Any patient who disagrees with a balance, or who believes an item has been charged in error, may ask for the account to be reviewed. Requests can be made in person, by phone, or in writing. The clinic will respond within a reasonable time and will explain the outcome clearly. Where an error is found, the account will be corrected and the patient told in writing.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the administration team at the front desk during opening hours or through the clinic's secure messaging service. Staff members who need guidance on applying this policy should raise it with their practice manager.
