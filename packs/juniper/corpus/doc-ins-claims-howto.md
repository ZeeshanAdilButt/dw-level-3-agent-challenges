# How to claim from your insurer

## Before you start

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice in Port Avery, and we work with many private health insurers on behalf of our patients. This guide explains, step by step, how to make a claim for care you have received here. It covers the time limits for each insurer, what you may pay on the day, the forms involved, and what to do if an insurer says no. Read it through once before you begin, and keep it handy for future visits.

Our reception team is always glad to help, but many claims can be completed by you at home in a few minutes once you know the process. If anything in this guide is unclear, please ask at the desk or call the clinic during opening hours before you leave.

## Step 1: Check your cover before your appointment

Start by looking at your policy documents or your insurer's app. Find out whether your plan covers the type of care you are planning, such as a child health check, a physiotherapy session, a minor procedure, or a blood test. Some plans cover consultations but not tests, and some have limits on the number of visits in a year. Knowing this in advance means fewer surprises when the claim is processed.

Next, note the claim window for your insurer. Each insurer sets its own deadline for receiving a patient claim, and the time you have depends on who insures you. Write this date down somewhere you will see it. Section two below lists the windows for the insurers we most often see.

## Step 2: Understand what you may pay on the day

Some insurers ask you to pay an excess, which is the portion of the cost that you cover before your plan contributes. If your plan has an excess, we collect it at reception on the day of the visit, so you do not need to arrange it separately. Please bring a payment method with you. We will tell you the amount at check-in, and we will always confirm it before treatment begins.

Please also be aware that insurers do not pay cancellation fees, so the patient pays them. If you need to change or cancel an appointment, please let us know as early as you can. This helps us offer the slot to someone else and keeps any charges to a minimum.

## Step 3: Ask for a clinician's request when you need one

Insured patients usually need a clinician's request before their insurer will pay for a blood test. A clinician's request is a written note from a doctor or nurse practitioner confirming that the test is medically needed. When a blood test is booked, our team will ask whether you need this document and will prepare it for you. If you are unsure whether your plan requires one, ask at the time of booking so that your test is not delayed.

## Step 4: Complete the insurer claim form

Most insurers ask for a claim form that you complete and sign yourself. Our team can help you find the correct form for your insurer and check that the details are filled in properly. Completing an insurer claim form for a patient costs EUR 40, and this fee covers our staff time to prepare and check the form on your behalf. If you prefer to complete the form yourself at no charge, you are welcome to do so, and we will still provide the clinical information the insurer needs.

When you fill in the form, include the date of each visit, the name of the clinician, the services you received, and your policy number. Missing details are the most common reason for delays, so please double-check before you submit.

## Step 5: Know the claim window for your insurer

The time you have to send a claim depends on your insurer. Use the following list as a guide, and always confirm against your own policy.

1. Tidewell Cover patient claims must be sent within 60 days.
2. Pinegate Assurance patient claims must be sent within 90 days.
3. Larkfield Friendly Society patient claims must be sent within 120 days.
4. Aldermere Health Plan patient claims must be sent within 30 days.

If your insurer is not on this list, please check its terms or contact its helpline. Claims sent after the window closes are normally refused, so it is worth acting early.

## Step 6: Receive and send your receipt

After your visit, we prepare an itemised receipt that shows each service, its cost, and the date it was provided. An itemised receipt for claims is emailed within 1 working day, so please check your inbox, including the spam folder, the next business day. Keep this receipt with your completed claim form. Your insurer will usually ask for both documents, and some ask for the original receipt, so retain it until the claim has been settled.

## Step 7: Submit your claim

Send your completed form and receipt to your insurer using the method it specifies, whether that is its app, an online portal, email, or post. Keep a copy of everything you send, and note the date you submitted it. If your insurer confirms receipt, keep that confirmation as well. Processing times vary, and most insurers will write to you once a decision has been made.

## If your claim is declined

Sometimes an insurer declines a claim. This can happen for many reasons, such as a service not being covered by your plan, a missing clinician's request, or a claim sent outside the window. If an insurer declines a claim, the patient pays the self-pay price for the care received. The self-pay price is the standard fee for the service when paid without insurance, and our reception team can tell you what it is before you decide how to proceed.

If you believe the decision is wrong, contact your insurer and ask for a review. Bring your receipt, your form, and any clinical letter we have provided. If you need help explaining the situation to us, please call the clinic and a member of our team will look into it with you.

## Final checklist

Before you leave the clinic, make sure you know your claim deadline, have your receipt or know when it will arrive, understand any excess you paid, and have a plan for sending the completed form. Keeping these steps in order will make the process much smoother for you.

Thank you for trusting Juniper Lane Clinic with your care. We are always happy to help you through the claims process, so please reach out whenever you need support.
