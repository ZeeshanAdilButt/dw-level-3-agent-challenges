# Hearing and vision support

**Document type:** Support guide | **Audience:** Public | **Effective from:** 2026-03-01

## Purpose of this guide

Juniper Lane Clinic wants every patient to be able to take part in their care with confidence. This guide explains the support we can offer if you have a hearing or vision need, or if you communicate in British Sign Language. Please tell us what you need when you book, and our team will arrange it where we can.

## Support at reception

Our reception desk has a hearing loop. If you use a hearing aid with a telecoil setting, switch it to the telecoil (T) position and stand within the marked area to receive clear sound from our staff. Please ask a member of the team if you would like help finding the loop.

## Sign language interpreters

If you use sign language and would like an interpreter for your appointment, please ask us at least 5 working days before your visit so we can arrange one. Interpreter bookings made with less notice may not be possible, so we encourage you to plan early. Let us know which appointment you need and whether the interpreter should attend in person or by video.

## Written information in large print

Letters, forms and appointment information are available in large print on request. You can ask for this when you book, by telephone, or at reception. If you would prefer a different format, such as an audio version, please let us know and we will do our best to help.

## Telehealth calls

If you have a hearing or vision difficulty during a telehealth call, please tell the clinician at the start of the call so they can adjust how they speak and share information. You are welcome to ask for information to be repeated or sent to you in writing afterwards.

## Giving feedback

If any of this support is not available when you need it, please let our practice manager know so we can improve. Feedback helps us keep this guide accurate and useful for everyone who visits Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery.
