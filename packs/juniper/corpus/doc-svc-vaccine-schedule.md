# Child vaccination visit schedule

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Effective from 1 March 2026**

## Purpose of this guide

This page sets out when families can book child vaccination visits at Juniper Lane Clinic. It is intended for parents and carers who want a clear view of the routine schedule and the extra clinic time available on weekends.

## Routine child vaccination visits

Child vaccination visits are offered at 8 weeks, 16 weeks and 12 months of age. Each visit is designed to fit within the standard childhood immunisation programme, and our nurses will confirm which vaccines are due at each appointment. If your child has missed a visit, please contact the reception team and we will arrange a catch-up appointment as soon as possible.

## Saturday vaccination clinic

The Saturday vaccination clinic runs on the first Saturday of each month, giving families who cannot attend during the working week an alternative option. Appointments for the Saturday clinic are limited, so we recommend booking early through reception or the patient portal.

## Before your appointment

Please bring your child's personal health record, any letters from other providers, and a list of current medicines. If your child has a fever or is unwell on the day, call the clinic before attending so we can advise whether the visit should go ahead or be rescheduled.

## Questions

If you are unsure which visit your child needs next, our team can help. Speak to reception, or book a telehealth call with one of our clinicians for guidance.
