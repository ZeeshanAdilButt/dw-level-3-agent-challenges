# How the virtual assistant treats your data

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Effective from 2026-02-01**

## Purpose of this policy

This policy explains how the virtual assistant on the Juniper Lane Clinic website and patient portal handles the information you type into it. The assistant can help you find appointment types, understand our travel clinic, child health checks, vaccinations, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription requests and telehealth calls. It is not a substitute for speaking with a clinician.

## What the assistant collects

When you use the assistant, the text of your messages and the assistant's replies are recorded as a conversation. Please avoid typing full medical histories, test results or identifying details unless a staff member has asked you to do so through a secure route.

## How long conversations are kept

Assistant conversations are kept for 90 days. After that period, the conversation records are deleted from the assistant system. Information you give us through formal channels, such as a booked appointment or a clinical record, is handled under our separate patient records policy instead.

## Who can see your conversations

Conversations are visible only to authorised clinic staff who maintain the assistant service, and only where needed to fix a fault or review a complaint. We do not use assistant conversations for marketing.

## Urgent matters

The assistant is not monitored in real time. If you have a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately. For urgent clinical concerns during opening hours, contact the reception desk by telephone.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, ask the reception team at the clinic or use the contact details on our website.
