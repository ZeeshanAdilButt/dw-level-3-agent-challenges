# Home visits

## Policy statement

Juniper Lane Clinic does not offer home visits. Our family practice provides care from the clinic premises in Port Avery, and clinicians do not attend patients at their home, workplace or residential care setting.

## What this means for patients

If you are unable to travel to the clinic, please contact us before deciding on another course of action. Many concerns can be discussed through a telehealth call, which lets you speak with a clinician from home. Where a physical examination is needed, our reception team can help you arrange an appointment at a time and in a format that suits your circumstances.

## Where to turn for urgent help

If you or someone in your care has a medical emergency or a sudden change in condition, do not wait for a clinic appointment. Call emergency services immediately. For urgent but non-emergency concerns outside clinic hours, contact your local after-hours service or nearest emergency department in Port Avery.

## Questions

Patients who need help finding suitable care, or who are unsure whether their situation qualifies for a telehealth call, are welcome to speak with our reception team during opening hours. Our staff will explain the options available and point you toward the most appropriate service.
