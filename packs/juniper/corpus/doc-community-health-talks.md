# Community health talks

## About the talks

Juniper Lane Clinic runs free community health talks for residents of Port Avery and the surrounding area. The talks are designed as a practical guide to everyday health, covering topics that families and individuals often ask our clinicians about during appointments. Each session is led by a member of our practice team and is open to anyone who would like to attend.

## When and where

Health talks run on the first Tuesday of the month at 18:30. Sessions take place in the main meeting room at the clinic, and seating is available on a first come basis. Please arrive a few minutes early if you would like a good place to sit.

## Topics we cover

Recent sessions have included child health checks and what to expect at each stage, the basics of vaccinations and how our travel clinic can help you prepare for trips abroad, understanding routine blood tests, simple exercises recommended by our physiotherapy team, and questions about women's health. Our team can also explain how repeat prescriptions are handled and when a telehealth call may be a suitable alternative to an in-person visit.

## Asking questions

Every talk ends with an open question period. Questions do not need to be submitted in advance, but if you have a specific concern about your own care, we recommend booking an appointment so that your clinician can review your records properly. The talks offer general information only and do not replace individual medical advice.

## Getting involved

If you have a topic you would like us to cover, or if you are part of a local group that would like a talk for its members, please contact the front desk and ask for the health talks coordinator. We welcome suggestions from patients and community members.
