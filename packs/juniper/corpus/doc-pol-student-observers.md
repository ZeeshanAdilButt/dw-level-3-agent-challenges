# Student observers

## Purpose

This policy sets out how students may observe care at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It applies to all clinical areas, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Consent

A student observer sits in only with the patient's consent. Staff will ask the patient before the consultation begins and will explain who the student is and what their role involves. A patient may decline at any point, and the clinician will then ask the student to leave the room without any effect on the care the patient receives.

## Conduct

Student observers must follow the instructions of the supervising clinician at all times. They must not record audio, video or images of patients, examine anyone, or take part in treatment unless the supervising clinician has explicitly authorised it and the patient has agreed to that specific activity. Students must keep all information they hear or see confidential, in line with the clinic's privacy arrangements.

## Telehealth and sensitive appointments

For telehealth calls, the observer's presence must be stated at the start of the call and the patient's consent recorded before the student joins. Appointments relating to women's health, intimate examinations and minor procedures will normally be observed only where the patient has given specific agreement, and the clinician may decide that no observer is appropriate.

## Responsibility

The clinician in charge of each session is responsible for confirming consent, managing the observer's presence and ending the observation if the patient's wishes or comfort require it. Concerns about a student's conduct should be raised with the practice manager.
