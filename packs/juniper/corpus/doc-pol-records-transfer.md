# Moving to another practice

## Purpose

This policy explains how patients of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can move their medical records to another practice. It applies to all patients registered with the clinic, including children whose records are held under a parent or guardian.

## Requesting your records

Patients may ask for their records to be transferred at any time by completing the transfer form at reception, by writing to the clinic, or by asking the team during a telehealth call. Requests should state the name of the receiving practice and, where known, its contact details so that the transfer can be sent without delay.

## Timing

Records move to another practice within 10 working days of a request. Where a request is incomplete, for example because the receiving practice's details are missing, the 10 working day period begins once the missing information has been received.

## Information included

The transfer includes the patient's clinical notes, test results, vaccination history, repeat prescription details and any referral letters held on file. Travel clinic records and women's health records are included unless the patient asks for them to be excluded.

## Ongoing care and repeat prescriptions

Patients should continue to attend Juniper Lane Clinic for care until their new practice confirms that it has accepted their registration. Active repeat prescriptions are not transferred automatically, so patients should tell the clinic which medicines they need so that arrangements can be made with their new practice.

## Privacy

Records are shared only with the receiving practice named by the patient and are handled in line with applicable data protection rules. Patients who have concerns about a transfer may raise them with the clinic team at any time.
