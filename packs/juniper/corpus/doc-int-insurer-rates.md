# Insurer payment terms

## Purpose

This document sets out the payment terms agreed with each insurer that Juniper Lane Clinic bills for care provided in Port Avery. Front desk, billing and finance staff should use these terms when following up on outstanding claims and forecasting cash flow.

## Scope

These terms apply to all insurer claims submitted from the clinic's family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth services.

## Key terms

Insurer claims are sent in a batch every Friday at 14:00, so claims submitted after that time roll into the following week's batch. The table below lists the payment terms for each insurer.

| Insurer | Term | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Harbourline Health | Payment rate against list price | 92 percent |
| Larkfield Friendly Society | Time to pay claims | 45 days |
| Tidewell Cover | Time to pay claims | 14 days |

## Actions for staff

Billing staff should check each claim against the insurer's rate before it enters the Friday batch. Any claim that has not been paid within the stated period for its insurer should be escalated to the finance lead. Staff must not agree alternative rates or payment periods with any insurer without written approval from the practice manager.

## Review

This document takes effect on 2026-04-01 and remains in force until replaced. The practice manager reviews these terms whenever an insurer notifies the clinic of a change.
