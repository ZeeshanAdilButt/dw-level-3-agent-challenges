# Child health checks

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Document type:** Product guide
**Audience:** Public
**Effective from:** 2026-03-01

## What a child health check is

A child health check is a planned appointment at Juniper Lane Clinic that looks at how your child is growing and developing. The check covers weight and height, general health, feeding or sleep questions, development milestones, and any concerns you would like the doctor or nurse to look at. It is a good time to ask questions, and it gives our team a chance to spot anything early and suggest next steps, such as vaccinations, a referral, or a follow-up visit.

The check is run by our family medicine team, and the appointment is booked through the normal clinic booking system. Child health checks are offered as part of our family medicine service, which also includes vaccinations, blood tests, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescriptions, and telehealth calls.

## Who the check is for

Child health checks are for children aged 0 to 5. This covers newborn babies through to children in the year before they start school. If your child is outside this age range, please speak to our reception team, who can advise you on the most suitable appointment type.

The booking system refuses a child health check for a patient aged 6 or over. This is so that each child is booked into the right kind of appointment, with the right length of time and the right clinical focus. If you try to book a child health check for an older child and the system does not allow it, please do not be concerned. Call or visit reception and we will help you find an appropriate appointment.

## Who needs to attend

A parent or legal guardian must attend the child health check with the child. This is required so that the clinician can discuss the child's history, answer your questions, and agree on any follow-up with the person who is responsible for the child's care. If you cannot attend in person, please contact us before booking so we can discuss the options with you.

When you attend, please bring the child's red book or any health records you have, a list of any medicines the child takes, and a note of any questions or concerns you want to raise. If your child has seen another clinic or hospital recently, please bring any letters you received.

## How long it takes

A child health check lasts 30 minutes. This allows time for the examination, a discussion about your child's health and development, and any questions you may have. Please arrive a few minutes early so that the appointment can begin on time. If you are running late, please call reception as soon as you can, as a late arrival may mean the appointment has to be shortened or rebooked.

## Cost

For self-pay patients, the child health check costs EUR 60. Please ask reception about payment options when you book. If your child is covered by another arrangement, our team can explain what applies to your situation, although we cannot quote charges that are not listed here.

## Service code

The child health check has the service code CH-30. You may see this code on booking confirmations, invoices, and referral letters. If you need to refer to this service when speaking to another provider or organisation, please quote this code.

## Booking and questions

To book a child health check, use the online booking system or call reception during opening hours. If you have a question about whether your child is eligible, or you are unsure which appointment is right for your child's needs, please contact us before booking and our team will be glad to help. If your child becomes unwell between planned checks, please do not wait for the next appointment. Contact the clinic promptly, and if the situation is urgent, seek help from emergency services.
