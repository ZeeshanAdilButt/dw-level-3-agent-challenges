# Missed appointments

**Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery**
**Effective from 1 March 2026**

## Purpose

This policy explains what happens when a patient misses a booked appointment at Juniper Lane Clinic. It applies to all services we offer, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and travel clinic visits, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls.

## Cancelling an appointment

We ask you to tell us as early as possible if you cannot attend. You can cancel by phone or through the patient portal. Early notice allows us to offer the time to another patient who needs care.

## No-show fee

A missed appointment with no cancellation is charged EUR 45. This fee applies to any booked appointment you do not attend and do not cancel before it starts. The fee is added to your account and is payable at your next visit or can be settled by contacting our reception team.

## Repeat no-shows

If you have two no-shows in 12 months, we will ask for payment when booking any further appointment. This means you will pre-pay for future bookings until the requirement is reviewed by the practice. We apply this step to help keep appointment times available for everyone.

## Exceptions

We understand that emergencies happen. If you were unable to attend or cancel because of illness, a family emergency or another circumstance outside your control, please contact reception. Each case is reviewed individually, and the practice may waive the fee at its discretion.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please speak with our reception team during opening hours.
