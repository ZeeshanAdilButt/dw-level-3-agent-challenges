# Flu clinic Saturdays 2026

*Effective from 5 October 2026*

## Announcement

Juniper Lane Clinic will run walk-in flu vaccination sessions on Saturdays 14 and 21 November 2026, from 09:00 to 13:00. The sessions are open to patients registered with the clinic and to members of the Port Avery community who wish to be vaccinated against influenza this season.

## Who can attend

Patients of all ages are welcome. Children and adults attending for their first flu vaccination are asked to book a short appointment through reception so the nursing team can review their medical history before the injection. Anyone who has had a reaction to a previous vaccine should speak with a clinician before attending.

## How to attend

No prior booking is needed for routine vaccinations on the day, although we encourage patients to arrive early to avoid waiting. Please bring your health card or medicare details if you have them, and wear clothing that allows easy access to the upper arm.

## Questions

If you have questions about eligibility, timing or side effects, please call the front desk during opening hours or request a telehealth call with one of our clinicians. Patients who are unwell on the day of their visit should phone ahead rather than attend in person.
