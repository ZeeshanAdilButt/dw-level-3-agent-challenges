# Pharmacy next door

## Where to collect your prescriptions

The nearest pharmacy to Juniper Lane Clinic is Mill Row Pharmacy, next door to the clinic. Patients who receive a prescription during a visit, a telehealth call or a repeat prescription review can usually collect their medicines there without travelling far.

## Sending prescriptions to Mill Row Pharmacy

When your clinician issues a prescription, ask the team which pharmacy you would like it sent to. If you choose Mill Row Pharmacy, the prescription can be sent electronically so that it is ready when you arrive. If you are unsure whether a medicine is in stock, please call the pharmacy directly before you visit.

## Repeat prescriptions

Repeat prescriptions are processed by our clinic team. Once approved, they are sent on to your chosen pharmacy. Please allow the usual processing time before you go to collect them, and let the reception team know if you change pharmacies.

## Questions about your medicines

For advice about how to take a medicine, possible side effects or interactions with other treatments, speak with the pharmacist at Mill Row Pharmacy or contact a member of the clinic team. If you feel unwell or have an urgent concern, do not wait for a repeat order; contact the clinic or seek urgent care straight away.
