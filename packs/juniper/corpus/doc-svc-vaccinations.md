# Vaccinations

Effective from 1 March 2026

## About this guide

This guide explains how vaccination visits work at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, what a visit covers, how long it takes, how long you wait afterwards, and how the vaccine itself is charged. It is written for patients booking routine childhood vaccines, travel vaccines and other injections offered by our family practice.

## What a vaccination visit covers

A vaccination visit is charged at a visit fee of EUR 35. The visit fee is the charge for the appointment itself, including the clinician checking your details, explaining the vaccine and giving the injection. The vaccine itself is charged on top of the visit fee, so your total cost is the visit fee plus the price of the vaccine.

Each vaccination visit is recorded under service code VX-15. You may see this code on your receipt or on any insurance or referral paperwork, so please keep it for your records.

One vaccination visit covers up to 2 injections for the visit fee. If you need more than two injections at the same appointment, the additional injections may be treated as a separate charge, and the reception team will tell you before you go ahead.

## How long the visit takes

The vaccination visit itself lasts 15 minutes. This time includes the check before the injection, the injection, and a short conversation about any questions you have about the vaccine.

Please allow for additional time afterwards, as explained below.

## Waiting time after a vaccination

Everyone waits 15 minutes in the waiting area after a vaccination. This waiting period is a safety measure, so that our staff can check that you feel well before you leave the clinic. Most people feel fine during this time, but a small number of people may have a brief reaction, and it is important that we are able to respond quickly if this happens.

Please plan your day so that you can stay for the full 15 minutes. If you have a young child, please bring a companion who can help supervise them during the wait. If you are unwell or feel faint during the waiting period, please tell the reception desk straight away.

## How the vaccine is charged

The vaccine itself is charged at cost, and the price is shown before the injection is given. This means you will always know the exact amount for the vaccine before the clinician prepares it. If you would prefer not to proceed once you see the price, you are free to decline the injection, and you will still be charged the visit fee for the appointment.

If you have questions about the cost of a particular vaccine, please ask the reception team when you book, or contact the clinic before your appointment.

## Booking and preparation

When you book a vaccination visit, please tell us which vaccines you need and whether you have a vaccination record to bring with you. Bringing your record helps us check which doses are due and avoids unnecessary repeat injections.

On the day of your visit, please arrive a few minutes early, bring any relevant paperwork, and let the clinician know about any allergies or recent illnesses. If you are unsure whether a vaccine is suitable for you, speak to the clinician before the appointment.

## Questions

If you have any questions about vaccination visits, the waiting time or charges, please contact Juniper Lane Clinic reception during opening hours. Our team is happy to help you understand what to expect before your visit.
