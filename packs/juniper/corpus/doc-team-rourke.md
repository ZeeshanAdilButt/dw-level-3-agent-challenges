# Nurse Aisling Rourke

## Team Profile

Nurse Aisling Rourke is a registered nurse at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. She leads our women's health service and works Monday to Thursday, so patients can book appointments with her during those days.

## Role at the Clinic

Nurse Rourke runs women's health appointments, which include routine consultations, contraception advice, and support with family planning questions. Cervical screening is done by Nurse Rourke in a private room, with the patient's comfort and privacy as the first priority. Patients who are nervous about screening are welcome to ask questions before the appointment begins, and she will explain each step in plain language.

## Background

Nurse Rourke has spent many years caring for patients across primary care settings. Her approach is calm, practical, and centred on listening. She works closely with our general practitioners, so that when a patient needs further tests or a referral, the handover is clear and unhurried.

## Booking and Contact

Appointments with Nurse Rourke can be booked through the front desk or by telephone during opening hours. If you are unsure whether a women's health appointment is the right choice for your needs, please contact the clinic and our reception team will help you find the right service.
