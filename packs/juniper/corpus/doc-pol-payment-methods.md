# How to pay

*Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery. Policy for patients and visitors. Effective from 2026-03-01.*

## Accepted payment methods

Juniper Lane Clinic accepts debit card, credit card and bank transfer for all services, including family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Payment can be made at the front desk on the day of your appointment, or arranged in advance where the reception team has offered this option.

## Cash payments and the cash limit

The clinic also accepts cash, up to a limit of EUR 200 per visit. Where the total charge for a visit is more than EUR 200, the amount above that limit must be paid by debit card, credit card or bank transfer. Please bring a payment card or arrange a bank transfer if you expect your visit to cost more than the cash limit.

## Receipts

A receipt is issued for every payment, whatever the method used. Please keep your receipt for your records, particularly if you intend to claim any part of the cost from an insurer or another organisation.

## Questions

If you are unsure which payment method suits your visit, please ask the reception team before your appointment. Our staff will be glad to explain the options and help you prepare.
