# Masks and infection control

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery manages masks and infection control for patients, visitors and staff. It applies to all areas of the practice, including the waiting room, consulting rooms, the travel clinic, the physiotherapy space and any minor procedure room.

## Mask availability

Free masks are available at reception for any patient or visitor who wishes to wear one. Staff at reception can also direct you to hand sanitiser and tissues. You do not need to explain why you are asking for a mask.

## Patient and visitor expectations

We ask that anyone with cold or flu-like symptoms wear a mask while in shared areas of the clinic. If you are attending for a child health check, vaccination or women's health appointment and feel unwell, please let reception know when you arrive so we can arrange seating and room use with care.

## Staff responsibilities

Clinical staff will wear masks when required by the procedure being carried out, including during minor procedures and when examining patients with respiratory symptoms. Staff must clean shared equipment between patients and follow the hand hygiene steps displayed in each room.

## Telehealth and home visits

Telehealth calls do not require any mask. Where a clinician must attend a patient at home, they will follow the same infection control practice as in the clinic.

## Review

This policy is reviewed regularly and whenever local guidance changes. Questions about this policy should be directed to the practice manager at reception.
