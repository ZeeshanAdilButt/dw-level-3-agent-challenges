# Pre-authorisation

## Purpose

This document sets out how Juniper Lane Clinic handles pre-authorisation requests for patients whose care is covered by an insurer or friendly society. It applies to all staff in the clinic's front desk, billing, and clinical teams, and it is published so that patients and partner insurers can understand the steps and lead times involved. The rules below take effect on 1 April 2026.

## Who this affects

Pre-authorisation applies to patients who hold cover with a partner insurer and who book services that the insurer only pays for after approval. The services most often affected include physiotherapy courses, minor procedures, and certain women's health appointments. Patients who pay privately, and patients whose services are not subject to approval, are not affected by these steps.

## Lead times at a glance

Lead times are counted in working days, excluding weekends and public holidays in Port Avery. The table below summarises the key deadlines. Each deadline is measured from the date of the planned visit or session.

- Coverage checks with the insurer must be completed 2 working days before the visit.
- Pre-authorisation requests must reach the insurer 5 working days before the visit.
- For Harbourline Health members, pre-authorisation is required from the fifth physiotherapy session onward.
- For Larkfield Friendly Society members, minor procedures are covered only if the society approves them at least 5 working days beforehand.

## Steps for pre-authorisation requests

1. **Confirm the patient's cover.** When a patient books a service that may need approval, the front desk records the insurer name and membership details at the time of booking.
2. **Check coverage with the insurer.** Billing staff contact the insurer or its online portal no later than 2 working days before the visit to confirm that the service is covered and whether approval is needed.
3. **Identify whether approval is required.** Where the insurer confirms that approval is needed, staff note the service, the clinical reason, and the planned dates on the request form.
4. **Gather clinical information.** The treating clinician records the diagnosis, the proposed treatment, and, for physiotherapy, the planned number of sessions. This information must be complete before the request is submitted.
5. **Submit the request.** The pre-authorisation request must reach the insurer at least 5 working days before the visit. Staff keep a copy of the submission and the date it was sent.
6. **Track the outcome.** Billing staff monitor the request and record the insurer's decision, reference number, and any conditions attached to approval.
7. **Inform the patient.** Patients are told in writing or by telephone whether the request has been approved, what it covers, and what they may need to pay themselves if it is declined.
8. **Rebook if needed.** If approval is not received in time, the appointment is moved or the patient is advised of their options before the visit takes place.

## Requests received late

Where a request reaches the insurer later than the deadline, the clinic will not guarantee coverage for the visit. Staff will inform the patient as early as possible and offer alternative dates where the lead time can be met. Late requests are logged so that the team can review patterns and improve booking practices.

## Contact

Questions about pre-authorisation can be directed to the Juniper Lane Clinic billing team during opening hours. Patients may also raise questions at the front desk when booking.
