# Joining the clinic as a new patient

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how a new patient joins Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, what is needed before a first visit, and what services are available at each stage. It applies to all individuals registering with the practice for the first time, including patients who have previously been seen at another practice. It takes effect from 2026-03-01 and applies to all registrations received on or after that date.

The clinic offers family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations and a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Some of these services are available as soon as registration is complete, while others depend on the registration steps described below.

## Step one: gather your documents

Before you contact reception or start the online process, gather the documents needed to confirm who you are and where you live. You will need your photo ID and proof of address dated from the last 3 months. Acceptable proof of address includes a recent utility bill, bank statement or official letter showing your name and current address. Please bring the originals to your registration visit so that staff can check them in person.

## Step two: complete form CF-12

Form CF-12 is the new patient registration form. It records your personal details, contact information, any current medications, known allergies, relevant medical history and your preferred pharmacy. Form CF-12 can be completed in the patient portal or on paper at reception, whichever is more convenient for you.

Form CF-12 must be received at least 2 working days before a first appointment. This gives the clinical team time to review your information and prepare your record before you are seen. If form CF-12 arrives later than this, your first appointment may need to be moved.

For a patient under 16, a parent or guardian completes form CF-12 on the child's behalf. The parent or guardian should bring their own photo ID and proof of address, along with proof of the child's relationship to them where requested by reception. Where a child has more than one parent or guardian with responsibility for their care, reception will confirm the arrangements at the time of registration.

## Step three: the registration visit

Once form CF-12 has been received, book your registration visit with reception. The registration visit is a short appointment where a member of staff checks your photo ID and proof of address in person, confirms the details recorded on form CF-12, and explains how the clinic works, including how to book appointments, how to request repeat prescriptions and how to contact the practice out of hours.

A new record is activated within 3 working days of receiving form CF-12. Your record is active once staff have verified your identity and documents at the registration visit. You will be notified when your record is active, and you can then use the full range of clinic services in line with the restrictions below.

## Step four: what is available and what is blocked

Until form CF-12 is on file, certain services are not available to you. Telehealth is not available to a new patient until form CF-12 is on file. This applies to all telehealth calls, including those booked for urgent but non-emergency concerns, so please complete form CF-12 as early as possible if you expect to need a remote consultation.

A new patient with no CF-12 on file can book only the registration visit. Other appointments, including clinical reviews, blood tests, physiotherapy, vaccinations and minor procedures, cannot be booked until form CF-12 has been received and your record is in place. Reception staff can explain the earliest date on which each service may be available to you.

Once form CF-12 is on file and your record is active, all services listed in the purpose section can be booked in the usual way, subject to clinical need and appointment availability.

## Urgent and emergency care

Joining the clinic does not change how urgent care is accessed. If you believe you have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately rather than waiting for a registration appointment. If you have an urgent but non-emergency concern before your record is active, contact reception to discuss the most appropriate next step.

## Questions and contact

If you are unsure whether you have the right documents, whether form CF-12 has been received, or when your record will be active, please contact reception during opening hours. Staff are happy to explain the process and to help you complete form CF-12 on paper if you prefer. This policy will be reviewed periodically, and any changes will be published with an updated effective date.
