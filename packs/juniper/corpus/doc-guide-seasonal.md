# Busy times of year

This guide helps patients of Juniper Lane Clinic plan their visits around the periods when our practice is busiest. Knowing when demand rises makes it easier to get the appointment you need, when you need it.

## When demand peaks

The busiest weeks are the first two weeks of January, so book early. Many families return from the holidays with questions about child health checks, vaccinations, repeat prescriptions and minor concerns that have built up over the festive period. If you are planning a routine check or a vaccination, we recommend booking as soon as you can, ideally before the end of the previous month.

Other busy spells tend to follow the school term and the start of the winter season, when coughs, colds and minor infections become more common. Demand can also rise before major holiday travel, when patients ask our travel clinic about vaccinations and advice.

## Tips for getting an appointment

- Book routine appointments in advance rather than waiting for a problem to arise.
- Order repeat prescriptions ahead of time so there is no gap in your medication.
- Consider a telehealth call for matters that do not need a physical examination.
- Use the earlier part of the week or the middle of the day, when slots may be easier to find.

## If you need urgent help

If your condition is urgent or you feel seriously unwell, do not wait for a routine appointment. Contact the clinic for advice, or seek emergency care immediately if you believe your situation is life-threatening.
