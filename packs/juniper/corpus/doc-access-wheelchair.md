# Step-free access and the lift

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how patients, carers and visitors can reach every part of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, including the physiotherapy rooms. It is written for anyone planning a visit who needs to know about entrances, lifts, stairs and support for assistance dogs. If you have a specific need that is not covered here, please contact the reception team before your appointment so that we can prepare.

## Entrances

The Mill Row entrance is step-free, so patients arriving on foot, with a pushchair, or using a wheelchair or mobility scooter can enter the building without using any steps. Please use this entrance if you need level access. The main street entrance is also available, and the reception desk is easy to reach from either door once you are inside.

## Getting to the first floor

The physiotherapy rooms are on the first floor, reached by lift or stairs. Most patients will find the lift the most comfortable option, particularly if they have limited mobility, are carrying equipment, or are accompanying a child in a pushchair. The lift serves the first floor, where physiotherapy is, and it is large enough to accommodate a wheelchair and an accompanying person.

If you prefer the stairs, they are well lit and have handrails on both sides. Reception staff are happy to help you decide which route suits you best on the day of your appointment.

## Using the lift

The lift is available during all opening hours. If you find the lift out of service, please let a member of staff know straight away so that we can arrange an alternative. In the meantime, our staff can help you reach your appointment, and we will do our best to see you without delay. Please do not wait in the lift lobby if you need to leave the building quickly; use the stairs or ask at reception for assistance.

## Assistance dogs

Assistance dogs are welcome throughout the clinic, including in consulting rooms, waiting areas and the physiotherapy rooms on the first floor. There is no need to notify us in advance, although it can be helpful to mention your dog when you book so that we can make sure the room you are assigned suits you. Staff may ask a few simple questions about the dog's role, in line with the law, but we will never ask for medical details about your disability.

## Accessible toilets and seating

Accessible toilet facilities are available on the ground floor near the reception desk. Seating in the waiting areas includes chairs with armrests, and staff can offer a seat close to the reception desk if you would find that easier.

## Telling us what you need

If you have a particular requirement, such as a longer appointment slot, a quieter room, help with paperwork, or information in a different format, please tell us when you book or at reception on arrival. Our team will make reasonable adjustments wherever we can, and we review this guide regularly to keep it accurate.

## Contacting us

For questions about access before your visit, please call the reception desk during opening hours or use the contact details shown on our main website. Our staff will be glad to talk through the route to your appointment with you.
