# Preparing for a Standard Blood Test

Juniper Lane Clinic offers blood tests as part of routine family care, and this guide explains how to prepare so your visit runs smoothly. For most patients, preparation is simple. No fasting is needed for a standard blood test, so you can eat and drink as you normally would before your appointment, including your usual breakfast and morning drinks.

## Before your appointment

Please bring a list of any medicines you currently take, including over-the-counter products and supplements, so our team has an accurate record. If you have a blood test booked alongside another appointment, such as a child health check or a women's health consultation, you do not need to change your routine for either visit. If you are unsure whether your test is a standard one, check your booking confirmation or ask our reception team when you call.

## On the day

Wear clothing with sleeves that roll up easily above the elbow, as this makes it easier for the phlebotomist to access a vein. Drink water as normal, since being well hydrated can make the blood draw more comfortable. Arrive a few minutes early so you have time to check in and settle before the test.

## Afterwards

Most people return to their normal activities straight away. If you feel faint or notice bruising at the site, rest briefly, keep the area gently pressed, and let a member of staff know. Results are reviewed by your clinician, who will contact you if any follow-up is needed.
