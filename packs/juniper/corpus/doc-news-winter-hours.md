# Winter and holiday hours reminder

Juniper Lane Clinic serves patients across Port Avery throughout the winter season, and this notice sets out the timing changes you should plan around over the coming months. Please read it before booking appointments, collecting repeat prescriptions or arranging travel vaccinations.

## Closure for Port Avery Harbour Day

The clinic is closed on Port Avery Harbour Day, Monday 14 December 2026. On that day, our reception lines, appointment bookings and in-person services will not be available. If you need a repeat prescription before the closure, please request it in good time so that your pharmacy has the supply it needs.

## Planning around the holiday period

Our holiday hours will be published separately on the clinic noticeboard and on our website as the season approaches. Patients with ongoing care needs, including those awaiting blood test results or physiotherapy sessions, are encouraged to check their appointment details ahead of the closure dates.

## Contacting us during closures

If you have an urgent concern while the clinic is closed, please contact your nearest emergency department or the appropriate after-hours service. For questions that can wait, you can leave a message through our telephone line or request a telehealth call once we reopen.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a closure affects your appointment or prescription, please speak with our reception team before the day in question. We are glad to help you plan so that your care continues without interruption.
