# Juniper Lane autumn newsletter 2026

Hello from everyone at Juniper Lane Clinic. As the evenings draw in and the leaves turn across Port Avery, we want to share a few updates, reminders and seasonal tips with our patients and their families.

## Autumn is flu and vaccination season

Now is a good time to book your seasonal flu vaccination, especially if you are over 65, pregnant, or living with a long-term health condition. Our nurses can also check that childhood vaccinations are up to date, and our travel clinic can advise on any vaccines you may need before a trip later in the year. Please call the front desk or book through the online portal to secure a slot.

## Saturday opening hours

Our Saturday opening runs to 14:00, so you have the morning and early afternoon to catch us for routine appointments, blood tests and repeat prescription collections. Please note that our 2025 brochure listed Saturday opening as 09:00 to 14:00, and that is the same window we are keeping this season. Weekday hours are unchanged, and the telehealth line remains available for many non-urgent queries outside those times.

## Physiotherapy for autumn aches

Cooler weather often brings stiff backs, sore knees and shoulder niggles, and our physiotherapy team is here to help. The first physiotherapy assessment costs EUR 70, and your physiotherapist will talk you through a plan that suits your daily routine. If you have been putting off treatment, autumn is a sensible time to get moving again before the winter months.

## Women's health and minor procedures

Our women's health appointments continue throughout the season, and we welcome questions about screening, contraception, menopause and pregnancy care. We also offer a range of minor procedures carried out in a calm, private room. If you are unsure whether a procedure is suitable for you, ask at reception or speak to your GP at your next appointment.

## Repeat prescriptions and telehealth

To keep your medicines flowing smoothly, please order repeat prescriptions in good time and check your supply before any holiday or long weekend. Many routine reviews can now be carried out by telehealth call, which saves travel and waiting time. Your clinician will let you know whether a video or phone consultation is appropriate for your needs.

## Staying well this season

Keep warm, stay active when you can, and try to get outdoors on brighter days. If you feel unwell, please do not hesitate to contact us. For emergencies, always call your local emergency number straight away.

Thank you for being part of the Juniper Lane community. We look forward to seeing you this autumn.
