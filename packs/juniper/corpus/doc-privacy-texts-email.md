# Texts and emails

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains what messages Juniper Lane Clinic sends to patients by text and email, and what each message contains. It applies to all patients of the practice in Port Avery, whether they attend for family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, the travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health, minor procedures, repeat prescriptions or telehealth calls.

## What reminder texts contain

Reminder texts show the date and time only, never the service. The message tells you when your appointment is, so that you can plan your visit, but it does not name the reason for the visit. This protects your privacy if your phone is seen by someone else or if the message is read on a shared device.

When you receive a reminder, please check the date and time against your own records. If you are unsure which appointment the message refers to, call the front desk during opening hours and the team will confirm the details with you.

## What emails contain

Emails from the clinic may include appointment confirmations, letters about test results, and information about repeat prescriptions. Where an email refers to a specific service, it is sent through the secure patient portal wherever possible. Please do not reply to a message with personal health information unless you are using the secure channel.

## Keeping your contact details up to date

Please tell the reception team if your mobile number or email address changes. Messages sent to an old number or address cannot be read by you, and this may cause you to miss an appointment. Updating your details is free and can be done at the front desk, by phone, or through the patient portal.

## If you have a concern

If a message does not seem right, or you think it was sent to you in error, contact the clinic directly rather than acting on it. In an urgent medical situation, do not wait for a reply to a text or email; call emergency services or seek urgent care straight away.
