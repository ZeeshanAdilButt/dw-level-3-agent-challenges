# School medical forms

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how families at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can request and complete school medical forms. Many schools ask for a completed medical form before a child can join a class, take part in sports, or attend an overnight trip. Our team is happy to help you through the process so that the paperwork is accurate and returned on time.

## Fees

A school medical form costs EUR 25. This fee covers the time our clinicians spend reviewing your child's records, completing the sections that require a medical assessment, and signing the final document. Payment is taken when the request is made, and the form is released once the fee has been received.

## How to request a form

Please ask at reception or contact us by telephone or through the patient portal. When you make your request, tell us the name of the school, the type of form required, and any deadline the school has given you. Bring any letter or template the school has sent, as this helps us complete the correct version the first time.

## What to expect

Some sections of a school form can be completed from your child's existing record. Others may require a short appointment, particularly where the school asks about vaccinations, allergies, or long-term conditions. Where an appointment is needed, our reception team will let you know before you pay the fee so that you can plan ahead.

## Returning the form

Please allow reasonable time for the clinician to review and sign the form before the school's deadline. Forms are collected from reception or sent to the school directly with your written permission. If the school needs additional information, contact us and we will advise on what can be provided.
