# Booking for your family

## Who this guide is for

This guide explains how families can book appointments at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, including appointments for children and for other people you are responsible for. It applies from 2026-03-01 and is written for patients, parents, carers and anyone who manages appointments on behalf of someone else.

## One account, several people

One portal account can book for linked family members once authority is on file. In practice, this means the adult who holds the account can see and manage bookings for the people who have been linked to it, such as children, a partner, or an adult relative who has asked for your help. Linking is a formal step, so the clinic needs to confirm your authority before you can book on someone's behalf.

## Setting up authority

To link a family member, sign in to your account and choose the option to add a linked person. You will be asked to confirm who you are and how you are connected to the person. For a child, this is usually a parent or legal guardian. For an adult, the person themselves may need to give consent. If you cannot complete the online form, you can ask the reception team to help, and they will explain what documents or confirmations are needed.

Once authority is on file, the linked person's appointments, reminders and repeat prescription requests can be managed from your account. Please keep your contact details up to date so that messages about bookings reach you.

## Booking children's appointments

Child health checks, routine vaccinations and minor concerns can be booked through the portal or by phone. When you book for a child, select the child's name from your linked list so that the appointment is recorded against the correct record. Please tell us if your child has any allergies, is due for a particular vaccination, or needs a longer appointment. If you are unsure which type of appointment is right, describe the concern when you book and the reception team will advise you.

Travel vaccinations for children are handled through our travel clinic. Because some vaccines need to be given well before travel, we recommend booking as early as you can once your plans are known.

## Choosing the right type of appointment

Juniper Lane Clinic offers family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health services, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. When booking for a family member, you can choose the service that best fits the need. Telehealth calls suit many follow-up conversations and some routine reviews, but an examination, a blood test or a procedure will need an in-person visit.

## Changing or cancelling bookings

You can change or cancel a booking through your account or by contacting reception. Please give as much notice as you can so that the appointment can be offered to someone else. If a child or linked person becomes unwell on the day, contact the clinic directly rather than waiting for the portal to update.

## Privacy and shared access

Linked access lets you manage bookings and routine requests, but each person's medical information remains confidential. Some details may only be shared with the person themselves once they reach an age or level of capacity where they can make their own decisions. If you have questions about what you can see or change, ask the reception team, who can explain how the clinic applies these rules.

## Getting help

If you have trouble linking a family member, booking an appointment or understanding the options, contact Juniper Lane Clinic during opening hours and a member of the team will assist you. For urgent medical concerns, do not wait for an online response; seek immediate care from the appropriate local service.
