# Interpreters

This guide explains how patients at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can request an interpreter for their appointments. It applies from 2026-03-01 and is intended for anyone booking care with the practice, including patients, carers and family members who attend on someone's behalf.

## Booking a free interpreter

A free interpreter for spoken languages can be arranged for most appointments, including family medicine visits, child health checks, vaccinations, blood tests and women's health consultations. To request one, please give the reception team at least 3 working days notice of your appointment. This allows us time to find an interpreter with the right language and availability. If you book less than three working days ahead, we will still try to help, but we cannot guarantee that an interpreter will be available on the day.

## Booking a sign language interpreter

For deaf patients who use sign language, a sign language interpreter needs 5 working days notice before the appointment. Because these interpreters are booked in advance and are in limited supply, we recommend making the request as soon as your appointment is confirmed. Please tell us which sign language you use so that we can match you with the correct interpreter.

## Telehealth and physiotherapy

Interpreter requests apply equally to telehealth calls and physiotherapy sessions. Please mention the request when you book, and let the team know if your preferred format changes so we can arrange support for the new appointment.

## Changing or cancelling a request

If you no longer need an interpreter, or your appointment changes, please contact reception as early as possible so that the booking can be released for another patient. If you have any questions about this guide, please speak to our reception team during opening hours.
