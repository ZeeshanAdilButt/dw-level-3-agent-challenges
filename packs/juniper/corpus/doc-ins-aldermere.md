# Aldermere Health Plan

## Purpose of this page

This page is written for members of the Aldermere Health Plan who are registered as hospital-only plan members. It explains what the plan covers, how costs work, and what you need to do if you receive care at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It takes effect from 2026-04-01 and applies to all claims submitted on or after that date.

## What the plan covers

Aldermere Health Plan is a hospital-only plan and covers no routine clinic services. This means that family medicine consultations, child health checks, vaccinations, travel clinic appointments, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health appointments, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls are not covered under this plan when delivered in a general practice setting. Members should plan to pay for these services directly unless another arrangement applies to them.

The plan covers a minor procedure only when a hospital consultant referred it. A referral letter from a hospital consultant must be on file before the procedure takes place. Without that referral, a minor procedure carried out at Juniper Lane Clinic will not be covered, regardless of its clinical need.

## Excess and member payments

Aldermere Health Plan members pay an excess of EUR 50 on each covered claim. The excess is the amount you pay before the plan contributes to the cost of covered care. Members should expect to settle the excess themselves for any covered service, and the clinic will tell you the amount due at the time of the appointment.

## How billing works at Juniper Lane Clinic

The clinic does not bill Aldermere Health Plan directly. Payment for your visit is made by you at the clinic, and the clinic will provide an itemised receipt that you can use for your own claim. Staff at reception can explain the receipt and confirm which services are listed. The clinic cannot submit claims on your behalf to this plan.

## Submitting a patient claim

Aldermere Health Plan patient claims must be sent within 30 days of the date of service. The 30 days begin on the date the care was delivered, not the date you received your receipt. Claims received after this window may be refused. To submit a claim, include the itemised receipt from Juniper Lane Clinic, the hospital consultant referral letter where one applies, and your completed claim form. Keep copies of everything you send.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a service is covered, contact Aldermere Health Plan member services before your appointment. Juniper Lane Clinic reception staff can help you prepare your receipt and paperwork but cannot make coverage decisions on the plan's behalf.
