# Preparing for a telehealth call

This guide explains how to get ready for a telehealth call with a clinician at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. Following these steps helps the call start on time and keeps your information secure.

## Before your appointment

1. Check your appointment details. Confirm the date and time of your telehealth call in your booking confirmation.
2. Choose your device. Join a telehealth call from a device with a camera and microphone in a private room. A phone, tablet or computer will all work if it meets these needs.
3. Find a private room. Choose a space where you will not be overheard or interrupted, and where other people cannot see your screen.
4. Charge your device. Plug your phone, tablet or laptop into power so the battery does not run out during the call.
5. Check your internet connection. Move closer to your router or switch to a stable network if your video is breaking up.
6. Update your browser or app. Install any updates before the call so the video link opens without delay.
7. Have your information ready. Keep your current medicines list, any recent test results and notes about your symptoms close by.
8. Write down your questions. Listing your main concerns in advance helps you use the time well.

## Receiving your video link

9. Watch for the secure video link. The secure video link is sent by text message 10 minutes before the call. Keep your mobile phone nearby and switched on in the lead-up to your appointment.
10. Do not forward the link. The link is meant only for you. If you received it by mistake, contact the clinic reception team.
11. Test the link early if you can. Tapping the link a few minutes ahead gives you time to fix any camera or microphone problems.

## At the start of the call

12. Confirm your identity. Identity is checked at the start of a telehealth call by name and date of birth. Have these details ready so the check can be completed quickly.
13. Allow your clinician to set out the plan. Your clinician will explain how the consultation will run and whether any follow-up is needed.

## If something goes wrong

14. Tell us if the connection drops. If the call disconnects, try to rejoin using the same link. If you cannot reconnect, contact the clinic so we can arrange a new time or a phone call.
15. Call the clinic if you cannot join. Our reception team can help with technical problems or urgent changes to your appointment.

## Emergencies

A telehealth call is not suitable for an emergency. If you or someone else is seriously unwell, call your local emergency number straight away.
