# You said, we did

## About this page

Juniper Lane Clinic listens to what patients tell us, whether through our feedback forms, the comments box in reception, phone calls or messages sent through the patient portal. This page sets out the changes we have made in response to that feedback, and it explains how we handle complaints so that you know what to expect when you raise a concern.

## How we handle complaints

When you make a complaint, we acknowledge it within 3 working days so you know it has reached the right person. We aim to give you a full written answer within 20 working days. If we need more time to look into something complicated, such as a referral that has been delayed or a result that has not reached you, we will tell you why and give you a realistic date for our reply.

Every complaint is reviewed by the practice manager, and clinical matters are also reviewed by a clinician who was not involved in your care. We do not treat feedback as a criticism of any individual. We use it to find out where our systems are letting patients down.

## What you told us and what we did

**Waiting times on the phone.** Many of you said the telephone line was hard to get through at the start of the morning. We added a second reception line and introduced a callback option, so you can leave your number and be called back rather than holding. Calls now reach a member of the team more quickly at peak times.

**Repeat prescriptions.** Patients told us they often arrived to collect a repeat prescription only to find it was not ready. We now ask you to request repeats at least two working days ahead, and we send a text message once your prescription is ready to collect or has been sent to your chosen pharmacy.

**Test results.** Some of you were unsure how to find out your blood test results. Results are now sent to your patient portal with a short note from your clinician where needed, and reception staff can tell you when to expect them. If a result needs action, we will contact you directly rather than waiting for you to call.

**Telehealth calls.** Several patients asked for clearer instructions before a video or telephone consultation. Our confirmation message now explains what you need, how long the call is likely to take, and what to do if the connection drops.

**Child health checks and vaccinations.** Parents asked for reminders before their child's checks and vaccinations were due. We now send reminders by text and letter, and the travel clinic team has produced a simple checklist for families preparing for trips abroad.

**Physiotherapy and women's health.** Patients using our physiotherapy and women's health services asked for more privacy when waiting to be seen. We have moved these appointments to a quieter area of the building and reviewed how names are called in the waiting room.

## Details left on the page

Where a patient gave their name, date of birth or contact details in their feedback, we have kept these records only as part of the original comment and they are not shared beyond the team responsible for responding. If you would like us to remove any personal information you have sent us, please contact the practice manager and we will do so.

## What we are working on next

We are currently reviewing how we send appointment letters and whether our opening hours on Saturday morning meet the needs of working families. We will publish an update on this page once those reviews are complete. Thank you to everyone who took the time to tell us what works and what does not. Your comments are the main reason this practice keeps getting better.
