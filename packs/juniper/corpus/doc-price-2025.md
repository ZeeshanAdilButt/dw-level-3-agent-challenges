# Self-pay price list 2025

Juniper Lane Clinic, Port Avery

Effective from 1 January 2025

## Purpose

This price list sets out the self-pay fees charged by Juniper Lane Clinic for services that are not covered by a public health plan or insurance arrangement. It applies to all patients paying directly for care at the clinic, whether in person or by telehealth call.

## Consultations

The standard consultation in the 2025 price list cost EUR 50. This fee covers a single appointment with a clinician for assessment, advice and any routine follow-up discussion within the same visit.

Longer or specialist-style consultations are priced on request at the reception desk before the appointment is booked.

## Physiotherapy

The first physiotherapy assessment in the 2025 price list cost EUR 75. This fee includes the initial examination, a documented treatment plan and advice on exercises to be carried out between sessions.

Follow-up physiotherapy sessions are charged at the rate displayed at reception on the day of booking.

## Other services

The following services are available on a self-pay basis. Fees are confirmed at the time of booking.

- Child health checks
- Vaccinations and travel clinic consultations
- Blood tests, with the laboratory fee passed on at cost
- Women's health appointments
- Minor procedures
- Repeat prescription administration
- Telehealth calls

## Summary of key fees

| Service | Fee in 2025 price list |
|---|---|
| Standard consultation | EUR 50 |
| First physiotherapy assessment | EUR 75 |

## Payment

Payment is due at the time of the appointment unless other arrangements have been agreed in advance. The clinic accepts card payments and cash. Receipts are issued for all self-pay services and can be used for personal records or reimbursement claims where applicable.

## Cancellations and missed appointments

Patients who cancel with at least 24 hours' notice are not charged. Appointments cancelled at shorter notice or missed without notice may be charged the full consultation fee.

## Changes to this list

This list is the 2025 price list. It is kept for reference and has been superseded by the 2026 list. Patients should check the current list at reception or on the clinic website before booking a new appointment.

## Contact

For questions about fees, please speak to the reception team during opening hours or contact the clinic by phone or through the patient portal.
