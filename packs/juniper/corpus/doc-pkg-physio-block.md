# Physiotherapy block

## About this guide

This guide explains the physiotherapy block of five follow-ups offered at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It is written for patients who expect to need several physiotherapy sessions over a course of treatment and want to understand how the block works before booking.

## What the block includes

The physiotherapy block of five follow-ups costs EUR 270, which saves EUR 30 compared with booking the same five follow-up sessions one at a time. Each follow-up is a standard physiotherapy appointment with one of our clinicians, and the sessions are used in the order your clinician recommends.

## How to use your block

Your clinician will confirm at your first physiotherapy appointment whether a block suits your treatment plan. Once purchased, the five follow-ups can be booked through the front desk or during a telehealth call with your clinician where appropriate. Sessions are intended to be used within the period of your treatment plan, and our reception team can explain any time limits that apply to your block.

## Questions and changes

If your treatment changes and you no longer need all five follow-ups, please speak with your clinician or the reception team so your plan can be reviewed. Our staff will explain the options available to you in plain terms.
