# Records request procedure

## Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how Juniper Lane Clinic handles requests for copies of patient records, including requests from patients, authorised representatives and other healthcare providers. It applies to all staff who receive, log, process or release clinical or administrative records. It takes effect from 2026-02-01 and replaces any earlier informal practice.

## Key rules

- The internal target for records requests is 25 days, measured from the date the complete request is logged to the date the records are released or the requester is informed of a delay.
- Clinic manager Rhys Penhallow approves every records request before any records are released, with no exceptions for urgent or repeat requests.
- No staff member may release records by any route, including email, phone or in person, until approval has been recorded in the request log.

## Roles

- Reception staff log incoming requests and check that the requester's identity and authority are documented.
- Clinical administrators locate the relevant records, redact third-party information where needed and prepare the copy.
- The clinic manager reviews each prepared request, confirms approval and signs off the release.
- The clinical lead on duty is consulted where a clinical judgement is needed about what may be released or whether any information should be withheld.

## Procedure

1. Receive the request in any form (in person, by phone, by post, by email or through the patient portal) and record it in the records request log the same working day.
2. Confirm the requester's identity using two identifiers, such as date of birth and address, and check that the person is the patient or holds documented authority to act for them.
3. Record the scope of the request, including the date range, the types of records wanted and the preferred format.
4. Acknowledge the request in writing to the requester and state the internal target of 25 days.
5. Assign the request to a clinical administrator and note the assignment in the log.
6. Locate the records in the clinical system and check that all entries relating to the requested period are included.
7. Identify any third-party information, such as details about other family members, and mark it for redaction.
8. Refer any entries that may cause harm if released to the clinical lead on duty, and record the outcome of that review.
9. Prepare the copy in the agreed format and attach a cover note listing what is included.
10. Submit the prepared request to Rhys Penhallow for approval and wait for written approval before proceeding.
11. Once approved, release the records through the agreed secure route and record the release date in the log.
12. If the 25 day target is at risk, inform the requester in writing of the reason for the delay and the expected date of release.
13. Close the request in the log and file the approval record with it.

## Records and audit

The records request log is the single source of truth for each request. Entries must be complete, dated and kept up to date. The clinic manager reviews the log at the end of each month to check that approvals were recorded before release and that targets were met or delays explained.

## Review

This procedure is reviewed annually by the clinic manager or sooner if the law, the clinical system or the clinic's services change.
