# Women's health

This guide explains the women's health services offered at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery, including cervical screening and the women's health consultation. It is written for patients who would like to know what to expect before, during and after an appointment. Effective from 2026-03-01.

## Cervical screening

Cervical screening is a routine test that checks for changes to the cells of the cervix, the lower part of the uterus. Screening helps find changes early, when they are easier to treat. At Juniper Lane Clinic, cervical screening is offered to patients aged 25 to 64, in line with the national screening programme. If you are outside this age range, please speak with your GP about whether screening is right for you.

Cervical screening has the service code CS-20 and the appointment lasts 20 minutes. The test is carried out by Nurse Rourke (Aisling Rourke), who is experienced in performing the procedure and in answering questions beforehand. For self-pay patients, cervical screening costs EUR 65. If you are covered by a scheme that includes this service, the clinic reception team can confirm the arrangements when you book.

Cervical screening results arrive by letter within 14 days. If your result shows that a further test or a referral is needed, we will contact you directly to explain the next steps. A letter with a normal result does not need any action from you until your next screening is due.

## Women's health consultation

The women's health consultation is a dedicated appointment with one of our GPs for concerns about menstrual health, contraception, menopause, pelvic health, pregnancy planning and other issues that relate to women's health. It gives you time to talk through your symptoms, your history and your options in a private and unhurried setting.

The women's health consultation lasts 30 minutes. For self-pay patients, the consultation costs EUR 75. You can ask for a longer or shorter appointment when you book, and our reception team will try to accommodate your request where possible. If a consultation identifies a need for blood tests, a scan or a referral, your doctor will explain what is recommended and why.

## Chaperones and your comfort

We understand that intimate examinations can feel uncomfortable, and you are entitled to feel safe and respected throughout your appointment. A chaperone is offered at every intimate examination. A chaperone is a trained member of staff who is present during the examination to support you and the clinician. You are welcome to accept or decline a chaperone, and you may also bring a friend or family member to the appointment if you wish, provided they are comfortable with this arrangement.

You may stop an examination at any time, and you are welcome to ask questions before, during or after it. If you would like to discuss any aspect of your care, please let the clinician know and we will do our best to meet your preferences.

## Booking and contact

To book cervical screening or a women's health consultation, please contact our reception team or use the online booking option available through the clinic website. If you have questions about these services before you book, our team will be glad to help. Where a telehealth call is suitable for your concern, your doctor may offer one as an alternative or as a follow-up.
