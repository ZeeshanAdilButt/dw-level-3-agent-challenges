# Phone lines

## Purpose

This guide explains when you can reach Juniper Lane Clinic by telephone and which number to use. It applies to all patients, carers and visitors contacting the practice from Port Avery and the surrounding area.

## Opening hours for the phone line

Our phone line is answered from 08:00 to 17:30 on weekdays, Monday to Friday. On Saturdays, the phone line is answered from 09:00 to 12:30. The phone line is not staffed on Sundays or on public holidays, so please plan calls around these hours where possible.

## Clinic address

Juniper Lane Clinic is based at 18 Mill Row, Port Avery. Please use this address when arranging visits, sending documents or giving directions to a taxi or a family member.

## Clinic phone number

For appointments, repeat prescriptions, test results, vaccination queries and general questions during opening hours, call the clinic phone number, 0155 555 0142. Please have your date of birth ready so our staff can locate your record quickly.

## Out-of-hours line

If you have an urgent problem that is not an emergency and the clinic is closed, call the out-of-hours line on 0155 555 0199. This line is for urgent but non-emergency concerns only. If you believe someone's life is at risk, call the emergency services immediately rather than any clinic number.

## Before you call

Where you can, check whether your question can be handled online or by a telehealth booking. Calling at quieter times, such as shortly after opening, can also help reduce waiting time.
