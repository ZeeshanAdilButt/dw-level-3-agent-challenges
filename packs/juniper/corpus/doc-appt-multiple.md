# Several bookings at once

## Purpose

This guide explains how patients at Juniper Lane Clinic can manage more than one appointment at a time, including bookings made for other members of their household. It applies from 2026-03-01.

## Holding multiple bookings

A patient can hold at most 3 upcoming bookings at one time. If you already have three upcoming appointments, the online booking system will not accept a fourth until one of them has taken place or has been cancelled. This limit covers all appointment types, including child health checks, vaccinations, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health visits and telehealth calls. Reception staff can help you decide which bookings to keep if you need to reorganise your schedule.

## Booking for family members

One portal account can book for linked family members once authority is on file. Authority is recorded when an adult account holder confirms, at the front desk or through the secure portal, that they are permitted to manage appointments for the other person. Once this is in place, you can select the family member's name when booking, and their appointments will appear alongside your own in the portal. Each linked person's bookings still count toward their own limit of three upcoming bookings.

## Making changes

To move or cancel a booking, use the portal or call reception during opening hours. Please give as much notice as you can so that the slot can be offered to another patient. If you are unsure whether a booking is linked to your account or to a family member's record, contact the front desk before making changes.

## Questions

For help with bookings or linked family access, speak to a member of the reception team at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery.
