# Patient questions answered (September 2026)

Document type: FAQ. Audience: public. Effective from 2026-09-01.

Welcome to the Juniper Lane Clinic patient FAQ. This page answers the questions we hear most often from patients in Port Avery and the surrounding area. If your question is not covered here, please contact the front desk during opening hours.

## About the clinic

### What services does Juniper Lane Clinic offer?

We are a family practice offering family medicine, child health checks, vaccinations, a travel clinic, blood tests, physiotherapy, women's health appointments, minor procedures, repeat prescription administration and telehealth calls. Not every service is available with every clinician, so please check when booking which service you need.

### Who can register with the practice?

Anyone living in or near Port Avery may ask to register as a patient. Registration involves providing your personal details and confirming your contact information so that your records stay accurate. Our reception team will explain what documents are helpful at the time you register.

## Booking and cancellations

### How do I book an appointment?

You can book by calling the front desk, by using the online booking system where it is available, or by speaking to a member of staff in person. For a child health check or a vaccination, please mention this when you book so that the right appointment length is arranged.

### What is the cancellation policy?

Our 2025 brochure says appointments can be cancelled up to 12 hours ahead without a fee. Please note that our September 2026 FAQ says the late cancellation fee is EUR 25. This FAQ reflects the current position and is the document to follow from 2026-09-01. If you are unsure whether your cancellation falls within the notice period, please call us and we will confirm the details for you.

### What happens if I arrive late?

If you arrive late, we will do our best to see you, but this depends on the clinic schedule and the needs of other patients. In some cases we may need to offer you a shorter appointment or a rebooked time. Please arrive a few minutes early where you can, especially for your first visit.

## Telehealth and repeat prescriptions

### How do telehealth calls work?

Telehealth calls allow you to speak with a clinician by phone or video for suitable concerns. Your clinician will let you know at the start of the call whether it is appropriate to continue remotely. If an in-person examination is needed, we will arrange one for you as soon as we reasonably can.

### How do I request a repeat prescription?

Please request repeat prescriptions through the method your practice has confirmed with you, or by speaking to the front desk. Requests are processed in the usual working time frame, and we ask that you do not wait until your medication has run out before asking. Some medicines require a review before they can be reissued, and our staff will tell you if this applies.

## Health checks, tests and procedures

### What should I expect at a child health check?

Child health checks look at growth, development and general wellbeing, and they are a good time to raise any questions you have about feeding, sleep or behaviour. Vaccinations may be offered at the same visit where your child's schedule makes this appropriate. Please bring the child's red book or vaccination record if you have one.

### Do I need to fast before blood tests?

Some blood tests require fasting and others do not. When you book, the team will tell you whether you need to avoid food or drink before the test. Please follow the instructions you are given, and tell us about any medicines you take.

### What are minor procedures?

Minor procedures are small treatments carried out in the clinic, such as removing a small skin lesion or treating a minor wound. Your clinician will explain the procedure, its benefits and any risks before going ahead, and will ask for your consent. Please let us know about any allergies or bleeding problems in advance.

## Contact and complaints

### How can I give feedback or make a complaint?

We welcome feedback, both positive and negative. You can raise a concern with the front desk, speak to the practice manager, or write to the clinic. We will acknowledge your concern and aim to respond in a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with the response, you can ask for the matter to be reviewed further.

### What if I have an urgent medical problem?

This FAQ is not a substitute for urgent care. If you believe your condition is serious or life threatening, please contact emergency services immediately. For urgent but non-emergency concerns, please call the clinic and follow the guidance given to you by our staff.
