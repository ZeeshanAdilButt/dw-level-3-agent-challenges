# Preparing for a Child Health Check

Juniper Lane Clinic is a family practice in Port Avery, and this guide explains how parents can prepare for a child health check. Read it before your appointment so that the visit runs smoothly and you leave with the information you need.

## Who the child health check is for

Child health checks are for children aged 0 to 5. These checks give our clinicians a chance to follow your child's growth, development, feeding, sleep and general wellbeing at each stage of early childhood. If you have a question about whether your child is due for a check, the reception team can confirm this with you when you call or book online.

## Who should attend

A parent or legal guardian must attend the child health check. We ask this so that the clinician can discuss the findings directly with the adult responsible for the child's care, answer questions, and agree on any follow-up. If you cannot attend in person, please contact us before the appointment to discuss your options so that we can make suitable arrangements.

## What to bring

Please bring the following items with you:

- The child's health record book, which holds the record of the child's growth, immunisations and previous checks. Our clinicians refer to it during the visit, and keeping it up to date helps everyone involved in your child's care.
- A list of any questions or concerns you would like to raise, written down so that nothing is forgotten during the appointment.
- Details of any medicines, supplements or allergies your child currently has, including the name and the reason for each.
- Any reports or letters from other services, such as hospital or specialist clinics, that relate to your child's health.

If your child uses a comforter, a favourite toy or a bottle or snack, bring these along as well, since they can help a young child settle.

## Before the appointment

Try to arrive a few minutes early so that you can complete any check-in paperwork without rushing. If your child is unwell on the day of the appointment, please call the clinic so that we can advise whether the visit should go ahead or be rebooked. Feeding your child shortly before the check can help keep them comfortable, although you should follow any specific instructions the clinic gives you when you book.

## During the appointment

The clinician will measure your child's height or length, weight and head circumference where relevant, and will ask you about milestones such as movement, speech, eating and sleep. They may also check your child's hearing, vision and general development. Please tell the clinician if you have any concerns, however small they may seem, because early discussion often makes a real difference.

## After the appointment

Your clinician will explain any findings and agree on a plan. You may be given advice, a referral, or a date for the next check or vaccination. Record any new information in the health record book so that it stays complete.

If you have questions about this guide or your child's care, please contact Juniper Lane Clinic through our reception team.
