# Preparing for a women's health consultation

This guide explains how to prepare for a women's health consultation at Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery. It is intended for patients who have booked an appointment and want to know what to expect. It does not give medical advice; for questions about your own health, please speak with your clinician.

## Before your appointment

Think about the reasons for your visit and write down any questions you would like to ask. It can help to note your recent symptoms, changes in your health, and any medicines or supplements you take. If you are attending by telehealth, check that your device, camera and connection work before the call starts.

## What to bring

Bring a list of your current prescriptions, your contact details, and any previous test results you have from other providers. If you have a repeat prescription with us, you may wish to check how much you have left so the next supply is not delayed.

## During the consultation

Your clinician will ask about your medical history and explain any examination they recommend. A chaperone is offered at every intimate examination, and you are welcome to ask for one or to decline. You may also ask the clinician to explain any step before it happens.

## After your appointment

If you have a cervical screening test, the result arrives by letter within 14 days. If you have not received it after that time, please contact the reception team. Any follow-up tests, referrals or prescriptions will be explained to you before you leave or in the follow-up letter.
