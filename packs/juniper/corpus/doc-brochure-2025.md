# Juniper Lane Clinic patient brochure 2025

Welcome to Juniper Lane Clinic, a family practice serving Port Avery and the surrounding community. Whether you are visiting us for a routine check, a child's health review, a travel vaccination, or a follow-up after a minor procedure, our team is here to make your care clear, friendly and unhurried. This brochure explains what we offer, how to book, what to expect on the day, and how to get the most from your appointments.

## Who we are

Juniper Lane Clinic was set up to give families a single place to go for most of their health needs. Our doctors, nurses, physiotherapists and administrative staff work together so that information moves smoothly between the people caring for you. We believe good primary care depends on continuity, so wherever possible we aim to offer you the same clinician when you return, and we keep your records up to date so that every visit builds on the last.

## Our services

Our family medicine service is the heart of the clinic. We manage ongoing conditions, acute illnesses, mental wellbeing concerns, preventive care and referrals to specialists where needed. If you are new to Port Avery, we will be glad to register you and talk through your medical history at your first visit.

Child health checks are an important part of our work. We follow the national schedule for developmental reviews, growth monitoring and immunisation, and we welcome parents who have questions about feeding, sleep, school readiness or behaviour. The 2025 brochure says child health checks are for children up to age 7, and our clinicians will always tell you if your child would benefit from a visit to another service.

Our vaccination service covers routine childhood and adult immunisations as well as seasonal vaccines. Our travel clinic offers advice and vaccinations for people planning trips abroad. We recommend booking travel appointments several weeks before departure, since some vaccines need time to take effect and some courses are given in stages.

We offer blood tests on site, including routine screening and fasting blood tests. Results are reviewed by a clinician, who will contact you if anything needs attention. Full guidance for fasting blood tests is given when you book, and the 2025 brochure says to fast for 6 hours before a fasting blood test. Please drink water as normal during this time unless told otherwise.

Our physiotherapy service helps with musculoskeletal pain, sports injuries, post-operative recovery and general mobility. Your physiotherapist will assess you, agree on goals and design a programme you can follow at home. Under the Harbourline Health arrangement described in the 2025 brochure, Harbourline Health covers 12 physiotherapy sessions a year for eligible members, and our reception team can confirm your entitlement before you attend.

Our women's health service provides consultations on contraception, menstrual health, pregnancy planning, menopause and screening. We understand these conversations can feel personal, and our clinicians are happy to take the time you need.

We also carry out a range of minor procedures in our treatment room, including wound care, removal of skin lesions and joint injections. Before a planned minor procedure, you will usually attend a short pre-check so that we can confirm your suitability and answer any questions. The 2025 brochure says the pre-check is the day before a minor procedure, and our nurse will confirm the timing when you are booked in.

For patients on long-term medication, we offer repeat prescription administration. Please request repeats through the patient portal or at reception, allowing a reasonable lead time so that your prescription is ready when you need it. Our team will let you know if a review with your clinician is due before a repeat can be issued.

Telehealth calls are available for many consultations that do not require an examination. Telehealth is a convenient option for medication reviews, test result discussions and some follow-up advice. Your clinician will tell you if an in-person visit is needed instead.

## Opening hours

We are open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00, with extended hours on certain evenings for appointments and telehealth. Saturday opening is 09:00 to 14:00, which the 2025 brochure lists as our Saturday opening hours. Urgent same-day slots are limited on Saturdays, so please book early in the week where you can. For emergencies outside our opening hours, contact the emergency services or your nearest hospital.

## Booking and cancellations

You can book appointments by telephone, through our online portal or in person at reception. New patients should complete the registration form CF-10, which the 2025 brochure names as the new patient form. Please bring photo identification and details of any current medication to your first visit.

We ask that you let us know if you need to cancel or change an appointment. The 2025 brochure says appointments can be cancelled up to 12 hours ahead without a fee. Giving us notice allows us to offer the slot to another patient who may be waiting for care.

## Fees

Our fees are set out in the 2025 price list. In the 2025 price list the standard consultation cost EUR 50. Some services, such as travel vaccinations, certain medical reports and private procedures, are charged separately and are quoted before you commit to treatment. Our reception team can give you a clear estimate on request.

## Getting here and parking

The clinic is a short walk from the town centre and is well served by local bus routes. Accessible entrances and a lift are available for patients with mobility needs. The 2025 brochure says parking is free all day in the spaces adjacent to the clinic, so you can plan your visit without worrying about the meter.

## Your privacy

We take the confidentiality of your health information seriously. Your records are kept securely, shared only with those involved in your care, and handled in line with applicable data protection law. You are welcome to ask to see your records or to request corrections at any time.

## Contact us

We look forward to seeing you at Juniper Lane Clinic. If you have a question that is not answered in this brochure, please call our reception team or send a message through the patient portal, and we will respond as quickly as we can.
