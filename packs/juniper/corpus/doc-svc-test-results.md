# Getting your test results

## Who this guide is for

This guide explains how and when Juniper Lane Clinic gives you the results of your blood tests. It applies to all patients of the clinic in Port Avery, from our family medicine, child health, women's health and travel clinic services.

## How long results take

Standard blood test results are ready in 3 working days, so you can expect to hear from us within that time after your sample is taken. Send-away blood tests take 5 working days, because the samples are processed at an outside laboratory before results return to us. Fasting blood test results are ready in 3 working days, the same as standard tests, provided you followed the fasting instructions given when you booked.

Working days exclude weekends and public holidays. If a test is taking longer than expected, our reception team can tell you where it is in the process.

## How we give you your results

Results are given through the patient portal or by a call from reception. Whichever route we use, results are released only to the patient whose test it is. We will not share results with family members, friends or employers unless you have given us written permission and the law allows it.

If you have not received your results within the expected time, please contact reception rather than assuming the news is fine. Some results need a conversation with your clinician, and we will arrange this with you directly.

## What to do next

If a result needs action, your clinician will explain the next steps, which may include a repeat test, a prescription change or a referral. Keep your contact details up to date so we can reach you quickly, and check the patient portal regularly if you have registered for it.
