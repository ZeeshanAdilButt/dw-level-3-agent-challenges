# Notes, letters and forms

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains the administrative documents that Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can prepare for patients, the fees that apply, and what to expect when you request one. It is intended for patients, families, employers, schools and insurers who need paperwork connected to their healthcare. Effective from 2026-03-01.

## Fees for administrative documents

The table below lists each document we provide, the fee charged, and any turnaround time that applies. Fees are shown in euros and are payable at the time the request is collected or sent.

| Document | Fee | Turnaround | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sick note | EUR 15 | Same day | Requires a consultation before the note is issued |
| Travel letter | EUR 25 | Not stated | For patients travelling with a medical need |
| Insurer claim form (completed for a patient) | EUR 40 | Not stated | Completed by the clinician from the patient's record |
| School medical form | EUR 25 | Not stated | For pupils with a health condition or care need |
| Full medical report for a third party | EUR 90 | 15 working days | Prepared for a named third party with patient consent |

## Sick notes

A sick note costs EUR 15. Because it confirms a clinical assessment, a consultation must take place before the note is issued. This can be an in-person appointment or a telehealth call, and the clinician will decide whether a note is appropriate. Notes are not issued retrospectively for periods the clinician has not assessed.

## Travel letters

A letter for travel costs EUR 25. These letters may confirm a patient's medical condition, current treatment or fitness to travel. Please tell us the destination and the purpose of travel when you make your request so that the letter contains the correct information.

## Insurer claim forms

Completing an insurer claim form for a patient costs EUR 40. The clinician completes the medical sections from the patient's record. Patients remain responsible for the rest of the claim and for submitting it to their insurer.

## School medical forms

A school medical form costs EUR 25. These forms record health conditions, medication needs or care plans that a school should know about. Parents or guardians should bring the request to the clinic so that the information can be checked against the current record.

## Full medical reports for third parties

A full medical report for a third party costs EUR 90 and takes 15 working days. Reports are prepared only for a named third party and only with the patient's written consent. The clinician will review the record before the report is released, so the timescale reflects that review.

## How to request a document

1. Tell the reception team which document you need and why.
2. Confirm any consultation required, especially for sick notes.
3. Pay the fee listed above.
4. Allow the turnaround time shown, or collect the document at the agreed time.

Please note that fees are charged for the preparation of the document, not for the outcome. The clinic reserves the right to decline a request where the clinical information needed cannot be supported by the patient's record. For questions about any document, please speak to our reception team during opening hours.
