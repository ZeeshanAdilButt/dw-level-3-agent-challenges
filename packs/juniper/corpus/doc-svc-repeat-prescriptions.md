# Repeat prescription requests

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains how patients of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery can request a repeat prescription, what it costs, how long it takes, and when a clinician needs to review the prescription. It is an administrative guide only. It does not contain advice about medicines, doses or changes to treatment.

## How to make a request

Repeat prescription requests are made through the patient portal or at reception. The patient portal is available at any time, and requests made there go straight into the clinic's administrative queue. Patients who prefer to ask in person, or who do not use the portal, can make the same request at reception during opening hours. Please include the name of the medicine and the strength shown on your current packet so that the request can be matched to the correct record.

Please make requests in good time before you run out. The clinic cannot guarantee same-day supply, and the processing time described below applies from the point the request is received.

## Fees

The administrative fee for a repeat prescription request is EUR 12. The fee covers the handling of the request by the clinic's administrative team and is charged in addition to any charge that may apply at the pharmacy. Reception will confirm the fee before the request is submitted, and the request is processed once payment has been taken.

## Processing time

A repeat prescription request takes 2 working days to process from the date it is received. Working days exclude weekends and public holidays. If your request has been outstanding for longer than this period, please contact reception so that the team can check its status. Reception can confirm whether the request is waiting for payment, waiting for a clinician, or has been sent to your pharmacy.

## Clinician review

A repeat prescription needs a clinician review every 12 months. This is a routine safety check that ensures your medicines remain appropriate for you. When a review falls due, the clinic will let you know through the patient portal or at reception and will invite you to book an appointment. Until the review has taken place, repeat requests may be held, so it is worth booking the review in good time.

## Questions about medicines

Reception does not give advice about medicines, doses or changes. If you have a question about how a medicine works, whether a dose is right for you, or whether you should stop, start or change a treatment, reception will pass your question to a clinician. You can also book a telephone or video consultation through the clinic. Please do not rely on reception staff for clinical guidance, as their role is to process requests and direct questions to the right person.

## Contact

Questions about this guide, or about the status of a request, can be raised through the patient portal or at reception during opening hours. For urgent medical concerns, please contact a clinician directly or seek urgent care rather than waiting for a repeat prescription request to be processed.
