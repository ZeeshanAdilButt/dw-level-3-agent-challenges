# Opening hours

## Purpose

This policy sets out the opening hours of Juniper Lane Clinic in Port Avery and explains how patients can plan visits around them. It applies to all in-person appointments, walk-in requests, blood test collections, vaccination visits, and physiotherapy sessions. Telehealth calls follow the availability published for each clinician, which may differ from the hours below.

## Effective date

This policy takes effect on 2026-01-15 and replaces any earlier opening hours notice for the clinic.

## Weekly opening hours

The clinic is open at the following times each week. Please note the table below shows the standard hours for the clinic's front desk and consulting rooms.

| Day | Opening hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | 08:00 to 18:00 |
| Tuesday | 08:00 to 18:00 |
| Wednesday | 08:00 to 18:00 |
| Thursday | 08:00 to 20:00 |
| Friday | 08:00 to 18:00 |
| Saturday | 09:00 to 13:00 |
| Sunday | Closed |

## Thursday extended hours

On Thursdays the clinic stays open later than on other weekdays, with the last consulting period running until 20:00. Patients who work during the day are welcome to book child health checks, repeat prescription reviews, women's health appointments, or physiotherapy sessions in the evening on this day.

## Saturday morning service

On Saturdays the clinic is open 09:00 to 13:00. Saturday appointments are limited, so patients who need a weekday slot should book through the front desk as early as possible.

## Sunday closure

The clinic is closed on Sundays. Patients who need urgent advice on a Sunday should contact their local out-of-hours service or emergency department rather than attend the clinic.

## Fasting blood test slots

Fasting blood tests are collected at Juniper Lane Clinic by appointment only. The first fasting blood test slot each day is at 08:00, and patients booking this slot should arrive a few minutes early so that the phlebotomy team can prepare for the day. Patients who need a fasting test should follow the fasting instructions provided at booking and should bring any referral letter they have been given.

## Booking and changes

Appointments can be booked in person at the front desk, by telephone during opening hours, or through the clinic's online booking system. If a patient needs to cancel or move an appointment, they should let the clinic know as soon as possible so that another patient can be offered the slot.

## Public holidays

Opening hours on public holidays may differ from the standard timetable above. The clinic will publish any changes in advance at the front desk, on its website, and through its telephone message line. Patients should check these notices before travelling to the clinic on a public holiday.

## Contact and queries

Questions about opening hours, appointment availability, or this policy can be directed to the front desk during opening hours. Patients who are unsure whether a service is available at a particular time should ask before attending so that they do not make an unnecessary journey.

## Review

The practice manager reviews this policy whenever clinic hours change and at least once each year. Updated versions will be issued with a new effective date and will replace this version from that date.
