# Minor procedures

Juniper Lane Clinic offers a range of minor procedures carried out in our treatment room at the clinic in Port Avery. This guide explains what a minor procedure involves, how to prepare, what it costs, and what happens to any tissue that is removed. It applies from 2026-03-01.

## What a minor procedure is

A minor procedure is a short, planned treatment performed under local anaesthetic in the clinic. The most common example is skin lesion removal, where a mole, skin tag, or other small growth is taken out and checked. Minor procedures are not suitable for every concern, so your doctor will confirm during the pre-check whether the treatment is appropriate for you.

## The pre-check consultation

Before any minor procedure, you must attend a procedure pre-check consultation. This is a separate appointment that lasts 15 minutes. During the pre-check, the doctor reviews your medical history, current medicines, and any blood-thinning treatment, examines the area to be treated, explains the procedure and its risks, and answers your questions. You will also have the chance to ask about aftercare.

The pre-check must take place at least 2 days before the procedure. This gap allows the doctor to review your notes properly and gives you time to make any arrangements. If you cannot attend your pre-check in time, please contact the front desk to rebook, as we are unable to proceed with the procedure without it.

The procedure pre-check costs EUR 30 for self-pay patients. Patients whose care is covered under a publicly funded arrangement should ask the front desk how charges apply to them.

## The procedure appointment

A minor procedure appointment lasts 30 minutes, which includes preparation, the procedure itself, and a short recovery period before you leave. Please allow a little extra time for check-in. Wear loose clothing that gives easy access to the treatment area.

Minor procedures are carried out by Dr Calloway on Wednesdays and Thursdays. Procedure slots are limited, so booking early is recommended. The minor procedure has the service code MP-30, which you may need to quote when booking or when your insurer asks for details.

A minor procedure such as skin lesion removal costs EUR 180 for self-pay patients. This fee covers the procedure, the local anaesthetic, and the dressing applied afterwards. It does not include the pre-check fee described above.

## Lab results for removed tissue

Where tissue is removed during a procedure, it is sent to an external laboratory for examination. Results take 10 working days to arrive. Dr Calloway or another member of the clinical team will contact you to discuss the result once it is available. Please do not assume that a result is normal because you have not heard from us, and do contact the front desk if you have not received an update after 10 working days have passed.

## Aftercare and contacting us

You will receive written aftercare instructions at the end of your appointment. Keep the dressing dry and follow the wound care advice given. If you notice bleeding that does not stop, spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or discharge from the site, contact the clinic promptly. Outside clinic hours, seek urgent care through the appropriate local service.

For questions about booking, fees, or your pre-check, please contact the Juniper Lane Clinic front desk during opening hours.
